# Credits

A personal game for learning math. Pokémon and all related names, characters and art are © Nintendo, Creatures Inc. and GAME FREAK inc.; this project is not affiliated with them.

- **Pokémon Orange** by PiaCarrot ([github.com/PiaCarrot/pokeorange](https://github.com/PiaCarrot/pokeorange)), a Pokémon Crystal ROM hack. It's the basis of the Orange Islands region:
  - its islands, routes and wild Pokémon;
  - its trainers, the Orange Crew, and the rival Cross;
  - the trainer sprites for Cissy, Danny, Rudy, Luana, Drake (`drake_orange.png`), Tracey, Prof. Ivy, Cross, Lawrence III, Butch, Cassidy, and Jessie & James.
- **Pokémon Showdown** ([play.pokemonshowdown.com/sprites/trainers](https://play.pokemonshowdown.com/sprites/trainers/)): most trainer sprites.
- **Pokémon Journeys** (fan game): town and route backgrounds, the shared Pokémon Center and Mart pictures for Kanto, Johto, Sevii, the Orange Islands and Hoenn, Pallet Town's and Twinleaf Town's homes and Prof. Rowan's lab (`dev/builders/render_interiors.py`), the Kanto music, and the Pokémon party icons in the Gym challenge minigames; its Pokémon Essentials data (PBS: abilities, each species' abilities, gender ratios and egg groups, move flags), and its TM disc, Z-Ring and Z-Crystal icons.
- **PokeAPI** ([github.com/PokeAPI/pokeapi](https://github.com/PokeAPI/pokeapi), its CSV data): learnsets and moves, and the abilities, gender ratios and egg groups of the Pokémon and forms the Journeys data doesn't have (Gen 8–9, Galarian / Hisuian / Paldean forms); the TM list and who can learn each TM; and from its sprites repository ([github.com/PokeAPI/sprites](https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites)) the Tera Orb and Tera Shard icons.
- Gym challenge minigame ideas from **pyGuru's Python-Games** ([github.com/itspyguru/Python-Games](https://github.com/itspyguru/Python-Games)): the games are our own code, made in the Pokémon style. The chase game's idea is from the "following" chapter of **GameGrid** ([python-online.ch](https://python-online.ch/en/gamegrid/gamegrid/)).
- **Pokémon Orre 2.1.1** (fan game): Orre's town and route backgrounds, its Pokémon Center and Mart, the Orre trainer sprites, the Shadow Pokémon fog, the pearls in the chase minigame, the Game Corner's Slot Machine and Voltorb Flip pictures, and the Ruins of Alph tile puzzle panels (Pokémon Essentials' set); and the move animations, with their pictures and sounds (its Pokémon Essentials animation set, `dev/builders/import_move_animations.py`).
- A fan's GBA-style Orre maps (Reddit, "Made some GBA Orre region maps as a fun art project"): Pyrite Cave's background.
- **Pokémon Black & White Advanced** (a FireRed ROM hack; its authors aren't named in the copy we have): Unova's town and route backgrounds from Nuvema Town to Driftveil City, drawn from its maps (`dev/builders/import_gba_maps.py`); and Nuvema Town's home, Prof. Juniper's lab, and the Striaton and Nacrene Gyms.
- **Pokémon Orange Generation** by pokeant (a FireRed ROM hack): the Orange Islands backgrounds for Routes 52–54 and Murcott Island, drawn from its maps.
- **Pokémon Ultra Violet** by LocksmithArmy (a FireRed ROM hack): the backgrounds for Viridian City, Mt. Moon, Rock Tunnel, Silph Co. and Pokémon Tower, and Sevii's Three Isle Port, Navel Rock, Birth Island and Rocket Warehouse, drawn from its maps.
- **Pokémon Mega Emerald X and Y** by jovefreak1993 (an Emerald ROM hack): 18 Hoenn cave and place backgrounds (Artisan Cave to Terra Cave), drawn from its maps; and Hoenn's Gyms, the Elite Four's and Champion's rooms, Littleroot Town's home and Prof. Birch's lab.
- **Pokémon Everlasting X and Devastating Y** (Beta v1.5, a Pokémon Essentials fan remake of X and Y): Kalos's backgrounds for Routes 2–7 and 22, Santalune Forest, Parfum Palace, Glittering Cave and Connecting Cave, drawn from its maps (`dev/builders/render_exy_backgrounds.py`); and the Santalune and Cyllage Gyms, Prof. Sycamore's lab and Vaniville Town's home.
- **Sword and Shield Ultimate Plus** (a FireRed ROM hack by Pclg and Jeanstars, translated by Phantonomy; tiles by KingTapir): Galar's town and route backgrounds, from the maps on its wiki ([github.com/Ddaretrogamer/sword-and-shield-ultimate-plus-wiki](https://github.com/Ddaretrogamer/sword-and-shield-ultimate-plus-wiki)), and Wyndon's, drawn from the maps of its v3.1.5 ROM.
- **Serebii.net** (PokéEarth) and **Bulbapedia**: the references for areas, wild Pokémon, trainers and story walkthroughs.
- **Bulbapedia** ([bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net)): the town pictures without a fan-game map, and the Safari Zone area pictures (FireRed/LeafGreen, HeartGold/SoulSilver, Emerald, and Platinum's Great Marsh) with their area encounter tables.
