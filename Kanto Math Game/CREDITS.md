- **Pokémon Journeys** (fan game), its Pokédex: the Pokédex's look (its list, INFO, AREA, FORMS and search screens, type labels and marks), the footprints, and each species' kind, height, weight, colour, shape and habitat (its PBS data; `dev/builders/import_pokedex.py`). Gen 8–9 species' facts from **PokeAPI**'s tables.
- **Pokémon Journeys** (fan game), its overworld sprites: the trainers, townsfolk, legendaries and objects on the route maps (`assets/dungeon/characters`, `dev/builders/import_dungeon_sprites.py`).
- **Pokémon Journeys** (fan game), its Pokémon Essentials Berry plants: the Berry-tree pictures at each stage and the soil (`assets/dungeon/berries`), how many hours each Berry takes a stage and how many it bears (its PBS berryplants.txt; `src/world/berries.py`), and the icons of the Berries we didn't have, the five glass flutes and the Wailmer Pail.
- **PokeAPI** ([github.com/PokeAPI/pokeapi](https://github.com/PokeAPI/pokeapi), its CSV data): learnsets and moves, and the abilities, gender ratios and egg groups of the Pokémon and forms the Journeys data doesn't have (Gen 8–9, Galarian / Hisuian / Paldean forms); the TM list and who can learn each TM; and from its sprites repository ([github.com/PokeAPI/sprites](https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites)) the Tera Orb and Tera Shard icons. Legends: Z-A's new Mega Evolutions' stats and types are from its tables too (`dev/builders/import_za_megas.py`); their Mega Stones' pictures are the game's own Venusaurite recoloured.

The chase game's idea is from the "following" chapter of **GameGrid** ([python-online.ch](https://python-online.ch/en/gamegrid/gamegrid/)).
- **Pokémon Orre 2.1.1** (fan game): its PikaFly minigame's art (`assets/minigames/pikafly`) and the skies behind the Fly scenes (`assets/fly`); the Pikafly Gym game is rebuilt from it; Orre's town and route backgrounds, its Pokémon Center and Mart, the Orre trainer sprites, the Shadow Pokémon fog, the pearls in the chase minigame, the Game Corner's Slot Machine and Voltorb Flip pictures, and the Ruins of Alph tile puzzle panels (Pokémon Essentials' set), the surfing Pokémon you and swimmers ride (`base_surf.png`); and the move animations, with their pictures and sounds (its Pokémon Essentials animation set, `dev/builders/import_move_animations.py`).

- **Pokémon Platinum** (Game Freak / Nintendo): the Sinnoh towns, routes and lakes are laid out tile for tile from its own map data (where you can walk, doors, ledges, trees, fences, water); Bulbapedia's Platinum pictures give the roof colours. Sinnoh's buildings, lamps, fences, statues and furniture are drawn from its own models (`assets/tiles/pt`). Sinnoh's ground (grass, paths, trees, water, cliffs, floors and walls) is its own too (`assets/tiles/pt_ground`), and the people on Sinnoh's maps are its overworld characters (`assets/dungeon/characters/pt_*.png`). The insides of Sinnoh's buildings, caves and Team Galactic's bases are laid out from its rooms the same way, with their floor, wall and furniture colours read from the game's own textures.
- **Pokémon SoulSilver** (Game Freak / Nintendo): Johto's towns and routes, from New Bark Town on, are laid out tile for tile from its own map data (where you can walk, ledges, doors, trees, fences, water, building colours).
- **Pokémon Emerald** (Game Freak / Nintendo): Hoenn's towns and routes are being rebuilt from its own map data (where you can walk, ledges, grass, water, doors, ways out) and drawn with its own tiles (`assets/tiles/em_ground`, and `em_top` for what is drawn over you); so far Littleroot Town to Fortree City (Routes 101-106, 109-123, Petalburg Woods, Rusturf Tunnel, Granite Cave, the Fiery Path, Meteor Falls, Mt. Where its people stand and how they move (wander, look about, walk up to you) is Emerald's too. The story scenes acted out on those maps follow Bulbapedia's Omega Ruby / Alpha Sapphire walkthrough, in our own words. Lisia's and Aarune's overworld sprites (`assets/dungeon/characters/lisia.png`, `aarune.png`) were supplied by Jordan. Where its soft soil lies and the Berry each tree starts with are Emerald's own (its new-game Berry-tree script). Gabby and Ty's battle picture (`assets/sprites/trainers/interviewers.png`) is Emerald's own Interviewer picture. Brendan's and May's wheelie and hop on the Jagged Pass's round stones are Emerald's own Acro Bike frames (`assets/dungeon/characters/em_acro_*.png`). Hoenn's trainers and the people on its rebuilt maps are its overworld characters, each trainer class as Emerald's own maps show it (`assets/dungeon/characters/em_*.png`), and so are Brendan and May when you play as them.
