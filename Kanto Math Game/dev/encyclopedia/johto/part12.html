Goldenrod City (again)

Team Rocket isn't finished. With the Mahogany hideout gone, they have marched into Goldenrod City and seized the Radio Tower. Grunts stand all over the streets, telling you to scram and boasting that Goldenrod is theirs now. The road east of Mahogany Town stays shut until you deal with them, so head back to Goldenrod.

{{journal:johto_m15b}}

The Grunt at the door

A Rocket Grunt guards the Radio Tower's front door. He recognises you from your badges and won't let you anywhere near the Director. Beat him and you're inside.

Radio Tower

Goldenrod's Radio Tower has five floors and an Observation Deck on top. Team Rocket has filled it with Grunts and scientists, and their Executives hold the top floors. They want to broadcast a message to the whole region, calling their boss, Giovanni, back.

{{tables:RADIO_TOWER}}

1F and 2F

The visitors and the quiz lady on 1F have all gone. On 2F the police officer who blocked the stairs has stepped aside, and the floor is full of Grunts.

{{trainers:RADIO_TOWER:2F}}

3F and 4F

More Grunts and a Rocket scientist wait on 3F. One door on this floor is locked and needs a Card Key, so for now take the stairs you can reach.

{{trainers:RADIO_TOWER:3F}}

4F has a Grunt and another scientist, and a TM for Captivate lies by a pillar.

{{trainers:RADIO_TOWER:4F}}

5F: The fake Director

On 5F a man is standing in for the Director. It's Executive Petrel. Beat him and he gives you the Basement Key. The real Director has been locked away somewhere under the city: the Basement Key opens the door in the Goldenrod Underground.

{{battle:RADIO_TOWER:Petrel}}

Goldenrod Underground

The Goldenrod Underground is a tunnel running under the whole city, with ways down from the streets. Shops and services line its side rooms. At the far end, a locked door marked "No Entry" leads to a warehouse, and from there to a back room where Team Rocket has hidden the Director.

{{tables:GOLDENROD_UNDERGROUND}}

The Max Potion and Revive in the back room, and the Paralyze Heal, Super Potion and Antidote in the Dept. Store's basement, are hidden: step on their spots to find them.

Rival battle

The first time you come down here with the Glacier Badge, your rival is waiting, and he wants a battle.

{{battle:GOLDENROD_UNDERGROUND:rival}}

The shops

A Rocket Grunt hangs around the shops, insisting there's nothing to see down here. The shops are still open, though.

  • The herb shop sells Energy Powder, Energy Root, Heal Powder and Revival Herb. Herbal medicine is cheap, but it's very bitter.
  • On Mondays, the bargain shop sells Nuggets, Pearls, Big Pearls, Stardust and Star Pieces.
  • Two brothers run the Pokémon Salon. Once a day, each gives the first Pokémon in your team a trim, which makes it fonder of you. The younger brother is still learning, so the older brother's trim pleases it more.
  • At the Memorial Photo Studio, a photographer takes a picture of you and your team.
{{trainers:GOLDENROD_UNDERGROUND:Booths}}

The warehouse

With the Basement Key, open the "No Entry" door. The warehouse behind it is guarded by Team Rocket Grunts and two Burglars. Metal shutters block the way: each is opened by its own switch on a control panel. Press switches 1, 2 and 3 to open all three. The stairs to the back room are behind shutter 3.

{{trainers:GOLDENROD_UNDERGROUND:Warehouse}}

The back room: the Director

Three more Grunts guard the back room. Past them is the real Director of the Radio Tower. Team Rocket locked him down here and took over his tower. He gives you the Card Key, which opens the locked door on the Tower's 3F, and begs you to stop them before they broadcast to the whole region.

{{trainers:GOLDENROD_UNDERGROUND:Back room}}

The back room's other stairs lead up into the Goldenrod Dept. Store's basement.

The Dept. Store basement

The Dept. Store's stockroom is full of crates. Two Black Belts and their Machoke are tidying up. Talk to them and the Machoke shift the crates out of the way; they say anything lying on the floor is junk, so you can take it. An attendant runs the lift up into the store.

Radio Tower (again)

3F: The locked door

Back in the Radio Tower, the Card Key opens the locked door on 3F, and the rest of the tower is open to you.

The Observation Deck: Archer

The stairs from 5F lead up to the Observation Deck. Executive Archer is here, sure that Giovanni will hear their broadcast. His Houndour and Houndoom are Dark and Fire types, so Water, Ground and Rock moves work well.

{{battle:RADIO_TOWER:Archer}}

Archer admits they've failed. Team Rocket is breaking up, for good this time. The Grunts leave Goldenrod, and the Radio Tower is back to normal: the quiz lady and the visitors return to 1F, and Buena is back on 2F.

With the Radio Tower free, the road east of Mahogany Town is open at last. Go back to Mahogany and take Route 44.

Route 44

Route 44 runs east from Mahogany Town towards the mountains. It's a route of grass, ponds and strong trainers, ending at the mouth of the Ice Path.

{{tables:JOHTO_ROUTE_44}}

The Elixir is hidden: step on its spot to find it.

{{trainers:JOHTO_ROUTE_44}}

Ice Path

The Ice Path is a frozen cave cut through the mountain between Route 44 and Blackthorn City. It has four floors: 1F, B1F, B2F and B3F. Much of the floor is sheet ice: step on it and you slide until something stops you, so look at where each slide will leave you before you set off.

{{journal:johto_m16}} {{tables:ICE_PATH}}

The Max Potion and Ice Heal on 1F, the Revive on B1F and the Carbos on B2F are hidden: step on their spots to find them.

{{trainers:ICE_PATH}}

1F

Slide across the ice to reach the stairs down. HM Waterfall is tucked away in a dark, icy corner of this floor: pick it up. You can use Waterfall outside battle once you have the Rising Badge.

B1F

Boulders stand near holes in the floor. Use Strength to push them in: each one drops through to the floor below. A TM for Avalanche is on this floor.

B2F and B3F

B2F is another big ice rink, and B3F has a cracked rock you can break with Rock Smash. Work your way down and back up again: the far stairs bring you back to 1F's other side, where the way out leads to Blackthorn City. A Never-Melt Ice, a PP Up and a Full Heal are on B2F, and a Max Potion is on B3F.
