Azalea Town

Azalea Town is a quiet, leafy town at the end of Route 33, famous for Kurt, who makes Poké Balls from Apricorns. Ilex Forest lies to the west, and the old Slowpoke Well sits at the edge of town. When you arrive, something is wrong: the town's Slowpoke have all vanished, and the Gym is empty because Bugsy has gone off to help.

{{journal:johto_m5}} {{tables:AZALEA_TOWN}}

A Full Heal is hidden in town. The White Apricorn tree can only be picked once you carry the Apricorn Box, and it grows a new Apricorn each day.

Team Rocket at the Well

Walk toward the Slowpoke Well and a Rocket Grunt stops you. He brags that Team Rocket is back, three years after it broke up, and tells you the Well is off limits. Another Grunt in town drops a hint about what they're up to: he's heard Slowpoke tails are delicious. An old man is sure the tails are being sold somewhere, and a man tells you Kurt is tough enough to sort the crooks out.

Kurt's House

Kurt is furious: Team Rocket is cutting the tails off the Slowpoke in the Well. He storms off to stop them himself, and the Grunt guarding the Well is gone after that. Follow him down.

Slowpoke Well

The Slowpoke Well is a damp cave under Azalea Town, with a shaft and two floors below it. It's dark and wet, so Zubat are everywhere, and Slowpoke live in the water.

{{tables:SLOWPOKE_WELL}}

Well shaft

Kurt is lying at the bottom of the shaft. He jumped down after the crooks and hurt his back on the landing, so it's up to you. A Great Ball is hidden down here.

B1F

Three Rocket Grunts are working on this floor, each proud of their tail business.

{{trainers:SLOWPOKE_WELL}}

Their leader, Executive Proton, is also in the Well. He calls himself the scariest of the Rocket Executives.

{{battle:SLOWPOKE_WELL:Proton}}

Beat him and Team Rocket gives up on the tails. The Well is clear, the Slowpoke can go home, and Bugsy heads back to his Gym.

B2F

On the lowest floor, a researcher is waiting patiently to see a Slowpoke evolve. He gives you a King's Rock: a Slowpoke holding one evolves when it's traded.

Azalea Town (again)

The Slowpoke are back, lazing around town as if nothing happened. Visit Kurt again: he thanks you with five balls and a Fast Ball he made from a White Apricorn. From now on, bring him Apricorns from the trees around Johto and he turns each one into a special ball on the spot.

Charcoal Kiln

The charcoal maker is worried: his Farfetch'd have run off, and without them he can't cut wood for charcoal. His apprentice has gone to look in Ilex Forest. Come back after you've helped them in the forest and the apprentice gives you Charcoal, which powers up Fire moves for the Pokémon holding it.

Pokémon Center and Poké Mart

Heal up and stock up before the Gym. A woman by the Center says lots of people come to Azalea just to have Kurt make them balls.

Azalea Gym

Bugsy trains Bug Pokémon. Until the Slowpoke Well is cleared, he's away helping Kurt and the Gym won't let you challenge him.

Four trainers stand in the way. The Twins Amy & Mimi battle you together, in a double battle.

Ilex Forest

Ilex Forest is a deep, dark wood west of Azalea Town. The trees are so thick that sunlight barely reaches the ground, and the path winds a long way before it comes out at the gate to Route 34. An old woman in Azalea reminds you that the forest has a protector, and to mind your manners.

{{tables:ILEX_FOREST}}

Many items here are hidden: mushrooms, medicine and a Repel among them. TM050 Rain Dance lies among the roots, and the Gate at the forest's north end holds TM087 Taunt.

{{trainers:ILEX_FOREST}}

The lost Farfetch'd

The charcoal maker's apprentice is in the forest, in a panic. His boss's two Farfetch'd ran off into the forest, and they won't listen to someone with no badge. Find them both: when you reach each one, it squawks and heads straight back to the charcoal maker.

With both birds home and the Slowpoke Well cleared, talk to the charcoal maker in the forest. He gives you HM Cut, which chops down small trees. A skinny tree blocks the way north, so teach Cut to one of your Pokémon. You need the Hive Badge before it will use it on the map.

The shrine and the Headbutt tutor

A small shrine stands in the forest, honouring the protector of the woods. A man nearby is shaking trees with Headbutt to see what falls out, and he offers to teach the move to one of your Pokémon.

Much later. After you become Champion and have shown Kurt the GS Ball from Goldenrod's Pokémon Center, bring it to the shrine to meet Celebi. It needs all eight Johto badges.

Gate

The gatehouse at the north end of the forest leads to Route 34. A woman here hears you're filling a Pokédex and gives you TM087 Taunt. A girl asks whether you've seen the shrine.

{{journal:johto_m7}}

Route 34

Route 34 runs north from Ilex Forest to Goldenrod City. It's busy with trainers, and the Pokémon Day Care stands beside the path. Drowzee are common in the grass, and Ditto turns up here too.

{{tables:JOHTO_ROUTE_34}}

Headbutt trees need the Rocky Helmet. A Rare Candy and a Super Potion are hidden on the route.

{{trainers:JOHTO_ROUTE_34}}

Pokémon Day Care

As you reach the Day Care, Lyra runs over: it belongs to her grandparents. She explains that a Pokémon left there grows while you travel, and that two Pokémon who get along sometimes find an Egg.

The Day-Care Man has a surprise of his own. He's found an odd Egg and nobody knows whose it is, so he gives it to you. Make room in your party first. After it hatches, come back on a later day: another odd Egg turns up, seven in all, each a different baby Pokémon.
