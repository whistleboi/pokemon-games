Professor Elm's Pokémon Lab stands next to your house in New Bark Town, and it's where your journey begins. Elm lets you choose your first Pokémon here (answer two math questions to make it yours), and his aide gives you five Potions for the road. Elm sends you off to see Mr. Pokémon on Route 30 (Part 1).

When you come back with the Mystery Egg, a Pokémon has been stolen from the Lab, and the thief sounds a lot like your rival. Elm keeps the Egg to study and gives you ten Poké Balls (Part 2). Visit him again after you become Champion and he gives you the S.S. Ticket for the ship to Kanto (Part 17).

Your house, in the middle of New Bark Town. You wake up upstairs in your bedroom. Talk to Mom downstairs any time and she heals your team, and once you have your first Pokémon she gives you the Pokégear (Part 1).

A neighbour's house. The lady here misses her son, who is away travelling.

Professor Elm's own house. His wife says he practically lives at the Lab.

The Guide Gent's house. When you first walk into Cherrygrove City, the old man shows you around town, and the tour ends here: he loads the Map Card onto your Pokégear so the Town Map always shows where you are (Part 2).

A fisherman's house. He gives you Mystic Water, which makes a Water-type's moves hit harder when it holds it.

A boy and a girl here talk about how Pokémon grow and evolve, and about natures.

The little house on Route 30, near Mr. Pokémon's. The man inside gives you the Apricorn Box ours, so you can pick Apricorns from the trees around Johto, one per tree each day. Kurt in Azalea Town turns them into special balls (Part 2).

Falkner's Gym, the first in Johto. It stays shut until you've beaten Elder Li at the top of Sprout Tower. Two Bird Keepers stand between you and Falkner at the far end. Falkner uses Flying Pokémon, so Electric, Ice and Rock moves are your friends.

Win the Zephyr Badge, TM268 Roost, five Poké Balls and the Johto Map. The badge lets you use Flash on the map and opens the road south to Route 32 (Part 3). See Gyms.

Earl's Pokémon Academy. Earl and his students chat about held items, trading and Abilities. Nobody gives you anything here, but it's a good place to pick up tips.

Violet's Pokémon Center has a visitor you'll want to see. After you win the Zephyr Badge, Professor Elm sends his aide here with the Egg from Mr. Pokémon: talk to her to get it back, and it hatches as you travel (Part 3). Primo, a TV star, hangs around here too.

A girl here has named her Pidgey Strawberry, after a food she loves.

Kyle lives here and collects Pokémon. Bring him a Bellsprout and he trades you his Onix, Rocky, which holds a Persim Berry. The man with him warns that a traded Pokémon may not obey you without the right badge. See In-game trades.

The Pokémon Center halfway down Route 32. A fishing fan here gives you the Old Rod, your first rod. If you carry the Apricorn Box, an Apricorn fan gives you a Lure Ball ours, which works best on Pokémon you fish up (Part 4).

Bugsy's Gym, over two rooms. Bugsy is away helping with the trouble at the Slowpoke Well, so you can't challenge him until Team Rocket is gone. Four trainers stand in the way, among them the Twins Amy & Mimi, who battle you together in a double battle.

Bugsy uses Bug Pokémon; Fire, Flying and Rock moves work well. Win the Hive Badge, TM060 U-turn, three Rare Candies and five Poké Balls. With the Hive Badge, your Pokémon can use Cut on the map (Part 5).

The Charcoal Kiln. The charcoal maker's Farfetch'd have run off into Ilex Forest, and his apprentice has gone after them. Find both birds in the forest, then come back here and the apprentice gives you Charcoal, which powers up Fire moves (Part 5).

Kurt makes Poké Balls from Apricorns. When you first arrive he's furious about Team Rocket at the Slowpoke Well and storms off to stop them. After you clear the Well, he thanks you with a Fast Ball, and from then on he turns any Apricorn you bring into a special ball on the spot (Part 5).

After you become Champion, show Kurt the GS Ball from Goldenrod's Pokémon Center: it's the first step toward meeting Celebi at the Ilex Forest shrine.

The Ruins Research Center studies the Ruins of Alph. The researcher tells you Unown come in 28 forms and asks you to catch them. Catch your first and he gives you the Unown Report, to keep track of every form you find (Part 4).

The ruins' main entrance, which leads to the inner hall where Unown live. Much later it hides one more secret: once the way has opened, bring Arceus in your party and the Unown on the walls carry you away to the Sinjoh Ruins.

The inner hall. Unown only appear here once you've solved one of the four stone slab puzzles.

One of the four small chambers. Put the stone slab back together to make a picture of Kabuto. Then the Unown letters on the far wall spell ESCAPE: carry an Escape Rope and the wall slides open to a hidden room (Part 4).

The hidden room behind the Kabuto chamber's wall, with four items to pick up.

The chamber with the Aerodactyl slab. Its wall spells LIGHT: bring HM Flash, from Sprout Tower, and the wall slides open (Part 4).

The hidden room behind the Aerodactyl chamber's wall. A Moon Stone is one of its four items.

The chamber with the Omanyte slab. Its wall spells WATER: carry a Water Stone and the wall slides open (Part 4).

The hidden room behind the Omanyte chamber's wall. A Star Piece is one of its four items.

The chamber with the Ho-Oh slab. Its wall spells HO-OH, so it only opens much later, when you bring Ho-Oh in your party (Part 4).

The hidden room behind the Ho-Oh chamber's wall. Its four items include a Life Orb.

The gatehouse at the north end of Ilex Forest, leading to Route 34. A woman here hears you're filling a Pokédex and gives you TM087 Taunt (Part 5).

The Pokémon Day Care belongs to Lyra's grandparents. Leave a Pokémon here and it grows while you travel, and two Pokémon who get along may find an Egg. The Day-Care Man also has a surprise: an odd Egg that nobody can explain, with more turning up on later days (Part 5). See Day Care.

The gatehouse between Goldenrod City and Route 35. The guard asks you to carry his Spearow, Kenya, to his friend dozing on Route 31; she has a letter for him. Deliver it and come back for an HP Up. A tinkerer here talks about turning a Pokégear into a radio (Part 6).

Whitney's Gym, home of Normal Pokémon. Five Lasses and Beauties stand between you and Whitney. Fighting moves work well against her team.

Win the Plain Badge, TM240 Attract, five Poké Balls and the Squirt Bottle ours, for watering the strange tree on Route 36. The badge lets your Pokémon use Strength on the map, once you have that HM. Afterwards, Whitney waits on the Dept. Store's top floor to swap phone numbers (Part 6).

The Miracle Cycle Bike Shop has only just opened, and nobody can find it. The owner lends you a Bicycle so you can ride around Johto and show it off (Part 6).

Goldenrod's Flower Shop. Floria, who works here, is out watering a strange tree on Route 36. Once you've cleared that tree with the Squirt Bottle she gives you two Berries there (Part 7).

Goldenrod's Pokémon Center has one special visitor much later: after you become Champion, an attendant hands you the GS Ball, a strange ball nobody can open. She suggests showing it to Kurt in Azalea Town (Part 17).

Goldenrod's Game Corner plays Voltorb Flip: flip cards to find the numbers while dodging the Voltorb, reading the totals along the side of the board. You need the Coin Case, which a man in the street gives you. Swap your coins for prizes at the Prize Corner; a Porygon is among them (Part 6). See Game Corners.

Johto's big department store: six floors joined by an elevator, in the middle of Goldenrod City. Its counters sell more than any Poké Mart, including TMs you can't buy elsewhere (Part 6). See Department Stores.

Special people

On 5F, a trader swaps his Machop, Muscle, holding a Macho Brace, for a Drowzee. On Sundays a lady on 5F checks how your first Pokémon feels about you and gives TM274 Return if it loves you or TM273 Frustration if not, once a week. On 6F, the Daily Drawing costs $300, once a day, for a Berry, a Poké Ball or even a TM. After you beat Whitney, she's up on 6F too.

The Magnet Train runs from here to Saffron City in Kanto. It needs a Pass, and it can't run until the power is back on in Kanto, so this is for much later.

Bill, who made the Pokémon Storage System, lives here with his mother and sister. He's away at first, checking the PCs in Ecruteak City. Meet him there, then come back and he gives you an Eevee (Part 8).

A man here looks at your first Pokémon and tells you how much it likes you.

Two people here explain what happens when a Pokémon runs out of PP, and how to fix it.

The Name Rater rates the nickname of a Pokémon you caught, and changes it if you like. You can't rename a Pokémon you got in a trade.

The gate from Route 35 into the National Park. The Contest helper at the desk signs you up for the Bug-Catching Contest, which you can enter any day, once a day ours (Part 7). A workman says the Pokéathlon Dome up ahead isn't finished.

An old man here remembers the day a huge Flying Pokémon blotted out the sun and flew away toward the sea.

A trainer here gives you the Itemfinder, which points out items hidden where you can't see them. A book on his shelf tells the story of Ecruteak's two towers (Part 8).

Morty's Gym is pitch dark. You can only see a little way around you, and the floor is a narrow path with drops on both sides: step off and you fall, landing back by the entrance. Feel your way forward to Morty at the far end, past Sages and Mediums. The Gym stays shut until you've seen the legendary beasts in the Burned Tower.

Morty uses Ghost Pokémon, which Normal and Fighting moves can't touch. Win the Fog Badge, TM114 Shadow Ball, an Amulet Coin and five Poké Balls; a Super Rod turns up in your Bag too ours. The badge lets you Surf on the map (Part 8).

Moomoo Farm on Route 39 is famous for its milk, but its Miltank, Moomoo, is sick. Feed her seven Oran Berries in the barn. Once she's well, the farmer's wife gives you TM303 Natural Gift, and the farmhouse sells Moomoo Milk from then on (Part 8).

Moomoo the sick Miltank lies in the barn. Talk to her with Oran Berries in your Bag to feed her; she needs seven.

Jasmine's Gym is a long hall with Jasmine at the far end and no other trainers. At first she isn't here: she's caring for the sick Ampharos at the top of the Lighthouse, and she only comes back once you bring medicine from Cianwood City.

Jasmine uses Steel Pokémon; Fire, Fighting and Ground moves work well. Win the Mineral Badge, TM265 Iron Tail, three Rare Candies and five Poké Balls (Part 10).

The Fishing Guru has fished here for thirty years, and he gives you the Good Rod, which hooks stronger Pokémon than the Old Rod (Part 9).

The Olivine Cafe, where sailors come for big meals and chat about the sea.

A man here wants a Krabby. Trade him one and you get his Voltorb, Billy, holding a Cheri Berry. See In-game trades.

A man here tells you about the four little islands on the way to Cianwood.

Ships leave from Olivine Harbor. The S.S. Aqua sails to Vermilion City in Kanto, once you have the S.S. Ticket that Professor Elm gives the new Champion (Part 17). A ferry to the Orange Islands docks here too ours, but it only takes the Champion of both Kanto and Johto.

Chuck's Gym is built like a mountain, with a roaring waterfall down the middle. Chuck trains under it and can't hear you over the water. Four Black Belts spot you and march over to fight. Climb round the east side and over the bridge to the top, and press the big red button: the winch stops the waterfall, and Chuck finally notices you.

Chuck uses Fighting Pokémon; Flying and Psychic moves work well. Win the Storm Badge, TM192 Focus Punch, five Poké Balls and the Rock Smasher ours. The badge lets you Fly on the map, and Chuck's wife gives you HM Fly (Part 10).

The pharmacist makes medicine the old way, to order. Once you've seen Jasmine's sick Ampharos at the Olivine Lighthouse, he makes you the Secret Potion to cure it. The Pharmacy has a shop counter too (Part 10).

Kirk is scared stiff: a red-haired boy came by and frightened him. He's afraid the boy will steal his Pokémon, so he asks you to look after his Shuckle, Shuckie, who comes holding a Berry Juice (Part 10).

A family here talks about the sea creature near the Whirl Islands, and the special bell it answers to.

The photographer takes a picture of you and your team.

An old man here says the bell at the top of Ecruteak's Bell Tower rings clear when the legendary Pokémon is near.

Pryce's Gym is a frozen hall over three rooms. Step onto the ice and you slide until something stops you, so work out which way to push off to reach each exit. Seven Skiers and Boarders wait inside. Pryce won't battle anyone while Team Rocket is up to something in town, so clear their hideout first.

Pryce uses Ice Pokémon; Fighting, Rock, Steel and Fire moves work well, but Electric moves can't touch his Piloswine. Win the Glacier Badge, TM241 Hail, five Poké Balls and the Ice Melter. The badge lets you use Whirlpool on the map (Part 11).

A man here has heard about a red Gyarados at the Lake of Rage, and a girl chats about the radio.

Mahogany's souvenir shop says its Rage Candy Bars are sold out, and something about it feels wrong. After the Red Gyarados at the Lake of Rage, Lance is waiting inside with his Dragonite: the shop is a front for Team Rocket, and the stairs behind the counter lead down to their hideout (Part 11).

A hermit has meditated by the lake for a long time, and he gives you TM272 Hidden Power (Part 11).

The Fishing Guru wants to see the biggest Magikarp in the lake. Bring one in your team and he measures it. Every new record earns an Ether, and your first record an Elixir as well (Part 11).

Clair's Gym, the last in Johto: one big hall with Clair at the far end and five Ace Trainers on the way. She only accepts challengers with seven Johto badges. Her team is Dragons and Water Pokémon, so Ice moves help against her Dragonair and Electric moves against Gyarados.

Beating her isn't the end. Clair won't hand over the badge, and sends you to the Dragon Shrine in the Dragon's Den behind the Gym. Pass the Elder's test there and she gives you the Rising Badge, with the Mt. Silver Map (Part 13).

An old woman here tells you about the dragon clan that lives in Blackthorn.

A trader here wants a Dragonair. Trade one and you get the trader's Dodrio, Doris, holding a Smoke Ball. See In-game trades.

Four people share this house. The Move Reminder and Move Deleter have nothing to do here, since your Pokémon never forget a move ours. The other two are tutors: Grandma Wilma teaches Draco Meteor to a Dragon-type that loves you very much, and an old man teaches Frenzy Plant, Blast Burn and Hydro Cannon to fully evolved first partners that love you. Each tutor sells the move once, at a TM's price, and it's yours for good ours (Part 13). See TMs and tutors.

A thoughtful man near Tohjo Falls asks to see your first Pokémon. If it's fond enough of you, he gives you TM051 Sandstorm; if not, come back after more time together (Part 15).

A little Rest House partway up Route 26, on the long road to the League. The woman inside heals your whole team (Part 15).

The Safari Zone's gatehouse, past Route 48. The receptionist can rearrange the Safari Zone's six areas, choosing from twelve layouts, so different habitats are next to each other (Part 17). See Safari Game.

Three Move Tutors at Frontier Access, off Route 40, teach moves for Battle Points from the Battle Frontier. The left one teaches punches and tricks like Fire Punch, Knock Off and Zen Headbutt; the middle one power moves like Outrage, Superpower and Heat Wave; the right one clever moves like Heal Bell, Tailwind and Gravity. Each costs 32 to 64 BP, and once paid for, the move is yours for good (Part 17). See TMs and tutors.

The last Pokémon Center before Mt. Silver's summit, in the clearing at the foot of the mountain. Heal up here before the climb (Part 17).

The Silver League's front hall, at the top of Victory Road on the Indigo Plateau. There's a counter to heal your team and a shop, so stock up: the Elite Four battle you one after another with no rest, and you can't leave until it's over (Part 16).

The first room of the League. Will uses Psychic Pokémon; Dark, Bug and Ghost moves work well. The door on the far side only opens once you've won (Part 16).

The second room. Koga uses Poison Pokémon and tricky moves like Toxic and Double Team; Ground and Psychic moves are good choices (Part 16).

The third room. Bruno's team is mostly Fighting Pokémon, with an Onix too; Psychic and Flying moves hit his fighters hard (Part 16).

The fourth room. Karen uses mostly Dark Pokémon, with a Vileplume and a Gengar; Fighting moves work well on her Umbreon and Houndoom (Part 16).

The Champion's room, where Lance waits with his Dragons. Three of his Pokémon are Dragonite, so bring Ice moves. Win and Professor Oak hurries in to congratulate you, then the door to the Hall of Fame opens (Part 16).

The Hall of Fame, where every Champion is honoured. Lance leads you in and your team is entered in the record, then you head home to New Bark Town (Part 16).

A celebrity hides out here, resting after years in the spotlight. Since you're not the press, she gives you TM239 Steel Wing (Part 17).

The Battle Tower, the tallest of the Battle Frontier's five facilities. Talk to the attendant to start a streak of single battles against Frontier trainers. Win 5 in a row and its Frontier Brain, Tower Tycoon Palmer, battles you; after that, 10 in a row for his stronger team. Each win earns Battle Points. Fans of the Frontier crowd the lobby (Part 17). See Battle facilities.

The Battle Factory, one of the five Frontier facilities. Win 5 battles in a row and Factory Head Thorton battles you, with a random strong team; then 10 in a row for his second battle. Wins earn Battle Points. See Battle facilities.

The Battle Hall: round after round of tough trainers. Win 5 in a row and Hall Matron Argenta battles you with a random strong team; then 10 in a row for her second battle. Wins earn Battle Points. See Battle facilities.

The Battle Castle, one trainer after another. Win 5 in a row and Castle Valet Darach battles you with his Empoleon, Staraptor and Houndoom; then 10 in a row for his stronger team. Wins earn Battle Points. See Battle facilities.

The Battle Arcade, all bright lights and big battles. Win 5 in a row and Arcade Star Dahlia battles you with her Medicham, Ludicolo and Dusknoir; then 10 in a row for her stronger team. Wins earn Battle Points. See Battle facilities.

The gate at the Battle Frontier's entrance. The attendant welcomes you to the five facilities, and the stairs behind him lead back down to Frontier Access and Route 40.
