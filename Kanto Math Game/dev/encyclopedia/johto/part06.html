Goldenrod City

Goldenrod City is the biggest city in Johto, so big that an old man in the street admits he can never find anything. It has a huge Department Store, the Radio Tower, a Game Corner, a Bike Shop and Whitney's Gym, with an Underground beneath it all. Route 34 leads back south, and Route 35 leads north out of the city.

The Journal's first step is a look at the strange tree on Route 36, north past Route 35 (see Part 7). You can't move it yet, so come back here for the Gym afterwards.

A Super Potion is hidden in the city. The GS Ball only turns up after you become Champion.

Around Goldenrod

A man in the street found a Coin Case in the Underground, but he never plays the slots, so he gives it to you. You need it to play at the Game Corner. A boy has heard about a new Bike Shop but can't find it, and a woman says the Radio Tower is running a big promotion that upgrades your Pokégear.

The Magnet Train station has trains to Saffron City in Kanto, but it needs a Pass and can't run yet. That's for much later.

Goldenrod Dept. Store

The Dept. Store has six floors, joined by an elevator. Besides the usual Poké Mart goods, its counters sell TMs you won't find in most Marts: Protect, Reflect, Light Screen, False Swipe, Thunder Punch, Fire Punch and Ice Punch, and stronger moves like Solar Beam, Fire Blast, Thunder, Blizzard, Hyper Beam and Focus Blast.

1F to 4F

Shoppers talk about bargains, X items and vitamins: X Speed to hurry a Pokémon up, Protein for Attack and Iron for Defense. A kid lists the Pokémon that only evolve when they're traded.

5F

A trader collects Pokémon. Bring him a Drowzee and he swaps it for his Machop, MUSCLE, which comes holding a Macho Brace. On Sundays a lady comes to see how your first Pokémon feels about you. If it loves you, she gives you TM274 Return; if not, TM273 Frustration. She gives one TM a week.

6F

The Daily Drawing costs $300 a go, once a day. You might win a Berry, a Poké Ball or even a TM. There are vending machines up here too. After you beat Whitney, she's on this floor and swaps phone numbers with you.

Bike Shop

The Bike Shop has only just opened, and nobody knows about it yet. The owner lends you a Bicycle so you can ride around Johto and show it off. Use it to get around faster.

Houses and shops

The Name Rater will rate the nickname of a Pokémon you caught, and change it if you like. In another house, someone checks how much your first Pokémon likes you, and in a third, people explain what happens when a Pokémon runs out of PP. Floria from the Flower Shop is out on Route 36, so the shop has little to say for now.

Bill's house

Bill made the Pokémon Storage System, but he isn't home: his mother says he's been called to the Pokémon Center in Ecruteak City. Once you've met him there, come back and he gives you an Eevee.

Pokémon Center

A little girl reminds you that types matter more than levels. The Wi-Fi Club and Union Room upstairs aren't open.

The gate to Route 35

The guard in the gatehouse north of the city has a favour to ask. His friend is dozing by the pond on Route 31, and he wants you to take his Spearow, Kenya, who is carrying a letter. Deliver it, then come back to the guard for an HP Up.

Game Corner

With the Coin Case, you can play Voltorb Flip here: flip cards to find the numbers and dodge the Voltorb. Players share tips about reading the numbers on the side of the board. Trade your coins for prizes at the Prize Corner; a gentleman is saving up for the Porygon.

Radio Tower

The Radio Tower is Goldenrod's landmark. For now you can only visit the first two floors.

1F

The quiz lady is running the Radio Card campaign. Answer five yes-or-no maths questions in a row and you win a Radio Card ours, which lets your Pokégear play the radio. Miss one and you can try again any time.

2F

Buena hosts Buena's Password. Listen to her show on your Pokégear's radio, then come and tell her the day's password for a point, once a day. Save up points for prizes. An officer at the stairs won't let you go higher: the floors above are staff only, and he feels something's not right with the Director.

The Underground

Two entrances lead down to the Underground, a tunnel under the city with little shops along it: an herb shop, a hair salon for your Pokémon and a photo studio, plus a bargain shop on Mondays. A locked door with a NO ENTRY sign stops you going further. What lies behind it comes in Part 12.

Goldenrod Gym

Whitney trains Normal Pokémon. Five trainers stand in the way.

Beat her for the Plain Badge. She also gives you TM240 Attract and the Squirt Bottle ours, a watering can for that strange tree on Route 36. With the Plain Badge, your Pokémon will be able to use Strength outside battle once you have that HM.
