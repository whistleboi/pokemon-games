Silver League

Victory Road comes out on the Indigo Plateau, the goal of every trainer. This is the Silver League, home of Johto's Elite Four and its Champion. The League building has a counter to heal your team and a shop, so stock up before you go in.

{{tables:SILVER_LEAGUE_PLATEAU}}

The Rare Candy outside is hidden: step on its spot to find it.

{{journal:johto_m17}}

Pokémon League

The attendants welcome you and remind you to heal your Pokémon first. The Elite Four battle you one after another with no rest in between, and the guard warns that once you go in, there's no coming back out until it's over. An old man says trainers from all over Johto climb up here, but only a few get past the Elite Four, and a Black Belt warns that Bruno's Fighting Pokémon are as tough as rock. The Wi-Fi and link rooms upstairs are closed for now.

When you're ready, go through to the first room. Each room's door only opens once you've won its battle.

Will

Will has trained all over the world to perfect his Psychic Pokémon. Dark, Bug and Ghost moves work well against most of them.

{{battle:SILVER_LEAGUE_PLATEAU:Will}}

Koga

Koga promises that his poisons and tricks will be your undoing. He fights with Poison Pokémon and tricky moves, so expect Toxic, Minimize and Double Team. Ground and Psychic moves are good choices.

{{battle:SILVER_LEAGUE_PLATEAU:Koga}}

Bruno

Bruno's Fighting Pokémon train to the limit, and he has an Onix in the mix too. Psychic and Flying moves hit his fighters hard.

{{battle:SILVER_LEAGUE_PLATEAU:Bruno}}

Karen

Karen of the Elite Four says strong and weak Pokémon don't matter: a real trainer wins with their favourites. Her team is mostly Dark types, with a Vileplume and a Gengar. Fighting moves hit her Umbreon and Houndoom hard.

{{battle:SILVER_LEAGUE_PLATEAU:Karen}}

The Champion: Lance

In the last room, Lance is waiting for you. He's the Champion, a trainer who leads Dragons, and he accepts your challenge. Three of his Pokémon are Dragonite, so Ice moves are your best weapon. Bring something for his Gyarados, Aerodactyl and Charizard too: Electric and Rock moves hit all three.

{{battle:SILVER_LEAGUE_PLATEAU:Lance}}

When you win, Professor Oak hurries in. He came as soon as he heard. He tells you that you and your Pokémon trusted each other all the way here, and that's what a Champion is. Then he leaves, and Lance asks you to come with him.

Hall of Fame

Lance leads you into the Hall of Fame, where every Champion of the Pokémon League is honoured. The Pokémon who fought beside you are recorded with you, and your team is entered in the Hall of Fame. Then you head home to New Bark Town to share your victory.

You're Johto's Champion now. Part 17 covers what opens up after the title.
