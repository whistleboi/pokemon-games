Route 32

Route 32 is a long road south from Violet City down to Union Cave. A gatehouse leads west to the Ruins of Alph, and the route has its own Pokémon Center. Mareep and Wooper are new here, and fishermen wait along the water.

If you haven't picked up the Egg yet, a man on the route stops you: Elm's aide was looking all over Violet for you, and she's in the Pokémon Center. Go back and see her first.

People on the route

An old man asks whether you've been to the Gym. Show him the Zephyr Badge and he gives you a Miracle Seed, which makes Grass-type moves hit harder. A very loud man is delighted that you didn't run away from his roar, and hands you TM172 Roar.

A shady man offers you a Slowpoke Tail for a million, then tells you to get lost. On Fridays, Frieda is out on the route, and she gives you a Poison Barb, which powers up Poison-type moves.

Pokémon Center

A fishing fan in the Pokémon Center thinks this is a great spot for fishing and gives you the Old Rod. Face the water and use it, and reel in when something bites. An Apricorn fan gives you a Lure Ball ours if you have the Apricorn Box: it works best on Pokémon you fish up. A woman wonders what her Pokémon should hold.

Ruins of Alph

The Ruins of Alph are ancient stone chambers west of Route 32. Nobody knows who built them. The Ruins Research Center studies them, and the strange Pokémon Unown live inside. Union Cave also has a way out into the ruins.

{{journal:johto_s1}} {{tables:RUINS_OF_ALPH}}

Natu and Smeargle live in the grass outside. Unown only appear inside the ruins' inner hall, and only after you've solved one of the four stone puzzles. Each hidden room's items wait behind its sliding wall.

{{trainers:RUINS_OF_ALPH}}

Ruins Research Center

The researcher explains that Unown come in 28 different forms, and asks you to catch as many as you can. Come back once you've caught your first and he gives you the Unown Report, to keep track of every form you find.

{{journal:johto_c1}}

The four chambers

Each of the four small chambers starts with a stone panel to put back together: slide the pieces until the picture is whole. The pictures are a Kabuto, an Omanyte, an Aerodactyl and a Ho-Oh. Solve any one, and Unown start to turn up in the inner hall, through the ruins' main entrance.

Once its panel is solved, you can walk into the chamber. Unown letters on its far wall spell out a word, and the wall slides open when you bring what the word asks for:

  • Kabuto chamber: the wall says ESCAPE. Have an Escape Rope with you.
  • Aerodactyl chamber: the wall says LIGHT. Have HM Flash, from Sprout Tower.
  • Omanyte chamber: the wall says WATER. Have a Water Stone.
  • Ho-Oh chamber: the wall says HO-OH. Bring Ho-Oh in your party, much later.

Around the ruins, a boy who moved some of the panels wonders what the pictures could be, and a man thinks you'd make a fine scientist.

Union Cave

Union Cave is at the south end of Route 32. Its first floor joins Route 32 to Route 33, with a side way out into the Ruins of Alph. Two lower floors, B1F and B2F, wind deeper into the rock.

Route 33

Route 33 is a short path from Union Cave's south exit west to Azalea Town. A girl who just came through the cave is too tired to look around.

{{tables:JOHTO_ROUTE_33}}

The Headbutt trees here hold different Pokémon from the routes before, Heracross and Aipom among them. They need the Rocky Helmet.

{{trainers:JOHTO_ROUTE_33}}

Hiker Anthony rings you later for rematches. Keep going west to reach Azalea Town.
