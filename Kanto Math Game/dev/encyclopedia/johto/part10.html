Cianwood City

Cianwood City is a port on the far side of the sea, washed by rough waves. Route 41 is the way back east to Olivine. The Pharmacy, the Gym, a Photo Studio and a few houses stand around town, and the Cliff Edge Gate leads on to Route 47, though workers are blocking it for now.

{{journal:johto_m12}} {{tables:CIANWOOD_CITY}}

The water can be fished and surfed. The rocks around town can be smashed with Rock Smash, and a wild Krabby or Shuckle sometimes jumps out. The Revive and Stardust are hidden: step on their spots.

Around town, a man says the boulders north of town might be hiding something, a girl tells you Chuck trains under a waterfall, and an old man has heard that a mythical sea creature lives on the islands between here and Olivine.

Suicune and Eusine

Walk past the rocks by the sea and you spot Suicune, standing and watching the waves. It turns, looks straight at you, and bounds away across the water. Eusine has followed it to Cianwood too. Talk to him: he has been chasing Suicune for years, and since you've seen it as well, he wants to find out what kind of trainer you are.

{{battle:CIANWOOD_CITY:Eusine}}

After the battle, he says he can see why Suicune keeps turning up near you.

Cianwood Pharmacy

The pharmacist makes his medicine the old way, to order. Tell him it's for Jasmine's Amphy in the Olivine Lighthouse and he makes you a Secret Potion, a very strong medicine. If you haven't been up the Lighthouse to see Amphy yet, he won't guess: he sends you to find out who it's for first. The pharmacy has a shop counter too.

Cianwood Gym

The Gym is built like a mountain, with a roaring waterfall down the middle. Chuck trains under it, and he can't hear a thing over the water, so he won't notice you yet. Four Black Belts guard the climb. They spot you and march over to fight, and once beaten they drop hints: the way up is round the east side and over the bridge across the waterfall. At the top, press the big red button. It works a winch that stops the waterfall, and then Chuck will see you.

{{trainers:CIANWOOD_CITY:Cianwood Gym}}

Chuck

Like the other Leaders, Chuck wants to play a game with you before you battle ours. Win it to face him. Chuck uses Fighting Pokémon that hit very hard. Flying and Psychic moves work well against both of them, and Electric and Grass moves are good against Poliwrath too.

{{battle:CIANWOOD_CITY:Chuck}}

Win and you get the Storm Badge. It lets you use Fly outside battle. Chuck also gives you a TM for Focus Punch and the Rock Smasher ours, which breaks rocks that are in your way.

Chuck's house

Chuck's wife thinks crossing the sea must have been hard work. Once you've beaten her husband, she gives you HM Fly. With the Storm Badge, your Pokémon can carry you to any town you've already visited, so you can fly straight back to Olivine.

Kirk's house

A nervous man called Kirk lives here. A boy with piercing eyes and long red hair came by and scared him half to death. Sounds like your rival! Kirk is afraid the boy will come back for his Pokémon, so he asks you to look after his Shuckle, Shuckie. Shuckie comes holding a Berry Juice.

{{journal:johto_s_shuckie}}

Other buildings

In one house, a woman, a girl and a boy talk about the sea creature on the islands near Olivine. It stirs up the whirlpools around them, and they say it only shows itself to someone carrying a special bell, like the Kimono Girls keep. In another, an old man says a bell at the top of Ecruteak's Bell Tower rings clear when the legendary Pokémon is near. At the Photo Studio, the photographer takes a picture of you and your team.

Heal your team at the Center. The Wi-Fi Club, the Union Room and the link desk upstairs are all closed.

Olivine City (again)

Fly back to Olivine, or surf back across Routes 41 and 40, and climb the Lighthouse to the top again with the Secret Potion.

{{journal:johto_m13}}

The Lighthouse top

Jasmine is thrilled to see the medicine, and asks you to give it to Amphy. Check Amphy and it swallows the Secret Potion. The light in its tail starts to glow brightly again: Amphy is cured! Jasmine thanks you and heads back to the Gym.

With Amphy well again, the workers at Cianwood's Cliff Edge Gate pack up and leave, so the road to Route 47 is open. That trip can wait.

Olivine Gym

Jasmine waits at the far end of her Gym. Walk up the hall and talk to her.

Jasmine

Jasmine wants to play a game before you battle, too ours. She uses Steel Pokémon with a rock-solid defence. Fire, Fighting and Ground moves work well against all of them, and Water moves are good against Steelix.

{{battle:OLIVINE_CITY:Jasmine}}

Win and you get the Mineral Badge. Jasmine also gives you three Rare Candies and a TM for Iron Tail.

Your next stop is Mahogany Town, east of Ecruteak. Go back up Routes 39 and 38 to Ecruteak, then surf east onto Route 42.

Route 42

Route 42 runs east from Ecruteak City to Mahogany Town. From Ecruteak you need Surf to get onto it. Mt. Mortar stands beside the route, and its entrances open onto the road.

{{tables:JOHTO_ROUTE_42}}

The Max Potion is hidden: step on its spot. The pink, green and yellow Apricorn trees each give one Apricorn a day, once you have the Apricorn Box. The water can be fished and surfed.

{{trainers:JOHTO_ROUTE_42}}

The Hiker

Partway along, a Hiker comes charging out of Mt. Mortar and stops you. He was training inside and got so pumped up he came running out. He thinks you could use some muscle, and gives you HM Strength ours. With the Plain Badge, your Pokémon can push big boulders out of the way.

Carry on east along Route 42 to reach Mahogany Town, or step into Mt. Mortar first.

Mt. Mortar

Mt. Mortar is a big cave, and the guard in Ecruteak was right: it's a maze inside. It has four areas: 1F, where you come in from Route 42, the inner part of 1F, 2F and B1F. Much of it is water, so Surf is a must. Some boulders need Strength, and a waterfall on 1F can't be climbed until you have Waterfall, much later.

{{journal:johto_s3}} {{tables:MT_MORTAR}}

Mt. Mortar is packed with items, and many are hidden: step on their spots to find them. Look out for the Dragon Scale and the Protector on 2F.

{{trainers:MT_MORTAR}}

The Karate King

Kiyo, the Karate King, trains alone deep in B1F. Flying and Psychic moves work well against his Pokémon.

{{battle:MT_MORTAR:Kiyo}}

Beat him and he gives you a Tyrogue, a young Fighting Pokémon.
