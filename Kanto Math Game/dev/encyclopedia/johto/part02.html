Cherrygrove City

Cherrygrove City is a little town by the sea at the west end of Route 29. It has Johto's first Pokémon Center and Poké Mart. Route 30 leads north toward Mr. Pokémon's house.

{{tables:CHERRYGROVE_CITY}}

The sea here can be fished once you have a rod.

The Guide Gent's tour

As you walk into town, an old man spots that you're a new trainer and offers to show you around. Follow him: he points out the Pokémon Center, where your team is healed for free, the Poké Mart for Poké Balls and Potions, and the sea, where some Pokémon live. The tour ends at his house, where he loads the Map Card onto your Pokégear so you can always see where you are.

Around Cherrygrove

A woman says every trip is more fun with your Pokémon along. Upstairs in the Pokémon Center, the Wi-Fi Club, the Union Room and the link desk aren't open yet.

The sleeping man

An old man is fast asleep in town. Wake him and he offers you his helmet. Say yes for the Rocky Helmet ours: with it in your Bag, your Pokémon can Headbutt trees to shake wild Pokémon out of them.

Houses

A fisherman gives you Mystic Water, which makes a Water-type's moves hit harder when it holds it. In another house, a boy explains that Pokémon grow through battle and some change their whole form, and a girl has heard that every Pokémon's nature changes how its stats grow.

Route 30

Route 30 runs north from Cherrygrove City to Route 31. Mr. Pokémon's house is near the north end. This is the first route with trainers, so expect a battle or two on the way.

{{tables:JOHTO_ROUTE_30}}

Headbutt trees need the Rocky Helmet from Cherrygrove's sleeping man. Apricorn trees need the Apricorn Box, from the house on this route.

{{trainers:JOHTO_ROUTE_30}}

Youngster Joey will ring you on your Pokégear later for a rematch, with a tougher team each time.

People on the route

A youngster is in the middle of a battle with his Rattata and won't let you interrupt. A girl who isn't a trainer warns that locking eyes with one means a battle, a man explains that a move with no PP left needs a rest at a Pokémon Center, and a boy reminds you that running through the grass still draws wild Pokémon. A sign says fruit grows on some trees.

The Apricorn house

The man in the house on the route says most visitors knock by mistake, looking for Mr. Pokémon. Since you came to see him, he gives you an Apricorn Box ours. Now you can pick Apricorns: each tree grows its own colour, once a day, and Kurt in Azalea Town knows what to do with them.

Mr. Pokémon's House

Mr. Pokémon lives in a house on Route 30, and he loves strange discoveries.

His latest is a mysterious Egg, unlike any he's seen in Kanto or Johto, and he asks you to take it to Professor Elm. You receive the Mystery Egg.

Professor Oak

Professor Oak from Kanto is visiting his old friend. He likes the look of you and your Pokémon, and gives you a Pokédex to record every Pokémon you meet. He adds his number to your Pokégear too, then hurries off: he's due on the radio in Goldenrod City.

Later visit. Much later, the Lake of Rage gives you a Red Scale. Show it to Mr. Pokémon and he trades you a full set of vitamins for it: HP Up, Protein, Iron, Calcium, Zinc and Carbos.

Back to Cherrygrove

As you leave, your Pokégear rings. It's Professor Elm, in a panic: something awful has happened at the Lab, and he needs you back right away. Head south down Route 30.

Rival battle 1

When you step into Cherrygrove City, the red-haired boy from outside the Lab is waiting. He sneers that a wimp like you doesn't deserve a Pokémon, and shows you the one he has. It's the Lab Pokémon strong against yours.

{{battle:CHERRYGROVE_CITY:rival}}

After the battle he stalks off. Heal at the Pokémon Center and go back east along Route 29.

New Bark Town (again)

In Professor Elm's Lab, a police officer stops you. A Pokémon was stolen from the Lab last night, and the thief was a boy with long red hair. When you say you battled someone like that in Cherrygrove, the officer asks his name: it's the name you gave your rival. The officer leaves to keep watch.

Show Professor Elm the Mystery Egg. He wants to study it, so he keeps it for now: once you've earned a Gym badge, his aide will bring it back to you. He thanks you with ten Poké Balls and points you to Falkner, the Gym Leader in Violet City.

Violet City is north of Route 30, along Route 31. Mom will heal your team before you go.
