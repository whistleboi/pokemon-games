After the title

You're the Champion of Johto, and a lot opens up:

  • Professor Elm's present. Visit Elm in his Lab in New Bark Town. He gives you the S.S. Ticket for the S.S. Aqua, the fast ship from Olivine City to Vermilion City in Kanto.
  • Route 28 and Mt. Silver. The Reception Gate's west door opens onto Route 28, the road to Mt. Silver, where a silent trainer waits at the summit. In HeartGold and SoulSilver this road only opens after Kanto's eight badges too; here the Johto title is enough ours.
  • The Battle Frontier. Its door at Frontier Access, off Route 40, lets you through.
  • The Embedded Tower. Its mouth on Route 47 opens, with three legendary Pokémon inside.
  • League rematches. You can take on the Elite Four and Lance again. This time they bring their stronger teams: the Elite Four from Lv 58 to Lv 64, and Lance from Lv 68 to Lv 75.

Remember that the Rising Badge also gave you HM Rock Climb ours. You'll need it, with Surf and Waterfall, on Mt. Silver.

S.S. Aqua

With the S.S. Ticket, go to the harbour in Olivine City and board the S.S. Aqua. The ship has a corridor with your own cabin, four blocks of passenger cabins, a lower deck and the captain's cabin. Most of the passengers are trainers who are itching for a battle. If your team gets hurt, take a nap in the bed in your cabin and they'll be good as new.

{{trainers:JOHTO_SS_AQUA}}

The sleepy sailor

A crewman on the lower deck asks you to find his buddy, who is slacking off somewhere in the cabins. You'll find Sailor Stanly snoring in the south-east cabins. Wake him up and he insists he was never asleep, then battles you to prove it, with a Machop (Lv 39), a Machoke (Lv 41) and a Psyduck (Lv 34). Afterwards he begs you to keep his nap a secret.

Hide-and-seek

A gentleman in the north-west cabins has lost his granddaughter again: she loves hide-and-seek. You'll find her in the captain's cabin, pestering the captain to play. Offer to play with her yourself and she runs off to hide. Find her on the lower deck, and she goes back to her grandpa. He thanks you with a Metal Coat.

The captain

Once the little girl is back with her grandpa, the captain thanks you for playing with her. If you hold all sixteen badges, Johto's and Kanto's, he gives you a little something from his voyages once a day: the sixteen Plates, one after another, and after them a Nugget.

Landing in Kanto

When you're ready, step off the gangway on the Vermilion side to land in Vermilion City. Kanto has its own journey, with its own walkthrough.

Route 28

Route 28 runs west from the Reception Gate's west door. Its door opens once you're Johto's Champion with all eight Johto badges. It's a quiet road to the foot of Mt. Silver, and the wild Pokémon here are strong, around Lv 51 to 54. A TM246 Brine lies in the grass.

{{tables:KANTO_ROUTE_28}}

Celebrity House

A small house hides a celebrity who came out here to rest after years in the spotlight. Since you're not the press, she gives you TM239 Steel Wing, the move that inspired her first dress. She asks you not to tell anyone where she is.

Mt. Silver (the clearing)

At the end of Route 28 is a clearing at the foot of Mt. Silver, with a Pokémon Center and the mouth of the cave. Heal up here: it's the last Center before the summit. TM125 Flamethrower lies by the path, and a Full Restore is hidden nearby.

A mountain guide says only trainers who have proven themselves may climb the mountain. He can also take you straight to the Silver League's plateau ours, for a trainer with all eight Johto badges.

{{tables:MT_SILVER_CITY}}

Mt. Silver

Mt. Silver is a huge cave mountain, open to Johto's Champion with all eight Johto badges. The way to the top winds in and out of the mountain: 1F from the clearing, the Mountainside outside, a side chamber, Moltres's chamber, 2F, the Upper Mountainside, 3F and finally the summit. You'll need Surf, Waterfall and Rock Climb on the way. The wild Pokémon are around Lv 40 to 43, and Larvitar and Pupitar live here.

{{tables:MT_SILVER}}

Many items are hidden, so look around.

The climb

From 1F, three ways lead out onto the Mountainside. A Reaper Cloth lies on 1F, and a Dawn Stone on the Mountainside. TM116 Stealth Rock is in the side chamber. 2F holds a Protein, an Expert Belt, a Calcium and a Max Elixir, and the Upper Mountainside has a Pure Incense and a Full Restore. Past 3F, the last way up opens onto the snowy summit.

Moltres

Deep inside the mountain, past its waterfalls, heat shimmers off the rocks of Moltres's chamber.

{{journal:johto_s5}}
Moltres, Lv 50. In Moltres's chamber, once you're Johto's Champion. It blazes down when you seek it out. Beat it and you can try to catch it.

The summit: Red

At the summit, a silent trainer stands in the snow. He doesn't say a word. He just wants to battle. This is the toughest battle in Johto, with a Pikachu at Lv 88, so bring your very best team.

{{battle:MT_SILVER:Red}}

When you win, the summit goes quiet and Red is gone. But you get the feeling you'll meet again.

Frontier Access

Frontier Access is the little area between Route 40, west of Olivine City, and the Battle Frontier. Walk through the gate on Route 40 to reach it. You can visit it before the title: it has a Pokémon Center, a Poké Mart and a house of Move Tutors. The gate north to the Battle Frontier only opens for Johto's Champion.

{{tables:FRONTIER_ACCESS}}

Move Tutors

Three Move Tutors teach moves for Battle Points, which you earn at the Battle Frontier. The one on the left teaches moves like Fire Punch, Ice Punch, Thunder Punch, Knock Off and Zen Headbutt; the one in the middle has power moves like Outrage, Superpower, Earth Power, Heat Wave and Iron Head; the one on the right has clever moves like Heal Bell, Tailwind, Gravity and Trick. Each move costs from 32 to 64 BP, and once you've paid for one, it's yours for good.

Battle Frontier

The Battle Frontier is a big plaza with five battle facilities: the Battle Tower, the Battle Factory, the Battle Hall, the Battle Castle and the Battle Arcade. In each one, talk to the attendant inside to start a streak of battles, one trainer after another. Win battles in a row to earn Battle Points. A guide in the plaza says each facility has a Frontier Brain, who comes out to face you once you've won enough battles in a row.

The plaza is full of people watching the battles. Two stalls sell items and TMs, and a clerk runs a Scratch-Off game. The gate attendant reminds you that the stairs behind him lead back down to Frontier Access and Route 40.

Cliff Edge Gate

Cliff Edge Gate is the passage through the cliff on the west side of Cianwood City. It leads down to Route 47, Route 48 and the Safari Zone. These places don't need the title: they open up earlier in the story. Until Amphy at the Olivine Lighthouse is cured, two workers stand in the gate saying the Safari Zone is still being built. The woman at the Safari Zone desk can hardly wait for it to open.

{{tables:CLIFF_EDGE_GATE}}

The Big Pearl is hidden.

Route 47

Route 47 is a wild road along the sea cliffs, with bridges, waterfalls and the mouths of Cliff Cave. Ditto and Miltank live in the grass. Two Gym Leaders are training here: Chuck from Cianwood, who loves how the wind up here builds strong legs, and Crasher Wake, a Gym Leader from Pastoria City in faraway Sinnoh, who came for the spray off the sea. A ruin watcher says a huge tower once stood past the cave, reaching up into the sky.

{{tables:JOHTO_ROUTE_47}}

The Stardust and the Pearl are hidden.

{{trainers:JOHTO_ROUTE_47}}

Cliff Cave

Cliff Cave runs inside the cliff on Route 47, with three mouths onto the route. A guide inside warns that when a cave splits in two, one way usually leads on and the other is a dead end. Dratini sometimes bite on a Super Rod here.

{{tables:JOHTO_CLIFF_CAVE}}

The Ultra Ball is hidden. Smash the cracked rocks with Rock Smash for Krabby and Kingler.

Embedded Tower

The Embedded Tower is the sealed ruin the watcher on Route 47 was talking about. Its mouth is on Route 47, and it only opens once you're Johto's Champion. Inside is a single chamber with an altar at its head.

Groudon, Lv 70. At the altar. Beat it and you can try to catch it.
Kyogre, Lv 70. Beside the altar, for Johto's Champion. A powerful presence fills the air, and Kyogre appears. Beat it and you can try to catch it.
Rayquaza, Lv 70. On the other side of the altar. It only comes once you've beaten both Groudon and Kyogre, descending from the sky. Beat it and you can try to catch it.

Route 48

Route 48 is a short, grassy road from Route 47 north to the Safari Zone Gate. A White Flute lies in the grass.

{{tables:JOHTO_ROUTE_48}}

Safari Zone Gate

The Safari Zone Gate is a busy plaza with a Pokémon Center, market tents and the Safari Zone's gatehouse. Visitors and their Pokémon are everywhere: a lass says she chased Pokémon all day, and a kid dreams of catching his very first Pokémon here. The shopkeeper is still getting set up, and a drink seller has just started selling drinks for Pokémon.

{{tables:SAFARI_ZONE_GATE}}

The three Tiny Mushrooms are hidden.

The gatehouse

The receptionist in the gatehouse can change how the Safari Zone is laid out. It has six areas, and you can pick one of ten setups, each with a different mix of areas: Plains, Meadow, Savannah, Peak, Wetland, Forest, Swamp, Marshland, Wasteland and Mountain. Each area has its own Pokémon. Then walk through the gatehouse into the Safari Zone.

Safari Zone

Each visit to the Safari Zone is a Safari Game: you get 30 Safari Balls and 500 steps. You can only catch Pokémon here, not battle them. When you run out of balls or steps, the visit is over and you're walked back out to the gatehouse.

{{tables:JOHTO_SAFARI_ZONE}}

The Pokémon you meet depend on the area you're in. Dratini sometimes bite on a Super Rod, and Lapras swim in the water.
