Mahogany Town

Mahogany Town is a small, quiet town at the east end of Route 42, known as the home of ninja masters. A gate on the north side leads to Route 43 and the Lake of Rage. Route 44 runs east towards Blackthorn City, but that road stays shut for now.

The townspeople are talking about the lake. An old man warns you about the Gyarados raging up there, and a woman has seen a man in a black cape going back and forth between the town and the lake.

{{journal:johto_m14}}

Souvenir Shop

The souvenir shop is Mahogany's shop. A man outside is keen for you to try a Rage Candy Bar, but they're sold out today. Something about this place isn't quite right: come back after you've been to the Lake of Rage.

Other buildings

In the house next to the shop, a man has heard that a red Gyarados turned up at the Lake of Rage, and a girl chats about her favourite radio show. The Pokémon Center heals your team as usual.

Mahogany Gym

Pryce's Gym is here too. While Team Rocket is up to something in Mahogany, Pryce won't battle anyone, so you can't challenge him yet. See Pryce's Gym at the end of this part.

Route 43

Route 43 is a grassy road running north from Mahogany Town to the Lake of Rage. Trainers line the path on both sides, and a gate sits in the middle of the route.

Team Rocket has taken over that gate. A Grunt inside charges $1000 to anyone who wants to walk through, while another boasts that everyone wants to see what's going on up at the lake.

{{tables:JOHTO_ROUTE_43}}

The Black Apricorn tree needs the Apricorn Box to carry its fruit, and gives one Apricorn a day. Headbutt the trees for Paras, Exeggcute, Parasect and even Scyther.

{{trainers:JOHTO_ROUTE_43}}

Two of the trainers here are Rocket Grunts, Lena and Kip, trying to show off to each other.

After the Rocket Hideout. Once Team Rocket's hideout is cleared out, the Grunts leave the gate and its real guard comes back. He's so grateful that he gives you TM148 Sludge Bomb, a Poison move that may poison the target.

Lake of Rage

The Lake of Rage is a huge lake at the top of Route 43, ringed with trees. People say it's a crater dug out by rampaging Gyarados long ago. Right now the lake is full of Gyarados, and a fisherman thinks it's a bad sign. A woman can't believe what she saw: a red Gyarados, out on the water.

{{tables:LAKE_OF_RAGE}}

The Full Restore, Rare Candy and Max Potion are hidden: step on their spots to find them. With so many Magikarp in the lake, any rod soon brings up a Gyarados.

{{trainers:LAKE_OF_RAGE}}

The Red Gyarados

The Red Gyarados is the strange one everyone is talking about. Go and battle it. It's a boss: you can catch it or knock it out, and either way the story moves on.

Gyarados (red)
Lv 35, five levels above the lake's own Pokémon. Electric moves hit it very hard.

With the Red Gyarados dealt with, the trail leads back to Mahogany Town. Someone is waiting for you in the souvenir shop.

Houses by the lake

A hermit lives alone in a house by the lake. He has meditated here for a long time, and shares what he's learned: TM272 Hidden Power, a move that draws out a power sleeping inside every Pokémon.

The Fishing Guru in the other house is after the biggest Magikarp in the lake. Bring a Magikarp in your team and he measures it. Every time you beat his record he gives you an Ether, and the first record you set earns an Elixir as well ours (from Pokémon Crystal).

On Wednesdays, Wesley is by the lake. He's one of seven brothers and sisters who each come out on their own day of the week, and he gives you a Black Belt, which powers up Fighting moves.

Mahogany Town (again)

Lance in the souvenir shop

Inside the souvenir shop, a Trainer in a cape is waiting with his Dragonite. It's Lance. He tells you the Red Gyarados was forced to evolve, and he followed the trail here. The shop is a front for Team Rocket's secret base. One Hyper Beam from his Dragonite, and the stairs behind the counter are open.

Lance explains that the Rockets are sending out a signal that drives the lake's Pokémon wild. He and Dragonite come down with you to shut it off, and they walk with you all the way through the hideout.

Rocket Hideout

Team Rocket's hideout lies under Mahogany Town, three floors deep: B1F, B2F and B3F. Grunts and Rocket scientists guard every floor, and the place is full of traps.

{{tables:ROCKET_HQ}}

The Revive on B1F, and the Full Heal and X Sp. Def on B2F, are hidden: step on their spots to find them.

B1F: The Persian statues

At the bottom of the stairs, Lance heals your whole team and warns you about the traps. Persian statues stand along the corridors, their eyes glowing red. Five of them are watching: walk past one of those and a Grunt comes running to battle you. Each alarm goes off only once.

{{trainers:ROCKET_HQ:B1F}}

The alarm Grunts' Pokémon are all Lv 18: Rattata, Zubat, Koffing, Raticate and Ekans. They are fought on the battle screen.

B2F and B3F: The passwords

Two locked doors stand between you and the end of Team Rocket's plan.

The boss's office on B3F needs two passwords. A beaten Grunt on B3F admits it: Lance has already been through here. Two Team Rocket Grunts know the passwords. Beat the one on B2F and he gives up the first; beat the one on B3F and he gives up the second. With both, the office door opens.

The door of the Radio Transmitter room on B2F checks the voice of whoever speaks the password. On B3F, a Murkrow keeps repeating a Rocket slogan it picked up from its owners. Talk to it: that slogan is the password.

{{trainers:ROCKET_HQ:B2F}} {{trainers:ROCKET_HQ:B3F}}

Executive Petrel

In the office, a man pretends to be Giovanni, then laughs: it's Petrel, one of Team Rocket's Executives. He won't tell you anything, but beating him is part of clearing the hideout.

{{battle:ROCKET_HQ:Petrel}}

Executive Ariana

With the Murkrow's password, the Radio Transmitter room on B2F opens. Executive Ariana is inside with a Grunt. Lance joins in, and you and he take them on together in a double battle: Lance sends out his Dragonite, and Ariana's Grunt uses a Drowzee and a Grimer.

{{battle:ROCKET_HQ:Ariana|A double battle, with Lance beside you.}}

Beating Ariana clears the hideout, but the transmitter is still running.

The Electrode

Three Electrode are powering the transmitter. Battle each of them (Lv 23) to shut it down. They're bosses, so you can catch them if you like.

Once all three are down, the signal stops at last. Lance says the lake will be back to normal, and thanks you with HM Whirlpool. With the Glacier Badge, you can use it to get past whirlpools at sea.

{{journal:johto_m15}}

The stairs on B1F lead back up to the souvenir shop.

Mahogany Town (again)

Team Rocket is gone from Mahogany, and on Route 43 the gate's real guard is back at his post.

Pryce's Gym

With the hideout cleared, Pryce is ready for you. His Gym is a frozen hall over three rooms: step onto the ice and you slide until something stops you. Work out which way to push off to reach each exit.

Pryce's students are Skiers and Boarders.

{{trainers:MAHOGANY_TOWN:Gym}}

Pryce uses Ice Pokémon. Fighting, Rock, Steel and Fire moves work well. Be careful with Electric moves: they're great against Seel and Dewgong, but Piloswine is part Ground and isn't hurt by them at all.

{{battle:MAHOGANY_TOWN:Pryce}}

Win the Glacier Badge. It lets you use Whirlpool outside battle. Pryce also gives you TM241 Hail, and the badge brings you an Ice Melter, which melts thick ice in your way. (TM046 Avalanche isn't his: it lies on the floor in the Ice Path, see Part 12.)
