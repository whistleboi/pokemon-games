Route 31

Route 31 runs on from the north end of Route 30, with Violet City to the west. The mouth of Dark Cave opens off the route too. More kinds of Pokémon live in this grass, with Bellsprout by day and by night, and Gastly after dark.

{{tables:JOHTO_ROUTE_31}}

Headbutt trees need the Rocky Helmet. The Apricorn tree needs the Apricorn Box.

{{trainers:JOHTO_ROUTE_31}}

Bug Catcher Wade rings you later for rematches, and he has a Berry for you each time.

People on the route

A boy is training a Pokémon he caught in Dark Cave so he can beat Falkner, the Gym Leader of Violet City. A man wishes one of his Pokémon could light up the cave, because it's pitch black inside. The guard by the Violet City gate asks whether you've taken on the Gym yet.

The sleepy man

A man is dozing on the route. He's waiting for a letter from a friend, and a Pokémon is supposed to bring it. Later, a guard in Goldenrod City hands you that Pokémon: bring it here and the man gives you TM085 Rest ours.

Dark Cave

Dark Cave is pitch dark inside. The light you need comes after Sprout Tower and Falkner's Gym, with the Zephyr Badge, so leave the cave for later: Part 13 explores it.

Violet City

Violet City is a town of old roofs and quiet water. The tall Sprout Tower and Falkner's Gym are both here. Route 31 leads east, Route 36 leads west, and Route 32 leads south, but the way south only opens once you hold Violet's badge.

The Apricorn tree needs the Apricorn Box.

Around Violet

An old woman says Falkner took over the Gym from his father and does him proud, and a girl has heard that Ghost Pokémon haunt Sprout Tower, where Normal-type moves pass right through them. A woman explains that the town looks after its old buildings, so even the Mart and the Pokémon Center are painted to fit in. A little boy wishes he had a Pokégear to mark notes on its map.

Earl's Pokémon Academy

Earl runs a school full of students. They talk about held items, trading, and Abilities: every kind of Pokémon has one, and some kinds can have either of two.

Houses

In one house, a girl has named her Pidgey Strawberry, after a food she loves. In another, Kyle collects Pokémon: bring him a Bellsprout and he'll swap it for his Onix, Rocky, which comes holding a Persim Berry. A man in the same house warns that a traded Pokémon may not obey you without the right badge.

Sprout Tower

Sprout Tower is a tall wooden tower where monks train with their Pokémon. A huge pillar sways at the tower's heart: an old woman says it was once a Bellsprout over a hundred feet tall. The Sages battle with Bellsprout, and Elder Li waits at the top. You must beat him before Falkner will battle you.

Beat him and the Elder gives you a Leaf Stone, which evolves Weepinbell into Victreebel, and HM Flash. Flash lights up dark caves, once you have the Zephyr Badge.

Violet Gym

Falkner's Gym is open now that you've beaten Elder Li. Falkner uses Flying-type Pokémon, so Electric, Ice and Rock moves are your friends here. Grass and Bug Pokémon will have a hard time.

{{journal:johto_m4}} {{trainers:VIOLET_CITY:Gym}} {{battle:VIOLET_CITY:Falkner}}

Beat him for the Zephyr Badge. He also gives you TM268 Roost, five Poké Balls and a Johto Map. With the badge, you can use Flash on the map, and the road south to Route 32 opens.

The Egg comes back

Soon after you leave the Gym, Professor Elm rings to congratulate you. He has sent his aide to Violet City's Pokémon Center with the Egg. Go inside and talk to the aide: you receive the Egg. Keep it in your party and it will hatch as you travel.

{{journal:johto_m5}}

Primo, the star of a TV show, is in the Pokémon Center too. When you're ready, head south from Violet City onto Route 32.
