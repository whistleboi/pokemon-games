Blackthorn City

Blackthorn City is a quiet mountain town on the far side of the Ice Path. It's the home of a clan of Dragon Tamers, and its Gym Leader, Clair, is one of them. The Dragon's Den lies behind the Gym, and Route 45 heads south down the mountain.

A girl is amazed that you made it through the Ice Path. A youngster says the Trainers called Dragon Tamers all come from Blackthorn.

{{tables:BLACKTHORN_CITY}}

The pool in town is for Surfing and fishing.

The Dragon's Den guard

A man guards the way to the Dragon's Den. Only Trainers who have been chosen may train there, so he turns you away for now.

Move house

Four people share the Move Deleter's house.

  • The Move Reminder and the Move Deleter have nothing left to do. Your Pokémon never forget a move here: every move they've learned stays in the Summary, and you pick which four to use. They're happy to grumble about it, though.
  • Grandma Wilma teaches Draco Meteor, the strongest Dragon move, to a Dragon-type Pokémon that loves you very much.
  • An old man teaches the ultimate moves, Frenzy Plant, Blast Burn and Hydro Cannon, to the fully evolved first partners, again only if they love you very much.

Both tutors charge the price of a TM, once. After that the move is yours for good ours.

Other buildings

In the house with the Dratini, an old woman tells you about the dragon clan that lives in Blackthorn. In another house, a trader thinks Dragonair are so cute and wants one. Trade a Dragonair and you get the trader's Dodrio, Doris, holding a Smoke Ball.

{{journal:johto_s_trades}}

On Saturdays, Santos is in town. He's very quiet, and gives you Soft Sand, which powers up Ground moves. The Poké Mart and the Pokémon Center are open as usual.

Blackthorn Gym

Clair only accepts challengers who have earned seven Johto badges. She and her students use Dragon Pokémon, along with Water Pokémon like Horsea, Seadra and Gyarados.

{{trainers:BLACKTHORN_CITY:Gym}}

Ice moves are great against Dragonair, and Electric moves against Gyarados. Kingdra shrugs off both, so Dragon moves are your best bet against it.

{{battle:BLACKTHORN_CITY:Clair|Clair keeps the Rising Badge until you pass the Elder's test.}}

Clair can't believe she lost, and she won't hand over the badge. Instead she sends you into the Dragon's Den behind the Gym: go to the Dragon Shrine at its heart and take the Elder's test. If you pass, she'll accept you. She does give you the Mt. Silver Map ours.

Now the guard at the Dragon's Den lets you in.

Dragon's Den

The Dragon's Den is a cave behind Blackthorn Gym, where the dragon clan's chosen Trainers train. A short 1F leads down to B1F, a wide underground lake. The Dragon Shrine stands on an island in the middle. To reach it you need Surf, and Whirlpool for the whirlpools in the water.

{{tables:DRAGONS_DEN}}

The Revive, Max Elixir and Max Potion are hidden: step on their spots to find them. Dratini live in the den's water, and a Super Rod can even hook a Dragonair.

{{trainers:DRAGONS_DEN}}

On Tuesdays and Thursdays your rival is down here, training his Pokémon. He's a bit embarrassed to be seen, but he means to get stronger together with them.

Dragon Shrine

Inside the shrine, the Elder of the dragon clan, Clair's grandfather, is waiting. Since Clair sent you, he asks five questions to learn how you see your Pokémon. There's no hurry. Whatever you answer, you pass, but the answers matter for his gift later.

The answers he likes: Pokémon are your friends; training wins battles; you'll battle anyone; love matters most in raising Pokémon; and both strong and weak Pokémon matter.

The Elder is pleased with you. Clair comes in, sure you've failed, but her grandfather says you passed, better than she did. She gives in and hands over the Rising Badge.

With the Rising Badge you can use Waterfall outside battle. It also gives you HM Rock Climb ours, so you can climb rocky walls later on, Mt. Silver's among them. The Elder tells you about Lugia, said to live around the Whirl Islands.

The Elder's gift

Talk to the Elder again with room in your team and he gives you a Dratini (Lv 15) as proof of his trust. If you answered all five questions his way the first time, it knows Extreme Speed.

{{journal:johto_s_dratini}}

Two other elders in the shrine say it's been a long time since a Trainer earned the Master's approval, not since young Master Lance.

Dragon's Den (again)

On your way out, Clair is waiting in the den. She says sorry for how she acted and admits you won fairly. She points you towards the Pokémon League, reached by surfing east from New Bark Town, and gives you TM115 Dragon Pulse as an apology.

Route 45

Route 45 is a steep mountain road running south from Blackthorn City. Hikers and other strong trainers line the way down. Ledges let you hop down quickly, but they only go one way: this route is walked from Blackthorn, downhill towards Route 46. The Blackthorn side of Dark Cave opens off the route.

{{tables:JOHTO_ROUTE_45}}

The PP Up and PP Max are hidden: step on their spots to find them. Look out for Gligar, Teddiursa, Phanpy and Skarmory in the grass.

{{trainers:JOHTO_ROUTE_45}}

Route 46

Route 46 is a small route running north from Route 29. Its wild Pokémon are very weak. The route leads up to Dark Cave's Violet side, and the ledges coming down from Route 45 drop onto it. You can't climb those ledges, so Route 45 is reached from Blackthorn City.

{{tables:JOHTO_ROUTE_46}}

Headbutt the trees for Spearow, Ekans, Cubone, Aipom and Heracross.

{{trainers:JOHTO_ROUTE_46}}

Route 46 runs south to Route 29, close to New Bark Town.

Dark Cave

Dark Cave is a long, pitch-black cave in two halves. The Violet side opens onto Route 31 and Route 46. The Blackthorn side opens onto Route 45. With Flash you can light the cave up and see where you're going.

{{tables:DARK_CAVE}}

The Poké Ball and Max Ether on the Violet side, and the Max Revive, Hyper Potion and Elixir on the Blackthorn side, are hidden: step on their spots to find them.

{{trainers:DARK_CAVE}}

The Violet side

From Route 31 you can explore part of the Violet side straight away. Coming in from Route 46, a cracked rock just inside the entrance needs Rock Smash. A lake fills part of this half, and the stairs to the Blackthorn side are across it, so you need Surf to go between the halves.

The Blackthorn side

Coming in from Route 45, the ledges carry you down through the cave towards the stairs to the Violet side. A man in sunglasses is startled to see you: he couldn't see you coming with his Black Glasses on. He gives you a spare pair of Black Glasses, which power up Dark moves. A TM for False Swipe and an X Speed lie on this side too.
