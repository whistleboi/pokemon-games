Route 35

Route 35 runs north from Goldenrod City to Route 36. A gatehouse on the way leads into the National Park. Nidoran♀ and Nidoran♂ are common in the grass, and there's water to fish in.

{{tables:JOHTO_ROUTE_35}}

A Nugget is hidden on the route. The Green Apricorn tree can be picked once a day once you carry the Apricorn Box.

{{trainers:JOHTO_ROUTE_35}}

National Park gate

A youngster in the gatehouse says Bug lovers come from all over for the Bug-Catching Contest, and a workman tells you the Pokéathlon Dome past the park is still being built. The Contest helper at the desk is where you sign up for the contest: see the Bug-Catching Contest below.

National Park

The National Park is a big, green park with paths for walking, and lots of people come here just to stroll with their Pokémon. A gate on the east side leads to Route 36, and one in the north leads to the Pokéathlon Dome. At night the grass is full of Hoothoot.

{{tables:NATIONAL_PARK}}

Two Full Heals are hidden in the park. TM055 Dig is tucked behind a tree.

{{trainers:NATIONAL_PARK}}

A teacher keeps acting as if class is on, and hands you some homework: a Quick Claw. A Pokémon holding it sometimes gets to move first. A woman keeps out of the grass to avoid trainers, and a lass can't decide which of her Pokémon should walk behind her.

Bug-Catching Contest

The Bug-Catching Contest takes place in the National Park. Talk to the Contest helper in the gate from Route 35 to enter. The sign in the park says Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays, but you can enter on any day, once a day ours. You need at least one Pokémon able to battle to take part.

  • You get 20 Sport Balls and 150 steps. Use only the Sport Balls.
  • Bug Pokémon roam the park. You can't battle them: catch them with MATH.
  • You keep only your best catch. Catch something better and the old one is let go.
  • When the steps or balls run out, or you leave by the gate, the judges score your best catch against the other contestants'.

Rarer bugs score higher, and so do higher levels: Scyther and Pinsir score best. The other contestants are Bug Catchers Don and Ed, Cooltrainer Nick, Pokéfan Beverly and Youngster Samuel, each with a Pokémon following them around. Nick is hunting for a Scyther, and Ed won last time.

{{tables:BUG_CONTEST}}

First prize is a Sun Stone, second an Everstone and third a Rare Candy. Everyone else gets a Potion for taking part. Whatever happens, the bug you caught is yours to keep.

Pokéathlon Dome

North of the National Park stands the Pokéathlon Dome, where Pokémon compete in running, jumping and other events instead of battles. It isn't open yet: a sign on the door says it's still under construction ours, and even the Aprijuice stand outside is waiting for the opening.

Athletes and their Pokémon are already training on the track around the Dome. A jogger runs with a Pokémon every morning, and a juice fan explains that the right food and drink, like juice made from Apricorns, helps Pokémon do better in the events.

{{tables:POKEATHLON_DOME}}

Both items here are hidden.

Route 36

Route 36 runs east from the National Park to Violet City, with Route 35 to the south and a path to the Ruins of Alph. Route 37 and Ecruteak City lie to the north, but a strange tree is standing right in the middle of the road. A girl grumbles that it's stopping her going shopping in Ecruteak.

{{tables:JOHTO_ROUTE_36}}

An Awakening is hidden on the route. The Blue Apricorn tree can be picked once a day with the Apricorn Box.

{{trainers:JOHTO_ROUTE_36}}

The strange tree

Walk up to the tree without the Squirt Bottle and it just seems to wiggle. Its leaves are drooping, as if it's thirsty. With the Squirt Bottle from Whitney, water it: it jumps up, because it's really a Pokémon, and battles you.

Sudowoodo. A wild battle, about five levels above the route's own Pokémon. Catch it or knock it out: either way the road north to Route 37 is open from then on.

Afterwards, two people come to thank you. A man who knows all about boulders in the way gives you HM Rock Smash, which cracks rocks open; your Pokémon can use it on the map with the Zephyr Badge. Floria from Goldenrod's Flower Shop, who watered the tree first, gives you an Oran Berry and a Pecha Berry.

Arthur

On Thursdays, Arthur is on the route. Thursday is his day, and he gives you a Hard Stone, which powers up Rock moves. He has brothers and sisters, each out on a day of their own.

Route 37

Route 37 is a short route between Route 36 and Ecruteak City, with three Apricorn trees: red, blue and black. Stantler graze in the grass.

{{tables:JOHTO_ROUTE_37}}

Headbutt trees need the Rocky Helmet. The Apricorn trees can be picked once a day with the Apricorn Box.

{{trainers:JOHTO_ROUTE_37}}

Both pairs of Twins battle you together, in a double battle.

On Sundays, Sunny is here. It's her day, and she gives you a Magnet, which powers up Electric moves. Her big sister Monica comes out on Mondays.

Keep going north and you reach Ecruteak City.
