The Pokémon League lies east of New Bark Town, across the border in Kanto. The road there is long and full of strong trainers: Route 27, the cave at Tohjo Falls, Route 26 up to the Reception Gate, and then Victory Road. Make sure you have Surf and Waterfall, and that your team is ready for a long trip.

Route 27

Go back home to New Bark Town. The water on the east side of town leads out to Route 27, but only for a trainer with all eight Johto badges. Surf east across the bay to reach it.

Route 27 is a long road by the sea. Tohjo Falls cuts it in two: its west mouth is on the western part, and its east mouth opens onto the rest of the route, which runs on east and north to Route 26. The trainers here are a big step up, with Ace Trainers everywhere, and the wild Pokémon are around Lv 44 to 47.

{{tables:KANTO_ROUTE_27}}

The Headbutt trees here use Kanto's route tree table, which has Scyther. Several items are hidden, so look around.

{{trainers:KANTO_ROUTE_27}}

The house

A thoughtful man in the house near Tohjo Falls asks to see your partner, since anyone heading to the League needs Pokémon that trust them. If your first Pokémon is fond enough of you, he gives you TM051 Sandstorm. If not, spend more time together and come back.

Tohjo Falls

Tohjo Falls is a cave on the border between Johto and Kanto, around a pool fed by a huge waterfall. Surf across the pool from Route 27's west mouth. To reach the far mouth, which leads back out onto the eastern part of Route 27, you need to climb the waterfall with Waterfall. You can use it outside battle now that you have the Rising Badge.

{{tables:TOHJO_FALLS}}

A Black Flute lies among the rocks.

The room behind the falls

There is a hidden room behind the waterfall. With all eight Johto badges, you find a man in a dark suit waiting there: Giovanni, the boss of Team Rocket. He has come back to rebuild, and wants to see if the trainer who keeps getting in his way is worth his time.

All four of his Pokémon are part Ground type, so Water and Ice moves work well. When you win, Giovanni says Team Rocket is finished, for now, and vanishes into the mist of the falls.

Route 26

Route 26 is the long climb north from Route 27 to the Pokémon League Reception Gate. Log bridges cross the water here and there, and you can walk over them or along the path underneath. More strong trainers line the way.

{{tables:KANTO_ROUTE_26}}

The Headbutt trees use the same Kanto route table as Route 27. The Tiny Mushroom is hidden.

{{trainers:KANTO_ROUTE_26}}

The Rest House

Partway up the route stands a little Rest House. The woman inside is glad you found a place to rest where the road gets hard, and heals your whole team.

Pokémon League Reception Gate

At the top of Route 26 is the Reception Gate, the building where the roads to both Leagues meet. Its guard checks your badges: with all eight Johto badges he sends you straight on through the great door to Victory Road and the Silver League. The gate's other doors lead west to Route 28 and east to Kanto's Route 22. They stay shut until you've become Champion.

Victory Road

Victory Road is the last cave before the Silver League: three floors of rocky tunnels, joined by stairs. Walk in from the Reception Gate on 1F, climb through 2F, and leave 3F by the way out at the top, which opens at the foot of the Silver League.

{{tables:JOHTO_VICTORY_ROAD}}

Smash the cracked rocks with Rock Smash for Geodude and Graveler. Many items are hidden.

1F

A Max Elixir, a Max Revive and a Full Heal lie on this floor.

2F

Look for TM149 Earthquake and an HP Up on this floor.

3F

TM094 Dark Pulse and a Full Restore are on 3F. Someone you know is waiting on this floor too.

Rival battle

Your rival stops you and asks if you're taking the League challenge. He laughs at you, and says he isn't like he was before: now he has the strongest Pokémon with him.

{{battle:JOHTO_VICTORY_ROAD:rival}}

When he loses, your rival says he hasn't given up on becoming the greatest. He'll find out why he can't win, get stronger, and challenge you again. Walk on out of the cave to the Silver League.
