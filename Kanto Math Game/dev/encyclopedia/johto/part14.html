With all eight badges in your case, two of Johto's legends are waiting for you: Ho-Oh at the top of the Bell Tower in Ecruteak City, and Lugia deep under the Whirl Islands. Neither is needed for the League, so you can visit them now or come back after the title. Both are strong, so bring plenty of healing and good Poké Balls.

Bellchime Trail (again)

Go back to Ecruteak City and through the sage's gate to Bellchime Trail. The Wise Trio are still guarding the trail, and the Journal now sends you to them.

{{journal:johto_m17b}}

Sage Gaku, Sage Masa and Sage Koji each have a Noctowl and one Eevee evolution, all at Lv 32: Flareon, Jolteon and Vaporeon. Koji is the last of the three. When all three are beaten, the trail opens, and they say they'll meet you inside the tower.

Bell Tower

The Bell Tower is the tall pagoda at the end of Bellchime Trail. It has ten floors and a roof, joined by stairs and warp tiles that send you up and down between the floors. At night, Gastly haunt its halls alongside the Rattata.

Some items are hidden: step on their spots to find them.

1F: Sage Li

Sage Li stands just inside the door, and he won't let anyone climb unless they hold the Rainbow Wing. Talk to him: since you've beaten the Wise Trio, he decides you've shown your strength and your bond with your Pokémon, and gives you the Rainbow Wing ours. With it, the stairs are open.

The Wise Trio now keep watch on 1F too. Gaku says the floors above are only for the one Ho-Oh chooses. Koji tells the story of the fire that took the tower to the west: three Pokémon were lost in it, and Ho-Oh gave them new life. Masa reminds you that only the holder of the Rainbow Wing may climb.

The climb

Work your way up floor by floor. The way up isn't always straight: on the higher floors, warp tiles carry you between rooms, and some of them send you back down a floor, so try each one. There are no trainers in the tower, only wild Pokémon and items on most floors: TM087 Taunt on 3F, a PP Up on 5F, a Rare Candy on 6F, a Max Revive, a Full Restore and a Max Elixir on 8F, and a Nugget on 9F.

The roof: Ho-Oh

The stairs from 10F lead out onto the roof, where Ho-Oh is waiting.

Ho-Oh, Lv 60. On the Bell Tower's roof, for the trainer who holds the Rainbow Wing. Beat it and you can try to catch it.

Whirl Islands

The Whirl Islands are the four small islands in the middle of Route 41, between Olivine City and Cianwood City. Inside is a maze of caves and underground water, going down from 1F to B1F, B2F, B3F and finally Lugia's chamber at the bottom. You'll need Surf.

Lugia only appears to a trainer carrying two things:

  • The Tidal Bell, from the five Kimono Girls at Ecruteak City's Dance Theater. They battle you once you have the Rising Badge, and give you the bell when all five are beaten.
  • The Silver Wing, from the Director of Goldenrod's Radio Tower. Once Team Rocket is gone, he's back at work on the Tower's Observation Deck. Talk to him there.

Down to the chamber

All four islands lead into 1F, but only the north-east island takes you on toward Lugia. TM297 Pluck lies on 1F. B1F is the biggest floor, with a Full Restore, a Carbos, a Nugget and an Ultra Ball lying about and more hidden. On B2F an old man says Lugia, the guardian of the islands, hid itself away beyond the waterfall basin, and nobody has seen it since. Keep going down: a Max Elixir waits on B3F, and the last stairs open into Lugia's chamber.

Lugia

Lugia, Lv 60. In Lugia's chamber, at the bottom of the Whirl Islands. It only appears if you carry both the Silver Wing and the Tidal Bell. Beat it and you can try to catch it.

When you're done, head back up and out to Route 41. The League is waiting: return to New Bark Town to start the trip east.
