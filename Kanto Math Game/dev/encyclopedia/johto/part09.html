Olivine City

Olivine City is a port town full of salt air and sailors. Route 39 leads back north, and Route 40 heads west out to sea, for trainers who can Surf. The tall Lighthouse stands by the shore, and its light guides the ships home at night. Ships leave from the harbour, too.

The Gym is here, but Jasmine, its Leader, isn't. Talk to the Gym and you hear she's at the Lighthouse, caring for a sick Ampharos, and won't leave its side until it gets medicine from Cianwood City.

{{tables:OLIVINE_CITY}}

Olivine's water can be fished from the shore once you have a rod. The Rare Candy and Full Heal are hidden in town, and the Protein and Pearl are hidden in the harbour: step on their spots to find them.

Around town, a sailor sings about the sea, and another says no ship could sail the pitch-black sea at night without the Lighthouse. A boy thinks your Pokégear is very cool.

Pokémon Center and Poké Mart

Heal your team at the Center. The Wi-Fi Club, the Union Room and the link desk upstairs are all closed. The Mart sells the usual goods.

Fishing Guru's house

The Fishing Guru has fished in Olivine for thirty years. He thinks you have an angler's soul, and gives you a Good Rod. It hooks stronger Pokémon than the Old Rod, like Krabby and Corsola here.

Olivine Cafe

Sailors come here for big, hearty meals. One of them mentions that you sometimes find Pokémon nobody has seen before up in the mountains.

Houses

A man in one house wants a Krabby. Bring him one and he'll trade you his Voltorb, Billy, who comes holding a Cheri Berry. In another house, a man says there are four little islands on the way to Cianwood.

Olivine Harbor

The S.S. Aqua sails from here to Vermilion City in Kanto, but you need a ticket to board. A ferry to the Orange Islands docks here too ours, but it only takes the Champion of both Kanto and Johto.

Much later. After you become Champion, Professor Elm gives you a ticket for the S.S. Aqua.

Olivine Lighthouse

The Lighthouse was built long ago in honour of the Pokémon that lit the sea around Olivine at night. Its name tag on the map calls it the Glitter Lighthouse. It has five floors, a ledge around the outside and the lamp room at the very top. Trainers come here to train, so expect a hard climb.

1F to 4F

On 1F, a sailor warns you the Lighthouse is full of trainers, and a woman tells you why it was built. Take the stairs up through 2F to 3F, then up again to 4F. You can pick up a Rare Candy on 3F and an Ether on 4F.

The Lighthouse's outside ledge

The stairs on 4F don't go any higher from where you arrive. Instead, go out through the door on 4F onto the ledge that runs round the outside of the tower, then back in through the door to 3F. That puts you on the other side of 3F, where more stairs lead up to the far part of 4F and on to 5F. A TM for Charge Beam lies among the rocks on the ledge.

Jasmine and Amphy

Jasmine is at the top, beside her Ampharos, Amphy. Amphy looks terribly weak, and the light in its tail is barely flickering, so the Lighthouse can't shine. Talk to Jasmine. She tells you the pharmacy in Cianwood City has a special medicine, but she can't leave Amphy alone. She asks you to go for her, and unlocks the lift so you can ride it straight back down to Olivine.

{{items:OLIVINE_LIGHTHOUSE}}

Cianwood City is across the sea to the west. You'll need Surf to get there, by way of Route 40 and Route 41.

Route 40

Route 40 starts on the beach west of Olivine and runs out into the sea. Surf out from the shore and follow the route out to sea until it joins Route 41. Swimmers are out in the water, and you meet them as you surf past.

The beach

A swimmer on the sand thinks the sea is warm enough to swim all the way to Cianwood, and a surfer is very happy with the waves. On Mondays, a girl called Monica is here. She gives you a Sharp Beak, which powers up Flying moves for the Pokémon holding it. She has brothers and sisters all over Johto, each out on their own day.

A gate on Route 40 leads to Frontier Access, on the way to the Battle Frontier.

Route 41

Route 41 is open sea between Olivine and Cianwood City. It's full of Swimmers: eleven of them, so you'll have plenty of battles on the way across. Mantine glide in these waters, and Chinchou and Shellder bite on a Good or Super Rod.

Four small islands sit in the middle of the route. These are the Whirl Islands, and you'll explore them much later in the story. For now, keep surfing on to Cianwood City.
