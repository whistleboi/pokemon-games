Ecruteak City

Ecruteak City is an old city full of history, where the past is never far away. Route 37 comes up from the south, Route 38 heads west toward Olivine City, and Route 42 lies to the east, though you'll need Surf to go that way. Two towers once stood here. The tall Bell Tower still stands, behind a gate and up Bellchime Trail, but the other burned down long ago, and its blackened ruin is the Burned Tower.

The Gym is closed for now. A man stands in its doorway and tells you Morty, the Leader, has gone off to the Burned Tower.

{{journal:johto_m9}} {{tables:ECRUTEAK_CITY}}

The city's pond can be fished from the shore once you have a rod. The four hidden items are found by stepping on their spots.

Around town, a girl says three Pokémon were lost when the tower burned, and an old man explains that Ecruteak once had a tower in the east and one in the west. A Kimono Girl is on her way to practise at the Dance Theater, and an old woman loves seeing a Pokémon walking along beside you. The guards at the edges of town say Olivine is a port to the west, and warn you that Mt. Mortar is a maze inside.

Bill

Bill, the Pokémon PC's inventor, is in town checking on the PC system. He invites you to visit his house in Goldenrod City. Go and see him there afterwards: he has an Eevee for you.

Pokémon Center and Poké Mart

Heal your team at the Center. Upstairs, the Wi-Fi Club, the Union Room and the link desk are all closed. The Mart sells the same goods as every other Poké Mart in Johto.

Houses

In one house, a trainer hands you an Itemfinder. It points out items hidden where you can't see them, and Ecruteak has four. A book on his shelf tells the town's history: two towers, each the home of a mighty Flying Pokémon, and three Pokémon born from the ashes of the one that burned.

In another house, an old man remembers the day the sky went dark: a huge Flying Pokémon blotted out the sun and flew away toward the sea.

Burned Tower

The Burned Tower is the ruin of the tower that burned down long ago. Its 1F is a ruin of charred beams, and a way down leads to the basement, B1F. Wild Pokémon live on both floors, and now and then a Magmar turns up, most often by day.

{{tables:BURNED_TOWER}}

The hidden items are found by stepping on their spots.

{{trainers:BURNED_TOWER}}

1F: Eusine and Morty

Two people are looking around inside. Eusine has come chasing the legendary Pokémon said to sleep under the tower, Suicune above all. Morty, the Gym Leader, came along with his old friend. He tells you the tower's Pokémon are said to sleep in the basement, and asks you to go and look. He'll be back at the Gym when you're ready.

B1F: Rival battle

Your rival has come looking for the legendary Pokémon too, and he's waiting in the basement. He thinks only the strongest trainers deserve them, and that you aren't one.

{{battle:BURNED_TOWER:rival}}

The Gym stays shut until you've beaten him here. If he wins, come back in and battle him again.

B1F: The legendary beasts

Walk on past where your rival stood. Three Pokémon lie sleeping under the tower: Raikou, Entei and Suicune. They wake at once. Raikou and Entei dash past you and are gone, and Suicune stops to look straight at you before it leaps away too. Now the beasts have run off, the Gym is open again.

Ecruteak City (again)

Ecruteak Gym

Morty's Gym is pitch dark. You can only see a little way around you, and the floor is a narrow path with drops on both sides. Step off it and you fall, landing back by the entrance. Feel your way forward slowly until you reach Morty at the far end.

Morty

Before he battles, Morty wants to play a game with you ours. Win it, and the battle starts. Morty uses Ghost Pokémon. Normal and Fighting moves can't touch them, and Gengar and Haunter are part Poison, so Psychic and Ground moves work well. Watch out for Hypnosis and Curse.

{{battle:ECRUTEAK_CITY:Morty}}

Win and you get the Fog Badge. It lets you use Surf outside battle. Morty also gives you an Amulet Coin and a TM for Shadow Ball. With the Fog Badge, a Super Rod turns up in your Bag too ours.

Dance Theater

The Kimono Girls perform at the Dance Theater, and they train every day, in battle as well as in dance. Today a Team Rocket Grunt is pestering one of them, and a gentleman in the audience begs you to do something about it.

{{tables:ECRUTEAK_DANCE_THEATER}}

The Rocket Grunt

Walk up to the Grunt and he wants to know who you are.

{{battle:ECRUTEAK_DANCE_THEATER:Rocket Grunt}}

Beaten, he worries about what his bosses will say if they find out he was goofing off. The gentleman is delighted, and gives you HM Surf to thank you. Teach it to one of your Pokémon from the Bag. With the Fog Badge you can now cross water, so Route 42, east of Ecruteak, is open, and so is the sea west of Olivine City.

A man in the audience is training his Psyduck to Surf and dance, hoping the Kimono Girls will notice them.

Later: the Kimono Girls

The five Kimono Girls don't battle yet. Come back once you have the Rising Badge, Johto's eighth, and each of them takes you on with one of the Eevee evolutions. Miki is the last of the sisters. Beat all five and they give you the Tidal Bell, which rings for the guardian of the sea.

Bellchime Trail

In Ecruteak, a sage guards the gate to the Bell Tower. Only trainers who hold the Fog Badge may pass, so the gate is open to you now. Beyond it, Bellchime Trail runs through autumn trees to the foot of the Bell Tower.

{{tables:BELLCHIME_TRAIL}}

The mushrooms are hidden: step on their spots to find them.

The Wise Trio

Three sages, the Wise Trio, stand guard on the trail ours, and they won't let you past until you've beaten all three. Their Pokémon are around Lv 32, far stronger than anything else in this part of the guide, so there's no hurry. The Journal brings them up once you hold all eight badges.

{{trainers:BELLCHIME_TRAIL}} {{journal:johto_m17b}}

Once all three are beaten, the trail to the Bell Tower is open, and the Trio go on ahead to wait for you inside.

Route 38

Route 38 runs west from Ecruteak City through grassy fields. It meets Route 39 at its west end. Look out for Miltank, Tauros and Farfetch'd in the grass, and Snubbull too.

{{tables:JOHTO_ROUTE_38}}

The white Apricorn tree gives one Apricorn a day, once you have the Apricorn Box. The second HP Up is hidden: step on its spot.

{{trainers:JOHTO_ROUTE_38}}

Route 39

Route 39 turns south from Route 38 down to Olivine City. Moomoo Farm sits along the way, and its Miltank graze in the fields beside the road.

{{tables:JOHTO_ROUTE_39}}

The green Apricorn tree gives one Apricorn a day, once you have the Apricorn Box. The Nugget, PP Up and X Attack are hidden: step on their spots.

{{trainers:JOHTO_ROUTE_39}}

Moomoo Farm

The farmer is worried: his Miltank Moomoo has stopped giving milk, and the farm is famous for it. Out in the barn, two girls tell you Moomoo is sick and needs lots of Oran Berries. Talk to Moomoo with Oran Berries in your Bag to feed her. She needs seven before she's well again.

Once Moomoo is healthy, the farmer's wife gives you a TM for Natural Gift, whose power and type depend on the Berry your Pokémon holds. From then on, the farmhouse sells bottles of Moomoo Milk.

Carry on south, and Route 39 brings you out in Olivine City.
