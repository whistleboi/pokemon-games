Introduction

Start a new game and pick Johto from the region list. Choose your look and type your name, then name your rival. In Johto your rival is a red-haired boy who doesn't think much of anyone, you included.

The game opens in your bedroom on the second floor of your house in New Bark Town. Your Journal (in the MENU) always shows your next main mission, and a gold marker on the map points the way.

How this guide plays

This guide is written for two settings in Options › Gameplay: Battle style: Overworld and Level cap: Story. The cap is on from the start; the battle style you switch on yourself.

Overworld battles

On routes, in caves and forests, and in places like Sprout Tower, you can see the wild Pokémon walking about, mostly in the tall grass. Walk into one and the battle happens right there on the map: it fights from where it stands, yours steps up beside it, and small HP bars float over both. Trainers on these maps spot you and walk over. Their words come up in the map's text box, they send out their Pokémon in front of them, and you fight on the map too. In the trainer tables, "on the map" marks these.

Every battle still works the same way: pick a move and answer its math question to land it. The buttons and questions are the usual ones.

The important story battles always open the battle screen: Gym Leaders, Team Rocket's bosses, your rival, the Elite Four and the Champion. So do the trainers inside Gyms and towns, story scenes, a bite on your rod, a shaken Headbutt tree and the grass inside a town. Each battle box says which.

Morning, day and night

Johto follows your computer's clock. Morning runs from 4:00 to 9:59, day from 10:00 to 19:59, and night from 20:00 to 3:59, and many routes have different Pokémon at each time. The tables show all three. A few people only come out on one day of the week, too.

The story level cap

Your Pokémon can't level past the next Gym Leader's best Pokémon plus two. At the cap they stop gaining Exp., and Rare Candies don't work. Beat the Leader and the cap moves up. Each part starts with a box giving the cap for that stretch.

Levels never rise to meet you: wild Pokémon always come at their place's own level, from two below it to one above, and trainers keep their own levels. So the levels in this guide's tables and battle boxes are the ones you'll face, however strong your team is.

Poké Marts and prizes

Every Poké Mart in Johto sells from the same counter, and it grows with the Johto badges you hold. TMs join the shelf as you earn badges too, and the Silver League's shop adds Revives, Rare Candy, Full Restores and Max Potions.

Route trainers don't pay in Poké Balls: beating one gives you a healing prize that grows with your badges.

New Bark Town

New Bark Town is a small, breezy town on Johto's east side. Your house, Professor Elm's Pokémon Lab, Elm's own house and a neighbour's house stand around the middle of town. Route 29 leads west to Cherrygrove City. Much later, with all eight Johto badges, Route 27 opens to the west as well, on the way to Kanto.

The water can be fished from the shore once you have a rod, and surfed much later. A rod never hooks anything below its own level: Old Rod Lv 5, Good Rod Lv 10, Super Rod Lv 15.

Home

2F

You start in your bedroom. Take the stairs down to the first floor.

1F

Mom says good morning. Your friend Lyra was just round, playing hide-and-seek with her Marill, and Professor Elm came by looking for you: he needs your help, and his Lab is right next door. Talk to Mom any time you're home and she heals your team.

Lyra and Marill

Step outside and a Marill comes bounding up, with Lyra close behind. It mistook you for her. Lyra tells you the Professor has been looking for you all morning.

A red-haired boy is hanging around outside the Lab. He mutters about the famous Pokémon Lab, then tells you to get lost. If you try to leave town by the west road without a Pokémon, a woman runs over: the tall grass is full of wild Pokémon, and she walks you back.

Around town, a woman admires your Bag and envies anyone with a Pokémon in it. The sign says New Bark Town is where fresh winds start every journey.

Professor Elm's Lab

Professor Elm is waiting for you. His friend Mr. Pokémon keeps finding odd things, and he's just emailed about a new discovery. Elm can't leave his research, so he asks you to visit Mr. Pokémon for him. He lives past Cherrygrove City, on Route 30. You'll need a Pokémon for the trip, so Elm lets you choose one of three.

Click a Pokémon to pick it, then answer two math questions correctly to make it yours.

Chikorita

Grass. Falkner's Flying Pokémon and Bugsy's Bug Pokémon both hit it hard, so it's the hardest start.

Cyndaquil

Fire. Strong against Bugsy's Bug Pokémon in Azalea Town.

Totodile

Water. Neither of the first two Gyms is strong against it, so it's an even start.

Elm's aide stands by the shelves. Once you have your Pokémon, talk to the aide for five Potions for the trip.

Mom's Pokégear

As you come out of the Lab, Lyra runs over to see your new Pokémon and tells you to show your mom before you go. Do it: Mom gives you a Pokégear. It has a phone, so Mom and the trainers you meet can call you. Mom still heals your team whenever you drop by.

When you reach the west edge of town, Professor Elm calls after you and hurries over. He forgot to give you his phone number, and adds himself to your Pokégear.

The other houses

In Elm's house, his wife says the Professor practically lives at the Lab, and their son hopes you'll come and show him your Pokémon. In the neighbour's house, a lady tells you her son is off travelling and the house feels quiet without him.

Route 29

Route 29 is a long country road west from New Bark Town to Cherrygrove City. Tall grass lines the way, and ledges let you hop back east quickly but not climb up again. There are no trainers here, so it's a good place to level your new Pokémon. The Pidgey and Sentret of the daytime give way to Hoothoot at night.

{{tables:JOHTO_ROUTE_29}}

The Apricorn tree needs an Apricorn Box to carry its fruit, from a house on Route 30. Each tree gives one Apricorn a day.

Lyra's catching lesson

Soon after you set off, Lyra catches up with you. Before you go any further, she wants to show you how to catch a Pokémon: weaken it in battle first, then throw a Poké Ball. She gives you five Poké Balls of your own, so you can catch wild Pokémon straight away.

People on the route

A woman near the ledges says hopping down them is a quick way back to New Bark Town without crossing the grass. A man is waiting for both the day and the night Pokémon to come out, and a youngster warns you to keep a worn-out team away from the tall grass. A big man has stopped to save his progress.

On Tuesdays, Tuscany waits on the route. She has a Twisted Spoon for you once you hold the Zephyr Badge, so come back after Violet City. A Pokémon holding it hits harder with Psychic-type moves.

Keep heading west to reach Cherrygrove City.
