Eterna Forest

Eterna Forest is a dark, old forest at the north end of Route 205. The sign says time seems to stand still here. Its paths wind through tall grass full of Bug Pokémon, and it comes out on the west side of Eterna City. A building in the woods leads into the Old Chateau.

{{journal:sinnoh_m4}} {{tables:ETERNA_FOREST}}

A Potion, an Antidote, a Paralyze Heal, a Net Ball, an Ether and a Green Shard lie on the ground, and the Insect Plate is hidden. A Trainer Tips sign reminds you that items hide in dark forests and caves, so look closely.

{{trainers:ETERNA_FOREST}}

Cheryl

In the forest, a trainer named Cheryl is nervous about the dark forest and about Team Galactic. She asks you to walk her to the other side. Say yes and she joins you: she walks along behind you, battles beside you, and heals your whole team after every battle. While she's with you, wild Pokémon come out two at a time, and pairs of trainers battle you both together. Her Pokémon is a Lv 20 Chansey.

When you reach Eterna City she thanks you and gives you a Soothe Bell, which helps a Pokémon grow fond of you faster. If you turn back the way you came, she waits for you where you met her.

Elsewhere in the forest, a boy is catching Bug Pokémon to take on the Eterna Gym, and a mossy rock feels cool to the touch.

Old Chateau

The Old Chateau is an old mansion reached from Eterna Forest. Its dining room, hall and five bedrooms are dark, and only Gastly roam the halls. It's a side trip, not part of the main story.

{{journal:sinnoh_s7}} {{tables:OLD_CHATEAU}}

The Rare Candy and Big Pearl are in the dining room, with Honey hidden there too. The Old Gateau is in the side room, the Dread Plate under a bed in Bedroom 1, and TM103 Substitute in Bedroom 5.

Ghosts in the Chateau

Now and then, as you step into the dining room, the ghost of a butler floats across it and drifts away. In Bedroom 4, a little girl is sometimes standing by the wall: she looks at you for a while, then wanders off. You can't speak to either of them.

Rotom

Come back at night, and something is waiting in Bedroom 2: Rotom. It only shows itself at night. Battle it, and you can try to catch it.

The Black Fog

The Black Fog ours. Somewhere in the Old Chateau, the air turns ice-cold and a black fog pours across the floor. A giant Haunter rises out of it: people once worshipped it as a god, and it feeds on dreams. It's always as strong as your strongest Pokémon, and it can't be caught. Beat it for 10 Poké Balls and 3 Rare Candies. It vanishes in a burst of black mist, whispering that it will rise again in another haunted place, and sometimes it gathers here again.

Eterna City

Eterna City is an old city. Its sign calls it a town that keeps its old stories, and a statue of an ancient Pokémon stands in the middle of it. It has a Pokémon Center, a Poké Mart, a Gym, a Bike Shop, the Eterna Condominiums and a tall Team Galactic building at the north end. Eterna Forest is to the west, Route 211 leads east toward Mt. Coronet, and Cycling Road runs south.

{{journal:sinnoh_m4b}} {{tables:ETERNA_CITY}}

TM018 Thief, a Guard Spec. and a Super Potion lie on the ground in town, and the Draco Plate, a Moon Stone and another Super Potion are hidden. An old lady in the Condominiums gives you TM301 Recycle. The pond can be surfed and fished later.

Eterna Gym

Gardenia's Gym is a garden built like a clock. Beds of flowers in full bloom wall off the paths, and you mustn't trample them. Each time you beat one of the Gym's trainers, the flower beds shift, like the hands of a clock moving on, and a new way opens toward the next trainer, until the path to Gardenia is clear. The Gym Guide's tip: Gardenia uses Grass types, and they hate fire.

{{trainers:ETERNA_CITY}}

The three fishermen in the table are outside, by the water.

{{battle:ETERNA_CITY:Gardenia}}

Beat her for the Forest Badge and TM081 Grass Knot. The Forest Badge also puts HM Cut in your Bag, so your Pokémon can cut down small trees outside battle.

Cynthia by the statue

With the Forest Badge in hand, a woman by the statue calls you over. She's Cynthia. She tells you Team Galactic has been cutting down trees and blocking roads around here, and that your Forest Badge lets your Pokémon use Cut outside battle, so small trees won't stop you now.

Around Eterna

A little boy says the statue shows an amazing Pokémon from long ago, and an old man says Eterna has changed a lot since he was young. One woman notes that the city is old, even with all its tall buildings; another says the Galactic building makes the whole city tense. A boy can't believe Team Galactic would be thieves, and a fisherman enjoys his slow hobby. A man and a boy both rave about Cycling Road, which runs all the way down toward Hearthome.

Pokémon Center and Poké Mart

Healing is free. In the Center, a visitor points out that a fainted Pokémon can still use field moves, and a kid says Team Galactic took his Pokémon. Upstairs and down, the Wi-Fi Club, Union Room, Colosseum and Wi-Fi Plaza are all closed today. In the Mart, a shopper says more badges mean more Mart items, and a hiker wishes he'd carried an Escape Rope in Eterna Forest.

Bike Shop

Rad Rickshaw's Cycle Shop is short-handed: the helper says the manager went to the Galactic building and hasn't come back.

Eterna Condominiums

The Name Rater lives here and will rate your Pokémon's nicknames. A girl will trade her Chatot, Charap, for a Buizel; it holds a Leppa Berry. The superintendent is fast asleep on watch. Upstairs, an old lady gives you TM301 Recycle, and a neighbour wonders why Gardenia keeps going into Eterna Forest. On the third floor, a girl is off to the Hearthome Contests and two kids are playing at capturing flags.

Houses

In one house, a man is upset that someone stole the plate off the statue, and two old folk remember when the statue was the heart of every festival. In another, a herb shop sells Heal Powder, Energy Powder, Energy Root and Revival Herb, all bitter but strong. The Underground Man's house is home to the man who dug the first tunnels under Sinnoh; he says they aren't ready for visitors yet.

Galactic Eterna Building

Team Galactic's building stands at the north end of Eterna City. Its sign says "Pokémon wanted. No questions asked." The door stays shut until you hold the Forest Badge. Inside are four floors of grunts, and the stolen Pokémon are being kept at the top.

{{journal:sinnoh_m4c}} {{tables:ETERNA_GALACTIC_BUILDING}}

An X Speed is on 2F and an X Sp. Atk on 3F. On 4F, an Up-Grade, a Blue Shard and a Revive lie around the room.

{{trainers:ETERNA_GALACTIC_BUILDING}}

Scientist Travon waits on 3F.

Commander Jupiter

Climb to 4F, where Commander Jupiter is waiting, amused that you came all the way up.

{{battle:ETERNA_GALACTIC_BUILDING:Jupiter|On 4F, at the top of the building.}}

Beaten, Jupiter says Team Galactic is leaving anyway: they have what they came for. The stolen Clefairy is rescued. Its owner is up here too, still wondering why anyone would take their Pokémon, and thanks you for coming.

Eterna City (again)

Cynthia's Egg

As you try to leave Eterna, Cynthia stops you: she wants to give you something before you go. She hands you a Pokémon Egg and asks you to raise it well. Keep it in your team as you travel, and it will hatch before long.

The Bicycle

Back at the Bike Shop, Rad Rickshaw thanks you for chasing off Team Galactic and gives you a Bicycle. You need it for Cycling Road, Route 206, which is now open to the south of town.
