Route 214

Route 214 starts at a gatehouse on the edge of Veilstone City and runs down to Valor Lakefront, on the shore of Lake Valor. It's a rocky road with grass and a stretch of water. Maniac Tunnel opens off the route, and there's a way to Sendoff Spring that stays closed until much later in the story.

A Rare Candy, a Max Repel, a Magmarizer, a Big Root and a Red Shard lie on the ground, and a Carbos, a Big Mushroom and a Razor Fang are hidden. The water can be surfed and fished later.

Maniac Tunnel

Maniac Tunnel is a short cave dug out from Route 214. A Ruin Maniac has been digging here, and he's proud of it: his tunnel broke right through into the Solaceon Ruins, and he wants you to thank him. Its far end comes out in the ruins' Room 8.

{{tables:MANIAC_TUNNEL}}

TM055 Dig lies on the ground. Hippopotas lives here.

Valor Lakefront

Valor Lakefront is the grassy shore south of Lake Valor, between Route 214 and Route 213. It's a holiday spot, with a restaurant and a couple of hotel suites. The path up to the lake itself is closed for now; you'll go there in Part 11.

{{tables:VALOR_LAKEFRONT}}

An Ultra Ball, an Iron and TM270 Dream Eater lie on the ground, and a Sun Stone, a PP Up and a Big Mushroom are hidden. The White Flute is a thank-you from a hotel guest (see below), and Cynthia's SecretPotion comes later in this part.

Two cameramen are waiting quietly by the lake: a legend lives in it, they say, and the lake's Pokémon hide if they sense people. One filmed the red Gyarados at the Lake of Rage, and wants to film this one too.

The restaurant

The restaurant is fine dining with battles on the side. Nine pairs of diners eat here, and five of them come in each day. Each pair battles you together, two against two. Beat a pair and they'll be back for another battle tomorrow. The waiter welcomes you and tells you to enjoy yourself.

The diners. Artist Ismael (Kricketune Lv 28) and Beauty Harley (Psyduck Lv 26); Rich Boy Roman (Lickitung Lv 26) and Lady Kylie (Cleffa Lv 24, Clefairy Lv 26); Gentleman Leonardo and Socialite Rebecca (Chatot and Purugly, Lv 27); Collector Eugene (Feebas Lv 28) and Aroma Lady Alison (Roselia Lv 28); Pokéfan Meredith (Pichu and Pachirisu, Lv 26) and School Kid Esteban (Quagsire Lv 26); Veteran Emanuel (Machoke and Bronzor, Lv 28) and Lass Blythe (Chingling and Buneary, Lv 25); Reporter Valerie (Kirlia Lv 26) and Cameraman Darryl (Magnemite and Mr. Mime, Lv 26); PI Kendrick (Rhyhorn Lv 31) and Beauty Gabriella (Finneon Lv 26); Scientist Emilio (Kadabra Lv 27) and Breeder Kaylee (Aipom, Marill and Swablu, Lv 26). Each trainer pays 2 Poké Balls.

The Suite Key

A worried hotel guest outside has dropped their Suite Key somewhere again, and is sure they had it when they left the reception by Route 213. The key is hidden on Route 213. Bring it back and the guest gives you a White Flute found by the lake.

Hotel suites

In one of the suites, a director wonders how you met every Pokémon in your Pokédex: each one has its own story.

Route 213

Route 213 is a long, sandy beach between Valor Lakefront and Pastoria City. The Hotel Grand Lake stands here, with its suites, and so does Dr. Footstep's house. Much of the route is sea, for swimmers and later for you, once you can Surf.

{{tables:SINNOH_ROUTE_213}}

PP Up, Protein, a Yellow Shard, TM172 Roar, TM027 Aerial Ace, a Water Stone and a Max Revive lie on the ground. Lots of things are hidden in the sand: Pearls, Big Pearls, Heart Scales, Max Revives and more, and the hotel guest's Suite Key. In the hotel suites, a clown gives you TM161 Trick Room and a quiet couple give you a Heal Ball.

{{trainers:SINNOH_ROUTE_213}}

A woman on the beach prefers pools to the salty sea, and a fisherman says the fancy people at the hotel restaurant only battle in pairs. A big man feels something is on top of the cliff, but has no way to climb up.

Hotel Grand Lake

The hotel is fully booked, but the desk clerk lets you rest a while, which heals your team. In the suites, a clown from a troupe of street performers gives you TM161 Trick Room instead of an introduction, and a guitarist and a fortune teller are staying with him. A couple who finally got a quiet room together give you a Heal Ball, and a traveller dreams of seeing Clefairy dance under the moon at Mt. Moon in Kanto.

Dr. Footstep

Dr. Footstep reads what a Pokémon is thinking from its footprints. Show him your first Pokémon and he tells you how it feels about you.

Route 213 Gate

In the gatehouse, a man talks about the footprints Pokémon leave on the beach, and a gentleman has heard of the Battle Frontier, where you battle under all sorts of rules.

Pastoria City

Pastoria City is the city by the marsh. Water Pokémon love it, and the Great Marsh just outside keeps its summers cool. Pastoria has a Pokémon Center, a Poké Mart, a Gym, and the Great Marsh Observatory, where the Safari Game starts. Route 213 is to the east and Route 212 to the west.

{{journal:sinnoh_m6c}} {{tables:PASTORIA_CITY}}

A Mystic Water lies by a path, and a Zinc is hidden. A boy north of the Mart gives you a Macho Brace if you bring both a male and a female Combee and show them to him together. The water can be surfed and fished later.

Rival battle

Your rival is in Pastoria, standing near Wake's Gym, and nearly plows into you again. He wants to test how tough you've got, and battles you.

Your rival (battle screen; rewards: 5 Poké Balls). If you chose Turtwig: Starly Lv 26, Buizel Lv 26, Roselia Lv 27, Monferno Lv 31. If you chose Chimchar: Starly Lv 26, Roselia Lv 26, Ponyta Lv 27, Prinplup Lv 31. If you chose Piplup: Starly Lv 26, Buizel Lv 26, Ponyta Lv 27, Grotle Lv 31.

Beaten, he admits you've got a bit tougher. He has joined Mr. Wake, the Gym Leader, as an apprentice, and wants his own theme song.

Outside the Great Marsh Observatory, a Team Galactic Grunt is grumbling: a package from the Veilstone warehouse hasn't arrived yet, and he wishes he had a job with lots of explosives.

Pastoria Gym

Crasher Wake's Gym is a pool with walkways at three heights, and the water level decides which ones you can walk on. Step on a button to change it: orange buttons lower the water, green ones set it in the middle, and blue ones raise it. A raised walkway can only be crossed when the water is low, a middle one when it's in the middle, and a low one when the water is high. Where the water's at the wrong height, you can't cross. The Gym Guide thinks you're brave to bring Fire or Ground types against a Water master.

The Galactic Bomb

Outside the Gym, your rival is thrilled you got the badge, and Wake comes out to see what the racket is. Wake tells him he never took him on as an apprentice, and that you two can get strong on your own. Your rival doesn't care, and anyway, there's trouble: a Team Galactic goon in front of the Observatory Gate has been talking about a bomb! Wake storms off to stop him, with your rival close behind.

At the Observatory Gate, Wake vows to protect the Great Marsh... and then the ground shakes with a huge boom. The grunt comes out, quite pleased with himself. The package was a bomb, he explains, the Galactic Bomb, and he was told to push the button, so he did. He runs off to report the test results, calling Wake's mask absurd. Wake tells you both not to follow him into the marsh, in case there's any bomb left, and goes in. Your rival stays to guard the gate and sends you after the grunt.

The chase: Route 213 and Valor Lakefront (again)

On Route 213, you catch the grunt muttering to himself: they needed the energy from the Valley Windworks to make the bomb, and their boss, who built it, is a scientific genius. He catches you eavesdropping, boasts that there's not much the bomb can't blow up, and runs off toward Valor Lakefront.

Further along the beach, Looker hurries over. He came because Team Galactic's cargo worried him. When he hears it was a bomb, and that it went off in the Great Marsh, he realises the man he saw running away was the bomber, and rushes off after him.

At Valor Lakefront, the grunt is out of breath and the lake is in sight. You're still on his tail, so he battles you.

He can't battle any more, he says, and he'll have to give "this" to the Commander.

Cynthia and the SecretPotion

Cynthia is at the lakefront, researching the lake's folklore: there's said to be an island in the lake, and on it a mirage of a Pokémon. Your rival runs up looking for the grunt, wonders if Cynthia is your big sister, and passes on a message from Mr. Wake: the explosion in the Great Marsh wasn't anything serious. Then he dashes off again.

Cynthia says a grunt just ran past her muttering about a new world. Since you're going north past Solaceon, she gives you a SecretPotion for the Psyduck with headaches on Route 210. That's where Part 9 begins.

Great Marsh

The Great Marsh is a big, muddy marsh next to Pastoria City. Rare Pokémon live here, and you catch them in the Safari Game. While your rival guards the gate after the bomb, nobody can go in; once you've caught the grunt at Valor Lakefront, it's open again.

The Safari Game

The receptionist in the Observatory Gate explains the rules. You start with 30 Safari Balls, and they're the only kind of Ball you can use in the marsh. The game ends when you run out of Safari Balls or have walked 500 steps. A cowgirl says that catching five Pokémon in one game makes you a real marsh hand.

The marsh is split into six areas, and the marsh train runs between them. Each area has its own mix of Pokémon. Deep mud can trap your feet, and Pokémon may jump out while you're stuck. A big man in the marsh is still deciding whether bait or mud works better. Another man out in the marsh doesn't collect Shards, so he gives you one he picked up, in a colour chosen at random: come back once a day for another.

{{tables:GREAT_MARSH}}

Items lie all over the marsh: four Shards, two Poké Balls and two Great Balls on the ground, and a Rare Candy, the Toxic Plate, a Leaf Stone, a Hyper Potion, a Big Mushroom and a Max Repel hidden in the mud.

Great Marsh Observatory

Upstairs in the Observatory, you can look out over the Great Marsh through binoculars to see which Pokémon live where. A man there recommends the trams for reaching the deeper areas.

Around Pastoria

A big man says he's standing right in the middle of town. A man mentions Mr. Backlot in the Pokémon Mansion on Route 212, who loves to show off his rare Pokémon, and a scientist studies the rare Pokémon that live only in the Great Marsh.

Houses

The Move Reminder lives here, though he's not sure your Pokémon need him: they already remember everything. In another house, a tram worker explains that the marsh trams run on rails so they don't harm the marsh. The Scarf man would give scarves to worthy Pokémon for Contests, but there are no Contests round here these days. A Berry hiker has planted Berries all over Sinnoh.

Pokémon Center and Poké Mart

Healing is free. A Black Belt tells you to see the marsh from the Observatory, and a girl says the Great Marsh used to be sea. In the Mart, a shopper says Croagunk is the town mascot.

Route 212

Route 212 runs between Hearthome City and Pastoria City. Its northern part, near Hearthome, is home to the Pokémon Mansion and its grounds, with policemen on patrol. The southern part, near Pastoria, is a wet, muddy marsh. Log bridges here need your Bicycle, and a Trainer Tips sign says to keep moving if you get stuck in the mud.

Coming from Pastoria, you can walk all the way to Hearthome now. From the Hearthome side, the gate is open once you've been to Pastoria.

{{tables:SINNOH_ROUTE_212}}

More than a dozen items lie along the route, including TM049 Sunny Day, TM300 Silver Wind, TM083 Poison Jab and TM175 Toxic, and ten more are hidden, among them a Dawn Stone and a Rare Candy.

At night, more policemen walk the route: they say hi, and one admits they patrol too much. A boy says Mr. Backlot just wants to show off his Pokémon, and another reminds you that you can ride your Bike across fallen logs. A lady stuck in the mud calls it a full-body mud pack.

The Shard tutor

A man in a house on the marshy part of the route teaches moves in return for coloured Shards. Each move has its own price in Red, Blue, Yellow and Green Shards. He can teach Dive, Fury Cutter, Icy Wind, Thunder Punch, Fire Punch, Ice Punch, Ominous Wind, Air Cutter, Zen Headbutt, Vacuum Wave, Trick, Knock Off and Sucker Punch. Two girls in the house chat about their daddy's Shard collection: he likes Blue Shards best.

Trophy Garden

The Trophy Garden is a garden reached through a gate on the northern part of Route 212. It's a quiet patch of grass where Pikachu and Pichu live.
