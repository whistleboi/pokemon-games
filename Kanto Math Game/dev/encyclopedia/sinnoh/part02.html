Sandgem Town

Sandgem Town is a small seaside town at the east end of Route 201. The sign calls it the town of sand. Professor Rowan's Pokémon Research Lab is here, and so are a Pokémon Center, a Poké Mart and the house of the Professor's assistant. Route 202 leads north toward Jubilife City, and Route 219 runs south to the sea.

{{tables:SANDGEM_TOWN}}

The Poké Radar is for much later: Roseanne in the Lab only hands it over once you have the National Pokédex. Route 219 is for later too (see Part 15).

Professor Rowan's Lab

Professor Rowan recognises you: you're the one he met at Lake Verity. Your Pokémon looks happy with you, he says, so he gives you a Pokédex, which records every Pokémon you meet. He asks you to record them all, because Sinnoh is wider than you think. Now you can catch wild Pokémon, with the Poké Balls you won from your rival.

Two aides work in the Lab. One is amazed by your Pokédex work. The other, Roseanne, tells you the Professor built a new Pokédex together with a professor from Kanto. Come back once you have the National Pokédex and she gives you the Poké Radar (see the Introduction in Part 1).

The assistant's house

Next to the Lab is the house of Dawn, the Professor's assistant. Talk to her at home and she tells you the Professor needs her on Route 202: meet her there, and she'll show you something useful. Her grandpa says his son and grandchild both help Professor Rowan now. Her little sibling cheers you on with your Pokédex. Much later, once you have the National Pokédex, the little sibling also tells you where today's swarm is, straight from the TV news.

Around Sandgem

A woman always saves before she takes a break, a man is jealous of your Pokédex, and a boy wishes he had a Pokémon of his own. In one house, the neighbours tell you a good Trainer never lets a Pokémon faint if they can help it, and to take it slow and heal at the Center often.

Pokémon Center

Heal your team for free at the counter, and use the PC in the corner to store your Pokémon. Upstairs, the Wi-Fi Club, the Union Room and the Colosseum are all closed, and downstairs, so is the Wi-Fi Plaza.

Poké Mart

This is your first Poké Mart. One shopper is stocking up on Potions, and another reminds you to always carry Poké Balls.

Route 202

Route 202 runs north from Sandgem Town to Jubilife City, through patches of tall grass. It's the first route where you can catch wild Pokémon, and Shinx, an Electric Pokémon, lives here too.

A Potion lies in the grass. On a swarm day (after the National Pokédex), Zigzagoon fill the grass here.

Dawn's catching lesson

As you walk up the route, Dawn catches up with you. Have you caught a Pokémon yet? She'll show you how. A wild Bidoof jumps out of the grass, Dawn's Pokémon knocks it about a bit, and she throws a Poké Ball. Gotcha!

The trick, she explains, is to weaken a Pokémon first and then throw a Poké Ball: the more it's hurt, the easier it is to catch. She gives you five Poké Balls to get you started.

A boy on the route wants to see every Pokémon, since some only show up in the morning and some only at night. The sign points north to Jubilife City.

Jubilife City

Jubilife City is a big, busy city: its sign calls it the city of joy. An old man tells you it was carved out of a mountain, with help from Oreburgh's people and Pokémon. Its tall buildings include the Pokétch Company, Jubilife TV, the Jubilife Condominiums, the Trainers' School and the Global Terminal. Route 202 leads south to Sandgem Town, Route 203 east toward Oreburgh City, Route 204 north toward Floaroma Town, and Route 218 west, across the water.

{{tables:JUBILIFE_CITY}}

A Potion lies by a path, and a Stardust is hidden in town. Route 218 needs Surf (see Part 10).

The Pokétch campaign

The President of the Pokétch Company is shocked to meet a Trainer without a Pokétch, the Pokémon Watch. He's giving them away! Three clowns are hidden around Jubilife, and each one asks a question. Answer it right and the clown gives you a Pokétch Coupon. Get one wrong, and you can have another think and try again.

The three questions are all about Pokémon basics: battles and Exp. Points, the types of moves, and items your Pokémon can hold. Bring all three Coupons to the Pokétch Company and the President gives you your very own Pokétch. In the building, a developer promises new Pokétch apps, and a girl says a TV show about a Pokétch detective makes her use hers more.

Until you have the Pokétch, you can't leave town to the east. A man in a trench coat steps in front of you at the way to Route 203: every Trainer in Jubilife is getting a Pokétch for free, he says, so find the clowns first.

The man in the trench coat

That same strange man is hiding behind a lamp post in town. Talk to him and he introduces himself as Looker, of the International Police. He's investigating a criminal group called Team Galactic, whose leader, Cyrus, has plans that threaten all of Sinnoh. If you learn anything, he says, come and find him.

Around Jubilife

A little girl was interviewed on TV, a woman says walking with your Pokémon makes it fonder of you bit by bit, and a boy reminds you that six Pokémon is the most you can carry. Two kids are busy trading Pokémon, one with a held item as a present for the other.

Trainers' School

The teacher says Trainers and Pokémon can grow at their own pace. The students share what they've learnt: a Potion restores HP, Pokémon use held Berries on their own but can't use Potions by themselves, and today's lesson is that Grass loses to Fire, Fire to Water and Water to Grass.

The tall house

One house in town has four floors. On the ground floor, a lady explains that some items only help when a Pokémon holds one, and gives you a Quick Claw, which sometimes lets a slow Pokémon move first. Climb all the way to the top floor and a lady there is impressed by your focus: she gives you a Focus Band, which may let a Pokémon hang on with 1 HP. On the floors in between, people talk about Heal Balls, new moves and trading with the world.

Jubilife TV

The TV station has a Lottery Corner (not open yet), a fitting room for dressing up your Pokémon on the second floor, and records rooms on the third, where some machines are still being tuned. On the fourth floor, the station's president thinks a fine Trainer like you belongs on their shows.

Global Terminal

The Global Terminal is where Trainers trade with the whole world. Its guide won't let you in until you have a Gym Badge. Even then, the Global Trade Station inside isn't open yet: the link isn't finished.

Pokémon Center and Poké Mart

Healing is free, as always. In the Center, someone explains that a Pokémon you catch with six already in your party goes straight to the PC. In the Mart, one shopper wishes they'd weakened Pokémon before throwing Poké Balls, and another suggests a Repel when you'd rather skip battles.

Other houses

In one house, a boy traded for a Pikachu that grows fast but doesn't always listen. In another, a helpful girl says that if you're ever stuck, talk to everyone and go everywhere, and a woman wonders whether Professor Rowan can solve the mystery of Evolution.

With the Pokétch on your wrist, head east to Route 203 (see Part 3).
