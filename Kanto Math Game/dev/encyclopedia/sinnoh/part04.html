Jubilife City (again)

Trouble in Jubilife

Back in Jubilife City, a Team Galactic Grunt is shouting in the street. Their commander, Mars, has a message for the city: the old world must be erased so their new universe can be born, and anyone who gets in the way will be erased too. Talk to the grunt to step in. It's a battle on the battle screen, against a Zubat and a Glameow.

Beaten, the grunt calls a retreat back to Eterna. With the Coal Badge, the Global Terminal's guide lets you in now, too.

{{journal:sinnoh_m3b}}

Route 204

Route 204 runs north from Jubilife City toward Floaroma Town. The route comes in two halves, and the only way from one to the other is the Ravaged Path, a cave in the middle of the route.

{{tables:SINNOH_ROUTE_204}}

Items lie along both halves: TM Bullet Seed, an Awakening, an HP Up, a Paralyze Heal and a Sea Incense. A teacher from the Trainers' School is giving a lesson about charm, and gives you TM302 Captivate, a move that works on Pokémon of the other gender. The water needs Surf or a rod.

{{trainers:SINNOH_ROUTE_204}}

The Twins Liv & Liz battle you together, two against two, with a Pachirisu each. Around the route, a boy near the cave thinks a Pokémon move could smash the rocks that block it, and another tells you to mix up your team's types, since one type alone loses to its weakness.

Ravaged Path

The Ravaged Path is a small, winding cave that joins the two halves of Route 204. You can only go in once you hold the Coal Badge, and inside, cracked rocks block the way across: break them with Rock Smash. Zubat and Psyduck live here, and there's water to Surf and fish later.

{{tables:RAVAGED_PATH}}

The cave has lots of items: TM Water Pulse, TM Rock Tomb, a Luck Incense (a Pokémon holding it earns more prize money) and a Potion.

Come out on the north half of Route 204 and follow it north to Floaroma Town.

Floaroma Town

Floaroma Town is the town of flowers, vivid and scented. Its people planted flowers on a bare hill long ago to cheer it up, and now they grow everywhere. The town has a Pokémon Center, a Poké Mart and the flower shop Pick a Peck of Colors. Route 205 leads east toward the Valley Windworks and Eterna Forest, and Floaroma Meadow is at the north edge of town.

{{journal:sinnoh_m3c}} {{tables:FLOAROMA_TOWN}}

Pick a Peck of Colors

On your first visit, the florist gives you a Berry Pouch, with three each of Cheri, Chesto, Pecha, Rawst and Aspear Berries. Berries grow on trees along Sinnoh's routes, the florist explains, and you can pick them once a day. Later you can cook them into Poffins in Hearthome City. After that, the shop sells Berries, with more kinds once you hold three badges.

Grunts at the meadow

Two Team Galactic Grunts stand guard at the way into Floaroma Meadow and won't let anyone in. One of them grumbles that guarding a field of flowers can't be their big mission. They stay until you've heard what's going on at the Valley Windworks from a little girl on Route 205.

Another grunt hangs about in town. Talk to the grunt, who boasts that the Valley Windworks belongs to Team Galactic now: you'll need the key to get in, if you can take it. Beat the grunt on the battle screen (a Stunky and a Zubat) and he hands over the Works Key, then the grunts run off to the Windworks.

Around Floaroma

A camper followed the smell of Honey all the way here, a woman says Gracidea flowers are given to say thank you, and a girl tells you the flower shop trades for Berries too.

Houses

In one house, two neighbours tell you some Pokémon only come out when sweet Honey draws them in, and can smell it from very far away. In another, a little girl thinks it's cute how Pokémon pluck Berries. Agree with her and she gives you TM297 Pluck: the move eats the Berry the foe is holding. Her Clefairy is there too.

Pokémon Center and Poké Mart

Healing is free, as always. In the Center, someone's Pokémon was saved by eating its Berry when its HP ran low. In the Mart, a shopper is glad Marts don't sell Berries or Accessories: less shopping!

Route 205

Route 205 leaves Floaroma Town to the east. The Valley Windworks is at its east end, a Rest House stands along the way, and the route goes on north to Eterna Forest. Shellos, Buizel and Pachirisu live in its grass.

{{tables:SINNOH_ROUTE_205}}

A Repel, a Super Potion, a Heal Ball and an X Sp. Def lie on the route, and a Stardust is hidden. The Fuego Ironworks, to the west, can only be reached this way with Surf.

{{trainers:SINNOH_ROUTE_205}}

A little girl in trouble

A little girl runs up to you, begging for help. Men in funny space suits have taken over the Valley Windworks and chased her out, and her papa is still inside. The Windworks is just east of here.

A line of Team Galactic Grunts also stands across the route, and nobody gets past them: Team Galactic has business at the Windworks. They block the way north to Eterna Forest until the Windworks is free again.

Rest House

A kind lady in the Rest House tells you to rest a while, and heals your Pokémon for free. A boy points out that a Repel keeps weak Pokémon away, but then there's nothing to catch.

Now that you've met the little girl, the grunts at the meadow in Floaroma Town have stepped aside.

Floaroma Meadow

Floaroma Meadow is a big field of flowers at the north edge of Floaroma Town. It's quiet, with no wild Pokémon, but Team Galactic has moved in. The path at the top of the meadow leads on to the Fuego Ironworks.

{{tables:FLOAROMA_MEADOW}}

The meadow is full of hidden items, among them six Honey, a Max Revive and a PP Up, and a Miracle Seed, an Ultra Ball, a Leaf Stone and a Rare Candy lie in the open.

{{trainers:FLOAROMA_MEADOW}}

Beat the two grunts. The second one has the Works Key for the Valley Windworks' door, and hands it over when you win.

Honey

The Honey man is proud of the meadow's sweet Honey, which Pokémon come running for. In the little house, a hiker thanks the Pokémon for the meadow's bright flowers. Use Honey out on a route and it draws in three wild Pokémon at once.

Valley Windworks

The Valley Windworks is a wind power plant at the east end of Route 205. Team Galactic has locked themselves inside, and the door won't open without the Works Key from the grunts in Floaroma Meadow.

{{tables:VALLEY_WINDWORKS}}

TM126 Thunderbolt, an Electirizer and a Potion lie on the ground outside. On a swarm day (after the National Pokédex), Electrike fill the grass here.

Commander Mars

Unlock the door with the Works Key and go inside. Two grunts guard the place, each sure the Windworks belongs to Team Galactic now.

{{trainers:VALLEY_WINDWORKS:Inside}}

At the back waits one of Team Galactic's commanders, Mars. The Windworks makes energy for Team Galactic's plans, Mars tells you.

{{battle:VALLEY_WINDWORKS:Mars|Inside the Windworks.}}

Beaten, Mars is furious, but says this isn't over. With every grunt beaten, Team Galactic leaves the Windworks. The little girl's papa works here: he tells you that a balloon Pokémon floats up outside one day each week, maybe because it likes the wind they make. On Fridays, a Drifloon drifts about outside the Windworks.

The grunts on Route 205 are gone too, so the way north to Eterna Forest is open.

Head back west along Route 205 and north into Eterna Forest (see Part 5).

Fuego Ironworks

The Fuego Ironworks is a big ironworks west of Route 205 and north of Floaroma Meadow. Inside, the floor is covered in moving panels that carry you along until something stops you. It's a place for much later: its wild Pokémon are around Lv 28–30, and the workers inside have teams up to Lv 35.

{{tables:FUEGO_IRONWORKS}}

The Ironworks is full of items, among them TM125 Flamethrower, a Fire Stone, a Rock Incense, a Calcium, all four colours of Shard, and a hidden Star Piece and Protein. Outside, an Ultra Ball and a Big Mushroom are hidden.

{{trainers:FUEGO_IRONWORKS}}
