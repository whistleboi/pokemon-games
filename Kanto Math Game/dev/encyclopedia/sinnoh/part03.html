Route 203

Route 203 runs east from Jubilife City to Oreburgh Gate, the tunnel that leads through to Oreburgh City. It's a bit tougher than the routes so far, with five trainers along the way, and Abra hides in its grass.

{{tables:SINNOH_ROUTE_203}}

A Poké Ball, a Repel and an X Defense lie along the route. The water is for later, once you can Surf or have a rod. On a swarm day (after the National Pokédex), Cubone fill the grass here.

Rival battle 2

Your rival has been waiting for you here. He's heading to Oreburgh City for his first Gym Badge, but first, a battle! He has a Starly now, as well as his starter.

{{battle:SINNOH_ROUTE_203:rival|As you walk onto Route 203 · can't be skipped.}}

Lose, and he jokes that you owe him a million. Win, and he vows to train even harder.

{{trainers:SINNOH_ROUTE_203}}

Lass Kaitlin sends out four Pokémon, one after another, so have a Potion ready. A girl on the route reminds you that the Pokémon at the top of your party goes out first in battle.

Oreburgh Gate

Oreburgh Gate is a short cave between Route 203 and Oreburgh City. Walk straight through 1F to come out in the city. Zubat and Geodude live in the dark, and Psyduck too.

{{tables:OREBURGH_GATE}}

Cracked rocks block the way to the stairs down to B1F: you need Rock Smash. B1F is for much later. It also has boulders and water in the way, a Veteran with a team around Lv 34, and items worth coming back for: TM Focus Punch, TM Brick Break, TM Flash, a Big Pearl and an Earth Plate.

{{trainers:OREBURGH_GATE:1F}}

A hiker on 1F says a brand-new Trainer like you can smash small rocks out of the way once you earn Oreburgh's Badge. Come back with the Coal Badge and he gives you HM Rock Smash.

Oreburgh City

Oreburgh City is a mining city, the city of energy. Coal from the Oreburgh Mine, just south of the city, keeps it going: piles of leftover sand stand around, workers and their Machop break up rocks, and vents blow hot air up from the mine. The city has a Pokémon Center, a Poké Mart, the Oreburgh Mining Museum and a Gym, whose Leader, Roark, uses Rock-type Pokémon. Route 207 leads north, but it's closed for now (see Part 6).

{{journal:sinnoh_m2}} {{tables:OREBURGH_CITY}}

A Yellow Shard and a Dire Hit lie on the ground, and a Pearl is hidden in town. A mine worker gives you a Super Potion to keep you healthy.

Where's Roark?

The Gym won't let you in yet: Roark is digging in the Oreburgh Mine, so go and find him there first. People around town tell you how busy he is: he's young, but he runs safety at the mine too. A hiker says the big mountain up ahead splits Sinnoh right down the middle.

Oreburgh Mining Museum

The museum tells the story of the mine, built by people and Pokémon working together, and visitors marvel at a huge lump of coal. It's also a fossil lab: bring it a fossil and it brings the ancient Pokémon back to life. You'll get one from Roark.

Pokémon Center and Poké Mart

Healing is free, as always. In the Center, a gentleman has heard that Team Galactic talks about a dream energy, but steals Pokémon. In the Mart, a shopper says more Badges means the Mart sells you more.

Houses

  • On the second floor of one house, a girl gives you a Dusk Ball, which works best at night or in caves. In the same room, a girl sighs that their Psyduck was named Yellow just because it's yellow.
  • In another, a girl will trade her Abra, nicknamed Kazza, for a Machop. Machop work in the mine and live on Route 207. Upstairs, someone who finds going outside a chore just wants to see a Geodude: show one in your party and you get a Heal Ball.
  • In a third, a boy upstairs is so happy to see your Pokémon's twinkling eyes that he gives you a Great Ball.
  • Elsewhere, people tell you Sinnoh has eight Gym Leaders, that Roark lets them train in the mine, and that Pokémon have natures, a bit like personalities.

Oreburgh Mine

The Oreburgh Mine runs under the south of the city, all the way out under the sea. It has two floors, 1F and B1F, full of Geodude, with the odd Onix. Every miner keeps Pokémon, a worker warns, so don't be surprised if someone wants a battle.

{{tables:OREBURGH_MINE}}

A Poké Ball lies on 1F, and a Potion and an Escape Rope on B1F.

{{trainers:OREBURGH_MINE}}

Roark in the mine

Deep on B1F, a man in a hard hat is breaking boulders. It's Roark! He digs up fossils here between challenges, he says. If you want a Gym battle, meet him back at the Gym: he'll be waiting.

A miner nearby met his Machop on Route 207, and now they're his best work partners. On 1F, a little girl thought some rocks were rocks, until they moved.

Oreburgh Gym

The Gym guide's tip: Rock types hate Water, and Grass moves work great too. Two Youngsters with Rock Pokémon stand in your way first.

{{trainers:OREBURGH_CITY:Gym}} {{battle:OREBURGH_CITY:Roark}}

Geodude and Onix both know Rock Throw and Stealth Rock, and Onix uses Screech to lower your Defense. Cranidos is his strongest, with Headbutt. Turtwig and Piplup have the easiest time here. With Chimchar, a Water Pokémon helps a lot: Psyduck lives in Oreburgh Gate.

Beat Roark and he gives you the Coal Badge, along with a Skull Fossil: take it to the Mining Museum to bring Cranidos back to life. With the Coal Badge, the hiker in Oreburgh Gate gives you HM Rock Smash, which breaks cracked rocks.
