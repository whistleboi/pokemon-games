Route 206 (Cycling Road)

Route 206 runs south from Eterna City. Most of it is Cycling Road, a long downhill road for bikes: you can't get onto it at either end without a Bicycle. There's grass along the way, and Wayward Cave is off to the west. At the south end the route meets Route 207.

{{journal:sinnoh_m5}} {{tables:SINNOH_ROUTE_206}}

A Poison Barb, a Super Repel, a Burn Heal and a PP Up lie on the ground, and an Ether and a Tiny Mushroom are hidden. In the gatehouse, a gardener girl gives you Damp Mulch, which keeps a Berry patch wet for longer.

{{trainers:SINNOH_ROUTE_206}}

A hiker has heard that Route 206 has two caves, but he's only ever found one way in. In the gatehouse, the cyclists tell you to shift your Bike's gears and hold on tight: it's a long way down.

Wayward Cave

Wayward Cave is a dark, winding cave on the west side of Route 206. Without Flash, you can only see a little way around you. It has two floors: 1F, full of trainers, and B1F below, where narrow log bridges cross the gaps. You need your Bicycle to keep your balance on those logs. Gible lives in this cave.

{{tables:WAYWARD_CAVE}}

On 1F, a Revive, an Escape Rope, TM275 Double Team and TM149 Earthquake lie on the ground, and four Shards, a Great Ball and a Super Potion are hidden. On B1F are a Rare Candy, a Grip Claw and a Max Ether, with Stardust and a Dusk Stone hidden.

{{trainers:WAYWARD_CAVE}}

Mira

Somewhere on 1F, a little girl named Mira is lost in the dark. She asks you to take her to the exit, and her Kadabra (Lv 26) will help you battle. Say yes and she joins you: she walks behind you, battles beside you, and heals your team after every battle. While she's with you, wild Pokémon come two at a time, and pairs of trainers battle you both together. Once you're out of the cave, she thanks you and says she'll get stronger so the two of you can battle one day.

Route 207

Route 207 is a short, rocky road. Its sign says Cycling Road is to the north and Oreburgh City to the south, and the entrance to Mt. Coronet is here too. Part of it is a steep slope: a cyclist tells you it's too steep to walk, so ride up it fast on your Bike.

{{tables:SINNOH_ROUTE_207}}

The wild Pokémon here are only Lv 5–7, since this route is right next to Oreburgh City. The trainers are much stronger. A Poké Ball, a Super Potion, an Iron, a Timer Ball and a Revive lie on the ground, and a Rare Candy, a Red Shard, a Poké Ball and a Great Ball are hidden.

{{trainers:SINNOH_ROUTE_207}}

Through Mt. Coronet

Go into Mt. Coronet from Route 207 and walk through the mountain's tunnel to come out on Route 208, on its east side. Mt. Coronet's wild Pokémon and items are in Part 11, when the story takes you deeper inside.

Route 208

Route 208 runs east from Mt. Coronet to Hearthome City. Along the way are tall grass, a river and the Berry Master's House.

{{tables:SINNOH_ROUTE_208}}

A Carbos, an Ether and a Great Ball lie on the ground, and a Star Piece and a Tiny Mushroom are hidden. A silent Black Belt hands you an Odd Keystone: an old stone, he says, for a tower on Route 209. The river can be surfed and fished later.

{{trainers:SINNOH_ROUTE_208}}

Hiker Alexander and Fisherman Cody are far stronger than everyone else here, so be ready for them.

Berry Master's House

The sign outside promises free Berries every day, and inside, the Berry Master keeps that promise: visit once a day and he gives you two Berries of one kind, picked from a long list. A woman says Mulch is good for Berry soil and reads from a Berry-grower's guide to Mulch, and a girl keeps forgetting where she planted her Berries. A Trainer Tips sign outside reminds you that pale, dry soil means a Berry plant is thirsty.

Hearthome City

Hearthome City is a big, friendly city: its sign calls it a city with a warm heart, and a visitor says it gets voted the nicest place to live in Sinnoh every year. It's famous for Pokémon Contests. The city has the Contest Hall, Amity Square, the Poffin House, the Pokémon Fan Club, the Pokémon Hotel, a Pokémon Center, a Poké Mart and a Gym. Routes 208, 209 and 212 all meet here.

{{tables:HEARTHOME_CITY}}

A Big Mushroom is hidden in town. The Fan Club Chairman gives you a Poffin Case, and a guest at the Pokémon Hotel gives you a Shell Bell.

Rival battle

Your rival has been waiting for you here. As soon as you arrive, he says it's time to pay you back, and battles you.

{{battle:HEARTHOME_CITY:rival|As you arrive in Hearthome, once you hold the Forest Badge.}}

He can't believe you're always one step ahead.

The Hearthome Gym

Fantina, the Gym Leader, isn't at her Gym: she's at the Contest Hall, watching the performances. She'll be back once you've proven yourself with four badges. Carry on east to Veilstone City for now; the Gym is in Part 9.

{{journal:sinnoh_m5b}}

Contest Hall

Pokémon Contests are held here. Your Pokémon is judged on its condition first, then makes five appeals with its moves; each appeal works by answering a maths problem ours. Win a rank and the next one opens. The people in the hall talk about winners' photos on the wall and practising the basics, and a clown and a fan explain the two rounds: treats raise your Pokémon's condition, which the Judges look at first. Upstairs, three coaches add tips: feed treats that suit the contest, don't use the same move twice in a row, and answer the Judges' sums quickly for a brighter appeal.

Amity Square

Amity Square is a park where you can stroll with a small, cute Pokémon. Its gates are at both ends of the park. A man at one gate is upset that they wouldn't let his Gyarados and Steelix in. Walk around with your partner and it sniffs about happily, though it doesn't always find anything.

Poffin House

Here you can cook Poffins, treats that make your Pokémon look their best in Contests. Pick up to three different Berries, then stir the batter through three stages by answering maths problems ours: right answers stir it, wrong ones spill it and slow ones burn it. The cooks inside share tips: stir gently at first, then fast as it firms up, and rare Berries make the best Poffins. You need a Poffin Case to keep them in: the lady in the Pokémon Center sends you to the Fan Club for one.

{{journal:sinnoh_s9}}

Around Hearthome

The streets are busy. A Black Belt has trained thirty years in the mountains and now wants to win Contests with his own dance, while another keeps rematching a trainer with his Vs. Seeker. A hiker found a feather in Amity Square for his Pachirisu. A man says people bring their sadness to the Lost Tower on Route 209, and another says an Egg was found at the Solaceon Day Care, just past here. Mr. Goods hunts for rare things. Two parents push a stroller about town, a visitor from Hoenn got lost and was shown the way, and a girl used a Fire Stone and her Pokémon evolved.

Pokémon Fan Club

The Chairman loves Pokémon and wants you to treat yours to Poffins: he gives you a Poffin Case to keep them in. A member says your Pokémon looks really happy with you.

Pokémon Hotel

On 1F, a little girl's Shroomish finds Berries in Amity Square, and a cleaner keeps the place tidy for visitors. Take the lift to 2F, where a guest gives you a Shell Bell, since they already had one.

Other houses

The man who built the Pokémon Boxes lives in one house and loves meeting people who use them. In a big house full of thinkers, a poet, a craftsman, an elder and others share their thoughts on life. In a block of flats with a lift, a big family with lots of children and Pokémon lives on 1F, and some grandparents on 2F like the lift better than stairs.

Pokémon Center and Poké Mart

Healing is free. A visitor says Fantina loves Contests too, and another talks about the Global Terminal in Jubilife. Upstairs and down, the Wi-Fi Club, Union Room, Colosseum and Wi-Fi Plaza are all closed. In the Mart, a shopper says Amity Square has Accessories you can't buy in any Mart, and another can't buy Revives yet with only two badges.

Gatehouses

In the Route 208 gate, a Contest fan's darling is entering today. In the Route 209 gate, a girl wonders about an old broken pillar on the route ahead. The Route 212 gate is blocked: a reporter and a cameraman are filming for TV and won't let you through. Once you've been to Pastoria City, the way opens (Part 8).

Route 209

Route 209 links Hearthome City with Solaceon Town to the north. Its sign says the Lost Tower is to the west. It's a long, grassy road with water along the way, and the rare Chansey hides in its grass.

{{tables:SINNOH_ROUTE_209}}

A Calcium, TM239 Steel Wing, an Ether, a Great Ball, a Hyper Potion and TM111 Giga Drain lie on the ground, and Stardust, a Great Ball, a Revive and a Super Repel are hidden. A fisherman teaches you how to fish and gives you the Good Rod, which catches better Pokémon.

{{trainers:SINNOH_ROUTE_209}}

A man says the Lost Tower is where departed Pokémon rest, and two joggers are out on their daily run.

Lost Tower

The Lost Tower stands on Route 209. It's a quiet place of five floors where people remember Pokémon that have passed away. A woman on 1F asks you to keep your voice down; another warns that fog fills the upper floors, and without Defog you won't see a thing up there.

{{tables:LOST_TOWER}}

An Oval Stone is on 2F with Honey hidden there, a Revive on 3F, and TM274 Return on 4F with a Great Ball hidden. The Spell Tag and Cleanse Tag come from the two old women on 5F.

{{trainers:LOST_TOWER}}

The foggy top floors

3F, 4F and 5F are full of fog. Clear it with Defog: HM Defog is in the Solaceon Ruins, just ahead. On 5F, two old women are lost in the fog. Once you have Defog, the first is so relieved that she gives you a Spell Tag, and the second gives you a Cleanse Tag to keep you safe on your way out.

Solaceon Town

Solaceon Town is a quiet farming town; its sign calls it a quiet town without a care. A farmer says it was just a road once, where people and Pokémon stopped to rest, and a town grew. It has the Pokémon Day Care, the Pokémon News Press, a Pokémon Center and a Poké Mart. The Solaceon Ruins are just outside town. Route 209 is to the south and Route 210 to the north.

{{tables:SOLACEON_TOWN}}

A Rare Candy and a PP Up lie on the ground, and a Big Mushroom is hidden.

Pokémon Day Care

The Day Care looks after your Pokémon while you travel, and raises them for you. A little girl in town says the Day Care lady raises her Pokémon because she's too little to battle well, and the town's old folk find it a mystery how Eggs turn up at the Day Care.

Around Solaceon

Two cowgirls tell you to slow down and take it easy; one visits the Lost Tower every Sunday. A Ruin Maniac says every Pokémon you meet is part of your story. At the Pokémon News Press, a reporter says Sinnoh's small newspaper is printed right here. In other houses, a rancher remembers the big Pokémon ranches that used to be here, and a lady loves decorating Poké Balls with Seals, while a little boy says the Pokémon in the ruins look like letters.

Pokémon Center and Poké Mart

Healing is free. An explorer tells you that Defog is in the Solaceon Ruins, and a girl says there's a cafe on the long road to Veilstone. Upstairs and down, the link rooms are closed. In the Mart, a shopper suggests Super Repel when Repel isn't enough.

Solaceon Ruins

The Solaceon Ruins are an underground maze of small stone rooms, nineteen of them, that all look much the same. Unown, the letter Pokémon, are the only wild Pokémon here. Room 8 has a second way out, into Maniac Tunnel (see Part 8).

{{tables:SOLACEON_RUINS}}

HM Defog lies in room 7, with a Nugget, the Mind Plate and an Odd Incense. A Fire Stone, a Water Stone and a Thunder Stone are hidden in three of the far rooms.

{{trainers:SOLACEON_RUINS}}

A hiker in room 2 is lost: he came for the Defog HM too. Come back once you've found it, and he gives you a Green Shard for your trouble.

Defog. With HM Defog you can clear the fog in the Lost Tower (and, later, on Route 210).
