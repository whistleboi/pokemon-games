Route 210

Route 210 leaves Solaceon Town to the north. It comes in two halves. The southern half is a grassy road with the Cafe Cabin; from it, Route 215 branches off to the east toward Veilstone City. The northern half climbs into thick fog toward Celestic Town, but for now a group of Psyduck stands in the way, holding their heads.

{{tables:SINNOH_ROUTE_210}}

Items lie on both halves; the ones in the fog of the north half have to wait. A girl resting by the road gives you TM268 Roost. Cynthia's Old Charm comes later, once the Psyduck feel better. Some log bridges here need your Bicycle.

{{trainers:SINNOH_ROUTE_210}}

The breeders, the twins, Belle & Pa, the jogger and the rancher (Lv 23–24) are on the southern half. Everyone else, from Lv 29 up, is past the Psyduck on the northern half.

Cafe Cabin

The Cafe Cabin's sign promises fresh Moomoo Milk every day. The owner points at the Psyduck outside: they get terrible headaches, so everyone just has to wait for them to feel better. The waitress says some of the customers like to battle.

The Psyduck

The Psyduck block the way north. They have terrible headaches, and a SecretPotion would help them. You'll get one in Part 8, after Pastoria City. For now, take Route 215 east to Veilstone City.

The north half (later)

Once you've given the Psyduck the SecretPotion, they wander off and Cynthia is there: she thanks you, and asks you to take the Old Charm to her grandmother in Celestic Town, north past the fog (Part 9). The fog is thick: a Black Belt wonders if flying Pokémon just flap it away, and Defog clears it. Grandma Wilma lives up here. She comes from a town of Dragon Tamers, and teaches Draco Meteor to a Dragon-type Pokémon that loves you dearly.

Route 215

Route 215 runs east from Route 210 to Veilstone City. Its sign points back west to Route 210. It's a road for training: Black Belts, joggers and Ace Trainers line the way.

{{tables:SINNOH_ROUTE_215}}

An HP Up, the Fist Plate, TM298 Shock Wave, a Max Ether, a Full Heal and a Hyper Potion lie on the ground, and a Revive, an Ultra Ball and an Iron are hidden. A Black Belt explains how Payback hits back, and gives you TM247 Payback.

{{trainers:SINNOH_ROUTE_215}}

Two joggers on the route are taking it easy: one says don't overdo it, the other stops to rest now and then.

Veilstone City

Veilstone City is a city carved out of the rock; a man says it even has meteorites. It's a big shopping city, with the huge Veilstone Department Store, the Game Corner and the Prize Exchange, a Pokémon Center and a Gym. Team Galactic's spiky headquarters, the Galactic Veilstone Building, stands here too, and so does the Galactic Warehouse. Route 215 comes in from Route 210, and Route 214 leads off toward Lake Valor.

{{tables:VEILSTONE_CITY}}

A Star Piece, a Rare Candy and a Full Incense lie on the ground in town, and a Calcium and a PP Up are hidden. A tough guy gives you TM280 Embargo, a kind lady in the Department Store gives you Honey, and a strongman on the rooftop gives you a Sticky Barb.

A loud stranger

As you head for the Gym, Dawn, Professor Rowan's assistant, catches up with you: she's been shopping at the Department Store, and working on her Pokédex too, of course. Then a big man in a mask comes bounding over, shouting his own theme song. He tells you the Gym Leader here is young, but a born genius: he trained with her once and was stunned by how tough she was. He's Crasher Wake, the Gym Leader of Pastoria City, and he says you'll meet again. Dawn wishes you luck with your Gym challenge.

{{journal:sinnoh_m5c}}

Veilstone Gym

Maylene's Gym is full of heavy punching bags and stacks of tires. Walk into a bag to hit it, and it slides away from you until something stops it. If a stack of tires is next in its way, the bag knocks the stack over and it's gone. A bag that can't move just swings. Clear a path this way through the Gym's rooms to reach the trainers and Maylene. If you leave the Gym, the bags and tires are set back when you come in again.

{{trainers:VEILSTONE_CITY:Gym}} {{battle:VEILSTONE_CITY:Maylene}}

Beat her for the Cobble Badge and TM073 Drain Punch.

Dawn's Pokédex

Once you have the badge, Dawn runs up to you near the Gym, upset. Some Team Galactic grunts have taken her Pokédex, and she needs your help to get it back. She'll wait for you in front of the warehouses.

{{journal:sinnoh_m6}}

Two grunts are guarding the Galactic Warehouse, and before you have the Cobble Badge they just tell you to go and play at the Gym. Now they laugh that you've brought a buddy, and agree to take you on two against two. It's a double battle: you and Dawn against both grunts. Dawn's team is Clefairy, Kadabra and a Prinplup, Grotle or Monferno, depending on the starter you chose.

Two Galactic Grunts (a double battle beside Dawn, in front of the Galactic Warehouse). One grunt has Zubat Lv 24 and Stunky Lv 26, the other Zubat Lv 24 and Croagunk Lv 26. Each pays 3 Poké Balls. Dawn's Clefairy and Kadabra are Lv 25, and her starter Lv 27.

Beaten, the grunts hand back the Pokédex. They let slip that the things they had in the warehouse have already been moved to Pastoria City, and run.

Dawn thanks you and says she's off to the Pokémon Mansion on Route 212. Then Looker, the man in the trench coat from Jubilife, turns up: he heard that children were feuding with Team Galactic. Dawn calls him "that funny man", apologises, and leaves. Looker says the warehouse needs investigating, and asks you to come and take a look with him.

Veilstone Department Store

The receptionist on 1F says each floor has its own goods, and the lift is behind her.

1F

One clerk sells Poké Balls, Great Balls, Ultra Balls, Escape Ropes, the Poké Doll and Repels; another sells medicine, from Potions up to Max Potions, Revives and Full Heals. A kind lady explains Honey: spread it on a sweet-smelling tree and wild Pokémon gather. She gives you some.

2F

The Battle Collection: one clerk sells X items, Guard Spec. and Dire Hit, and another sells vitamins (Protein, Iron, Calcium, Zinc, Carbos and HP Up).

3F

The TM Corner, with two clerks: one sells everyday TMs, the other the big moves.

4F

The Secret Base goods and Dolls are all sold out today.

Rooftop

A strongman gives you a Sticky Barb: it hurts whoever holds it, so hand it to the foe if you can. A vending machine sells cold drinks.

B1F

Food and produce. A Berry seller has Figy, Wiki, Mago, Aguav and Iapapa Berries, and a cookie seller has Lava Cookies from Lavaridge. The Rage Candy Bars are sold out, and the Poffin seller isn't selling today.

Game Corner

The Game Corner has slot machines, and the Prize Exchange swaps Coins for prizes. The players inside share tips about the slots and following Clefairy in the bonus round. Maylene's dad is here too, having a bad day: the Gym Leader is his daughter, he says, and she's a tough girl.

Around Veilstone

A lady came to shop but finds the rock walls chilly. A man has heard Team Galactic hides all sorts of things in their warehouse, even an HM. A girl won a TM at the Game Corner, and a man wonders where to get a Coin Case. A traveller greets you in another language.

Houses

In one house, a man can sense the type of a Pokémon's Hidden Power. In another, a sad clown spins a Coin. In a third, a myth fan says Mt. Coronet split Sinnoh in two. A neighbour doesn't get the spiky style of the Galactic building.

Pokémon Center

Healing is free. A visitor says Maylene looks sweet, but even grown-ups lose to her. Upstairs and down, the link rooms are closed.

Galactic Warehouse

The Galactic Warehouse belongs to Team Galactic. Its sign says "Private. Keep out!" Once the two guards are beaten, the door is open.

{{tables:GALACTIC_WAREHOUSE}}

A Dusk Stone and HM Fly are in the warehouse. The Zinc, the Galactic Key and TM148 Sludge Bomb are in the Underpass, behind a locked door (see Part 12).

Inside with Looker

Looker is waiting inside. A rusty door is locked and needs a special key, so for now you can't chase the grunts who ran away. But Team Galactic left an HM behind: Fly. Looker says you should keep it, so Team Galactic can't use it. He also tells you what he overheard in the Game Corner: Team Galactic has sent something to Pastoria City. He doesn't know what they're up to, but he has a bad feeling about it.

The Underpass. Behind the locked door is the Underpass, with two grunts and the Galactic Key that opens Team Galactic's headquarters. That comes much later in the story (Part 12).
