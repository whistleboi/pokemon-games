Celestic Town

Celestic Town is a small, quiet town in the mountains, at the end of the foggy north part of Route 210. Route 211 leaves to the west, toward Mt. Coronet and Eterna City. The town sign says simply that the past lives here: ancient ruins with a cave painting sit at its heart. There's a Pokémon Center, but no Poké Mart.

{{journal:sinnoh_m7}} {{tables:CELESTIC_TOWN}}

A Dragon Fang lies by a path in town. The Great Ball in the Pokémon Center is a gift for a trainer whose lead Pokémon loves them very much. HM03 Surf comes from the Elder inside the ruins once you've handed over the Old Charm. The water can be surfed and fished once you have Surf and a rod.

A bomb for Celestic

An old woman in town is worried: an odd "spaceman" is standing in front of the ruins, cross that there's nothing there, and he's saying he'll blow the whole town up with a bomb. It's a Team Galactic Grunt. Talk to him and he calls Celestic worthless, then battles you for getting in his way.

{{battle:CELESTIC_TOWN:Galactic Grunt|In front of the Celestic Ruins.}}

Beaten, the grunt decides the town isn't worth the trouble and gives up on it.

The Elder and the Old Charm

With the grunt gone, walk toward the ruins and the town's Elder hurries over to thank you. She spots the Old Charm you're carrying: Cynthia sent it, and Cynthia is her granddaughter. You hand the charm over. The Elder explains it was made in Celestic long ago, for a mythical being said to have created Sinnoh, and she invites you to look inside the ruins.

The Celestic Ruins

Inside, by the old cave painting, is Cyrus, the boss of Team Galactic. He has been listening to the Elder, and talks of bringing the beings of time and space together. He remembers meeting you at Mt. Coronet, asks why you'd protect an incomplete world, and battles you to show you your mistake.

{{battle:CELESTIC_TOWN:Cyrus|In the Celestic Ruins, after you deliver the Old Charm.}}

Even beaten, Cyrus says it changes nothing, and tells you to stand aside and watch him change the world.

The Elder is shaken by the strange man. She says Sinnoh's time and space hold the memories of countless people and Pokémon, so why change it? As thanks for your help, she gives you something her granddaughter never uses: HM03 Surf. A Pokémon that knows Surf can carry you across water and the sea, and that opens up a lot of Sinnoh.

Back outside, Cynthia is waiting. She asks whether you enjoyed the ruins, and suggests the library in Canalave City if you'd like to read more about Sinnoh's history. That's where Part 10 goes, after Hearthome's Gym.

Around Celestic

An old man asks whether you've come to learn the town's history, a little boy has heard that a Pokémon made the whole of Sinnoh, and a woman points out that there's no Mart here.

The Elder's house

A girl here says her big sister studies Sinnoh's myths, and wonders whether people and Pokémon were different back then.

Houses

In one house, a man with a thing for glasses has a gift that depends on the time. Visit in the daytime and he gives you the Choice Specs; visit at night and he gives you the Wise Glasses. Come back at the other time of day for the second pair. In another house, a small boy wonders if you could see all of Sinnoh from the top of Mt. Coronet, and a man calls the mountain the closest place to the sky. In a third, a Black Belt says watches are useless for training, while his Happiny chatters away.

Pokémon Center

Heal your team for free. An old woman tells you to see the cave painting while you're here, and a little girl says the town has been here since Sinnoh began. A man who believes in trust between trainer and Pokémon gives you a Great Ball if your lead Pokémon loves you enough; if not, he tells you to spend more time together. Upstairs and down, the Wi-Fi Club, Union Room, Colosseum and Wi-Fi Plaza are all closed.

Route 211

Route 211 is a rocky mountain path on both sides of Mt. Coronet. Its west end starts at Eterna City and its east end comes out at Celestic Town, with doors into Mt. Coronet in between. The way into Celestic Town from this route only opens once you hold the Fen Badge. Wild Meditite, Chingling, Bronzor, Machop and Graveler live in its grass, with Zubat about at night.

TM087 Taunt, TM120 Psychic and a Carbos lie on the ground, and a Great Ball and a Calcium are hidden. A man who keeps telling himself how strong he is jumps when you talk to him, then gives you TM203 Psych Up, which copies the foe's stat changes.

The trainers here are a real mix: Ninja Boy Zach's three Zubat and Hiker Louis are at about the level you'd meet coming from Eterna, while Bird Keeper Katherine's Noctowl and Black Belt Sean's team are well past the Lv 30 mark.

Hearthome City (again)

Back in Hearthome City, Fantina has left the Contest Hall and returned to her Gym. The Gym is dark, and the way through it is a test of your maths.

{{journal:sinnoh_m8}}
When the Gym opens. The Hearthome Gym lets you in as soon as you hold four Sinnoh badges, so you can take it on before or after Celestic Town. The Journal sends you here once the Old Charm is delivered.

Hearthome Gym

The Gym Guide in the entrance hall warns you that Fantina's Gym is dark and its doors are tricky. In each of the two trainer rooms, a voice asks you a sum as you come in ours. Every door in the room holds one of the answers: choose the right one and that door opens the way on. Choose a wrong one and you walk through it into the dark... and straight back to the entrance. The first room has three doors (circle, square and triangle), and the second has five (sun, donut, moon, star and heart), with a slightly harder question. If you leave a room and come back in, you hear a new question.

The Guide's tip for the Leader: Fantina uses Ghost types, so Ghost and Dark moves hit them hard.

Fantina

Before she battles, Fantina wants to play a game with you ours. The game is picked for her Gym the first time you challenge her and stays the same after that. Win it, and the battle starts.

{{battle:HEARTHOME_CITY:Fantina}}

Beat her for the Relic Badge and TM061 Shadow Claw. Once she's beaten, the way to the warp panel at the back of her room opens, and it takes you back to the entrance.
