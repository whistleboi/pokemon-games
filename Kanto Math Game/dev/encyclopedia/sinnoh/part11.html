Lake Valor

Lake Valor is the big lake north of Valor Lakefront. Since your last visit to the lakefront, the path up to the lake was closed. After the meeting at the Canalave Library it opens, and you find the lake in ruins: Team Galactic's explosion has drained it. Fly to Pastoria City or Veilstone City and hurry up through Valor Lakefront.

{{journal:sinnoh_m10b}} {{tables:LAKE_VALOR}}

An HP Up is hidden on the bed of the drained lake. TM166 Thunder lies among the rocks once the lake is full again.

The drained lake

Where the water used to be, there's only mud now, and Magikarp flop about weakly all over it. Team Galactic Grunts are everywhere. Some just stand in your way and boast: one says it's too late to make any difference, another that even Team Galactic has no use for Magikarp, and a third that their next stop is Lake Verity, near Twinleaf Town. Two of them battle you.

{{trainers:LAKE_VALOR}}

In the middle of the old lakebed stands the island, and in it the entrance to Valor Cavern.

Commander Saturn

Inside Valor Cavern, Commander Saturn is waiting. He says the mission is going without a hitch, and he recognises you as the kid who broke into their building in Eterna. Anyone who stands against Team Galactic must be crushed, so he battles you.

Commander Saturn (battle screen): Golbat Lv 38, Bronzor Lv 38, Toxicroak Lv 40.

Beaten, Saturn says he only had to buy them time. With the power of the lake Pokémon, Team Galactic will make a new universe. And by now, he says, Commander Mars should have caught the Pokémon of Lake Verity...

When you're done here, the water comes back: from now on, Lake Valor is a full lake again, with Psyduck and Golduck on the water. Deep in the cavern, Azelf's lair stays shut until much later, after the Spear Pillar.

Lake Verity (again)

Head back to Twinleaf Town, the town Saturn's grunt was boasting about, and go west along Route 201 to Lake Verity, where your journey began.

{{journal:sinnoh_m10c}}

Team Galactic is here too. Professor Rowan is shouting at the "misguided thugs", and a grunt complains about the old man. Dawn calls you over: Team Galactic is taking the Pokémon of the lake, and she couldn't beat the one in charge. Four grunts guard the way to the water's edge, and every one of them battles you.

{{trainers:LAKE_VERITY}}

Commander Mars

At the lake's edge is Commander Mars. Your face brings back bad memories of the Valley Windworks, where she got into a lot of trouble because of you. She says the Pokémon of this lake is theirs now, and battles you.

Commander Mars (battle screen): Golbat Lv 38, Bronzor Lv 38, Purugly Lv 40.

Beaten again, after the Windworks and now Lake Verity, Mars calls her team to pull out: the boss is waiting. Team Galactic leaves the lake.

That leaves Lake Acuity, the third lake, far up in the snowy north.

Mt. Coronet

Mt. Coronet is the huge mountain down the middle of Sinnoh. Inside, it's a maze of ten floors. You've already walked its tunnel on the first floor, between Route 207 and Route 208 (Part 6). Now go in through the Mt. Coronet door on Route 211, between Eterna City and Celestic Town: it leads into floor 8, and the way north is up from there.

{{journal:sinnoh_m12}} {{tables:MT_CORONET_BASE}}

The table lists every floor's items. The Adamant Orb and the Lustrous Orb lie on floor 5. An old cave painting is on floor 2. The water can be surfed and fished, and a Super Rod sometimes hooks a Dratini.

{{trainers:MT_CORONET_BASE}}

The Team Galactic Grunts on floors 3, 5 and 7 guard the way up to the summit. That's for later (Part 12).

The way north

From floor 8, climb the stairs to floor 9. A thick fog fills it, so you can only see what's close to you, unless you have HM Defog from the Solaceon Ruins. Find the stairs up to floor 10, and its exit leads out onto Route 216. The way out to Route 216 needs HM Strength, which you got on Iron Island.

Route 216

Route 216 is the first snowy route of the north. It starts at Mt. Coronet's door, and its sign says Route 217 is to the north. Snow Pokémon such as Snover, Sneasel and Snorunt live in its grass, and Skiers and Ace Trainers are out in the snow. Some lumpy rocks here can only be climbed with Rock Climb.

{{tables:SINNOH_ROUTE_216}}

An HP Up, a Mental Herb, TM135 Ice Beam, a Full Heal, a Max Potion and a Revive lie on the ground, and a PP Up is hidden.

{{trainers:SINNOH_ROUTE_216}}

Snowbound Lodge

On the route stands the Snowbound Lodge. It has seen better days, but its owner lets you rest, which heals your team. A Skier staying there explains that the Thick Fat Ability halves Fire and Ice damage, and since they haven't got it, they just wear layers.

Route 217

Route 217 runs north from Route 216 through deep snow, with snow falling all the while. It comes out at Acuity Lakefront, next to Snowpoint City. It's a long walk with lots of trainers and lots of things buried in the snow.

{{tables:SINNOH_ROUTE_217}}

HM Rock Climb, a Rare Candy, TM241 Hail, an Iron, an Ultra Ball and a Blue Shard lie in the snow. A lot more is hidden, including a Max Revive, a Revival Herb and a Nugget: a Skier is out searching the snow with a Dowsing Machine, because things get buried out here.

{{trainers:SINNOH_ROUTE_217}}

Maylene

On your way up the route, Maylene, the Veilstone Gym Leader, comes over to you. She's walking to Snowpoint City as training, and says she's used to the cold, even barefoot. Then she sneezes, says that was nothing, and goes on her way.

HM Rock Climb

Keep an eye out for HM Rock Climb, lying in the snow on this route. You'll need it to climb up to Lake Acuity later.

Houses

In one house, a hiker lost an HM in the blizzard. Once you've found HM Rock Climb, he tells you to keep it, and gives you the Icicle Plate he found under the snow. In the other house, a quiet woman is surprised to see a visitor at all, and gives you a Spell Tag, which powers up Ghost-type moves.

Out on the route, a frozen boulder is freezing to the touch.

Acuity Lakefront

Acuity Lakefront is the snowy ground at the top of Route 217. Snowpoint City is to the east, and Lake Acuity is up to the north, past a stretch of lumpy rocks.

{{tables:ACUITY_LAKEFRONT}}

An Ultra Ball and a Reaper Cloth lie on the ground.

Your rival

Your rival is here, and says you're way too slow. He points out the lumpy rocks: you can't climb those until you have the Snowpoint Gym's badge. Then he runs off after Team Galactic.

Head east into Snowpoint City.

Snowpoint City

Snowpoint City is a city in the far north; its sign says the snow never stops. It has a Pokémon Center, a Poké Mart, Candice's Gym and two houses. At the north end is the Snowpoint Temple, but a guard turns everyone away: only the most accomplished trainers may go in, after the Champion. A boat to the Fight Area sails only then too.

{{journal:sinnoh_m12b}} {{tables:SNOWPOINT_CITY}}

An Ultra Ball lies by a path, and a Rare Candy is hidden.

A boy has heard that Pokémon dragged the whole city here, and that the Temple is where they rest. A girl is learning from Candice, who told her to start by making friends with her Pokémon. A sailor likes the town because there's nothing special about it, and a woman says the trainers here burn hot even in the cold.

Snowpoint Gym

Candice's Gym is all ice. Step onto it and you slide until something stops you, so plan each slide. Big snowballs block the way. Walking into one won't move it; slide into it across the ice and you crush it. The Gym Guide's tip is to slide fast enough to smash the snowballs, and that Fire melts Ice.

Around Snowpoint

Pokémon Center

Heal your team for free. Maylene is here, warming up before she goes anywhere. Before your Gym battle, she tells you how much she admires Candice, who practised against her to learn about Fighting types. After it, she spots your Icicle Badge and says Rock Climb is yours to use now. A visitor wonders whether Ice types live in the Temple too, another says the snow swallows up sound, and someone's Psyduck says hello. Upstairs and down, the link rooms are closed.

Poké Mart

A shopper keeps Ice Heals handy, and another is mixing TM moves of different types to take on the Elite Four.

Houses

In one house, a lady will trade her Haunter, Gaspar, for a Medicham. Gaspar holds an Everstone, so it stays a Haunter. Her husband is proud of his lively daughter. In the other house, a tutor teaches moves in exchange for coloured Shards, each move at its own price: Snore, Spite, Helping Hand, Synthesis, Magnet Rise, Last Resort, Swift and Uproar. A trendy old man is still thinking of a trendy saying for you.

Lake Acuity

With the Icicle Badge, go back west to Acuity Lakefront. Use HM Rock Climb, from Route 217, to climb the lumpy rocks, and walk north to Lake Acuity. It's a cold lake in the falling snow. Your rival went on ahead to face Team Galactic on his own.

{{journal:sinnoh_m10d}} {{tables:LAKE_ACUITY}}

TM143 Blizzard lies among the rocks.

Commander Jupiter

You're too late. Your rival is standing up to Commander Jupiter, but she has already beaten him and taken the lake's Pokémon. She doesn't think much of him, says the cold is horrible, and that she's going back to their headquarters in Veilstone. Then she recognises you from Eterna City. Team Galactic is going to do something huge, she says, so keep out of their way, and don't bother coming to the HQ. She leaves without a battle.

Your rival admits he couldn't do anything against Team Galactic. Uxie, the lake's Pokémon, was suffering. He's going to get tougher. It's not about winning or losing, he says, but honest effort and a determined heart.

Team Galactic now has all three lake Pokémon. Head for Veilstone City and Team Galactic's headquarters (Part 12). Uxie's lair, in Acuity Cavern, stays shut until after the Spear Pillar.
