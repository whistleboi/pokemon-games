Route 218

Route 218 leaves Jubilife City to the west. It's mostly sea, so you need Surf to cross it. On the far side, the route turns north into Canalave City.

{{journal:sinnoh_m9}} {{tables:SINNOH_ROUTE_218}}

A Rare Candy, an X Accuracy and a Hyper Potion lie on the ground along the route.

{{trainers:SINNOH_ROUTE_218}}

On the shore, a coach is drilling two Clefairy to be cuter, and a fisherman is coaching two Pikachu to dazzle.

Canalave City

Canalave City is a busy port on Sinnoh's west coast. A man at the entrance tells you that long ago this was the centre of all the shipping. The Canalave Library stands over the drawbridge. The city has a Pokémon Center, a Poké Mart, Byron's Gym and a harbour with boats out to the islands, Iron Island among them.

{{tables:CANALAVE_CITY}}

TM060 U-turn lies by a path in town, and a Big Pearl is hidden. In one house, a dock worker found TM098 Skill Swap in a load of cargo from overseas and gives it to you. Fullmoon Island and Newmoon Island are further out to sea, and their boats only sail much later.

Rival battle

As you head for the Gym, your rival bumps into you. He's just won a badge of his own, and decides somebody should check you're ready for the Gym Leader: him.

{{battle:CANALAVE_CITY:rival|On the way to the Gym, once you have the Relic Badge.}} {{journal:sinnoh_m9b}}

Beaten again, your rival still says he'll be the Champion, and tells you to train on Iron Island, as he did before the Gym.

Canalave Gym

Byron's Gym is a tower of four floors. Byron only takes on challengers with five Sinnoh badges. The way up is by moving platforms: step onto one and it carries you to its other end, either up or down to another floor or sliding along the same floor. It stays there until you ride it back, so watch where you leave each one. The Gym Guide reminds you that Steel types have high Defense, so hit them hard.

Byron

Byron waits on the top floor. Before he battles, he wants to play a game with you ours. Win it, and the battle starts.

{{battle:CANALAVE_CITY:Byron}}

Beat him for the Mine Badge. He's delighted, and calls out to Roark, his son, that the kid is amazing. He also gives you TM093 Flash Cannon and an Armor Fossil, which the fossil lab in Oreburgh City can bring back to life.

The Canalave Library

With the Mine Badge, Professor Rowan wants to meet you at the library. Your rival is waiting at its door. If you don't have HM04 Strength yet, he tells you to hurry to Iron Island and get it, and he won't budge until you do.

{{journal:sinnoh_m10}}

Once you have it, go in. Professor Rowan and your rival are on the top floor. The Professor tells you about Sinnoh's myth of three lake Pokémon, Uxie, Mesprit and Azelf, and says Team Galactic is after them, so each of you should watch over a lake. Then the whole library shakes with a huge BOOM! A sailor runs in: there's been an explosion at Lake Valor. The Professor sends you there at once (Part 11).

After that, the library's book on Sinnoh's myth tells of three Pokémon born from the Original One, two of time and space and one of antimatter, and three more born to give the world its spirit: knowledge, emotion and willpower. A reader asks not to be disturbed, and the librarian asks you to keep quiet.

Around Canalave

A girl says her Psyduck's love makes its Return really strong. A woman is off to the library to read about time and space, an old man says the Gym's trainers train on Iron Island, muscles and all, and a big man guesses from your damp clothes that you've just come back on the boat.

Pokémon Center

Heal your team for free. A researcher has learned that one kind of Pokémon can have different Abilities, a woman reminds you Iron Island is just a boat ride away, and a girl got HM Strength on Iron Island. Upstairs and down, the Wi-Fi Club, Union Room, Colosseum and Wi-Fi Plaza are all closed.

Houses

The dock worker with TM098 Skill Swap lives with a girl who dreams of the lands far across the sea. The Move Deleter lives here too, though these days he mostly forgets his own lines.

Iron Island

Iron Island is a small island off Canalave, reached by boat from the harbour. Most of it is an old mine, dug deep into the rock in several floors, with a west side of its own. It's full of Rock and Steel Pokémon and the trainers who come here to toughen up.

{{journal:sinnoh_s3}} {{tables:IRON_ISLAND}}

Items lie all through the mine, including a Protector, a Magnet, a Shiny Stone, a Red and a Yellow Shard and TM265 Iron Tail, and Star Pieces and an Iron Plate are hidden. Byron's Metal Coat is a gift for much later, once you have the National Pokédex.

{{trainers:IRON_ISLAND}}

Riley

Outside, by the mine, you meet a trainer named Riley. He admits it's a bit odd to be training here, but he's glad to meet another trainer, and gives you HM04 Strength. He trains on the island with his Lucario.

Deep in the mine, on B2F, Riley is waiting again: his Lucario senses something wrong down here. Talk to him and he walks with you, a step behind your Pokémon. While he's with you, wild Pokémon come two at a time, he battles beside you against pairs of trainers, and he heals your team after every battle. His Lucario is Lv 41.

Team Galactic in the mine

Further on, two Team Galactic Grunts block the passage. Without Riley they're too much for you alone; with him, it's a double battle, you and Riley against both grunts. One has a Zubat, a Houndour and a Golbat, and the other a Glameow, a Croagunk and a Stunky, all at Lv 34. Your team is healed afterwards.

When the grunts are beaten, Riley says you fought bravely, and gives you a Pokémon that has chosen you: a Riolu. It joins your team, or goes to your PC if your team is full.

The house

Byron has a house on the island. Before the National Pokédex, he chats about his son Roark, a proper Gym Leader whom he still treats like a child. Later, once you've seen Pokémon from beyond Sinnoh, he gives you a Metal Coat, which helps some Pokémon evolve into Steel types.

Take the boat back to Canalave, and go to the library with HM Strength in your Bag.
