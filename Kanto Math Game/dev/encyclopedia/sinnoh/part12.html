Galactic Warehouse (again)

Team Galactic has carried Uxie, Mesprit and Azelf off to its headquarters in Veilstone City. Fly to Veilstone and walk into the Galactic Warehouse, where you met Looker after Maylene's Gym.

{{journal:sinnoh_m11}}

Looker's Storage Key

Looker is waiting for you. Storming the front door is no good, he says, but he has got hold of a Storage Key from Team Galactic. He uses it on the rusty door that was locked last time, and it opens. Behind it is the Underpass, which runs to Team Galactic's building in Veilstone. He sends you on ahead and promises to follow when he can.

The Underpass

The Underpass is a long tunnel full of crates. Two Galactic Grunts guard it: one with a Houndour and a Murkrow, one with a Golbat. A scientist cackles that Team Galactic will own every last Pokémon. Pick up the Galactic Key lying on the floor: the locked doors further on, in the Underpass and all through the headquarters, open only with it. The Zinc and TM148 Sludge Bomb are down here too. The Underpass comes out inside Team Galactic HQ.

With the Galactic Key in your Bag, you can also walk into the headquarters through its front door in Veilstone City.

Team Galactic HQ

Team Galactic's headquarters is the spiky building in Veilstone City. Inside, it's a maze of machines, crates and warp panels over four floors, with a Lab and the room where the lake trio are held beyond them. Grunts and scientists battle you on the way. There are no wild Pokémon.

{{tables:TEAM_GALACTIC_HQ}}

On 1F: a Dubious Disc and TM299 Snatch, with a PP Up hidden among the crates. On 2F: a Green Shard and a Max Revive, with a Poké Doll and a Max Potion hidden. On 3F: TM273 Frustration, a Protein and a Max Elixir, with an Elixir, a Rare Candy and a Nugget hidden. On 4F, a Razor Claw is hidden. A Full Restore lies in the Lab.

1F

The ground floor has doors out to Veilstone City and back down to the Underpass. A scientist here works on new energy day and night, but nobody will tell him what it's for. One Grunt battles you, and so does Scientist Fredrick, with a Kirlia and a Kadabra (both Lv 40). A door here needs the Galactic Key, and a warp panel on this floor takes you straight up to 4F and back.

{{trainers:TEAM_GALACTIC_HQ:1F}}

2F

The second floor is where the team lives, with beds, a TV and a kitchen among the machines. Three Grunts battle you here, and Scientist Darrius battles with a Porygon2 (Lv 42). Warp panels hop you around the floor, and two of them lead to a small side room that links up with 4F.

{{trainers:TEAM_GALACTIC_HQ:2F}}

3F

Rows of machines fill the third floor. Four Grunts battle you here, and there's another door that only the Galactic Key opens.

{{trainers:TEAM_GALACTIC_HQ:3F}}

4F: Cyrus

The top floor is the boss's own room, behind one more Galactic Key door. Cyrus is waiting. He admits the speech he gave his team was a lie: the new world he wants isn't for Team Galactic at all, but for himself alone. He no longer needs Uxie, Mesprit and Azelf, so you can save them if you want to. He calls caring about them a useless feeling, and battles you to prove it.

Cyrus (battle screen): Sneasel Lv 44, Golbat Lv 44, Honchkrow Lv 46. Rewards: Poké Ball ×15, Master Ball.

Cyrus grants that you are strong, and that your strength is how much you care for your Pokémon, which he thinks is a waste. Because you came alone, he hands you his Master Ball: he has no use for it. To save the lake Pokémon, he says, take the warp panel ahead. He's leaving for the peak of Mt. Coronet, where he means to make his new world.

The Lab

Go on past Cyrus to the Lab. Two scientists here are upset by what they've built: one feels sick wondering what the machine is for, and the other says the experiment went too far and he can't stomach what Cyrus has planned.

The lake trio's room

In the last room, Uxie, Mesprit and Azelf are each sealed inside a machine, and all three are clearly suffering. Commander Saturn stands guard. He can't believe you came all this way just to save some Pokémon, and wants payback for what happened at the lake.

Commander Saturn (battle screen): Golbat Lv 42, Bronzor Lv 42, Toxicroak Lv 44. Reward: Poké Ball ×10.

Beaten, Saturn gives up: do what you like with the three Pokémon, he says, and tells you to press the button on the machine. Do it, and Uxie, Mesprit and Azelf are set free and fly back to their lakes. The machines stand empty after that.

Talk to Saturn again and he tells you why Team Galactic took them: Cyrus made a Red Chain from crystals he took from the three Pokémon, to bind something on Mt. Coronet. Saturn doesn't know what Cyrus plans to do up there.
