Viridian City

Viridian City sits at the top of Route 1. Route 2 leads north toward Viridian Forest and Pewter City, and Route 22 leads west toward the Pokémon League. The city has a Pokémon Center, a Poké Mart, a Trainer school and a Gym, but the Gym's doors are locked.

{{tables:VIRIDIAN_CITY}}

The Macho Brace is hidden inside the Gym, which stays shut until much later. Viridian's water can be fished once you have a rod.

Oak's Parcel

As soon as you step into the Poké Mart, the clerk calls you over. Since you're from Pallet Town, the clerk asks you to take a parcel to Professor Oak: his order has just come in. You get Oak's Parcel. Until it reaches Oak, the clerk won't sell you anything, so head back south to Pallet Town.

The way north is closed for now too. A girl stops you on the road: her grandpa is lying right across it, and he gets grumpy if anyone disturbs him. Come back later.

Around Viridian

A youngster near the Gym wonders who its Leader even is, since the doors are always shut. The Gym's sign says the Leader is away. A bug fan warns that only Weedle has a poison stinger, and the girl who blocked the road says she goes through Viridian Forest to shop in Pewter City.

A sleepy man dreams about a feast, then offers to teach one of your Pokémon Dream Eater, if it can learn it.

Trainer school

The teacher gives five short lessons, one at a time: types (Water beats Fire, Fire beats Grass, Grass beats Water), real-time battles, catching, Mega Evolution and looking after your team. A student is busy learning notes by heart.

Pokémon Center

Heal your team for free at the counter. The PC in the corner is yours to use. Upstairs, the Union Room, Trade Center and Colosseum are all closed.

Pallet Town (again)

Take the parcel back to Professor Oak's Lab. Oak is thrilled with his order and hands you a Pokédex, which records every Pokémon you see and catch, and five Poké Balls. Now you can catch wild Pokémon.

Your rival bursts in just then. Oak gives him a Pokédex too, and asks you both to help him record every kind of Pokémon there is. Your rival boasts he'll fill his first, and rushes off.

Viridian City (again)

With the Pokédex in hand, the Poké Mart opens for business and the road north is clear.

The sleeping old man

The girl's grandpa is still dozing on the road north. Wake him and he offers you his helmet: say yes to get the Rocky Helmet ours. It hurts any Pokémon that bumps into yours, and it lets you shake Headbutt trees ours. Later you'll find him at home in the house in town, glad of his nap. His little girl and their Spearow, Speary, live there too.

Investigation at Viridian

Strange people have been seen around the locked Gym. Walk up to its doors before you've won a badge and Jessie and James of Team Rocket jump you. They don't know where their boss is, and they want you gone.

{{battle:VIRIDIAN_CITY:Jessie and James|At the Viridian Gym doors, before your first badge.}}

Once they've blasted off, the doors stay locked tight. The Gym opens only after you hold the other seven badges (see Part 15).

Route 22

Route 22 runs west from Viridian City to the Pokémon League Front Gate. The guard there only lets trainers with all eight Kanto badges through, so for now this is a short side trip for wild Pokémon and a battle.

{{tables:KANTO_ROUTE_22}}

Surfing and fishing come later. The Alolan forms show up only after you've played Alola.

Rival battle 2

Your rival headed west to Route 22. As you walk west, he comes over to battle. He's only here before you win your first badge.

Route 2

Route 2 runs north from Viridian City to Pewter City, with Viridian Forest in between: the forest's gatehouse is on the west side of the route.

{{tables:KANTO_ROUTE_2}}

A sign points to Diglett's Cave, a long tunnel that comes out near Vermilion City. A small tree blocks the way, so it needs Cut.

Forest Gate

In the gatehouse before the forest, a lady tells you Pewter's Gym Leader uses Rock Pokémon, and a boy suggests packing Antidotes.

The east side

The east side of Route 2 has a Trade House and the Route 2 Gate, where one of Professor Oak's aides waits. Show the aide you've caught ten different kinds of Pokémon and he gives you HM Flash, which lights up dark caves. In the Trade House, a boy will trade his Mr. Mime, MIMIEN, for an Abra.

Viridian Forest

Viridian Forest is a natural maze of tall grass and trees. It's full of Bug Pokémon and the Bug Catchers who love them, and Pikachu hides here too. Signs along the way give tips, such as Bug Pokémon being weak to Fire. Find the gate at the far end to come out on the north part of Route 2.

{{tables:VIRIDIAN_FOREST}}

Headbutt trees need the Rocky Helmet from Viridian City's old man. FireRed has no Headbutt trees.

{{trainers:VIRIDIAN_FOREST}}

A Bug Catcher says he and his friends came to catch bugs and are itching for battles. A youngster has thrown all his Poké Balls and run out.

Forest Gate

In the north gatehouse, a guide calls the forest a natural maze, a boy warns about Weedle's sting, and a girl is just glad her sore feet made it through. Pewter City is a short walk north.
