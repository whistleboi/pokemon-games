Cinnabar Island

Cinnabar Island is a small volcanic island out at sea, south of Pallet Town. Routes 20 and 21 reach it over the water. It has a Pokémon Center, a Poké Mart, the Pokémon Lab, Blaine's Gym and a harbour. The burned-out Pokémon Mansion stands at the north of the island.

The Gym's door is locked, and an islander tells you its key has been lost in the old Mansion for ages. A scientist outside the Lab says it can bring fossils back to life.

Pokémon Lab

The Lab studies rare Pokémon that people bring in. In the entrance hall, a photo of the Lab's founder turns out to be Dr. Fuji. Doors lead to three rooms.

Meeting Room

Two people here want to trade:

  • A woman trades her Tangela (TANGENY) for a Venonat, and a Stardust comes with it.
  • An old man trades his Electrode (ESPHERE) for a Raichu.

A scientist mentions the rare fossil he once found inside Mt. Moon.

R & D Room

A scientist is studying how Eevee decides what to evolve into. Another is fascinated by Metronome, and he teaches it to one of your Pokémon.

Testing Room

This is where fossils come back to life. Show the scientist a fossil and he revives it on the spot:

  • Helix Fossil: Omanyte
  • Dome Fossil: Kabuto
  • Old Amber: Aerodactyl

The Super Nerd who kept the other fossil in Mt. Moon sends it here for you, so on your first visit you get it too ours. That way you can revive both Omanyte and Kabuto. The Lab can also revive fossils from other regions.

A boy in the Testing Room trades his Seel (SEELOR) for a Ponyta.

Pokémon Mansion

The Pokémon Mansion was once a research lab, and now it's a burned-out ruin. Diaries left in its rooms tell the story: a new Pokémon, named Mew, was found deep in the jungles of Guyana. Later Mew gave birth to Mewtwo, which grew far too powerful to control. The Mansion has four floors (1F, 2F, 3F and B1F), and the key to the Cinnabar Gym is somewhere inside.

Rattata and Raticate, Grimer and Koffing, Growlithe and Vulpix, and Ditto live in the ruins, at the same level on every floor.

The statue switches

Heavy gates block the halls. Each floor has a Pokémon statue (B1F has two) with a secret switch on it. Press one and every gate in the Mansion swings the other way: the open ones shut and the shut ones open. If a gate is in your way, find a statue and press its switch, then try again.

The Secret Key lies in the basement, B1F. It opens the door of the Cinnabar Gym. Head back out to Cinnabar Island.

Cinnabar Gym

With the Secret Key, the Gym's door opens. Blaine loves quizzes, so his Gym is full of quiz machines. Each one has a question, and a right answer slides open the gate beside it. Answer them one by one to reach Blaine. The Gym Guide says to bring Water types.

Win the Volcano Badge, TM141 Fire Blast and a Volcano Pass. With the Volcano Badge you can use Waterfall outside battle. The Volcano Pass lets you into the island's volcano ours.

To One Island

After the battle, Blaine tells you that Bill is waiting at the harbour. Bill wants to take you to One Island in the Sevii Islands, where a legendary bird of fire is said to nest at the top of Mt. Ember. With the Volcano Badge, go to the harbour and Bill waves you aboard the Seagallop Ferry for One Island.
