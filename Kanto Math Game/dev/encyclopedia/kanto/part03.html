Pewter City

Pewter City is a stone-gray city north of Viridian Forest. It's home to the Pewter Museum of Science and to Brock's Gym, the first Gym on your journey. Route 2 leads back south, and Route 3 leads east toward Mt. Moon once you've earned the Boulder Badge.

A Poké Ball is hidden on the ground in the northwest of town.

Pewter Gym

Brock trains Rock Pokémon. The Gym Guide by the door says Normal moves don't do much to them, but Water and Grass moves hit them hard. Camper Liam stands between you and Brock and tells you you're light years from facing him.

{{trainers:PEWTER_CITY:Pewter Gym}}

Before he battles, Brock wants to play a game with you ours. The game is picked for his Gym the first time you challenge him and stays the same after that. Win it, and the battle starts.

{{battle:PEWTER_CITY:Brock|His Onix knows Tackle, Harden, Bind and Rock Tomb; Geodude knows Tackle and Defense Curl.}}

Beat him for the Boulder Badge. He also gives you TM036 Rock Tomb, a Town Map and a Rare Candy.

Pewter Museum of Science

A child's ticket costs 50. On the first floor, two fossils stand on show: a prehistoric flying Pokémon, Aerodactyl, and an ancient sea Pokémon, Kabutops. An old man is amazed by what he calls the bones of a dragon.

A wrestling fan in the Museum offers to teach one of your Pokémon Seismic Toss, which hits as hard as your Pokémon's level, every time.

The Museum has a second door at the back. Come in that way and a scientist hands you the Old Amber: it has something ancient sealed inside, and the Pokémon Lab on Cinnabar Island can bring it back to life.

2F

This month's exhibit is about space: models of the Space Shuttle and a meteorite from Mt. Moon that's thought to be a Moon Stone. An old man remembers the day people first walked on the moon, and a little girl begs her dad for a Pikachu.

Around Pewter

A girl tells you Clefairy are said to have come from the moon, and a museum fan raves about the fossils from Mt. Moon. A gardener sprays Repel to keep wild Pokémon out of his flowers.

If you try to leave east before beating Brock, a boy stops you and leads you all the way to the Gym. His tip: try Water or Grass moves.

House (northeast)

A family's Nidoran came from a trade and won't listen. The dad explains that a traded Pokémon may ignore a trainer who doesn't have enough badges.

House (southwest)

An old man says some moves can't be learned by growing up, only taught by people. A boy points out that a Pokémon with a status problem, like sleep, is easier to catch.

Pokémon Center and Poké Mart

In the Pokémon Center, a Jigglypuff's song is making a man sleepy, a boy and a girl are trading a Pikachu for a Clefairy, and a man on the phone has heard that Team Rocket is in Mt. Moon. Heal up here before you head east.

In the Poké Mart, a boy grumbles about the fish Pokémon a shady old man sold him for 500, and a shopper says even weak Pokémon can surprise you if you raise them with care.
