Introduction

Start a new game and pick Kanto from the region list. Choose your look and type your name, then name your rival. Your rival lives next door, and he is Professor Oak's grandson.

The game opens in your bedroom on the second floor of your house in Pallet Town. Your Journal (in the MENU) always shows your next main mission, and a gold marker on the map points the way.

How this guide plays

This guide is written for two settings in Options › Gameplay: Battle style: Overworld and Level cap: Story. The cap is on from the start; the battle style you switch on yourself.

Overworld battles

On routes, in caves and forests, and in places like Silph Co. and the Rocket Hideout, you can see the wild Pokémon walking about, mostly in the tall grass. Walk into one and the battle happens right there on the map: it fights from where it stands, yours steps up beside it, and small HP bars float over both. Trainers on these maps spot you and walk over. Their words come up in the map's text box, they send out their Pokémon in front of them, and you fight on the map too. In the trainer tables, "on the map" marks these.

Every battle still works the same way: pick a move and answer its math question to land it. The buttons and questions are the usual ones.

The important story battles always open the battle screen: Gym Leaders, Team Rocket's bosses, your rival, the Elite Four and the Champion. So do the trainers inside Gyms and towns, a bite on your rod, a shaken Headbutt tree and the grass inside a town. Every battle box in this guide is one of these.

The story level cap

Your Pokémon can't level past the next Gym Leader's best Pokémon plus two. At the cap they stop gaining Exp., and Rare Candies don't work. Beat the Leader and the cap moves up. Each part starts with a box giving the cap for that stretch.

When your lead Pokémon reaches the next Leader's best level, wild Pokémon catch up: in places below that level they come from three under it to one over it. The wild tables show this in the "At the cap" column, next to the place's own levels. Trainers always keep their own levels, so the levels in this guide's trainer tables and battle boxes are the ones you'll face.

Poké Marts and prizes

Every Poké Mart in Kanto sells from the same counter, and it grows with the badges you hold. TMs join the shelf as you earn badges too, and the Indigo Plateau's shop adds Revives, Rare Candy, Full Restores and Max Potions.

{{mart}}

Route trainers don't pay in Poké Balls: beating one gives you a healing prize that grows with your badges.

Pallet Town

Pallet Town is a small, quiet town in the southwest of Kanto. Your house is on the west side and your rival's is on the east. Professor Oak's Pokémon Research Lab sits to the south. Route 1 leads north to Viridian City, and later you can Surf south to Route 21 and Cinnabar Island.

{{tables:PALLET_TOWN}}

Pallet's water can be fished from the shore once you have a rod. A rod never hooks anything below its own level: Old Rod Lv 5, Good Rod Lv 10, Super Rod Lv 15.

Home

2F

You start in your bedroom. The game console in the corner tells you that you played all last night, and the note on the wall reminds you to open the MENU and look around. Take the stairs in the corner down to the first floor.

1F

Mom is up already. She tells you today is the big day: Professor Oak was asking about you, and his Lab is just down the road. Leave by the mat at the bottom of the room.

{{journal:m0a}}

Oak appears!

Oak isn't at his Lab yet, so head for the tall grass at the north edge of town. As you reach it, Oak calls out to stop you: wild Pokémon hide in that grass, and walking in without one of your own is dangerous. He leads you back to his Lab.

Around town, a girl tells you she's raising Pokémon too, and a technology fan explains that Pokémon can be stored on a PC as data. The sign in town has a practice sum on it: two Pidgey plus two Pidgey make four.

Professor Oak's Lab

Your rival is already waiting and complains that Oak took his time. Three Poké Balls sit on the table, each holding a Pokémon. Walk up to one to look at it, then pick your first partner. Oak won't let you leave until you've chosen.

Bulbasaur

Grass/Poison. Strong against Brock's and Misty's Gyms. The easiest start.

Charmander

Fire. Struggles against the first two Gyms, so it's the hardest start.

Squirtle

Water. Strong against Brock and the most even all through.

Your rival then takes the Pokémon that has the type advantage over yours. The third ball stays on the table: it's Professor Oak's last Pokémon, and it stays in the Lab.

As you turn to leave, he challenges you. Each turn, pick a move and answer its math question. Get it right and the move lands.

When the battle ends, your rival says he'll train his Pokémon out in the wild and leaves. Professor Oak then gives you five Potions for the walk to Viridian City.

Later visit. Professor Oak is waiting for a parcel from the Viridian City Poké Mart. When you bring it, he gives you a Pokédex and five Poké Balls, and your rival gets a Pokédex too. Only then can you catch wild Pokémon. Much later, show Oak all 251 Kanto and Johto Pokémon for the Shiny Charm.

Your rival's house

Your rival's older sister Daisy is home. She tells you her little brother is off training again, and that a short rest makes all the difference when your Pokémon look tired. The shelves are full of Pokémon books, and there's a picture of a Clefairy on the wall.

Route 1

Route 1 is a short country road north from Pallet Town to Viridian City. Patches of tall grass line the way, and ledges let you hop south quickly but not climb back up. You can't catch anything yet, since you have no Pokédex or Poké Balls. Battling the wild Pokémon here is still a good way to level your starter before Viridian.

A boy near the ledges points out that hopping down them is the quickest way back to Pallet Town. The sign reads "Wild Pokémon hide in the tall grass. Answer fast to catch them!"

Free sample

A clerk from the Viridian City Poké Mart stands on the south part of the route. Talk to the clerk for a free Potion. After that the clerk just invites you to shop in Viridian. Keep going north to reach Viridian City.
