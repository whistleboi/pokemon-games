Cerulean City

Cerulean City is a busy town on the far side of Mt. Moon. Nugget Bridge (Route 24) leads north, Route 5 leads south toward Saffron City, and Route 9 leads east. Misty's Gym is here, but she's out when you arrive: she's visiting a friend on Route 25, so start with the trip north to Bill's Sea Cottage.

{{journal:m4}} {{tables:CERULEAN_CITY}}

A Rare Candy is hidden somewhere in town. The Machine Part only turns up much later, once the Power Plant's manager has asked you to look for it ours.

Around Cerulean

A little boy is off to see Misty, a gentleman thinks a Pokémon encyclopedia sounds like great fun, and a girl dreams of a bright red bicycle. A trainer's Slowbro is fast asleep and won't listen to a word. A guard stands outside Cerulean Cave, where terribly strong Pokémon live: it stays closed until you're Champion and have finished a job on the Sevii Islands.

The robbed house

Thieves have broken into a house in the northeast of town. Until you've been to Bill's, a policeman stands at the door and won't let anyone in. Inside, the owner says Team Rocket dug right through his wall and stole his TM for Dig.

The Rocket Grunt who did it hangs around in the yard behind the house. Go near him and he comes at you, pretending he's never heard of any TM.

{{battle:CERULEAN_CITY:Team Rocket Grunt}}

Beat him and he hands back TM055 Dig and runs off.

Houses

In one house, an old man wants a Poliwhirl and will trade his Jynx, ZYNX, for it. In another, a man grinds Berries into powder. A badge fan explains that badges help your Pokémon obey you, and an old man has heard the Bike Shop's prices are sky-high, but that there's a voucher in Vermilion.

Pokémon Center and Poké Mart

People in the Center are talking about Bill, who they say will do anything for a rare Pokémon. In the Mart, a girl explains that Rare Candy isn't sold anywhere, and a shopper tells you to put your strongest Pokémon first so Repel works best.

Bike Shop

The Bike Shop is full of shiny new Bicycles, but the shoppers agree the prices are far too high. Come back later with a Bike Voucher and you can claim one.

Nugget Bridge (Route 24)

Route 24 starts with Nugget Bridge, just north of Cerulean City. A row of trainers waits on the bridge to test you, then the route bends east into Route 25.

{{journal:k_bill}}

Rival battle 3

As you step onto the bridge from Cerulean, your rival walks up. He claims he's already beaten Brock and cleared the bridge, and wants to see how your team has grown. He's only here while you have fewer than three badges.

Route 25

Route 25 runs east to Bill's Sea Cottage at the very end of the cape. A couple out for a walk say the cape is a famous date spot.

{{tables:KANTO_ROUTE_25}} {{trainers:KANTO_ROUTE_25}}

Bill's Sea Cottage

Bill made the PC Storage System. He's grateful you came all this way and gives you an S.S. Ticket: the S.S. Anne is docked in Vermilion City. Once you've visited Bill, Misty is back at her Gym.

Nugget Bridge challenge

Bill has heard about the challenge on Nugget Bridge. Beat every trainer on Route 24, Rocket Grunt included, and talk to him: he gives you an Eevee ours.

Cerulean Gym

Back in Cerulean City, Misty is home. The Gym Guide says her Water Pokémon are tough, and Grass and Electric moves will help a lot.

{{trainers:CERULEAN_CITY:Cerulean Gym}}

Like Brock, Misty wants to play a game before you battle ours. Win it to face her.

{{battle:CERULEAN_CITY:Misty}}

Beat her for the Cascade Badge. She also gives you TM011 Water Pulse and a Rare Candy.

Route 5

Route 5 runs south from Cerulean City toward Saffron City. There are no trainers here, just tall grass, the Pokémon Day Care and the entrance to the Underground Path.

{{tables:KANTO_ROUTE_5}}

Headbutt trees need the Rocky Helmet.

Pokémon Day Care

The Pokémon Day Care sits on the route, where you can leave a Pokémon in their care.

Saffron Gate

The guard in the gatehouse to Saffron City is terribly thirsty and won't let anyone through. He'll need a drink before you can pass, so for now take the Underground Path instead.

Underground Path

The Underground Path is a long tunnel under Saffron City. From the entrance on Route 5, the north–south tunnel comes out on Route 6, on the way to Vermilion City. A second, east–west tunnel joins Route 7 and Route 8.

In the entrance building, a girl will trade her Nidoran♀, MS. NIDO, for a Nidoran♂. It comes holding a Tiny Mushroom.

{{tables:UNDERGROUND_PATH}}

Every item down here is hidden. The first seven are in the north–south tunnel; the other seven are in the east–west tunnel, for later.

Fossil Maniac

A Fossil Maniac waits in the north–south tunnel ours. He brings ancient Pokémon back to life, so if you picked up a fossil in Mt. Moon, he can revive it for you right away.

At the far end, a girl in the exit building says people lose things in the dark down here all the time. Head out onto Route 6.
