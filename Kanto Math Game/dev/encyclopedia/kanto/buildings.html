Your own house, on the west side of Pallet Town. The game starts upstairs in your bedroom, and Mom is downstairs on the first floor. See Part 1.

Your rival's house, on the east side of town. His older sister Daisy is home, with tips about resting tired Pokémon.

Professor Oak's Pokémon Research Lab, at the south of Pallet Town. This is where your journey starts: you pick Bulbasaur, Charmander or Squirtle, and your rival takes the one strong against yours and battles you on the spot (Part 1).

Come back with Oak's Parcel from the Viridian City Poké Mart and he gives you the Pokédex and five Poké Balls. After you beat Lt. Surge he has a rowdy Pikachu waiting for you here. Once you're Champion, he upgrades your Pokédex to the National Pokédex if you've seen 60 kinds of Pokémon (Part 18), and much later he gives the Shiny Charm ours to anyone who has caught all 251 Pokémon from #1 to #251.

Home of the old man who naps across the road north of Viridian City. Once you've woken him and taken his Rocky Helmet ours, he's back here with his little girl and their Spearow, Speary. A nickname fan lives here too.

Giovanni's Gym, the eighth and last in Kanto. Its doors stay locked until you hold the other seven badges, and Jessie and James hang around outside it for a while once you have the Pokédex (Part 2).

Inside, the floor is covered in arrow tiles that slide you along until something stops you. Black Belts, Tamers and Cooltrainers with Ground and Fighting Pokémon line the way, and the Leader turns out to be Giovanni, the boss of Team Rocket. As at every Gym in this game, a game set by the Gym comes before the Leader ours.

Beat him for the Earth Badge, TM149 Earthquake, a Rare Candy, ten Poké Balls, and a map to Cerulean Cave ours. A Macho Brace is hidden somewhere on the floor. See Part 15 and Gyms.

The Trainer school. The teacher gives five short lessons, one at a time: types, real-time battles, catching, Mega Evolution and looking after your team. A good first stop if you're new to the game.

Viridian's Poké Mart. The first time you walk in, the clerk asks you to carry Oak's Parcel to Pallet Town, and won't sell you anything until you've delivered it. After that it's an ordinary Kanto Poké Mart. See Part 2.

The gate at the west end of Route 22. Its guard only lets trainers with all eight Kanto badges through to Route 23 and Victory Road (Part 16).

The gatehouse at the south edge of Viridian Forest. A lady here warns that Pewter's Gym Leader uses Rock Pokémon.

The gatehouse at the north end of Viridian Forest, the way out to Pewter City. A guide here calls the forest a natural maze.

A small house on the east side of Route 2, reached from Diglett's Cave or with Cut. A boy here trades his Mr. Mime, MIMIEN, for an Abra. See In-game trades and Part 7.

The gatehouse on the east side of Route 2, where one of Professor Oak's aides waits. Show him you've caught 10 kinds of Pokémon and he gives you HM Flash, which lights up dark caves like Rock Tunnel (Part 7).

The Pewter Museum of Science. A child's ticket costs 50. The first floor shows the fossils of Aerodactyl and Kabutops, and a wrestling fan teaches Seismic Toss (like every tutor in this game, he can sell you the move's TM too ours; see TMs and tutors).

Come in by the back door and a scientist hands you the Old Amber, which the Pokémon Lab on Cinnabar Island can bring back as Aerodactyl.

2F

This month's exhibit is about space, with a Space Shuttle model and a meteorite from Mt. Moon. See Part 3.

Brock's Gym, the first on your journey. Brock trains Rock Pokémon, so Water and Grass moves are your best bet; the Gym Guide by the door says so too. Camper Liam stands in the way.

Before the battle Brock sets you a game ours, picked for his Gym the first time you challenge him and kept the same after that. Win it to face him. Beat him for the Boulder Badge, TM036 Rock Tomb, a Town Map and a Rare Candy. See Part 3 and Gyms.

A family whose traded Nidoran won't listen. The dad explains that a traded Pokémon may ignore a trainer without enough badges.

An old man here says some moves can only be taught by people, and a boy explains that a sleeping Pokémon is easier to catch.

The Pokémon Center at the Mt. Moon end of Route 3, the last place to heal before the cave. A salesman here sells you a Magikarp at Lv 5 for 500, no refunds. See Part 4.

A badge fan explains that badges help your Pokémon obey you.

The house Team Rocket robbed. A policeman keeps everyone out until you've been to Bill's. Inside, the owner says a Rocket dug through his wall and took his TM for Dig. The Grunt hangs around in the yard behind the house: beat him and he hands back TM055 Dig (Part 5).

An old man here trades his Jynx, ZYNX, for a Poliwhirl. See In-game trades.

An old man has heard the Bike Shop's prices are sky-high, but that there's a voucher in Vermilion City.

A man here grinds Berries into powder.

Misty's Gym. She's out when you first reach Cerulean, visiting Bill's Sea Cottage on Route 25, and is back once you've been there. Her Water Pokémon are tough; Grass and Electric moves help a lot.

Like Brock, Misty sets you a game before the battle ours. Beat her for the Cascade Badge, TM011 Water Pulse and a Rare Candy (Part 5).

The Gym matters again much later: once the Power Plant's manager asks about his stolen Machine Part, search the Gym's pool to find it (Part 15).

The Bike Shop, full of shiny new Bicycles at prices nobody can afford. Bring the Bike Voucher from the Pokémon Fan Club in Vermilion City and you can swap it for a Bicycle. You need one to ride Cycling Road. See Part 5 and Part 6.

The Pokémon Day Care on Route 5, just south of Cerulean City, where you can leave a Pokémon in their care. See Day Care.

The gate from Route 5 into Saffron City. The guard is terribly thirsty and won't let anyone through until you bring him something to drink: the Tea from the old woman in the Celadon Mansion. Until then, take the Underground Path (Part 5, Part 11).

The north–south tunnel under Saffron City, from Route 5 to Route 6. A Fossil Maniac waits down here ours: bring him a fossil and he revives it on the spot, so the Dome or Helix Fossil from Mt. Moon needn't wait for Cinnabar. Seven items lie hidden along the tunnel. See Part 5.

The Underground Path's exit building on Route 6. A girl says people lose things in the dark tunnel all the time.

The east–west tunnel, joining Route 7 and Route 8 under Saffron City. It's the way to Celadon City while the Saffron gates are shut. Seven more items are hidden along it (Part 8).

The Underground Path's entrance building on Route 8.

The Fishing Guru's house in the north of Vermilion City. He gives you the Old Rod, or a Rare Candy if you already have a rod. His brothers live in Fuchsia City (Good Rod) and on Route 12 (Super Rod). See Part 6.

The Pokémon Fan Club, where every member thinks their own Pokémon is the cutest. Sit through the Chairman's whole story and he gives you a Bike Voucher for the Bike Shop in Cerulean City (if you don't have a Bicycle yet). After the Power Plant is working again, the club also gives you the Lost Item, the Copycat's missing Clefairy doll. See Part 6.

The house next to the Fan Club. A girl here trades her Farfetch'd for a Spearow. See In-game trades.

A girl and a boy send letters to faraway friends by Pidgey.

Lt. Surge's Gym, behind a small tree you need Cut for. Surge has locked his door with two electric locks, and the switches are hidden in the rows of trash cans. Find the first switch, then the second is always in a can right next to it. A wrong can resets both locks and moves the switches. The Gym Guide and the Engineer both drop hints.

With both locks open, the beam across the door goes off. Surge's Electric Pokémon hit hard and fast, so bring a Ground type. Win the Thunder Badge, TM298 Shock Wave and a Rare Candy. See Part 6.

Vermilion Harbor, at the south end of town. The sailor at the door lets you board the S.S. Anne if you carry the S.S. Ticket from Bill. Once you leave the ship with HM Cut, it sails away for good (Part 6).

After you become Champion, ships and a blimp to other places leave from here, including the Rocket Blimp to the Orange Islands.

The S.S. Anne's kitchen, off the 1F corridor. The chefs are far too busy to talk, but a Great Ball lies on the floor and three Berries are hidden in the bins.

The first cabin on the S.S. Anne's 1F corridor. A secret agent here whispers that she's on Team Rocket's trail. See Part 6.

A mother on a world cruise with her children, Lass Ann and Youngster Tyler, who both want to battle. A TM for Brick Break lies on the floor.

2F

The Pokémon Center right by the entrance to Rock Tunnel. Heal up before the dark tunnel. One of Professor Oak's aides here gives you an Everstone once you've caught 20 kinds of Pokémon. See Part 7.

After you rescue him from the top of the Pokémon Tower, he's back here and gives you the Poké Flute, which wakes the Snorlax sleeping on Routes 12 and 16. See Part 8 and Part 9.

A woman here tells you how Team Rocket took a Cubone's mother, who never came back.

The Name Rater's house. He rates any of your Pokémon's nicknames and helps you change them.

The Rocket Hideout's lift, under the Celadon Game Corner. It won't move without the Lift Key from a Grunt on B4F; then it takes you down to the far side of B4F, where Giovanni waits (Part 9).

The lift in Silph Co.'s tower, stopping at all eleven floors.

The tall Celadon Mansion in the north of the city. On 1F an old woman offers you a cup of Tea: take it, because it's what gets you past the thirsty guards at Saffron City's gates. On 3F the game's makers (designer, programmer, artist and writer) are hard at work in the Development Room. See Part 9.

A small room at the very top of the Celadon Mansion, reached by the back stairs. A Poké Ball on the table holds an Eevee (Lv 25) for you to look after, if you have room in your team. A man here is happy to talk about TMs.

The Prize Corner, next to the Game Corner, swaps your Game Corner coins for prizes: Abra (180 coins), Clefairy (500), Dratini (2800), Scyther (5500) and Porygon (6500), or a Rare Candy (500) and a Nugget (1000). See Game Corners.

Erika's Gym, in the south-west corner of Celadon City behind a small tree that needs Cut. The Celadon Hotel, a hotel for people, not Pokémon, and fully booked with holidaymakers.

The Fishing House on the Route 12 pier, home of the Fishing Guru's younger brother. He gives you the Super Rod, the best rod there is. See Part 10.
