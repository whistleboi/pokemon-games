Route 3

Route 3 runs east from Pewter City to the foot of Mt. Moon. It's a rocky road packed with trainers, so keep your team healthy. There's a Pokémon Center near the Mt. Moon end of the route.

{{journal:m3}} {{tables:KANTO_ROUTE_3}}

Headbutt trees need the Rocky Helmet.

{{trainers:KANTO_ROUTE_3}}

A youngster has tripped over a Geodude that looked just like a rock, and a boy suggests stocking up on Potions before the cave. A tired hiker has just come through the tunnel from Cerulean.

Pokémon Center

A man in the Center is selling what he swears is a real deal: a Magikarp for 500. Buy it if you have room in your team (it comes at Lv 5, and there are no refunds). Others remind you that you can only carry six Pokémon, and that any Center's PC can store the rest. One man says Team Rocket has been in the news, bothering the people of Cerulean.

{{journal:s_magikarp}}

Mt. Moon

Mt. Moon is a long cave with three floors: 1F, B1F and B2F. It's the only way from Route 3 to Route 4. Moon Stones are said to have fallen here, and a few wild Clefairy live in the cave.

{{tables:MT_MOON}}

1F

Seven trainers wait on the first floor.

{{trainers:MT_MOON:1F}}

B1F

No trainers here, but mushrooms are hidden all over this floor.

B2F

Team Rocket is down here: four Rocket Grunts, and Jessie and James too. TM018 Thief is lying where the Rockets left it.

{{trainers:MT_MOON:B2F}} {{battle:MT_MOON:Jessie and James}}

The fossils

Also on B2F, Super Nerd Miguel has found two fossils and wants them both for himself.

{{battle:MT_MOON:Super Nerd Miguel}}

Beat him and he agrees to share: pick the Dome Fossil or the Helix Fossil, and he keeps the other. Either one can be brought back to life at the lab on Cinnabar Island.

{{journal:s_fossil}}

Route 4

Route 4 starts at Mt. Moon's exit and leads east to Cerulean City.

{{tables:KANTO_ROUTE_4}}

The Surfing and fishing tables are for later.

{{trainers:KANTO_ROUTE_4}}

Lass Crissy's Pokémon are far stronger than anything else around here.

The punching fan and the kicking fan

Two men stand side by side and can't stand each other. One swears by fists and offers to teach Mega Punch; the other swears by legs and offers to teach Mega Kick. Each one teaches one of your Pokémon that can learn his move.
