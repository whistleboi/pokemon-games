Route 19

With six badges and HM Surf, it's time to head for Cinnabar Island. You can Surf south from Pallet Town down Route 21, or go the long way from Fuchsia City. This part takes the long way. Route 19 leads south from Fuchsia City, and from there on it's open sea, so you need a Pokémon that knows Surf (and Koga's Soul Badge) to go any further.

Tentacool are the only Pokémon you meet here unless you fish. Fishing pulls up Krabby and Horsea, and Gyarados bites on the Super Rod.

{{trainers:KANTO_ROUTE_19}}

Summer Beach House

A surfer dude runs the Summer Beach House on the beach. He rides the waves with his Pikachu, and he thinks surfing Pikachu are the coolest thing around. Talk to him for his Pikachu's Beach surfing game ours (from Pokémon Yellow).

Route 20

Route 20 crosses the sea to Cinnabar Island. Swimmers are everywhere, along with a Bird Keeper and two Picnickers. A swimmer tells you that Misty of the Cerulean Gym trains in these waters. On the way you pass the entrance to the Seafoam Islands.

{{tables:KANTO_ROUTE_20}} {{trainers:KANTO_ROUTE_20}}

Seafoam Islands

The Seafoam Islands hide a deep, icy cave. The cave goes down five floors, from 1F to B4F, and the lowest floors are full of fast-flowing water. The legendary bird Articuno lives on the bottom floor. You go in from Route 20 and come back out the same way, so the trip is your choice: you don't have to pass through to reach Cinnabar.

{{journal:s_articuno}} {{tables:SEAFOAM_ISLANDS}}

Each floor has its own cave Pokémon. Seel are everywhere below 1F, and the deepest floors have Dewgong. Alolan Vulpix and Alolan Ninetales turn up rarely on B3F.

The currents

B3F and B4F have fast currents that sweep you along. A sign on B4F warns of the fast current, and another gives a hint: boulders might change the flow of the water. You need Strength (with the Rainbow Badge) and Surf here.

  • Push the two boulders on 1F down their holes. They land in B3F's water and stop its current.
  • Then push the two boulders on B3F down too. They land on B4F and calm the current there.

Each boulder push is a slide puzzle: solve it to move the boulder.

Articuno

Articuno waits on B4F, the bottom of the cave. Walk up to it to start the battle.

Articuno, Lv 50. The Ice and Flying legendary bird, Seafoam's area boss. If it faints before you catch it, it's back the next day, so you can try again until it's yours.

When you're done, climb back up to 1F and Surf on along Route 20 to Cinnabar Island.
