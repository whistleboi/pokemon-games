Celadon City

Celadon City is the biggest city you've reached so far, full of trees and flowers. It has a huge Department Store, the tall Celadon Mansion, a hotel, a restaurant and the noisy Rocket Game Corner. Route 7 is back to the east, and Route 16 leads west. The Gym is in the south-west corner, behind a small tree, so you'll need Cut to reach it.

Something isn't right here, though. Team Rocket Grunts hang around town, and a worried man tells you someone stole the Silph Scope and ran into the Game Corner.

The PP Up is hidden: step on its spot to find it. The pond in town is only for fishing until you have Surf.

Pokémon Center

Heal up here. A gentleman explains that a Poké Flute's tune wakes sleeping Pokémon, and a Biker has ridden all the way up Cycling Road from Fuchsia City.

Celadon Department Store

The Department Store has five floors and a rooftop, and a lift that runs between them. The board on 1F shows what's on each floor.

2F: Trainer's Market

The shop counter is here. It sells the same goods as every other Poké Mart in Kanto, so what you can buy grows with your badges.

3F: TV Game Shop

Kids are swapping Pokémon and talking about trades. The man behind the counter loves the move Counter, and he teaches it to one of your Pokémon that can learn it.

4F: Wise Man Gifts

The clerk sells Poké Dolls for $1000 each. A youngster says a Poké Doll can distract a wild Pokémon long enough for you to run away.

5F and the roof

Shoppers on 5F talk about stat boosters like HP Up and Protein. Up on the roof, the vending machines are out of order, and a little girl is very thirsty.

Celadon Mansion

The Mansion is the tall building in the north of the city.

1F

An old woman lives here with her pet Pokémon. She offers you a cup of hot Tea, and wonders if the thirsty guards around Saffron City would like some too. Take it now: it's what gets you past those guards.

{{journal:k_tea}}

The Journal only brings this up once you hold five badges, but the old woman gives you the Tea on your first visit.

3F

This floor is the game makers' Development Room. The game designer, programmer, artist and writer are all hard at work, and each tells you a little about their job.

The rooftop room

Climb all the way to the top by the back stairs. In a small room up there, a Poké Ball sits on the table with a note asking you to look after the Eevee inside (Lv 25). You need room in your team to take it. A man here says he knows everything, and is happy to talk about TMs.

{{journal:s_eevee}}

Restaurant

A man at one table has lost all his money at the slots and is giving up gambling for good. He hands you his Coin Case, which you need to play at the Game Corner. Another diner whispers that there's a basement under the Game Corner.

{{journal:s_coincase}}

Other buildings

In the house next to the restaurant, a Team Rocket Grunt boasts about shipping Pokémon off as slot prizes, and another insists there's no secret switch behind the Game Corner's poster. The Celadon Hotel is full of holidaymakers. The Prize Corner swaps your Game Corner coins for prizes.

Later, with Surf. A man waits on the far side of the pond. Surf across and he teaches Soft-Boiled to one of your Pokémon that can learn it, like Chansey.

Celadon Gym

Cut the small tree to reach the Gym. Erika and her students all use Grass Pokémon, many of them part Poison. Fire, Ice, Flying and Psychic moves work well here. The Growlithe and Vulpix from Route 8 are a great help.

{{trainers:CELADON_CITY:Celadon Gym}} {{battle:CELADON_CITY:Erika}}

Win and you get the Rainbow Badge. It lets you use Strength outside battle once you have it.

Celadon Game Corner

The Game Corner is full of slot machines and players. Without a Coin Case the clerk won't let you play, but you can still look around. A man quietly says the place is rumoured to be run by Team Rocket.

{{journal:k_hideout1}}

A Grunt stands guard in front of a poster at the back of the room. Beat him, then check the poster. There's a switch behind it, and pressing it opens a hidden staircase down to Team Rocket's hideout.

{{trainers:CELADON_GAME_CORNER}}

Rocket Hideout

Team Rocket's base has four floors below the Game Corner: B1F to B4F. Grunts guard every floor. On B2F and B3F, arrow tiles on the floor whisk you along in the direction they point, so look where each one goes before you step on it.

{{journal:k_hideout2}} {{tables:ROCKET_HIDEOUT}}

B1F

Five Grunts wait on the first floor. The lift is on this floor too, but it won't move without a key.

{{trainers:ROCKET_HIDEOUT:B1F}}

B2F

The arrow maze starts here. One Grunt guards this floor, and four items lie around it, including a Moon Stone and a TM for Taunt.

{{trainers:ROCKET_HIDEOUT:B2F}}

B3F

More arrow tiles, with two Grunts. A Rare Candy, a TM for Frustration and a pair of Black Glasses are on this floor, and a Nugget is hidden here.

{{trainers:ROCKET_HIDEOUT:B3F}}

B4F

The stairs from B3F bring you to one side of B4F. A Grunt here has the Lift Key: beat him and pick it up. Then go back up and ride the lift from B1F or B2F down to B4F's far side.

Jessie and James ours are on B4F too, and they won't let you disturb their Boss.

{{battle:ROCKET_HIDEOUT:Jessie}}

Two Grunts stand guard by a shut door. Beat both of them and the door opens.

{{trainers:ROCKET_HIDEOUT:B4F}}

Giovanni

Behind the door is Giovanni, the boss of Team Rocket. He uses Ground and Rock Pokémon along with a Kangaskhan, so Water and Grass moves work well.

{{battle:ROCKET_HIDEOUT:Giovanni}}

Giovanni leaves the hideout, but he warns you Team Rocket isn't finished. He drops the Silph Scope. In all the commotion you also rescue a Porygon ours. B4F also has a TM for Snatch, a Max Ether and a Calcium, and a Nest Ball and a Net Ball are hidden there.

With the Silph Scope in your Bag, go back to Lavender Town and the Pokémon Tower.

Pokémon Tower

The Pokémon Tower is a place to remember Pokémon that have passed on. It has seven floors, joined by stairs. Wild Pokémon only appear from 3F up.

{{tables:KANTO_POKEMON_TOWER}}

Gastly and Haunter are Ghost types: Normal and Fighting moves can't touch them.

Rival battle

Your rival is here, paying his respects. As you walk in, he challenges you to show how healthy his Pokémon are. (If you looked inside on your first visit to Lavender, you've already battled him.)

{{battle:KANTO_POKEMON_TOWER:rival}}

Afterwards he reminds you that the ghost upstairs can't be seen without a Silph Scope.

1F and 2F

Mourners have come to pray for their Pokémon, and a Channeler senses spirits high up in the Tower. On 2F, another Channeler says a Silph Scope would show the ghosts for what they really are.

3F to 5F

Channelers guard each of these floors: three on 3F, three on 4F and four on 5F. Their Pokémon are mostly Gastly. You can find an Escape Rope on 3F, and an Awakening, an Elixir and a Great Ball on 4F.

{{trainers:KANTO_POKEMON_TOWER:3F}} {{trainers:KANTO_POKEMON_TOWER:4F}}

On 5F, a Channeler has made a safe, purified space. Talk to her and your whole team is healed. A Cleanse Tag and a Nugget are on this floor, and a Big Mushroom is hidden here.

{{trainers:KANTO_POKEMON_TOWER:5F}}

6F: The ghost

Three more Channelers wait on 6F, along with a Rare Candy and an X Accuracy.

{{trainers:KANTO_POKEMON_TOWER:6F}}

A ghost blocks the stairs at the top. Without the Silph Scope you can't make out what it is, and it tells you to go away. With the Silph Scope, you can see it for what it really is, and battle it.

7F: Mr. Fuji

Team Rocket is holding Mr. Fuji at the top of the Tower. Jessie and James ours are here again, along with two Grunts.

Get past them and talk to Mr. Fuji. He thanks you for coming all this way and asks you to visit him at his house in Lavender Town. A Soothe Bell is hidden on this floor.

Lavender Town (again)

Mr. Fuji's house

Mr. Fuji is back home at the Volunteer Pokémon House. He tells you the ghost was the spirit of a Marowak. Team Rocket took her life while she was protecting her child, a Cubone, and now she can finally rest. To thank you, he gives you the Poké Flute. Its soft tune wakes even the deepest-sleeping Pokémon, like the Snorlax lying across Route 12.
