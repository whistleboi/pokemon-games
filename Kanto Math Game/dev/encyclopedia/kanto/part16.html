Route 22 (again)

Route 22 runs west from Viridian City to the Pokémon League Front Gate. The first time you came here your rival was just starting out. Now, with all eight badges, you find him waiting on the route again, and he wants one last warm-up before the League.

Rival battle

{{battle:KANTO_ROUTE_22:rival@2}}

When you win, your rival says he'll see you at the Indigo Plateau and tells you not to keep him waiting. Then go into the Front Gate.

Pokémon League Front Gate

The guard explains that only trainers with all eight Kanto badges may go on to the Pokémon League. Walk through the gate and out the north side onto Route 23.

Pokémon League Gate

This is a different gate building, where Route 22 meets Route 26 and Route 28, as in HeartGold and SoulSilver ours. You don't need it for the Kanto story. Its doors open as you go:

  • Victory Road, toward the Indigo Plateau: with all eight Kanto badges.
  • Route 26, toward Johto: once you've beaten the Elite Four (or with all eight Johto badges).
  • Route 28: once you've become Johto's Champion and hold all eight Johto badges.

Route 23

Route 23 is the long road north to Victory Road and the Indigo Plateau, with water to Surf along the way. Guards stand along the road, one for each badge. Each one stops you unless you hold his badge: from south to north they check the Cascade, Thunder, Rainbow, Soul, Marsh, Volcano and Earth Badges. The Boulder Badge guard just looks at your badge and waves you on.

{{tables:KANTO_ROUTE_23}}

The Headbutt trees here use Kanto's mountain and route tree tables. Shaking them needs the Rocky Helmet. FireRed has no Headbutt trees. Many items are hidden along the route, so look around.

Past the guards is the entrance to Victory Road. The cave brings you back out onto Route 23, and the Indigo Plateau stands at the route's north end.

Victory Road

Victory Road is the last cave before the League, three floors of rocky tunnels full of strong trainers. On every floor, rock barriers block the way. Push a boulder onto a floor switch with Strength and the barrier lowers. You can only go on to the Indigo Plateau with all eight Kanto badges.
