One Island

Once you hold the Volcano Badge, Blaine tells you that Bill is waiting at the harbour on Cinnabar Island. Bill wants to take you to One Island, the first of the Sevii Islands, so step into the harbour and ride the Seagallop Ferry with him. The same ferry can take you back to Vermilion City whenever you like.

One Island is a small, sunny island. Kindle Road leads from it to Mt. Ember, a huge volcano, and with Surf you can reach Treasure Beach to the south. The island's Pokémon Center is the Pokémon Network Center, run by Bill's friend Celio.

{{tables:ONE_ISLAND}}

One Island has no tall grass. Its water is for Surfing and fishing only.

Bill shows you around

As you step off the ferry, Bill tells you where you are and leads you straight to the Pokémon Network Center to meet Celio.

Pokémon Network Center

Celio is glad to see Bill. He explains that he looks after the PC network for these islands, and that Bill is staying on to help him for a while. Then he gives you the Tri-Pass. With it, the Seagallop Ferry carries you between One, Two and Three Island.

{{journal:sevii_m1}}

Talk to Celio again and he tells you about his Network Machine. It will link Kanto's PCs with faraway regions, but it needs two special gems to run: a Ruby and a Sapphire. People say Team Rocket has been sneaking around the islands looking for gems, and Celio has heard they were seen on Mt. Ember.

The Center also has a counter to heal your team and a shop. A man here talks about the enormous volcano, and a woman wishes she could trade with her boyfriend back in Kanto. Upstairs, the link and trade counters are all closed for now.

Houses

In the east house, an old couple love to gaze at the volcano, though neither can remember when it last erupted. In the west house, an old woman says young people all leave for the big cities, but she loves her island.

Treasure Beach

Treasure Beach is a sandy strip south of One Island. You need Surf to reach it. It isn't on the way to anything, but the sand is full of things washed up by the sea.

{{tables:TREASURE_BEACH}}

Dig by the driftwood for two Rare Candies. Lots more is hidden in the sand, so walk the whole beach.

{{trainers:TREASURE_BEACH}}

Kindle Road

Kindle Road runs north along the coast from One Island to the foot of Mt. Ember. Fighters train on the shore and swimmers in the water all along the way.

{{tables:KINDLE_ROAD}} {{trainers:KINDLE_ROAD}}

Ember Spa

Along the road, a hot spring bubbles inside the mountain. The old man here found the water when he smashed a rock, and he gives you HM Rock Smash so cracked rocks won't stop you any more. The other bathers enjoy the warm water, and a soak is said to be good for your Pokémon too.

The sign at the end of the road says to go straight on for Mt. Ember.

Mt. Ember

Mt. Ember is the great volcano at the top of Kindle Road. Its slopes are open ground, with paths leading inside the mountain: the Summit Path climbs to the top, and the Ruby Path goes deep underground.

{{tables:MT_EMBER}} {{trainers:MT_EMBER}}

Team Rocket's dig

Researchers stand at the foot of the mountain. They tell you two men in black chased them away while they were studying the volcano's heat. The men were digging near the top and talking about a gem called the Ruby. Talk to the researchers again and one of them offers to teach a Pokémon Explosion.

{{journal:sevii_m2}}

Two Team Rocket Grunts are digging by a cave door. As you come near, you overhear one of them: they've broken through, anything they find goes to their warehouse, and the warehouse door opens with two passwords. The first one is "Goldeen need log". Remember it for later! Then they notice you listening.

{{battle:MT_EMBER:Grunt@1}} {{battle:MT_EMBER:Grunt@2}}

With both Grunts beaten, Jessie and James ours jump out to defend their dig.

{{battle:MT_EMBER:Jessie and James}}

Ruby Path

The door the Grunts were guarding leads into the Ruby Path, which winds down through the mountain floor by floor. Go down to B3F, then take the stairs up into a separate part of B2F. From there a passage leads to the Ruby's own room, where a red gem glitters in the dark: the Ruby.

{{journal:sevii_m2b}}

The summit

The Summit Path goes inside the mountain and up three floors, then out onto the slopes again. Keep climbing to reach the summit, where the legendary bird of fire has its nest.

Moltres. Moltres waits at the summit, Lv 50.

One Island (again)

Take the Ruby back to Celio. He is thrilled: now his Network Machine only lacks a Sapphire, and he'll keep looking for one.

Two Island

Two Island is a quiet island with a beach, a shop stall run by a man from Kanto, and the Joyful Game Corner. Cape Brink is to the north. Signs warn that the current off the island is too fast for fishing or swimming.

{{tables:TWO_ISLAND}}

Joyful Game Corner

The first time you walk in, a Biker is complaining about the place. He says there's nothing fun on this island and his gang is over on Three Island, then rides off. Inside you can play the Joyful Games and the Game Corner's games.

The owner is very worried. His daughter Lostelle went to pick Berries on Three Island and never came home, and bikers have taken over the bridge there. He asks you to find her. She went to the Berry Forest, past Bond Bridge on Three Island.

{{journal:sevii_m3}}

Around town

The Move Maniac lives in the house here. He says he knows every move a Pokémon can learn. Several people mention an old woman up on Cape Brink who teaches amazing moves. There's a Revive on the beach, and the Pokémon Center has a counter to heal your team.

Cape Brink

Cape Brink is a grassy cape at the north end of Two Island, with water to Surf and one house. It's a dead end, but well worth the walk.

{{tables:CAPE_BRINK}}

The Move Tutor's House

An old woman here teaches the ultimate moves of the starter Pokémon. Show her a fully evolved starter you've raised with love and she'll teach it: Frenzy Plant for Venusaur, Blast Burn for Charizard and Hydro Cannon for Blastoise. She teaches the same moves to the final forms of other regions' starters too ours.

Three Island

Three Island is the busiest of the Sevii Islands, but when you arrive a gang of bikers has taken over the town. They are bothering everyone and blocking the way west to Bond Bridge. Three Isle Port is to the north.

{{tables:THREE_ISLAND}}

The biker gang

The islanders want the bikers gone, so take them on. Three Biker Goons come at you one after another, and then their leader steps in.

{{battle:THREE_ISLAND:Biker Goon@1}} {{battle:THREE_ISLAND:Biker Goon@2}} {{battle:THREE_ISLAND:Biker Goon@3}} {{battle:THREE_ISLAND:Paxton}}

Paxton gives up and his gang rides off. Bond Bridge is open now. One of the islanders thanks you with a Full Restore.

Around town

Lostelle's house is the one in the south, with rare rocks and gems on display. In another house, a girl says she isn't Lostelle, but she guesses Lostelle has gone to the Berry Forest again. Other people warn you never to go into the forest alone, and the shoppers in the Poké Mart say most folks here just use Berries from the forest instead of buying medicine. A sailor in the Pokémon Center explains that the currents between the islands are too strong to Surf.

Three Isle Port

Three Isle Port lies just north of Three Island. Its patch of grass is home to only one kind of Pokémon.

{{tables:THREE_ISLE_PORT}}

Bond Bridge

Bond Bridge leads west from Three Island across the water, with patches of tall grass along the way. The sign asks you to be quiet when crossing, but plenty of trainers want a battle. The Berry Forest lies beyond it.

{{tables:BOND_BRIDGE}} {{trainers:BOND_BRIDGE}}

Berry Forest

The Berry Forest is a misty wood full of Berry bushes. The signs ask you to share Berries with others and warn of wild Pokémon that love Berries. Many Berries are hidden around the forest, so search everywhere.

{{tables:BERRY_FOREST}}

Finding Lostelle

Deep in the forest you find Lostelle crying, while a Hypno swings its pendulum at her!

Hypno. Lv 30. You can't catch it. Beat it and it runs off into the mist, and you get 5 Poké Balls. Lostelle asks to go home to Two Island.

Two Island (again)

Lostelle runs to her father at the Joyful Game Corner and tells him how you saved her from the scary Hypno. To thank you, he gives you a Moon Stone. Some Pokémon evolve when they touch it. Lostelle stays at the Game Corner after that, asking you to show her how cool you are again.

That's all for the Sevii Islands for now. Celio still needs a Sapphire, but you'll find it later. Your last Gym badge is waiting back in Kanto, in Viridian City.
