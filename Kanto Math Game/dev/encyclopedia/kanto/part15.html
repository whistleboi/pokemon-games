Route 21

Route 21 is the sea route between Pallet Town in the north and Cinnabar Island in the south. You need Surf to cross it. Most of it is open water full of swimmers and fishermen, with one stretch of tall grass where Tangela grows.

{{tables:KANTO_ROUTE_21}} {{trainers:KANTO_ROUTE_21}}

Surf north to Pallet Town, then walk up Route 1 to Viridian City for your last badge.

Power Plant

The Power Plant is an old building full of humming machines, reached by Surfing along the channel on Route 10. It isn't on the way to the League, but it's a good side trip before your last badge: Electric Pokémon live here, the floor has plenty of items, and a legendary bird is waiting inside.

The missing Machine Part ours

The manager is in a panic. Someone stole the generator's Machine Part, so Lavender's Radio Tower has gone dark and the Magnet Train can't run. A suspicious man in black was seen near the Cerulean Gym. You can only help once you've been to Lavender Town.

{{journal:k_power}}

Search the Cerulean Gym's pool to find the part, then bring it back. The manager puts it in the generator, the Radio Tower comes back on the air, and he gives you 20 Poké Balls as thanks.

The trader ours

A woman here would like a Dugtrio. Bring one and she'll trade you her Magneton, Maggie, holding a Metal Coat.

Zapdos

Somewhere in the plant, the legendary bird of lightning has made its home.

Zapdos. Zapdos has no fixed level. Before you have all eight badges, it comes in at three levels above the next Gym Leader's best Pokémon. With all eight badges, or after you become Champion, it's Lv 70.

Viridian City Gym

Back in Viridian City, the Gym that was locked for so long is open at last. Its doors open only once you hold all seven of the other Kanto badges. The Gym Leader has returned, and you'll find out who he is when you reach him.

{{journal:m15}}

The Gym floor is covered in arrow tiles. Step on one and it slides you along in the direction it points until something stops you. The Gym Guide tells you to watch where each arrow points and to look for the spots that stop you. Black Belts, Tamers and Cooltrainers stand along the way, using mostly Ground and Fighting Pokémon. Water and Grass moves work well here.

Giovanni

The Gym Leader is Giovanni, the boss of Team Rocket. He tells you this is the last time you'll meddle with Team Rocket.

{{battle:VIRIDIAN_CITY:Giovanni}}

Giovanni admits you're skilled. He tells you a powerful Pokémon is waking in Cerulean Cave, something Team Rocket left behind after the trouble on Cinnabar Island, and gives you a map that leads there ours. Then he hands over the Earth Badge, your eighth and last. Along with it you get TM149 Earthquake, a Rare Candy and 10 Poké Balls.
