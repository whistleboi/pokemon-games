The Rainbow Pass

You're the Champion, but the Sevii Islands aren't finished with you. The Tri-Pass only covers One, Two and Three Island. To sail on to Islands 4 to 7 you need Celio's Rainbow Pass, and Celio only hands it to a Champion who carries the National Pokédex.

The National Pokédex

Go home to Pallet Town and talk to Professor Oak in his Lab. If you've seen at least 60 kinds of Pokémon, he upgrades your Pokédex to the National Pokédex, which can record Pokémon from far beyond Kanto. If you've seen fewer, he tells you how many you have so far, so go and meet a few more first.

{{journal:sevii_m4a}}

Celio's Rainbow Pass

Take the Seagallop Ferry back to One Island and talk to Celio in the Pokémon Network Center. He gives you the Rainbow Pass, and the ferry can now take you to Four, Five, Six and Seven Island. He hopes that somewhere out there you'll find where Team Rocket hid the gems.

{{journal:sevii_m4}}

Celio's machine still needs a Sapphire. A new main mission starts as soon as the Rainbow Pass is in your Bag: it runs across Four, Five and Six Island and ends in the Rocket Warehouse.

{{journal:sevii_m5}}

Four Island

Four Island is a small, warm island with a cold secret: Icefall Cave, just to the north. It's Lorelei's home island, and the Pokémon Day Care is here too. The town sign calls it Floe Island.

{{tables:FOUR_ISLAND}}

Your rival at the Day Care

Walk up through town and your rival stops you. He's surprised to see you in the Sevii Islands and tells you to quit following him: he came to see the Day Care, because he heard you can get Eggs there. He doesn't think you'd know what to do with one, and off he goes.

Pokémon Day Care

Leave two Pokémon with the Day-Care Man's wife inside and you might find an Egg. He waits outside, keeping an eye on the Pokémon they're raising.

Lorelei's House

Lorelei came home to rest after the League. Her house is full of stuffed Pokémon dolls (a girl hiding outside lets slip that she collects them). Lorelei tells you that Team Rocket is in Icefall Cave, catching Lapras to sell, and asks you to stop them.

Come back after you've saved the Lapras and she offers a battle, just for fun ours.

{{battle:FOUR_ISLAND:Lorelei|After Icefall Cave · once}}

Around town

The Body Slam tutor lives in one of the houses: he teaches one of your Pokémon Body Slam, and you can come back for more lessons ours. A boy outside made a slushie from ice scraped off the walls of Icefall Cave, and a shopper in the Poké Mart says a really famous Trainer grew up here.

Icefall Cave

Icefall Cave sits at the north end of Four Island. It's the one cold spot in the warm Sevii Islands, so bring a coat. Talk to Lorelei before you go: until you have, you only hear voices echoing from deep in the ice.

{{tables:ICEFALL_CAVE}}

1F and thin ice

Watch the floor on 1F. Some patches are thin ice: the first step cracks it, and a second step on the cracked spot drops you through to B1F, right below. Several ladders join 1F and B1F too.

An item ball on 1F holds HM07 Waterfall, which lets you climb waterfalls.

The back cave

Deep inside, two Rocket Grunts are netting the Lapras of the island, and Lorelei has come to help drive them out. Jessie and James are leading the job ours.

{{battle:ICEFALL_CAVE:Jessie and James|Can't be skipped}}

Beat them and they blast off once again. The Lapras are safe. Go back to Lorelei's House on Four Island for her thanks and her battle, then take the ferry to Five Island.

Five Island

Five Island is a quiet port town. The sign calls it Chrono Island. The Water Labyrinth lies to the west, and a path leads on to Five Isle Meadow. There's a Pokémon Center but no Poké Mart.

{{tables:FIVE_ISLAND}}

The locals aren't happy. A little girl complains that someone built a big ugly building on the Meadow, and in the house on the east side the Meadow's owner admits he sold a corner of it, then saw what they built. In the Pokémon Center a chef says he's off to cook for a very fancy lady at the resort.

Five Isle Meadow

Five Isle Meadow is a grassy field beside the sea, with Memorial Pillar further on. Team Rocket grunts keep watch here, because the big building on the Meadow is the Rocket Warehouse.

{{tables:FIVE_ISLE_MEADOW}} {{trainers:FIVE_ISLE_MEADOW}}

The warehouse door

The warehouse shutter is locked tight, and the keypad by the door wants two passwords. You heard the first one from Team Rocket on Mt. Ember. The second you'll only get on Six Island, from whoever takes the Sapphire in the Dotted Hole. Once you know both, come back and key them in, and the shutter rolls up.

Memorial Pillar

Memorial Pillar stands at the far end of the Meadow, by the sea. Three Bird Keepers train their flying Pokémon here.

{{tables:MEMORIAL_PILLAR}} {{trainers:MEMORIAL_PILLAR}}

The stone for Tectonix

A man is scrubbing the stone clean. It's in memory of his Onix, Tectonix, who always wanted a cold Lemonade on hot days. Bring a Lemonade and leave it for Tectonix, and he thanks you with TM025 Facade, a move that hits harder when the user is hurt, burned or poisoned.

Water Labyrinth

The Water Labyrinth is a maze of water west of Five Island. Resort Gorgeous lies beyond it, to the north.

{{tables:WATER_LABYRINTH}} {{trainers:WATER_LABYRINTH}}

The old man's Egg

An old man here asks whether you're kind to your Pokémon. If the first Pokémon in your party loves you as much as a Pokémon can, he gives you an Egg he was given on his travels. If not, he asks you to come back when it does. The Egg hatches into a Togepi.

Resort Gorgeous

Resort Gorgeous is a sunny strip of beach with a grand villa. Painters, ladies and swimmers come here to relax. The Lost Cave is at its north end.

{{tables:RESORT_GORGEOUS}} {{trainers:RESORT_GORGEOUS}}

Selphy's Villa

The villa belongs to Lady Selphy, but she isn't home. Her butler tells you she went to look at the Lost Cave and hasn't come back. Go and find her.

Lost Cave

The Lost Cave is dark and full of rooms that look the same. Most of its doors lead nowhere and drop you back in Room 1, so it's easy to get lost. Flash lights it up.

{{tables:LOST_CAVE}}

The way through

From the entrance, go down into Room 1. From there the right doors are: east, north, south, south, east, west, south, east, north. That takes you through Rooms 2 to 9 to Room 10, where Selphy is waiting. Every other door takes you back to Room 1, except four that open into item rooms:

  • Room 2, south door: Lax Incense.
  • Room 4, north door: Sea Incense, and something much worse (see below).
  • Room 6, east door: a Max Revive.
  • Room 8, west door: a Rare Candy, left in the dark.

Two trainers wait on the way, in Rooms 1 and 4.

{{trainers:LOST_CAVE}}

Lady Selphy

Selphy is so glad to see someone. She came to see the cave and can't find the way out, but a lady never turns down a battle.

{{battle:LOST_CAVE:Lady Selphy|Room 10 · can't be skipped ours}}

After the battle she asks you to take her home. Back at Resort Gorgeous she's waiting outside her villa, delighted to be home.

From then on, visit her inside the villa. She's bored and wishes to see a certain kind of Pokémon. Bring one to show her and she gives you a little something for your trouble: a Nugget, a Pearl, a Big Pearl, Stardust, a Star Piece or a Rare Candy.

The Black Fog

The Black Fog ours. In the item room off Room 4, the air turns ice-cold and a black fog pours across the floor. A giant Haunter rises out of it, once worshipped as a god, and it feeds on dreams. It's always as strong as your strongest Pokémon, and it can't be caught. Beat it and you get 10 Poké Balls and 3 Rare Candies. It vanishes in a burst of black mist, with a whisper that it will rise again in another haunted place.

Rocket Warehouse

Come here after the Sapphire is stolen in the Dotted Hole on Six Island. With both passwords, the shutter on Five Isle Meadow rolls up and you can walk in. This is Team Rocket's hideout in the Sevii Islands.

{{tables:ROCKET_WAREHOUSE}} {{trainers:ROCKET_WAREHOUSE}}

The scientist Gideon is the man who grabbed the Sapphire. A PC screen shows a report on how magnetic waves affect Pokémon evolution, far too hard to understand.

The locked doors

Inside, more doors are locked with number pads ours. Each door's code is three numbers added up: work out the sum and pick it. Get all three doors right and the way to the Admin's office opens. A wrong code resets the doors, so try again.

The Admin, Archer

Archer won't battle until you're through the locked doors and past all his men. Then he tells you the plan: with the Ruby and the Sapphire, Team Rocket would force any Pokémon to evolve.

{{battle:ROCKET_WAREHOUSE:Archer|Can't be skipped}}

Beat him and the stolen Sapphire is yours, on the Admin's desk. Team Rocket's Sevii hideout is shut down for good. Now take the Sapphire to Celio on One Island.
