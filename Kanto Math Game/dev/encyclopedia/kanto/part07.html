Route 11

Route 11 runs east from Vermilion City. Near its west end, a sign marks the mouth of Diglett's Cave. At the far east end, a gatehouse leads on to Route 12, but there's no way past what's waiting there yet. For now, this route is for training, a trade and a useful gift.

{{tables:KANTO_ROUTE_11}}

The Headbutt trees need the Rocky Helmet. FireRed has no Headbutt trees. The Escape Rope is hidden: step on its spot to find it.

Ten trainers line the route: Gamblers, Youngsters and two Engineers. The Engineers' Magnemite and Magneton are Electric and Steel, so Fire and Ground moves work best on them.

{{trainers:KANTO_ROUTE_11}}

Route 11 Gate

Downstairs, a man says nicknames are hard to think up and points you to a man in Lavender Town who rates them. The guard has heard that a huge Pokémon sleeps on Route 12's pier.

2F

Another of Professor Oak's aides waits upstairs. Show him you've caught 30 kinds of Pokémon and he gives you the Itemfinder, which beeps when a hidden item is close. A boy here wants a Nidorino and will trade you his Nidorina for one.

Look through the binoculars. One pair shows a huge Pokémon fast asleep across the road far to the east. The other shows that Rock Tunnel is the way to Lavender Town from Cerulean City.

Diglett's Cave

Diglett dug this long tunnel all the way from Route 11 to Route 2, near Pewter City. An old man at the Route 11 end is amazed by it, and a Hiker at the Route 2 end wishes he had a Pokémon that knew Flash for Rock Tunnel.

{{tables:DIGGLETT_CAVE}}

The wild Pokémon here are much stronger than on the routes nearby: Lv 22–25. A Diglett or Dugtrio is a big help against Lt. Surge, because Ground types aren't hurt by Electric moves at all.

Walk the whole tunnel and you come out on the east side of Route 2.

Route 2 (east side)

The east side of Route 2 is cut off from the main road by small trees. Come out of Diglett's Cave, or use Cut on a tree, to reach it. A man in the gatehouse here points out that cutting the small tree outside is the way to reach Diglett's Cave from this side.

Trade House

A boy in this house is looking for an Abra. Bring him one and he trades you his Mr. Mime for it.

Route 2 Gate

One of Professor Oak's aides waits in the gatehouse on the east side. Show him you've caught 10 kinds of Pokémon, and he gives you HM Flash. Thanks to your Boulder Badge, you can use it outside battle to light up dark caves like Rock Tunnel.

Route 9

Route 9 runs east from Cerulean City toward Rock Tunnel. A small tree blocks the way in at the Cerulean end, so use Cut to clear it. The route is a long, winding path full of trainers.

{{journal:m7}} {{tables:KANTO_ROUTE_9}}

Three items are hidden: step on their spots to find them. The water here can only be fished. With a Super Rod you might even hook a Dratini.

Nine trainers wait here, including three Hikers with Rock and Fighting Pokémon. Water and Grass moves make short work of Geodude and Onix.

{{trainers:KANTO_ROUTE_9}}

Route 10 (north)

Route 10 picks up where Route 9 ends and leads down to the mouth of Rock Tunnel. Wild Voltorb are common in the grass. A channel of water leads east to the Power Plant, but you need Surf to get there, so leave it for later.

Four items are hidden on the route: step on their spots to find them.

Pokémon Center

The Pokémon Center stands right by the entrance to Rock Tunnel, so heal up before you go in. A girl has heard that ghosts haunt Lavender Town, just past the tunnel.

Yet another of Professor Oak's aides is here. Show him 20 kinds of Pokémon caught and he gives you an Everstone. A Pokémon holding it won't evolve.

Before Rock Tunnel. The tunnel is pitch dark. You can find your way through without Flash, but you'll only see a little around you. With Flash on one of your Pokémon, the whole cave lights up.
