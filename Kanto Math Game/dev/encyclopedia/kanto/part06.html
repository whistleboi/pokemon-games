Route 6

Route 6 is where the Underground Path from Route 5 comes out. It's a short, grassy road that runs south to Vermilion City, the port of Kanto. There's water here too, but you'll need Surf before you can go out on it.

{{tables:KANTO_ROUTE_6}}

The Headbutt trees need the Rocky Helmet. FireRed has no Headbutt trees. Both hidden items are found by stepping on their spots.

Six trainers wait along the way, including a Camper with a Squirtle and a Bug Catcher with a Butterfree.

{{trainers:KANTO_ROUTE_6}}

Saffron Gate

The gatehouse on this route leads to Saffron City, but the guard inside won't let anyone through. He's terribly thirsty, and if you walk up to the far door he steps in your way. Remember him: once you have something for him to drink, he'll let you pass.

Vermilion City

Vermilion City sits on the south coast, and the luxury liner S.S. Anne is tied up at its harbour. Route 6 leads back north, and Route 11 heads east toward Diglett's Cave. A small tree stands in front of the Gym. You'll need Cut to clear it, and the S.S. Anne is where you'll find it.

{{journal:m5}} {{tables:VERMILLION_CITY}}

Vermilion's water can be fished from the shore once you have a rod. The Lost Item ours comes later: the Fan Club Chairman gives it to you after the Power Plant is working again.

Around town, a builder's Machop is stamping a lot flat for a new building, and an old man tells you the S.S. Anne only visits once a year. A sailor near the harbour says ships leave from the dock inside.

Pokémon Center

Heal your team here. A sailor is glad to be back on dry land, and a man explains that a trainer's Pokémon is stronger than a wild one of the same level. Upstairs, the link rooms are closed.

Fishing Guru's house

The Fishing Guru lives in the house in the north of town. Talk to him and he gives you an Old Rod, so you can fish anywhere there's water. If you already have a rod, he gives you a Rare Candy instead.

Pokémon Fan Club

The Chairman of the Fan Club loves to talk about his Pokémon. Sit through his whole story and he thanks you with a Bike Voucher. Take it to the Bike Shop in Cerulean City and swap it for a Bicycle.

{{journal:s_voucher}}

The members each think their own Pokémon is the cutest. Two signs on the wall set the club rules: listen politely, and if someone brags, brag right back.

Houses

A girl in the house next to the Fan Club wants a Spearow. Bring one and she'll trade you her Farfetch'd for it. In the house to the east, a girl and a boy send letters to far-off friends by Pidgey.

Vermilion Harbor

The harbour is at the south end of town. The sailor at the door only lets you board the S.S. Anne if you have an S.S. Ticket, which Bill gave you at his Sea Cottage north of Cerulean City. Show it and walk up the gangway.

After you become Champion, the harbour also has ships and a blimp to other places. Those come much later.

S.S. Anne

The S.S. Anne is a luxury liner full of travelling trainers, and lots of them want to battle. The ship has five levels: 1F, where you come aboard; 2F, with the Captain's office; 3F, which leads out onto the Deck; and B1F, below decks.

{{journal:k_ssanne}} {{tables:KANTO_SS_ANNE}}

The kitchen's three Berries are hidden in its bins, and the Hyper Potion is hidden on B1F. Most of the trainers wait inside the cabins.

{{trainers:KANTO_SS_ANNE}}

1F

A waiter offers to bring you anything you like. The kitchen is on the left: the chefs are far too busy to talk, but a Great Ball lies on the floor. Seven cabins line the corridor. In one, a Lass and a Youngster want to battle, and a TM for Brick Break sits on the floor. Two more cabins each have a Gentleman ready for a fight. In another cabin, a kind lady lets your Pokémon rest, which heals your whole team for free.

2F

The stairs up from 1F bring you into the 2F corridor, with six cabins and the Captain's office at the far end.

Rival battle

Your rival is on board too. He's standing in the corridor, and he challenges you as soon as he sees you.

{{battle:KANTO_SS_ANNE:rival}}

Two cabins on this floor have a pair of trainers each, and you can pick up Stardust and an X Attack in them. A girl in another cabin tells you the Captain is feeling awfully sick.

Captain's office

The Captain is slumped over his desk, terribly seasick. Rub his back to help him feel better, and he gives you HM Cut to thank you. Teach it to one of your Pokémon from the Bag. Thanks to your Cascade Badge, you can use Cut to chop down small trees outside battle.

3F and the Deck

Another set of stairs on 2F leads up to 3F, and from there out onto the Deck. Two Sailors are battling out in the sea air, and a passenger who feels ill has come out for fresh air.

B1F

A second set of stairs on 1F leads down to B1F. Its five cabins are full of Sailors and a Fisherman, so expect plenty of Water Pokémon. You'll find a TM for Rest, an Ether and a Super Potion in the cabins.

Don't miss anything. The S.S. Anne sails as soon as you're back in the harbour with HM Cut, and it won't come back. Pick up the items and battle the trainers you want before you leave.

Vermilion City (again)

As you walk back into the harbour, the ship sounds its horn and sails away. Now use Cut on the small tree in front of the Gym.

Vermilion Gym

Lt. Surge has locked his door with two electric locks, and the switches are hidden in the rows of trash cans. Look in the cans until you find the first switch. The second is always in a can right next to it: above, below, left or right. Pick the wrong one and both locks reset, and the switches move somewhere new. The Gym Guide by the door gives the same hint, and the Engineer drops one too once you beat him.

{{trainers:VERMILLION_CITY:Vermilion Gym}}

Lt. Surge

Once both locks are open, the beam across the door goes off and you can reach the Leader. Lt. Surge uses Electric Pokémon that hit hard and fast. Ground types aren't hurt by Electric moves at all, so a Diglett or Sandshrew is a great help here.

{{battle:VERMILLION_CITY:Lt. Surge}}

Win and you get the Thunder Badge. It lets you use Fly outside battle once you have it.
