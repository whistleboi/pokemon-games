One Island (again): the Network Machine

With the Sapphire back from the Rocket Warehouse, sail to One Island and talk to Celio in the Pokémon Network Center.

{{journal:sevii_m5b}}

He fits the Sapphire next to the Ruby, and the Network Machine finally works: Kanto is linked to the world. Celio also found something in the warehouse files, an old sea chart. He gives you the Old Sea Map ours. It shows an island that isn't on any map, where sailors keep seeing a pink Pokémon.

Fixing the machine opens two more places: the Seagallop can now sail to Faraway Island, and the guard at Cerulean Cave stands aside.

{{journal:s_mewtwo0}}

What the National Pokédex opens

The National Pokédex you got from Professor Oak can record Pokémon from far beyond Kanto. Besides the Rainbow Pass, it opens a few more things in Kanto:

  • Silph Co. in Saffron City: once the Network Machine is running, a scientist on 1F gives you an Up-Grade. A Porygon holding it evolves into Porygon2. Before then, he says the part needs a link to the faraway islands, and only goes to a Trainer with the National Pokédex.
  • The Trainer Tower: the Single Battle prize is an Up-Grade too. Without the National Pokédex the owner keeps it for later.
Mew. When you've caught all 150 Kanto Pokémon before Mew, Professor Oak tells you that Celio has heard of sailors seeing a pink, floating Pokémon near an uncharted island. It could be Mew.

Cerulean Cave

Cerulean Cave is the cave just north of Cerulean City, home to terribly strong Pokémon. A guard blocks the way: only a Champion who has finished the Network Machine job may go in. Once both are done, walk in.

{{journal:s_mewtwo}} {{tables:CERULEAN_CAVE}}

Mewtwo

The cave has three floors: 1F, 2F and B1F. Mewtwo waits on B1F, at the very bottom.

Mewtwo, Lv 70. The cave's boss, on B1F. Beat it and you can try to catch it. Bring your strongest team and plenty of healing, and Ultra Balls.

Faraway Island

Faraway Island ours is the uncharted island on Celio's Old Sea Map. With the map in your Bag, any Seagallop harbour in the Sevii Islands, from One Island to Seven Island, has a ferry there.

{{journal:sevii_m6}}

You land on the shore, where an old sign is too faded to read. The tall grass of the forest lies beyond. Something pink giggles there and darts away.

Mew ours. Mew only shows itself to a trainer who has caught all 150 other Kanto Pokémon (#1 to #150). Until then, following the pink shadow just tells you how many you've caught and how many are still to go. Once you have them all, challenge Mew in the forest. The Seagallop takes you back to One Island.

Navel Rock

Navel Rock is a tall rock far out at sea, with two legendary Pokémon inside: Ho-Oh at the top and Lugia at the bottom.

Getting there ours. The Seagallop at One Island sails to Navel Rock for a Champion holding the Mystic Ticket. In this game the Mystic Ticket and the Aurora Ticket come from the ferry clerk at Lilycove City's harbour in Hoenn, who gives them to the Hoenn Champion. The tickets travel with you between regions. Once you've been to Navel Rock, Fly can take you back.
{{journal:sevii_m9}} {{journal:sevii_m10}}

Up to Ho-Oh

From the harbour, go in to 1F and on to the fork, then the long stairs. One way climbs four floors to the Summit, where Ho-Oh waits. A Sacred Ash is hidden at the Summit.

Ho-Oh, Lv 70. At the Summit of Navel Rock. Beat it and you can try to catch it.

Down to Lugia

The other way from the long stairs goes down eleven floors to the Base, where Lugia waits.

Lugia, Lv 70. At the Base of Navel Rock. Beat it and you can try to catch it.

A sailor in the harbour takes you back to One Island.

Birth Island

Birth Island is a bare little island far out at sea. A Pokémon from space was seen here.

Getting there ours. The Seagallop at One Island sails to Birth Island for a Champion holding the Aurora Ticket, from the ferry clerk at Lilycove City in Hoenn (see Navel Rock). Once you've been, Fly can take you back.
{{journal:sevii_m11}}
Deoxys, Lv 30. A powerful presence fills the air on Birth Island, and Deoxys appears. Beat it and you can try to catch it.

A sailor in the harbour takes you back to One Island.
