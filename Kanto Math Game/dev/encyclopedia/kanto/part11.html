Saffron City

Saffron City is the big city in the middle of Kanto, with gates out to Routes 5, 6, 7 and 8. The guard in each gate is thirsty and turns you back, but once you carry Tea they let you through. Inside, Team Rocket has taken over: grunts stand all over the streets, one blocks the door of the Gym, and the Silph Co. tower in the middle of the city is in their hands.

Silph Co. opens up once you've rescued Mr. Fuji from the Pokémon Tower. Until then, a grunt at its door claims to be security and won't let you in.

{{tables:SAFFRON_CITY}}

Silph Co. comes first

You can't reach Sabrina while Team Rocket holds the city. Go into Silph Co. and drive them out first: see Silph Co. below. Once Giovanni is beaten, the grunts are gone from the streets and the townspeople come back out.

Fighting Dojo

The Black Belts of the Fighting Dojo train every day, and they're ready for anyone. Their sensei, the Karate Master Koichi, takes on challengers himself, and a prize waits for anyone who beats him.

{{trainers:SAFFRON_CITY:Fighting Dojo}} {{battle:SAFFRON_CITY:Koichi|Hitmonlee kicks, Hitmonchan punches.}}

After you beat him, talk to him again. Once you hold the Marsh Badge, he gives you one of his top students: a Hitmonlee or a Hitmonchan, picked at random. Since you already have Koga's Soul Badge, talk to him once more and he gives you the other one too ours. In FireRed you only get to pick one.

Mr. Psychic's House

Mr. Psychic knows what you want before you say a word. He gives you TM120 Psychic, a strong move that may lower the foe's Sp. Def.

Copycat's House

Downstairs, her mom and dad say their daughter likes to copy everyone. Upstairs, the Copycat is sad because she has lost her Clefairy doll. Give her a Poké Doll (they sell them at the Celadon Dept. Store) and she teaches one of your Pokémon Mimic. If you ever find her lost Clefairy doll, she gives you her Magnet Train Pass for the train between Saffron and Goldenrod City in Johto. The train only runs once the Power Plant is working again.

A Nugget is hidden in the Copycat's room.

Other buildings

The Pokémon Trainer Fan Club is full of fans of Bruno, Sabrina, Lance and Brock. In the house on the west side, a boy is getting the Copycat a Poké Doll. The Poké Mart and the Pokémon Center are open as usual.

Saffron Gym

Sabrina's Gym is a maze of warp pads. Step on one and you land somewhere else in the building. Sabrina waits in the middle room, so keep trying pads until one takes you there. The Gym Guide says Bug and Ghost moves work well against her Psychic Pokémon.

{{trainers:SAFFRON_CITY:Saffron Gym}} {{battle:SAFFRON_CITY:Sabrina}}

Win the Marsh Badge and TM129 Calm Mind. With the Marsh Badge you can use Rock Smash outside battle.

Silph Co.

Silph Co. is famous everywhere for its Pokémon products, and its tower has eleven floors. Team Rocket wants Silph's newest invention, the Master Ball, which catches any Pokémon without fail. Their boss, Giovanni, is holding the President in the boardroom on 11F. The floors are full of grunts and Rocket scientists, and many doors are locked.

Up to the Card Key

Ignore the locked doors for now. Take the stairs in the top-right corner of each floor and climb to 5F. A Rocket grunt waits on 1F, and more are on every floor. On 5F the Card Key lies on the floor in the bottom corridor. It opens every locked door in the building.

Up to the boardroom

With the Card Key, go back down to 3F and open the locked door in the middle of the floor. The warp pad behind it carries you straight up to 7F, where your rival is waiting. In that same room, another warp pad takes you to 11F. Follow the corridor around to the boardroom.

The executives

Three of Team Rocket's leaders are in the building ours. If you climb by the stairs, Admin Proton waits on 8F and Archer on 9F. Executive Ariana guards the boardroom on 11F. She says the President is about to sign the Master Ball over to Team Rocket. FireRed has none of them: they come from HeartGold and SoulSilver.

Jessie and James are on 11F too, and they want a battle (from Pokémon Yellow).

Giovanni

Giovanni, the boss of Team Rocket, is in the boardroom. He's annoyed that a child keeps getting in his way.

{{battle:SILPH_CITY:Giovanni}}

Beaten, Giovanni leaves Silph Co. to you, for now, and warns that this isn't the last you'll see of Team Rocket.

The President's thanks

Talk to the President in the boardroom. He gives you the Master Ball, which catches any Pokémon without fail. He also gives you Porygon, the first digital Pokémon, built at Silph ours. (In FireRed, Porygon is a Game Corner prize.)

The Lapras on 7F

In a room on 7F behind a locked door, a Silph worker is overjoyed that you came to the rescue. As thanks, the worker gives you a Lapras at Lv 25. It was going to be a research subject, and it's much happier with you.
