Six Island

Six Island is a quiet, old island far from any city. The sign calls it Fortune Island of aged wisdom. The Water Path leads east, and Ruin Valley lies south along it. The Sapphire is hidden somewhere in that valley.

{{tables:SIX_ISLAND}}

Pokémon Center

Your rival is here. Professor Oak sent him out to fill in his Pokédex, and he tells you not to get in his way. A ruin hunter has spent twenty years looking for ruins and has a feeling Ruin Valley hides one.

The Ruin Maniac's notes

In one of the houses, a Ruin Maniac's notes describe a sealed cave in Ruin Valley called the Dotted Hole, its walls covered in raised dots. Next to the notes lies the Unown Report, which keeps track of every Unown shape you catch in the Tanoby Ruins.

Water Path

The Water Path runs east from Six Island. Go south along it for Ruin Valley, or north for Green Path. A hiker in town gives the same directions.

{{tables:WATER_PATH}} {{trainers:WATER_PATH}}

The Heracross fan

A sign on the path asks for a Heracross with a truly giant horn. In the northern house, a woman who adores Heracross asks to measure one. Bring a Heracross in your party and she gives you a Nest Ball. Her neighbour in the southern house knows his height to the inch, because she measures everything.

Ruin Valley

Ruin Valley is a rocky valley at the south end of the Water Path, full of Ruin Maniacs and Hikers. A scientist is puzzling over a door in the rock that's covered in dots.

{{tables:RUIN_VALLEY}} {{trainers:RUIN_VALLEY}}

The dotted door

The raised dots on the rock face read CUT. With HM Cut and the Cascade Badge, your Pokémon cuts the rock face and a doorway opens into the ruin: the Dotted Hole.

Dotted Hole

The Dotted Hole is a small ruin of holes in the floor. Each floor below the first has raised dots on the wall that tell you which hole to drop through. Pick the wrong one and you fall all the way back to 1F.

  • 1F: drop through the hole to B1F.
  • B1F: the dots read GO UP. Take the top hole.
  • B2F: GO LEFT. Take the left hole.
  • B3F: GO RIGHT. Take the right hole.
  • B4F: GO DOWN. Take the bottom hole, into the Sapphire's room.

The Sapphire

The dots on the pedestal read: THE BLUE GEM SLEEPS HERE. As you reach for the Sapphire, a man in a lab coat shoves past and grabs it. It's Gideon, and he thanks you for opening the way: Team Rocket will pay well for it. He mutters the warehouse's second password, "Yes, nah, Chansey", and runs off toward Five Island.

Now you know both passwords. A way out of the Sapphire's room leads back up to 1F. Head back to Five Isle Meadow and into the Rocket Warehouse to get the Sapphire back.

Green Path

Green Path joins the north end of the Water Path. It leads on to Pattern Bush and to Outcast Island. Its sign asks you to take your litter home.

{{tables:GREEN_PATH}} {{trainers:GREEN_PATH}}

Pattern Bush

Pattern Bush is a thicket off Green Path, busy with Bug Catchers and other trainers. The wild Pokémon here are only around Lv 10, but the trainers are strong. It's the place to catch a Heracross for the fan on the Water Path.

{{tables:PATTERN_BUSH}} {{trainers:PATTERN_BUSH}}

Outcast Island

Outcast Island lies north of Green Path. A Rocket Grunt hangs around with the swimmers and fishermen. The Altering Cave is at its north end.

{{tables:OUTCAST_ISLAND}} {{trainers:OUTCAST_ISLAND}}

Altering Cave

The Altering Cave is a small cave on Outcast Island. No trainers live here, but nine kinds of Pokémon do, all at about the same chance.

{{tables:SEVII_ALTERING_CAVE}}

Seven Island

Seven Island is the last of the Sevii Islands, almost untouched. The sign calls it Quest Island of Infinity. The Trainer Tower stands here, and Canyon Entrance leads south to Sevault Canyon and the Tanoby Ruins.

{{tables:SEVEN_ISLAND}} {{trainers:SEVEN_ISLAND}}

Psychics Dario and Rodette battle anyone who comes by. Afterwards Dario tells you the Tower calls to the strong.

Swords Dance tutor

A trainee who says hardly anyone comes out this far teaches one of your Pokémon Swords Dance, and you can come back for more lessons ours.

Trainer Tower

The Trainer Tower has eight floors of trainers, with the owner waiting on the roof. The lobby has a nurse who heals your Pokémon and a clerk who sells Ultra Balls, Great Balls, Full Restores, Max Potions, Hyper Potions, Revives, Full Heals, Escape Ropes and Max Repels.

The challenge

Sign up with the receptionist and pick a challenge: Single Battle, Double Battle, Knockout or Mixed. Double and Mixed need at least two Pokémon that can battle.

  • A clock starts when you sign up and stops when you reach the owner on the roof.
  • Beat a floor's trainers and the stairs up open.
  • The trainers' Pokémon are as strong as your strongest.
  • Lose once and the challenge is over: your team is healed and you're back in the lobby.

The owner gives a prize the first time you finish each challenge, and keeps your best time for each one. The lift takes you back down to the lobby.

Prizes. Single Battle: Up-Grade (only with the National Pokédex). Double Battle: Dragon Scale. Knockout: Metal Coat. Mixed: King's Rock.

Canyon Entrance

Canyon Entrance runs south from Seven Island toward Sevault Canyon. The sign asks trainers not to disturb the Pokémon here.

{{tables:CANYON_ENTRANCE}} {{trainers:CANYON_ENTRANCE}}

Sevault Canyon

Sevault Canyon is a deep, rocky canyon with the Tanoby Ruins at its south end. Cooltrainers and Pokémon Rangers train here, and Skarmory and Larvitar can be found among the rocks.

{{tables:SEVAULT_CANYON}} {{trainers:SEVAULT_CANYON}}

The Chansey dance

In the house in the canyon, someone dances the Chansey dance and gets you to join in. A Lucky Punch lies on the floor. A Black Belt outside says training alone is good, but aiming for the top with friends is good too.

The Tanoby Key

The Tanoby Key opens all seven chambers of the Tanoby Ruins. Each chamber door is carved with a number pattern: say what comes next (2, 4, 6, ...) ours. Get all seven right and Unown of every shape swirl out of the chambers. You can't catch Unown until this is done.

Tanoby Ruins

The Tanoby Ruins are a string of seven stone chambers along the coast south of Sevault Canyon: Monean, Liptoo, Weepth, Dilford, Scufib, Rixy and Viapois. Unown live inside the chambers, every one at Lv 25. Four Heart Scales are hidden around the ruins outside.

{{tables:TANOBY_RUINS}}

Unown come in 28 shapes. The common letters are easy to meet, but O, ! and ? are rare ours: the chances are half FireRed's and half even, so every shape can be found.

Trainers

Four trainers walk the ruins outside the chambers.
