Rock Tunnel

Rock Tunnel is a dark, rocky cave on two floors, 1F and B1F, linked by ladders. You come in from Route 10 at the north end of 1F, and the way out to Lavender Town is at its south end. The cave is pitch dark, so Flash makes the trip much easier. Without it you only see a small circle around you.

{{tables:ROCK_TUNNEL}}

Geodude and Onix are Rock and Ground, so Water and Grass moves work well on them. Zubat often appear too.

The tunnel is full of trainers: seven on 1F and eight on B1F, all Hikers, Picnickers and PokéManiacs.

{{trainers:ROCK_TUNNEL}}

Rock Slide hiker

A Hiker down on B1F is fed up with falling rocks and decides to put them to use. He teaches Rock Slide to one of your Pokémon that can learn it.

Route 10 (south)

The south end of Rock Tunnel opens onto the last stretch of Route 10. It's a short walk through the grass down to Lavender Town, with four trainers on the way.

Lavender Town

Lavender Town is a small, quiet town where people come to lay their Pokémon to rest. The tall Pokémon Tower stands on the north side of town. Route 8 leads west toward Celadon City, Route 12 runs south, and Route 10 goes back north to Rock Tunnel.

The townspeople are uneasy. A boy says ghosts have been seen in the Pokémon Tower, and a little girl asks if you believe in them. A sign advertises Silph Co.'s new Silph Scope, which makes the invisible visible.

Pokémon Center

Heal up here. The people inside talk about Team Rocket: they'll do anything for money, and a girl saw Cubone's mother try to escape from them.

Volunteer Pokémon House

This is Mr. Fuji's house, where he cares for Pokémon that have nobody to look after them. He isn't home, and nobody knows where he went. A heavy sadness seems to hang over the Pokémon Tower.

Houses and shops

In the house on the west side, a woman tells you how Team Rocket took a Cubone's mother, who never came back. The Name Rater lives next door: he'll rate any of your Pokémon's nicknames and help you change them. There's a Poké Mart in town too.

The town's Radio Tower ours is dark: the power is out. Someone will need to fix the Power Plant before it can go back on the air.

Pokémon Tower

You can look inside the Pokémon Tower now, but you won't get far. A ghost blocks the stairs on 6F, and you can't tell what it is. You'll need the Silph Scope, and Team Rocket in Celadon City might have one. Head west along Route 8 for now.

Route 12. Route 12 heads south from Lavender, but a huge Pokémon is asleep across it. You'll need a way to wake it, so come back later.

Route 8

Route 8 runs west from Lavender Town toward Saffron City. Its gatehouse to Saffron has another thirsty guard, so you can't go that way yet. Instead, take the Underground Path entrance on this route to cross under Saffron to Route 7.

{{tables:KANTO_ROUTE_8}}

Growlithe and Vulpix are in the grass here, a good chance to catch a Fire type before Erika's Gym. The Headbutt trees need the Rocky Helmet; FireRed has no Headbutt trees. The three Berries are hidden: step on their spots to find them.

This is a busy route with twelve trainers. The Bikers and Super Nerds use Poison Pokémon like Koffing and Grimer, so Psychic and Ground moves help.

{{trainers:KANTO_ROUTE_8}}

Saffron Gate

Like the guard on Route 6, this one is far too thirsty to let anyone into Saffron City. He blocks the way if you try to walk past.

Underground Path (east–west)

This long tunnel runs under Saffron City from Route 8 to Route 7. Just follow it to the other end.

Route 7

Route 7 is a short route between Saffron City and Celadon City. There are no trainers here, just a little grass. The Saffron gate's guard is thirsty too, so head west into Celadon City.

{{tables:KANTO_ROUTE_7}}

The Headbutt trees need the Rocky Helmet; FireRed has no Headbutt trees. The Wepear Berry is hidden: step on its spot to find it.
