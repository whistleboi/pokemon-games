Indigo Plateau

The Indigo Plateau stands at the top of Route 23. It's home to the Pokémon League, where the Elite Four and the Champion wait. Inside the League building there's a counter to heal your team and a shop.

Pokémon League

The guard warns you that once you go in to face the Elite Four, there's no turning back. The Gym Guide adds that the four of them battle you one after another with no rest in between, so stock up first. A man nearby says Agatha's Ghost Pokémon are terrifying, so bring something that can hit them. The link and trade counters upstairs are closed for now.

When you're ready, walk through the door at the back. Each room's door only opens once you've won its battle.

The Champion

In the last room, your rival is waiting. He's the Champion now, and he says you're too late. While working on his Pokédex, he searched everywhere for powerful Pokémon. Now he wants to prove he's the strongest trainer in the world.

When you win, your rival can't believe his reign is over already. Then Professor Oak walks in. He congratulates you on becoming the Champion, and tells your rival he lost because he forgot to trust and care for his Pokémon. Oak asks you to follow him.

Hall of Fame

Oak leads you into the Hall of Fame, where every Champion of the Pokémon League is honoured. The team that won you the title is entered in the records. Then you head home to Pallet Town to share your victory.

After the title

{{badges:post}} {{cap}}

You're the Champion of Kanto now, and new places open up:

  • Professor Oak's National Dex. Visit Oak in Pallet Town. Once you've seen 60 kinds of Kanto Pokémon, he upgrades your Pokédex to the National Dex, and he mentions that Celio on One Island has been asking after you.
  • The Rainbow Pass. As Champion with the National Dex, Celio gives you the Rainbow Pass, and the Seagallop Ferry can take you to Four, Five, Six and Seven Island.
  • To Johto. A new door at the Indigo Plateau leads to Johto's Route 29.
  • League rematches. You can take on the Elite Four and your rival again, and this time they use stronger teams, from Lv 63 up to Lv 75.
