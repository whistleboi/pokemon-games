After the title

You're the Champion of Hoenn, and a lot opens up:

  • Professor Birch is out on Route 101 with Pokémon a colleague sent from Johto. Pick one of Chikorita, Cyndaquil and Totodile (Lv 5). After the Delta Episode he has Unova's three to choose from, and after you enter the Hall of Fame a second time, Sinnoh's.
  • Steven's house in Mossdeep City has a Poké Ball on the table, with a note for the new Champion: a Beldum (Lv 5).
  • The S.S. Tidal now sails between Slateport City and Lilycove City, and takes you to the Battle Frontier (Part 20). The ferry clerk in Lilycove also has two tickets addressed to you, the Mystic Ticket and the Aurora Ticket, for ferries to Navel Rock and Birth Island.
  • New places: the Desert Underpass off Route 114 (Part 20), the Sealed Chamber under Route 134 and the Scorched Slab's legend (Part 19), and the Safari Zone's new areas.
  • The skies. With the Eon Flute, legends now wait in the sky too (Part 19), and the DexNav can find Pokémon from later generations.
  • League rematches. The Elite Four and Steven come back with stronger teams, from Lv 70 to Lv 79.

The Delta Episode

A meteoroid is heading for Hoenn, and the only Pokémon that can stop it sleeps at the top of the Sky Pillar. This story takes you all over Hoenn, one step at a time. Each step opens the next.

{{journal:hoenn_d1}}

Littleroot Town: Mom's news

Go home to Littleroot Town and talk to Mom. The news is full of a huge meteor coming toward Hoenn. She also says Wally's family in Petalburg City called: some strange people are at their house, asking about you.

Petalburg City: the admins' questions

In Petalburg City, two familiar faces are waiting: Courtney of Team Magma and Matt of Team Aqua. Both want to know what's coming with the meteor, and each one battles you to find out. You meet both of them, whichever version you play.

{{battle:PETALBURG_CITY:Courtney}} {{battle:PETALBURG_CITY:Matt}} {{journal:hoenn_s38}}

Granite Cave: Zinnia

Once you've battled both admins, a trainer called Zinnia is waiting deep in Granite Cave, off Dewford Town. She's heard everyone talking about you and wants to see for herself. Her three are all part Dragon, so Fairy moves work on every one. Ice moves hit Altaria and Salamence four times as hard, and Fighting, Ground or Steel moves are good on Tyrantrum, which is part Rock.

{{battle:HOENN_GRANITE_CAVE:Zinnia}} {{journal:hoenn_s40}}

Rustboro City: trouble at Devon

Next, three grunts of Team Magma (or Team Aqua in Alpha Sapphire) turn up outside the Devon Corporation in Rustboro City. Their boss sent them, and with the meteor coming, they don't want you getting in their way. Beat all three and they promise to leave Devon alone.

{{battle:RUSTBORO_CITY:Team Magma Grunt (Devon 1)|Omega Ruby.}} {{battle:RUSTBORO_CITY:Team Aqua Grunt (Devon 1)|Alpha Sapphire.}} {{battle:RUSTBORO_CITY:Team Magma Grunt (Devon 2)|Omega Ruby.}} {{battle:RUSTBORO_CITY:Team Aqua Grunt (Devon 2)|Alpha Sapphire.}} {{battle:RUSTBORO_CITY:Team Magma Grunt (Devon 3)|Omega Ruby.}} {{battle:RUSTBORO_CITY:Team Aqua Grunt (Devon 3)|Alpha Sapphire.}}

After that, more grunts show up at the Space Center in Mossdeep City. Battling them is up to you.

Mossdeep City: the admins again

With the grunts at Devon beaten, Courtney and Matt are waiting again, this time in Mossdeep City, with stronger Pokémon.

{{battle:MOSSDEEP_CITY:Courtney}} {{battle:MOSSDEEP_CITY:Matt}}

After this, a few grunts are back in the team's old hideout near Lilycove City too, if you want more battles.

Route 131: Wallace

Once both admins in Mossdeep are beaten, Wallace is waiting on Route 131, by the Sky Pillar. Hoenn's legends are stirring, and he wants to see your resolve. This time he brings six Pokémon. Electric moves are still a good choice for most of them, and they hit Gyarados four times as hard. Use Grass moves on Whiscash, and Flying, Poison or Bug moves on Ludicolo, which is part Grass.

{{battle:HOENN_ROUTE_131:Wallace}} {{journal:hoenn_s39}}

Beat him and the Sky Pillar opens.

Sky Pillar (the climb)

Climb the Sky Pillar from the Entrance, along the Outside path and up through its five floors. Remember the cracked tiles on some floors: ride the Bicycle over them, or you fall through to the floor below. Golbat, Ariados, Claydol, Sableye and Mawile live in the tower.

Zinnia at the top

At the top, Zinnia is waiting. She says only Rayquaza can stop the meteoroid, and if you want to call it, you have to beat her first. Her Dragons are stronger now, and there are five of them. Fairy moves work on all five. Ice moves hit Altaria, Noivern and Salamence four times as hard, and Fighting, Ground or Steel moves are good on Tyrantrum.

{{battle:HOENN_SKY_PILLAR:Zinnia}}

When you win, she admits you're the real deal and tells you Rayquaza is waiting.

{{journal:hoenn_d2}}

Rayquaza

Summon Rayquaza at the top of the Sky Pillar. It's a Dragon and Flying type: Ice moves hit it four times as hard, and Rock, Dragon and Fairy moves work too.

Rayquaza, Lv 70. At the top of the Sky Pillar, once you've beaten Zinnia there. Weaken it and try to catch it. If you beat it without catching it, it comes back the next day.
{{journal:hoenn_d3}}

Into space

Once Rayquaza has awoken, ride it into space to meet the meteoroid. Something is waiting inside it: Deoxys. It's a Psychic type, so Bug, Ghost and Dark moves are good choices.

Deoxys, Lv 70. In space, after you've summoned Rayquaza. Beat it and you can try to catch it.

With the meteoroid dealt with, the Delta Episode is over. Back home, Mom has found an odd stone in the yard after the meteor shower: the Latiasite in Omega Ruby, or the Latiosite in Alpha Sapphire. Professor Birch on Route 101 has Unova's starters for you now, and Professor Cozmo in Fallarbor Town has news about a comet (Part 19).

The other legend

One more legend stirs after the Delta Episode: the one your version's team didn't wake. In Omega Ruby, Kyogre sleeps in the Marine Cave under Route 127. In Alpha Sapphire, Groudon sleeps in the Terra Cave off Route 114. See Part 19.
