Legends of Hoenn

Hoenn is full of legendary Pokémon. A few open up during the journey, but most wait until you're Champion. Each legend is a single battle: weaken it and try to catch it. If you beat one without catching it, it comes back the next day.

This part goes through them in groups: the three Regi and their leader, the other box legend, the Scorched Slab, the Mirage spots you reach by Soaring, the legends in the sky, Emerald's Mirage Island, and the Millennium Comet.

The Regi

Three ancient Pokémon of rock, ice and steel sleep in sealed caves around Hoenn. The old man in Pacifidlog Town knows the story, and his grandchild's saying, "six dots open three doors", is the clue: the dots are braille. The way in starts at the Sealed Chamber.

{{journal:hoenn_s6}}

Sealed Chamber

The Sealed Chamber lies deep under the sea on Route 134. Once you're Champion, Dive down there to find its entrance. Inside are two rooms with braille carved on the walls.

  • The outer room. The braille says "Dig here". Bring a Pokémon that knows Dig and it opens a way through the wall into the inner room.
  • The inner room. The braille says Wailord comes first and Relicanth comes last. Put a Wailord first in your party and a Relicanth last, then read the wall. There's a rumble far away: something has stirred in the Desert Ruins, the Island Cave and the Ancient Tomb.
{{tables:HOENN_SEALED_CHAMBER}}

Desert Ruins

The Desert Ruins stand in the desert on Route 111. Their door stays sealed until you've solved the Sealed Chamber. Inside, the braille on the wall says to go left, left, down, down and then use Rock Smash. Do that and the braille wall slides open. Regirock is waiting beyond it.

{{tables:HOENN_DESERT_RUINS}}
Regirock, Lv 40. A Rock type: Water, Grass, Fighting, Ground and Steel moves all work well.
{{journal:hoenn_s16}}

Island Cave

The Island Cave is on Route 105, and it opens once you've solved the Sealed Chamber. The braille here says to keep close to the walls and walk the whole way around once. Do a full lap of the room along its walls and the braille wall slides open. Regice is inside.

{{tables:HOENN_ISLAND_CAVE}}
Regice, Lv 40. An Ice type: use Fire, Fighting, Rock or Steel moves.
{{journal:hoenn_s17}}

Ancient Tomb

The Ancient Tomb is on Route 120, and it opens with the other two. The braille says to shine a light in the middle: use Flash in the middle of the room, and the braille wall slides open. Registeel waits inside.

{{tables:HOENN_ANCIENT_TOMB}}
Registeel, Lv 40. A Steel type: Fire, Fighting and Ground moves work best.
{{journal:hoenn_s18}}

Regigigas

Once you've battled all three Regi, go back to the Island Cave on Route 105. A colossal Pokémon now sleeps where Regice stood, and the three Regi's stirring wakes it.

Regigigas, Lv 50. In the Island Cave, after you've battled Regirock, Regice and Registeel. A Normal type: Fighting moves are your best choice.
{{journal:hoenn_s19}}

The other legend

After the Delta Episode (Part 18), the legend your version's team didn't wake can be found. Only one of these two caves opens, depending on your version.

Marine Cave

Omega Ruby only. The Marine Cave is under the sea on Route 127: Dive down to the seabed there to find it. Kyogre is sleeping in the deepest cave.

{{tables:HOENN_MARINE_CAVE}}
Kyogre, Lv 70. Omega Ruby, after the Delta Episode. A Water type: use Grass and Electric moves.

Terra Cave

Alpha Sapphire only. The Terra Cave opens off Route 114. Before the Delta Episode, it's just a dark cave mouth where nothing stirs. Groudon is sleeping in the deepest cave.

{{tables:HOENN_TERRA_CAVE}}
Groudon, Lv 70. Alpha Sapphire, after the Delta Episode. A Ground type: use Water, Grass and Ice moves.

Scorched Slab

The Scorched Slab is a hot cave on Route 120. You need Surf to reach it. Inside, TM049 Sunny Day lies on the scorching rock, the Charizardite Y glints on the ground, a Full Heal lies nearby, and an Escape Rope, a Super Potion and a Nugget are hidden. The cave is open early, but its legend only appears once you're Champion.

{{tables:HOENN_SCORCHED_SLAB}}
Heatran, Lv 50. In the Scorched Slab, once you're Champion. A Fire and Steel type: Ground moves hit it four times as hard, and Water and Fighting moves work too.
{{journal:hoenn_s31}}

Soaring in the Sky

Steven gives you the Eon Flute in Sootopolis City after the Cave of Origin (Part 16). Play it anywhere outside and Latios (or Latias) flies you high over Hoenn. Fly over the map and land on the spots below. You can also take off from Mossdeep City.

Mirage spots are islands, caves, forests and mountains you can only reach from the sky. They're open as soon as you have the flute. Each time you land on a spot with legends, one of the legends you haven't caught yet may be there, picked at random, though now and then none comes out. To leave, choose to soar away.

The Mirage islands, mountains, forests and caves each come in eight places of their kind, and each place keeps one item in its own nook. Most spots also hide one more item, a new one each day:

  • Mirage Islands: a Pearl, a Big Pearl or a Razor Fang.
  • Mirage Forests: a Tiny Mushroom, a Big Mushroom or a Razor Claw.
  • Mirage Caves: a Red Shard, a Blue Shard or a Deep Sea Tooth.
  • Mirage Mountains: a Yellow Shard, a Green Shard or a Deep Sea Scale.
  • Crescent Isle: a Heart Scale, a Deep Sea Tooth, a Razor Claw, a Deep Sea Scale or a Razor Fang.
{{journal:hoenn_s30}}

Legends in the sky

Once you're Champion, two strange sights appear in the sky. A huge gap in the sky, south of Dewford Town, holds Dialga, Palkia and Giratina. A black storm cloud north of Fortree City holds Tornadus, Thundurus and Landorus. Fly into one and you meet one legend each time, in that order.

Dialga, Palkia, Giratina, Tornadus, Thundurus and Landorus. In the sky, once you're Champion. Palkia, Giratina, Tornadus and Landorus are Lv 50; Dialga and Thundurus are Lv 70.

Crescent Isle

A little island far out in the south-east. Cresselia, a Pokémon of good dreams, lives here.

{{tables:HOENN_CRESCENT_ISLE}}
Cresselia, Lv 50. A Psychic type: Bug, Ghost and Dark moves work well.
{{journal:hoenn_s32}}

Fabled Cave

An island with a cave in the middle of Hoenn's sea. Inside, the misty room is home to two dragons of legend: Reshiram and Zekrom.

{{tables:HOENN_FABLED_CAVE}}
Reshiram (Lv 50) and Zekrom. In the misty room, one per visit. Both are part Dragon: Dragon moves work on both, Ground and Rock on Reshiram, and Ground, Ice and Fairy on Zekrom.
{{journal:hoenn_s33}}

Gnarled Den

An ashen slope in the north-west of Hoenn, with a hollow at the top. Kyurem, an icy dragon, sleeps in the hollow.

{{tables:HOENN_GNARLED_DEN}}
Kyurem, Lv 50. A Dragon and Ice type: Fighting, Rock, Steel, Dragon and Fairy moves all work well.
{{journal:hoenn_s34}}

Nameless Cavern

An islet off the east of Hoenn with a chamber inside. The three Pokémon of the lakes hide here: Uxie, Mesprit and Azelf.

{{tables:HOENN_NAMELESS_CAVERN}}
Uxie, Mesprit and Azelf, Lv 50. In the chamber, one per visit. All three are Psychic types: Bug, Ghost and Dark moves work well.
{{journal:hoenn_s35}}

Pathless Plain

A wide plain in the south of Hoenn. Three Pokémon of justice roam here: Cobalion, Terrakion and Virizion.

{{tables:HOENN_PATHLESS_PLAIN}}
Cobalion, Terrakion and Virizion, Lv 50. One per visit. All three are part Fighting. Use Fire, Fighting or Ground moves on Cobalion; Water, Grass, Fighting or Psychic on Terrakion; and Flying (four times as hard) or Fire on Virizion.
{{journal:hoenn_s36}}

Trackless Forest

A forest in the far west of Hoenn. The three legendary beasts run through it: Raikou, Entei and Suicune.

{{tables:HOENN_TRACKLESS_FOREST}}
Raikou, Entei and Suicune, Lv 50. One per visit. Use Ground on Raikou; Water, Ground and Rock on Entei; and Grass and Electric on Suicune.
{{journal:hoenn_s37}}

Mirage Island (Soaring)

The Mirage Islands are sandy islands that come and go. Legends from far-off lands have been seen here: Articuno, Zapdos, Moltres, Mewtwo and Celebi. Wild Wynaut, Ditto and more live in the grass. Each of the eight islands also keeps one item in its sand.

{{tables:HOENN_MIRAGE_ISLANDS}}
Articuno, Zapdos and Moltres (Lv 50), Mewtwo (Lv 70) and Celebi (Lv 30). One per visit. Mewtwo is the toughest: Bug, Ghost and Dark moves are your best choices against it.
{{journal:hoenn_s41}}

Mirage Mountain

Rocky mountains you can only land on from the sky. There are no legends here, but there are wild Pokémon in its caves, an item lying among the stones at each of the eight mountains, and one more hidden each day.

{{tables:HOENN_MIRAGE_MOUNTAIN}}

Mirage Forest

Small forests full of wild Pokémon, with an item tucked under the roots at each of the eight forests and one more hidden each day.

{{tables:HOENN_MIRAGE_FOREST}}

Mirage Cave

An islet with a cave inside. Unown and Ditto are among its wild Pokémon. Each of the eight caves keeps an item wedged in the rock, and one more is hidden each day.

{{tables:HOENN_MIRAGE_CAVE}}

Mirage Island (Route 130)

This is Emerald's Mirage Island. On some days, roughly one day in seven, Route 130 has a small island in the middle of it. Each save has its own Mirage days. On those days, the island's grass is home to Wynaut, Ditto and many others. The rest of the route is the usual sea.

{{tables:HOENN_MIRAGE_ISLAND}}

Meteor Falls (again): the Millennium Comet

After the Delta Episode, visit Professor Cozmo in Fallarbor Town. The Millennium Comet, which only comes once every thousand years, is passing over Hoenn. Old stories say a Pokémon wakes under it, and its light shines brightest deep in Meteor Falls. Go and look, and you find Jirachi there ours. (In the source games, Jirachi had no place to find it; it came only as a special gift.)

{{journal:hoenn_s9}}
Jirachi, Lv 70. In Meteor Falls, after Cozmo tells you about the comet. A Steel and Psychic type: Fire, Ground, Ghost and Dark moves work well.
