Petalburg City (again)

With four badges from Rustboro, Dewford, Mauville and Lavaridge, it's time to go home to Petalburg City. Your dad, Norman, runs the Gym here, and he promised to battle you once you had four badges. Before that, the Gym won't let you challenge him.

{{journal:hoenn_m10}}

Petalburg Gym

Norman trains Normal Pokémon. His Gym is a row of small rooms, one after another, each with an Ace Trainer inside. A room's doors stay shut until you beat the Trainer in it, and most rooms have two doors onward, so you choose your own path toward Norman at the back. The Gym Guide by the entrance explains the idea.

{{trainers:PETALBURG_CITY:Petalburg Gym}} {{battle:PETALBURG_CITY:Norman|Both Slaking and Vigoroth know Retaliate and Feint Attack. The first Slaking also has Yawn and Encore, Vigoroth has Encore and Fury Swipes, and the last Slaking has Swagger and Chip Away.}}

Win and your dad hands over the Balance Badge, along with a TM of his favourite move, Retaliate. He couldn't be prouder. With the Balance Badge, your Pokémon can use Surf outside battle.

Wally's house

Remember Wally, the boy you helped catch his first Pokémon? Visit his family's house in Petalburg. His father is amazed you beat Norman at his own Gym, and as thanks he gives you HM Surf. Teach it to a Pokémon and it can carry you across rivers, lakes and the sea. A lot of Hoenn is water, so this opens up a big part of the map.

Route 118

Route 118 runs east from Mauville City. A stretch of water cuts across it, so you need Surf to explore all of it. To the north it meets Route 119, the way to Fortree City, and to the east it joins Route 123. Electrike are common in the grass, and Kecleon hide here too.

{{tables:HOENN_ROUTE_118}}

A Leaf Stone, a Stardust and a Hyper Potion lie on the route, and a patch of soft soil gives you six Sitrus Berries. A Heart Scale and an Iron are hidden. The DexNav can track down hidden Pokémon here.

{{trainers:HOENN_ROUTE_118}}

The Good Rod

A fisherman by the water asks if you agree that a Good Rod is really good. Say yes and he gives you the Good Rod, which hooks stronger Pokémon than the Old Rod. Another man reminds you that Surf lets you cross the sea without a boat.

Gabby and Ty

The TV crew, Gabby and Ty, are on the route again with their camera. They want to see how strong you've become, and they battle you together in a double battle. Win and they promise the interview will be on the news.

Steven and Mega Evolution

Once you have the Balance Badge, Steven is waiting for you on Route 118. He wants to show you something and takes you with him to Southern Island, a small island far to the south.

{{journal:hoenn_m11}}

On Southern Island a Pokémon of legend is waiting: Latios in Omega Ruby, or Latias in Alpha Sapphire. It wants to come with you, and it joins your team (or goes to the PC if your party is full). It comes with its Mega Stone, Latiosite or Latiasite.

Steven then gives you the Mega Bracelet. With it, a Pokémon holding its own Mega Stone can Mega Evolve. It happens when that Pokémon enters battle, once per battle.

Mega Stones. Mega Stones lie all over Hoenn, often as a glint on the ground. Steven gives you the one for your starter later, on Route 120 (see Part 12).

Southern Island

Southern Island is a small, quiet island out at sea, south of Slateport City. A stone tablet near the shore carries an old saying about memories carved into the heart. You can't sail there yourself yet: the ship from Slateport's harbour only takes you if you have the Eon Ticket, which Wallace gives you with the eighth badge (see Part 16).

When you do come back, a member of Team Magma (or Team Aqua in Alpha Sapphire) is lurking in the clearing at the island's heart.

{{trainers:HOENN_SOUTHERN_ISLAND}}

Past them, the other Eon Pokémon is waiting: Latias in Omega Ruby, or Latios in Alpha Sapphire, at Lv 30. This one doesn't come along by itself, so you have to battle it and catch it. A sailor by the shore takes you back to Slateport whenever you're ready.
