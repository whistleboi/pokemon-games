Petalburg City

Petalburg City is a green, peaceful city between Route 102 and Route 104, built around ponds. Your dad, Norman, is its new Gym Leader. The Gym stands in the middle of town, with the Pokémon Center and Poké Mart to the east. Route 104 leaves to the west, toward Petalburg Woods and Rustboro City.

{{tables:PETALBURG_CITY}}

A Rare Candy lies on the ground in the south of town. A Max Revive and an Ether sit on little bits of shore that you can only reach once you can Surf. The ponds can be fished once you have a rod.

Dad and Wally

As soon as you walk into town, your dad spots you. He can hardly believe you came all this way on your own, with a Pokémon of your own too, and he asks you into the Gym to talk.

Inside, a thin boy comes in. His name is Wally. He's moving to Verdanturf Town, where the air is cleaner, and he'd like a Pokémon to keep him company, but he's never caught one. Your dad lends him his Zigzagoon and a Poké Ball, and asks you to go with Wally to Route 102.

In the grass, a wild Ralts appears. Wally nervously asks Zigzagoon for help, Zigzagoon tackles it, and Wally throws the Poké Ball. Ralts is caught! Wally is overjoyed: he and Ralts are going to be best friends.

Your dad is proud of you both. He reminds you that he's a Gym Leader: earn four badges, then come back and challenge him. He suggests you start with Roxanne in Rustboro City, through Route 104 and Petalburg Woods.

The Petalburg Gym. The Gym stays closed to challengers until you hold four Hoenn badges: your dad won't battle you before then. Come back after Lavaridge Town (see Part 11).

Around Petalburg

Talk to the guide near the entrance and he walks you to the Pokémon Center, the first stop in every new town. A gentleman explains that if you already carry six Pokémon, the next one you catch goes straight to a Box in the PC. Wally's mother is looking for Wally, since the family is about to leave for Verdanturf Town, and a boy gazes at his own face in the pond.

Wally's house

Wally's father thanks you for helping Wally catch his Pokémon. Wally has been frail since he was a baby, so they're sending him to stay with family in Verdanturf, where the air is cleaner. Wally's mother hasn't seen him smile like that in ages. Come back with the Balance Badge and his father has a gift for you (see Part 11).

Other houses

In one house, an old traveller remembers roaming every sea and mountain in Hoenn, and a dreamy girl wishes she could go on an adventure with Pokémon. In another, a neighbour tells you Norman brought his family here from far away, and a trainer who once battled him says he was far too strong.

Pokémon Center and Poké Mart

Healing is free, as always. In the Center, a youngster says an Oran Berry got his Pokémon some HP back, and an Ace Trainer explains that each type is strong against some types and weak against others. In the Mart, a hiker tells you to carry Antidotes, and a little boy recommends Repels for keeping weak wild Pokémon away.

Route 104

Route 104 runs along Hoenn's west coast in two halves, with Petalburg Woods in between. The southern half starts at Petalburg City's west gate and runs along the beach, past Mr. Briney's cottage, to the woods. The northern half comes out of the woods, crosses a bridge over the water, passes the Pretty Petal Flower Shop and ends at Rustboro City.

{{tables:HOENN_ROUTE_104}}

Berries and items lie on the ground on both halves: Pecha, Oran and Chesto Berries, a Poké Ball, a Luxury Ball, an X Attack, a Revive and a PP Up. A youngster gives you TM277 Echoed Voice, a woman gives you a White Herb and an old woman a Chesto Berry. The sea is for later: Route 105 to the south needs Surf.

{{trainers:HOENN_ROUTE_104}}

Rich Boy Winston on the southern half, and Lady Cindy on the northern half, each have one Zigzagoon, and each pays a Nugget on top of the usual prize. On the northern half, the Twins Gina & Mia battle you together, two against two.

Mr. Briney's cottage

A girl near the cottage by the sea tells you it belongs to Mr. Briney, who was a great sailor once but hardly sails now. His boat is moored at the end of the pier. Mr. Briney himself, with his Wingull Peeko, says he'll sail you across the sea, but only once Mr. Stone sends you his way. You'll come back here in Part 4.

Along the beach, a lady reminds you that other Trainers' Pokémon aren't yours to catch, a boy says to weaken a wild Pokémon before you throw a Poké Ball, and a girl explains that when two Trainers lock eyes, a battle starts.

Head north into Petalburg Woods.

Petalburg Woods

Petalburg Woods is a shady forest between the two halves of Route 104. Bug Pokémon fill its tall grass, and Shroomish and the rare Slakoth live here too. The Trainer Tips signs remind you that a Pokémon earns Exp. Points just by appearing in a battle, and that a poisoned Pokémon keeps losing HP as you walk.

Items lie on the ground all through the woods: a Paralyze Heal, a Balm Mushroom, an Ether, a Net Ball, a Great Ball and an X Attack. A girl gives you a Miracle Seed. The Scizorite only appears long after the main story, once the Cave of Origin is over.

{{trainers:HOENN_PETALBURG_WOODS}}

Trouble in the woods

Deep in the woods, a Devon researcher has come looking for Shroomish. A Team Magma Grunt (or a Team Aqua Grunt in Alpha Sapphire) corners him and demands the Devon papers he's carrying. When you step in, the grunt turns on you.

{{battle:HOENN_PETALBURG_WOODS:Grunt|In the middle of the woods, in a story scene.}}

Beaten, the grunt gives up on the papers and runs. The researcher thanks you and heals your Pokémon. Then he remembers the grunt saying something about Rustboro, and hurries off to get back to the city.

Around the woods, a camper says to keep out of the tall grass if your Pokémon are worn out, and a boy who always watches where he's walking says things lie hidden on the ground. Find the north exit to reach the northern half of Route 104.

Route 104 (north)

As you leave the woods, a grunt of the team is talking on the phone to their Boss: the one in the woods got beaten by some kid, but they're still on the trail of an energy reading. They move on before you can do anything.

Pretty Petal Flower Shop

The three sisters who run this little shop love Berries. The eldest explains how a Berry planted in soft soil grows into a tree, stage by stage, and the middle sister gives you the Wailmer Pail: water a growing plant once at each stage and it bears more Berries. The youngest gives you a Berry every day, one of eight kinds. Outside, a berry lover says you can pick any Berry growing in soft soil, as long as you plant a new one to say thanks.

Rustboro City is at the top of the route.
