Route 107

Route 107 is open sea between Dewford Town and Route 108. There's no land to walk on, so you can only cross it with Surf, which comes much later (see Part 11). Mr. Briney's boat sails straight past it for now.

The seabed below Route 107 holds two more trainers and two hidden items. You'll need Dive to reach it, much later still.

Swimmers patrol the waves here. Lisa & Ray, a sister and brother, battle you together in a double battle.

Route 108

Route 108 is more open sea, between Route 107 and Route 109. It's another Surf-only route. In the middle of it sits the rusting hull of the Abandoned Ship, a side trip of its own (see Part 20).

The Star Piece has washed up on the rocks. The Rare Candy is hidden.

Route 109

Route 109 is Slateport City's sandy beach, with open sea to the south and west. Mr. Briney and his Wingull, Peeko, tie up here whenever they bring someone to Slateport. Head north up the beach and you're in the city.

{{tables:HOENN_ROUTE_109}}

The beach hides several items, three Heart Scales among them: step on their spots to find them. The water to the west leads on to Route 108 once you can Surf.

{{trainers:HOENN_ROUTE_109}}

Tubers, sailors and beachgoers are spread along the sand, and more trainers wait out in the water.

A girl on the beach invites you to her papa's Seashore House. An old man says his Zigzagoon helps keep the beach clean by picking up litter. A little boy is struggling to finish a sandcastle, a watchful mom keeps an eye on the kids, and a young tuber hands you some Soft Sand.

Seashore House

The Seashore House belongs to Mr. Sea, who loves a red-hot battle. Beat all five of the trainers he wants you to face: Tuber Simon, Delinquent Destinee, Street Thug Blair, Beauty Johanna and Sailor Dwayne. Then come back to him and he cools you down with six bottles of Soda Pop. After that, he sells Soda Pop whenever you like.

Slateport City

Slateport City is Hoenn's big port town. It's full of things to see: the Oceanic Museum, the busy Slateport Market, Captain Stern's Shipyard, a Contest Hall, the Pokémon Fan Club and the harbour. Route 110 leads north toward Mauville City, but at first the way is closed. Route 134 heads west across the sea, for later.

Surfing and fishing come later, from the city's shore.

A Grunt on arrival

As you arrive, someone calls out to you: a Grunt of Team Magma (or Team Aqua in Alpha Sapphire) wants to battle. The team is up to something in Slateport.

{{battle:SLATEPORT_CITY:Grunt@3|When you first arrive in Slateport.}}

Stern's Shipyard

Your job is to deliver the Devon Parts from Rustboro to Captain Stern, so head for his Shipyard. The Captain isn't there, though. His assistant, Dock, tells you Stern has gone to the Oceanic Museum to look at an exhibit, and asks you to take the parts to him there.

Around the Shipyard, a worker is muddled over where everything goes, and a helper who gets seasick works on dry land instead. Upstairs, a ship builder explains that a big ship is built more like a building than a car.

Later. If you find the Scanner aboard the Abandoned Ship (Part 20), bring it to Captain Stern here. He trades you a DeepSeaTooth or a DeepSeaScale for it, whichever you choose.

Slateport Market

Slateport's open-air Market stands by the beach. Its sign promises items found nowhere else. One merchant has had an odd stone on his stall for ages, and he gives it to you: it's the Alakazite, a Mega Stone. Shoppers wander between the stalls, and an old woman says a market always grows wherever people gather.

Around Slateport

The townsfolk have plenty to say. A long queue of Grunts snakes outside the Oceanic Museum, grumbling about the wait. A man says Captain Stern built the Museum and leads an undersea exploring team. A sailor wonders whether the sea is made of Pokémon tears, and an old sailor says ships too creaky to sail are sunk and become homes for Pokémon.

Pokémon Fan Club

The Fan Club's chairman loves Pokémon that love their Trainers. Talk to him and he gives your team five scarves: the Red, Blue, Pink, Green and Yellow Scarves. The members share tips: a Pokémon that faints again and again stops trusting you, so be kind.

Name Rater's house

The Name Rater will rate the nicknames of your Pokémon, and change them if you like.

Pokémon Center and Poké Mart

Heal up and shop before the Museum. A sailor in the Center advises raising lots of different Pokémon, not just one.

House

A man explains natures: his Hasty Pokémon is faster than his others, because a nature changes how a Pokémon grows.

Oceanic Museum

Once Dock has told you where the Captain went, the Grunts outside the Museum file inside. Follow them in. The Oceanic Museum has two floors of exhibits about the sea: fossils of ancient ripples, sand and soil samples, tide and wave machines, and models of ships and submarines.

Slateport City (again)

{{journal:hoenn_m6b}}

Talk to Captain Stern at his Shipyard and hand over the Devon Parts. With them he can finish his submarine and explore the sea floor. Your errand for Devon is done.

Lisia, the Contest Idol

As you walk toward the north edge of town, a girl with a real sparkle runs up to you. She's Lisia, the Contest Idol. She explains that Pokémon Contests are where Pokémon show off how Cool, Beautiful, Cute, Smart or Tough they are, and she gives you everything you need to start: a Contest Pass, a Pokéblock Case with three each of five colours of Pokéblock, and a Pokéblock Kit for making Pokéblocks from Berries. Contests are held in the Contest Hall.

The harbour

Mr. Briney's boat runs back to Dewford Town from the harbour. A sailor here wishes he could have gone under the sea with Captain Stern, but the sub is too small for him. Other ships leave from here later in your journey: the S.S. Tidal after you become Champion (see Part 20), and a ship to Southern Island if you ever hold an Eon Ticket.
