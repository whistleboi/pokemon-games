Route 121

Route 121 carries on east from Route 120 to Lilycove City. The Safari Zone's entrance is on this route, and Route 122 heads off south across the water toward Mt. Pyre. Shuppet are the most common Pokémon in the grass.

Once you have the Feather Badge, a group of grunts from Team Magma (or Team Aqua in Alpha Sapphire) comes hurrying along the route. They're racing to get to Mt. Pyre before the other team, and they talk about a treasure up on the mountain. A pilgrim tells you Mt. Pyre is where the spirits of Pokémon rest.

{{journal:hoenn_m15}} {{tables:HOENN_ROUTE_121}}

An HP Up, a Full Heal and a Max Revive are hidden on the route.

{{trainers:HOENN_ROUTE_121}}

Safari Zone gate

The gatehouse on Route 121 leads into the Safari Zone. The attendant explains the rules: each visit you get 30 Safari Balls and 500 steps, and inside you can only catch with Safari Balls.

Safari Zone

The Safari Zone is a big nature park full of Pokémon that are rare anywhere else in Hoenn. It's split into areas, and each one has a few Pokémon of its own: Pikachu and Girafarig, Xatu and Wobbuffet, Rhyhorn and Heracross, Donphan and Pinsir. Some areas are hard to reach without a Bike. Pokéblock feeders stand around the park, and wild Pokémon like to gather near them.

{{journal:hoenn_s5}} {{tables:HOENN_SAFARI_ZONE}}

Areas 5 and 6 are closed off by a worker: they're still being built, and they open once you've beaten the Champion. A Revive is hidden in the park, and the Absolite glints on the ground. The park is good for TMs too: Solar Beam, Wild Charge and Energy Ball lie on the ground, and a Street Thug hands you TM Dream Eater.

Tips from the visitors

The people in the park are full of advice. In the rest house, one says getting close to a Pokémon makes it easier to catch, but it might run. Another says a Pokémon that has eaten a Pokéblock is slower to run away, and a third says Pokémon of a certain nature are drawn to certain Pokéblocks in the feeders.

Lilycove City

Lilycove City sits where the land ends and the sea begins. It's a big seaside city, with a huge Department Store, a Contest Hall, an art museum, a seaside motel and a harbour. Route 124 leads out to sea, toward Mossdeep City.

The team is up to something here too. One of their grunts tells you to keep away from a cave in the cove at the edge of town, and their trained Wailmer are blocking the way out to sea. Sailors are stuck in port, and an old fisherman says someone has been digging the cave out bigger.

{{tables:LILYCOVE_CITY}}

A Heart Scale, a Poké Ball and a PP Up are hidden around town. The Glass Ornament and the Diploma come much later (see below).

Lilycove Department Store

Five floors and a rooftop of shopping. The Lottery Corner on 1F lets you draw once a day for a prize. On the other floors:

  • 2F: Repels and Escape Ropes, and a full range of Poké Balls, potions and status heals.
  • 3F: vitamins (HP Up, Protein, Iron, Calcium, Zinc, Carbos) and battle items such as X Attack and Dire Hit.
  • 4F: TMs, four for attacking and four for defending.
  • 5F: Pokéblocks of every colour.
  • Rooftop: Fresh Water, Soda Pop and Lemonade from a stand.

Contest Hall

Lilycove's Contest Hall is where Pokémon Contests are held, with stages for each kind of Contest. The fans in the lobby are busy blending Berries into Pokéblocks, and the people by the stages swap tips: match the kind of move to the kind of Contest.

{{journal:hoenn_s7}}

Pokéblock Master's house

A mother of four teaches you how Pokéblocks work. Each colour raises one condition: red for Cool, blue for Beauty, pink for Cute, green for Smart and yellow for Tough. Every Pokéblock also adds sheen, and once a Pokémon's sheen is full it won't eat any more. She gives you one Pokéblock of each colour to start you off.

Lilycove Museum

The museum's ground floor is full of paintings: dreamlike landscapes, Berries, and a legendary Pokémon painted from old stories. The curator is keeping the hall upstairs for portraits of Contest champions, so it stays shut until one of your Pokémon wins a Master Rank Contest. Win all five kinds at Master Rank and the hall is complete: the curator thanks you with a Glass Ornament.

Around town

Cove Lily Motel

The motel owner says hardly anyone stays since the team moved into the cove, though a big group calling themselves Game Freak has booked the upper floor. Upstairs, their game designer made the Hoenn Pokédex: fill it completely and they give you a Diploma. Scott is staying here too, checking out the Contests.

Houses

A sleepy man mumbles that sleep is the best medicine, then hands you TM Rest without quite waking up. The Move Deleter used to help Pokémon forget moves, but he's forgotten how. At the Pokémon Trainer Fan Club, the members argue every day about who the strongest Trainer is: become famous and they might start cheering for you. Another house has a man who says the sea's biggest mysteries lie on the seabed, and that there's a deep trench under the sea between Mossdeep and Sootopolis.

Out in town

A gentleman gives you a Berry once a day. A man gives you TM Sleep Talk. A man called the Collector is a fan of Lisia, whose partner is an Altaria: show him an Altaria in your party and he gives you the Altarianite. Two old sailors have heard of a tall pillar reaching to the sky somewhere out around Route 131.

Pokémon Center

Heal up here. A hiker has heard about scoundrels who steal Pokémon and break bits off meteorites.

Lilycove Harbour

Nothing sails from the harbour for now. The S.S. Tidal starts running after you become Champion, and the other ferries need special tickets (see Part 20).

Route 122

Route 122 is a short stretch of sea south of Route 121. You need Surf to cross it. Mt. Pyre rises out of the middle of the water, and Route 123 is on the far side.

{{tables:HOENN_ROUTE_122}}

Mt. Pyre

Mt. Pyre is a quiet mountain where people come to remember the Pokémon who have passed away. Inside, its building is a memorial hall of six floors, full of graves. Outside, a path climbs the mountainside to the summit. Ghost Pokémon such as Shuppet and Duskull drift around, and Chimecho turns up rarely.

{{tables:HOENN_MT_PYRE}}

The mountainside has many hidden items. Some floors have holes that drop you to the floor below, as a hiker on 2F found out the hard way.

{{journal:hoenn_s22}}

The memorial hall

On 1F, an old woman says all sorts of beings wander the slopes and gives you a Cleanse Tag: if your lead Pokémon holds it, wild Pokémon keep their distance. A mourner and a boy are visiting their Pokémon's graves. On 3F a keeper reminds you to cherish every Pokémon, and some Sea Incense lies by a grave. Right at the top, on 6F, TM Shadow Ball lies before the memorial, and the Medichamite glints nearby. Out on the mountainside you'll find TM Will-O-Wisp, a Dusk Stone and a Shiny Stone, and the Banettite glints at the summit.

The summit

Climb the mountainside to the summit. Team Magma's leader, Maxie (or Team Aqua's leader, Archie, in Alpha Sapphire), is already there, and he's taken the Red Orb (the Blue Orb in Alpha Sapphire) from the mountain. He says he no longer needs the Meteorite, only to reach the place where the ancient Pokémon sleeps. He leaves an admin to deal with you and goes.

{{trainers:HOENN_MT_PYRE}} {{battle:HOENN_MT_PYRE:Courtney|Courtney in Omega Ruby, Matt in Alpha Sapphire.}}

The old couple

With the team gone, talk to the elderly couple who guard the summit. Without the orb that was taken, Groudon (Kyogre in Alpha Sapphire) could run wild. They give you the other orb to keep safe: the Blue Orb in Omega Ruby, or the Red Orb in Alpha Sapphire. They say that one day the two orbs must be brought back together. The old man also gives you the Magma Emblem, which fell from one of the team's uniforms: it may open a way they keep hidden (see Part 20).

{{journal:hoenn_m16}}

The Black Fog

The Black Fog ours. Somewhere on Mt. Pyre, the air turns ice-cold and a black fog pours across the floor. A giant Haunter rises out of it: people once worshipped it as a god, and it feeds on dreams. It's always as strong as your strongest Pokémon, and it can't be caught. Beat it for 10 Poké Balls and 3 Rare Candies. It vanishes in a burst of black mist, whispering that it will rise again in another haunted place, and sometimes it gathers here again.

Route 123

Route 123 runs from the water around Mt. Pyre west to Route 118, so it joins this part of Hoenn back up with Mauville. It has the Berry Master's house and a lot of Berry patches.

{{tables:HOENN_ROUTE_123}}

A Super Repel, a Hyper Potion, a Revive and a PP Up are hidden on the route. The Abomasite appears only after the Cave of Origin (see Part 16).

{{trainers:HOENN_ROUTE_123}}

Cooltrainer Wendy is the toughest trainer on the route, and she gives you a Rare Candy when you win.

{{battle:HOENN_ROUTE_123:Wendy}}

People on the route

Chomper, the Poochyena guard dog from the 123 Go Fish shop, bounds over and sniffs your pockets. Give it a scratch behind the ears and it digs up a Mega Stone for you: the Gyaradosite. A fisherman who loves Magikarp gives you an Eviolite, for Pokémon that haven't evolved yet. A man who loves Grass Pokémon gives you TM Giga Drain if you have a Grass-type Pokémon with you. A Fairy Tale Girl gives you TM Dazzling Gleam, and a girl southwest of the pier gives you a Big Root. A Gracidea, a Wide Lens and a Revival Herb lie on the route too.

Berry Master's house

The Berry Master gives you two Berries each day, and his wife gives you one more of her own.

The team's hideout

Once you have the orb from Mt. Pyre, head back to Lilycove City. As you walk through town, your rival catches up with you: they've found out that the team is hiding in the cove at the edge of town, and they want one more battle before you go after them.

{{battle:LILYCOVE_CITY:rival}}

Win, and the grunt guarding the cave is gone. The cave in the cove is Team Magma's hideout (Team Aqua's in Alpha Sapphire), dug out of the rock, with water inside and three floors.

{{tables:HOENN_TEAM_HIDEOUT}}

Some of the Poké Balls lying on B1F are really Electrode in disguise (Lv 34). The real ones hold a Nugget and, in the leader's room, the Master Ball, which catches any wild Pokémon without fail. TM Dark Pulse lies on 1F, and the Sharpedonite glints on B2F.

{{trainers:HOENN_TEAM_HIDEOUT}}

B2F

The grunts say you're too late: the submarine has already left. On B2F, five grunts gang up on you at once, each with a Poochyena. Past them, the admin is waiting, and boasts that their boss is already on his way to the Seafloor Cavern, a cave under the sea.

{{battle:HOENN_TEAM_HIDEOUT:Courtney|Courtney in Omega Ruby, Matt in Alpha Sapphire.}}

Beat the admin and the hideout is cleared. The team's Wailmer leave the cove, so Lilycove's way out to sea is open: Surf east along Route 124 to Mossdeep City.
