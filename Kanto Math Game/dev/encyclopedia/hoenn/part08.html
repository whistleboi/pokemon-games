Route 117

Route 117 runs west from Mauville City to Verdanturf Town. It's a gentle, flowery road with a pond, a few trainers and the Pokémon Day Care. It isn't on the way to your next goal, but it's a short trip, and Wally has just gone home to Verdanturf.

{{tables:HOENN_ROUTE_117}}

Volbeat and Illumise are common in the grass here. A few items lie on the ground, and the Berry trees by the path give you a handful of Pinap Berries.

{{trainers:HOENN_ROUTE_117}}

Anna & Meg, a senior and junior pair, battle you together in a double battle.

Pokémon Day Care

The Day-Care Man waits outside the gate, and his wife looks after Pokémon inside. Ask inside to leave a Pokémon with them. A visitor says their Pokémon came back knowing a move they never taught it. Pokémon play in the yard: a Zigzagoon, a Kecleon, an Azumarill and a Pikachu.

Verdanturf Town

Verdanturf Town sits high on a windswept plateau. The wind keeps Mt. Chimney's ash away, so the air here is clean and sweet, and that's why Wally lives here with his uncle. Rusturf Tunnel, at the north of town, leads back to Rustboro City now that it's been dug through. The town also has a Contest Hall.

{{tables:VERDANTURF_TOWN}}

The Repel is hidden. The Mawilite glints on the ground in town.

Around town

Wanda, whose boyfriend dug Rusturf Tunnel to reach her, thanks you for your help and gives you the Gardevoirite, a Mega Stone. A little girl has lost her Shroomish: she thinks it ran off near the town sign. Check the sign, and the Shroomish turns up. Tell her and she gives you an odd stone she found, an Intriguing Stone.

Wally's uncle's house

Wally is home, training hard with Ralts, and he promises that one day he'll be strong enough to challenge you. His uncle says Wally's health has really improved out here. Wanda's mother and Wanda's boyfriend are here too, and he's happy he can see Wanda every day now the tunnel is open.

Houses

In one house, a man checks how much your Pokémon like you. In another, two people talk about the Pokémon League in faraway Ever Grande City, where you face the Elite Four one after another.

Pokémon Center and Poké Mart

A boy in the Center says some moves, like Rollout, get stronger the more you use them in a row. In the Mart, a lass recommends the Nest Ball for low-level Pokémon.

Route 111

Route 111 runs north from Mauville City. A little way north of town, cracked rocks block the road, so you need Rock Smash and the Dynamo Badge to go further. The middle of the route is a desert where a fierce sandstorm rages. You can't keep your eyes open in it without Go-Goggles, so for now go around: Route 112 branches off to the west, and the Fiery Path leads you past the desert to the route's north end, where Route 113 heads west to Fallarbor Town.

{{journal:hoenn_m7b}} {{tables:HOENN_ROUTE_111}}

Geodude hide in the cracked rocks. Several items are hidden around the route.

{{trainers:HOENN_ROUTE_111}}

The Winstrates' house

A family of four battle-lovers lives in a house near the south end of the route, before the cracked rocks. Talk to Victor and he challenges you to take on the whole family: four battles in a row, with no break to heal in between. After Victor, his wife Victoria steps up, then their daughter Vivi, then Grandma Vicky. They're in the Trainers table above.

Beat all four and Victoria hands you a Macho Brace, for raising strong Pokémon. A tired Trainer on the route admits the Winstrates flattened them.

Along the route

Gabby and Ty, a TV interviewer and a cameraman, want to film a battle with you. They fight together, in a double battle. Aarune, a wanderer from Unova who has roamed Hoenn for eleven years, tells you that odd trees, wall markings and bushes each hide a Secret Spot. His Secret Bases aren't ready yet, so he gives you TM292 Secret Power instead.

A Bug Maniac gives you some Safety Goggles, and a girl a Razz Berry. A man's uncle in Mauville told him to bring Rock Smash out here, and he forgot, and a ruin seeker says a tower of sand appears around here some days and not others (see Part 20).

Old lady's rest stop

A kind old lady lives in the little house on the route. Talk to her and she lets your Pokémon rest, healing them completely.

Route 112

Route 112 lies at the foot of Mt. Chimney, the smoking volcano. The Mt. Chimney Cable Car station is here, a short walk up the steps, and Lavaridge Town is to the west, up some ledges you can't climb from here. The Fiery Path tunnel cuts through the mountain from the lower part of the route to the upper part.

{{tables:HOENN_ROUTE_112}}

Numel and Machop live in the grass. A few items lie on the ground, and two kinds of Berry grow here.

{{trainers:HOENN_ROUTE_112}}

Your rival's gift

As you come onto the route, your rival runs over, points out Mt. Chimney, and explains the cable car is blocked, so you'll both have to take the Fiery Path. Boulders block its side tunnels, so your rival gives you HM Strength. You need the Heat Badge before your Pokémon will use it on the map. Your rival heals your team and heads off to Fallarbor Town to meet Professor Cozmo, a friend of Professor Birch's.

{{journal:hoenn_s13}}

The Cable Car

Grunts of the team are guarding the Cable Car station. They tell you it's closed, and one wonders aloud whether their leader really needs a Meteorite to wake something up. You can't ride it yet; come back once the trouble in Fallarbor is sorted out (Part 9).

A hiker by the ledges would like to get to Mauville, but once he hops down there's no climbing back up to Lavaridge.

Fiery Path

The Fiery Path is a hot, smoky tunnel through the side of Mt. Chimney. Walk through it from the lower part of Route 112 to the upper part. Boulders block its side tunnels: come back with Strength and the Heat Badge to push them aside.

{{tables:HOENN_FIERY_PATH}}

The Charizardite X, a Mega Stone, glints on the ground. TM175 Toxic and a Fire Stone also lie in the tunnel.

{{trainers:HOENN_FIERY_PATH}}

Route 113

Route 113 runs west from Route 111 to Fallarbor Town. Ash from Mt. Chimney piles up in the grass here, and you can see every footprint you leave behind.

{{tables:HOENN_ROUTE_113}}

Spinda are everywhere in the ashy grass, and Skarmory turn up now and then. Two items are hidden.

{{trainers:HOENN_ROUTE_113}}

A rest in the ash

Soon after you set off along it, you find your rival taking a break: walking through all this ash is tiring. Your rival heals your team and explains that Professor Cozmo, in Fallarbor, studies Meteorites, and is working with Professor Birch on how they affect Pokémon. Then it's off to Fallarbor.

Glass Workshop

The Glass Workshop turns volcanic ash into glass. Its owner sells glass flutes: Blue, Yellow, Red, Black and White. A man on the route can't get over how ash can be made into glass.

Fallarbor Town

Fallarbor Town is a quiet farming town of small gardens, at the west end of Route 113. Route 114 leads on to the west. The town has a Pokémon Center, a Poké Mart, a Contest Hall, Professor Cozmo's house and the Move Reminder's house.

{{tables:FALLARBOR_TOWN}}

Trouble at Professor Cozmo's

As you walk into town, your rival calls you over: something has happened at Professor Cozmo's house. His wife tells you that some strange people took her husband away, Meteorite and all, and said they were going to Meteor Falls. Your rival suggests meeting up there. Meteor Falls is along Route 114 (Part 9).

Around Fallarbor

A Bug Maniac gives you some Honey. A meteor fan says a meteor carved out Meteor Falls long ago, and a girl shows off her squishy Azurill. Someone else asks whether you've battled Flannery at the Lavaridge Gym.

Pokémon Center

Lanette is here. She looks after the Pokémon Storage System that you use on the PC, and hopes it's working well for you.

Move Reminder's house

The Move Reminder has a sad tale: these days every Pokémon remembers every move it has ever learned, so you can just pick them in the Summary. It's put him right out of business ours.
