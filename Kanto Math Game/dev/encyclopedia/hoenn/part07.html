Route 110

Route 110 runs north from Slateport City to Mauville City. It has two roads, one above the other. Up top is the Seaside Cycling Road, a long bridge for bikes only. Underneath runs the low road, the one you can walk, with tall grass and plenty of trainers. On the way you'll pass the Trick Master's house, and the west side of the route leads to Route 103 across the water, once you can Surf.

{{journal:hoenn_m7a}} {{tables:HOENN_ROUTE_110}}

Plusle and Minun both live in the grass here. Wild Pokémon sometimes come as a Horde of five at once. The Manectite, a Mega Stone, glints on the ground; the other items are hidden. A few Berry trees grow along the low road too.

{{trainers:HOENN_ROUTE_110}}

Rival battle

On the low road, your rival spots you and rushes over. It feels like ages since you last met, and your rival wants to see how strong you've got.

{{battle:HOENN_ROUTE_110:rival|After you've delivered the Devon Parts, before your third badge.}}

Afterwards your rival heals your team, says Mauville City is just up ahead, and heads off.

Along the low road

Since the Museum, a handful of Grunts have been loitering on the route. They won't battle you; they just tell you to scram and keep their plans secret. An old man muses that one road is for people and the other for Pokémon, a camper can't decide which road to take, and a lady without a Bike is happy to stroll the low road.

Seaside Cycling Road

The Cycling Road has a gate at each end of the route, and a judge invites you to ride from Slateport to Mauville as fast as you can without bumping into anyone. You can't go up without a Bicycle, though. Rydel in Mauville City gives you one, so come back and ride it later. Up on the bridge, riders show off long hair and Bike tricks, and one spots Rydel's name on your frame.

Mauville City

Mauville City is the crossroads of Hoenn, the bright and shiny city of fun. Roads leave it in all four directions: Route 110 south to Slateport, Route 117 west toward Verdanturf Town, Route 118 east, and Route 111 north. The town has a Pokémon Center, a Poké Mart, Rydel's Cycles, a Game Corner, a tall apartment block called Mauville Hills, and Wattson's Gym.

{{journal:hoenn_m7}} {{tables:MAUVILLE_CITY}}

Three items are hidden around town. The Reveal Glass only comes out for a Trainer travelling with Tornadus, Thundurus or Landorus.

Wally's challenge

As you arrive, you find Wally standing at the Gym's door with his uncle. Wally has been training with Ralts every day, and before he challenges the Gym he wants to battle you, to see how strong they've become.

{{journal:hoenn_m7w}}
Wally. Ralts Lv 17. Rewards: five Poké Balls, and HM Rock Smash from his uncle.

Wally is amazed by you and promises to keep getting stronger. His uncle thanks you for looking after Wally and gives you HM Rock Smash. It breaks cracked rocks, but you'll need the Dynamo Badge before your Pokémon will use it on the map. Wally heads home to Verdanturf Town, and the Gym is open.

Rydel's Cycles

Rydel is delighted that you've come all the way from Littleroot Town, and gives you a Bicycle for free. All he asks is that you ride it everywhere and tell people about his shop. Handbooks at the back explain that bikes are fastest on long, straight roads, and that some slopes can only be crossed on a bike.

Around Mauville

A man in a suit wants a stylish Trainer to have a Mega Stone, and gives you the Lopunnite. A girl gives you TM256 Round, an old man a Nugget, and a young delinquent a Poké Toy. A shopper tells you Rydel is a very generous man.

Rock Smash Dude's house

The man here calls himself the Rock Smash Guy, though he'd rather be called the Rock Smash Dude. He explains that a Pokémon that knows Rock Smash breaks cracked rocks to bits.

Mirror shop

The owner sells mirrors of every shape. One of them is said to show the true form of the Pokémon that command the wind, the thunder and the land. Bring one of them along one day and the owner gives you the Reveal Glass.

Mauville Game Corner

The Game Corner has slot machines and roulette tables. You need a Coin Case and Coins to play, and the counter swaps your Coins for prizes. The regulars share tips: you can bet up to three Coins on the slots, and stopping a reel right on 7 is hard but pays well.

Mauville Hills

Mauville Hills is a smart block of flats by the Mart, and Wattson lives here. Someone has left TM060 U-turn out on the balcony. A jumpy collector has spent all the rent money on a collection, and hides from the man who keeps asking for it.

Two move tutors live here too. One teaches Pledge moves: Grass Pledge, Fire Pledge and Water Pledge. The other teaches the ultimate moves, Frenzy Plant, Blast Burn and Hydro Cannon, but only to a fully evolved first partner that loves its Trainer. Like every tutor in this game, they sell their moves as TMs, so a move stays unlocked for good ours.

Much later. After the Rain Badge, Wattson meets you in the square: the light on the tower is on, and something is up in New Mauville. See Part 20.

Pokémon Center and Poké Mart

Heal and stock up before the Gym. An old man in the Mart explains X items, which raise a stat for one battle.

Mauville Gym

Wattson trains Electric Pokémon. He's only taking challengers with two Hoenn badges, and you have to battle Wally first. His Gym is a maze of electric barriers. Step on a switch and every barrier flips: the open ones close and the closed ones open, so plan your route.

{{trainers:MAUVILLE_CITY:Mauville Gym}} {{battle:MAUVILLE_CITY:Wattson}}

Beat Wattson for the Dynamo Badge, plus TM048 Volt Switch. With the Dynamo Badge, your Pokémon can use Rock Smash outside battle, which opens the way north onto Route 111.

Trick House

The Trick Master's house stands beside Route 110. It belongs to the Trick Master, a man who loves building puzzles and loves hiding even more. This whole house is from Emerald. Each time, find him hiding somewhere in the front room, then go through the hole behind the scroll on the wall to his newest puzzle. Every puzzle room has a scroll with a secret code: read it, and use the code to open the locked door at the end. The Trick Master waits on the other side with a prize.

{{tables:HOENN_TRICK_HOUSE}}

The first time, he's hiding under the desk. He gives you an Escape Rope in case you get lost. After each puzzle, look for him again in a new hiding place: in the potted tree, the dresser's drawer, behind the round window, in the cupboard, and at last under a lumpy cushion.

Field moves. The puzzles use the moves you collect on your journey: the first is full of small trees to Cut, the second has cracked rocks for Rock Smash (after the Dynamo Badge), and the fourth has boulders for Strength (after the Heat Badge). From the second puzzle on, Mechadoll 3 sells Escape Ropes in the puzzle rooms.

Trick House: the first puzzle

A maze of little trees that the Trick Master stayed up all night planting.

Trick House: the second puzzle

A maze of cracked rocks to smash through.

Trick House: the third puzzle

This one is pitch dark, so feel your way through.

Find him in the dark and he gives you TM161 Trick Room. The Ace Duo Pike & Shiel battle you together.

Trick House: the fourth puzzle

Boulders everywhere: push each one just right with Strength.

{{tables:HOENN_TRICK_HOUSE_4}}

His prize is a Smoke Ball.

Trick House: the fifth puzzle

Five Mechadolls stand in your way, and each one asks a quiz question about your journey so far. Get it right and it lets you through; get it wrong and it asks again. Mechadoll 3 also gives you an Escape Rope for a correct answer.

{{tables:HOENN_TRICK_HOUSE_5}}

Answer them all and the Trick Master gives you a Magnet.

Trick House: the last puzzle

The Trick Master's greatest puzzle is a maze of doors. Step on a switch and the doors swap: the shut ones open and the open ones shut.

{{tables:HOENN_TRICK_HOUSE_6}}

Beat it and he admits he has no tricks left. He gives you a Rare Candy, his very last reward, and sets off on a journey to dream up new ones.
