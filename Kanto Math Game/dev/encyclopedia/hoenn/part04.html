Rustboro City

Rustboro City is a big stone city at the top of Route 104, home of the Devon Corporation, the Trainers' School and Hoenn's first Gym. The sign calls it the city probing the integration of nature and science. Route 116 leaves to the east, and Route 115 to the north (that one comes much later, see Part 9).

{{tables:RUSTBORO_CITY}}

A Great Ball and an X Defense lie on the ground in town. Around the streets, a businessman gives you TM057 False Swipe, an Ace Trainer a Float Stone, and a boy a Premier Ball.

Your rival in town

As you walk up the main street, your rival runs over, amazed at all the tall buildings, and says they're off to take on the Gym.

Trainers' School

The teacher welcomes you and gives you a Quick Claw: a Pokémon holding it sometimes gets to move first. The blackboard lists the five status problems (Poison, Paralysis, Sleep, Burn and Freeze), each of which stays until it's healed. One student explains that a confused Pokémon snaps out of it when you switch it out, and another that a Pokémon holding a Berry eats it just when it needs it.

Cutter's house

The Cutter can tell you're a skilled Trainer and gives you HM Cut. A Pokémon that knows Cut can clear thin trees, but only once you have the Stone Badge. His daughter remembers his Pokémon cutting the land for Rustboro's new houses.

Around Rustboro

In one house, Trader Ken would trade his Makuhita, Makit, for a Slakoth (you can find one in Petalburg Woods). Makit comes holding an X Attack. Elsewhere, Pekachu the Pikachu lives with an owner who's very proud of that nickname, and the Devon flats are home to the company's workers. A woman in the street warns that Fire Pokémon struggle at the Gym, since its Leader uses Rock types.

Pokémon Center and Poké Mart

In the Center, a Battle Girl says the man next door gave her an HM: that's the Cutter. In the Mart, a bug catcher is buying an Escape Rope in case he gets lost in a cave.

Devon Corporation

Devon makes items and medicine, and the Poké Balls and PokéNav in the glass case downstairs. It started out digging stones from the ground. On 2F, its researchers are working on new Poké Balls, a machine for talking to Pokémon and a screen for Pokémon dreams, and the Devon Fossil Lab brings fossils back to life: bring it any you find. The President, Mr. Stone, has his office on 3F, and his secretary mentions Captain Stern at the Slateport Shipyard.

If you ever find an Intriguing Stone, Mr. Stone will trade you a Pidgeotite for it.

Rustboro Gym

Roxanne is the Rock-type Leader, and a Roxanne fan in town says she knows everything about Pokémon. The Gym Guide says Water and Grass moves will serve you well. Three trainers stand between you and her.

Beat her for the Stone Badge and TM036 Rock Tomb. With the Stone Badge, your Pokémon can use Cut outside battle.

Thief!

Once you have the Stone Badge, a grunt of Team Magma (or Team Aqua in Alpha Sapphire) dashes past you through town, with a Devon researcher chasing after him and shouting to give back the parts.

Go into the Devon Corporation and up to the President's office on 3F. The researcher runs up to you, out of breath: the grunt stole the Devon Parts and ran east toward Rusturf Tunnel on Route 116. He asks you to get them back.

Route 116

Route 116 runs east from Rustboro City to Rusturf Tunnel. It's grassy and full of trainers, and the Tunneler's Rest House stands near the tunnel's mouth. The sign by the tunnel says it will link Rustboro and Verdanturf.

{{tables:HOENN_ROUTE_116}}

Items lie on the ground along the route: a Repel, a Potion, an X Sp. Atk, an Ether, six Chesto Berries and an HP Up. A Devon researcher gives you a Repeat Ball. A short-sighted man has lost his glasses somewhere around here, and the Black Glasses are hidden on the ground nearby.

{{trainers:HOENN_ROUTE_116}}

Mr. Briney

Old Mr. Briney is at his wits' end near the tunnel: a thug grabbed his Wingull, Peeko, and ran into Rusturf Tunnel. He begs you to help her.

{{journal:hoenn_s1}}

Tunneler's Rest House

The tunnel workers explain that they started boring through with big machines, but the noise upset the wild Pokémon, so they stopped. Without the tunnel, getting to Verdanturf Town means sailing to Dewford, then Slateport, then walking through Mauville. Now one man is digging through by hand so the Pokémon aren't disturbed.

Rusturf Tunnel

Rusturf Tunnel is a short, unfinished tunnel east of Route 116. Whismur are the only wild Pokémon in its passages. Wanda waits inside: her boyfriend is digging through the rock from the Verdanturf side to see her.

{{tables:HOENN_RUSTURF_TUNNEL}}

A Poké Ball and a Max Ether lie on the ground, and the Dire Hit is hidden. The Aggronite only comes after Peeko is safe (see below).

{{trainers:HOENN_RUSTURF_TUNNEL}}

Peeko and the Devon Parts

The grunt is in the tunnel with Peeko, complaining that the bird is no use as a hostage. When he sees you followed him, he battles you.

{{battle:HOENN_RUSTURF_TUNNEL:Grunt|Inside the tunnel, in a story scene.}}

The grunt gives back the Devon Parts and Peeko too, and Peeko flies home. Mr. Briney comes running in after her. He thanks you and introduces himself properly: if you ever need to cross the sea, come to his cottage by Petalburg Woods.

With the tunnel quiet again, Wanda's boyfriend can dig in peace. He's found a Mega Stone in the rock, the Aggronite, and gives it to you.

Rustboro City (again)

Mr. Stone

Back in Rustboro, the Devon researcher is overjoyed to have the parts back. He gives you a Great Ball and tells you the President wants to thank you himself.

Up on 3F, Mr. Stone thanks you for getting the Devon Parts back, then asks two favours: deliver a Letter to his son Steven in Dewford, and the Devon Parts to Captain Stern. Mr. Briney's boat can take you.

{{journal:hoenn_m4b}}

Go back down Route 104 and through Petalburg Woods to Mr. Briney's cottage. With the Stone Badge and Mr. Stone's errand, Mr. Briney's boat at the end of the pier will take you across the sea to Dewford Town (see Part 5).
