Route 114

Route 114 runs west from Fallarbor Town to Meteor Falls. It's a rugged road of grass, rocks and a river, with a couple of houses along the way: the Fossil Maniac's house and Lanette's house. At the far end, the cave mouth of Meteor Falls waits.

{{journal:hoenn_m7c}} {{tables:HOENN_ROUTE_114}}

Zangoose and Seviper both live in the grass here, and Swablu too. Geodude hide in the cracked rocks. Four items are hidden, a Comet Shard and a Rare Bone among them.

{{trainers:HOENN_ROUTE_114}}

Hurry to Meteor Falls

Along the route, your rival catches up with you: the team was seen taking Professor Cozmo into Meteor Falls. Your rival dashes on ahead and tells you to follow.

Along the route

An old man walking his Poochyena gives you TM172 Roar, and a man nearby sighs that his Pokémon's Roar scares everyone away. A rich boy gives you a Berry, and has another for you each day. The Berry trees southeast of Lanette's house give you six Aspear Berries.

Fossil Maniac's house

The Fossil Maniac's little brother says his big brother digs for fossils in the cave out back, and gives you TM055 Dig. Out back, the Fossil Maniac himself is digging away. He has no fossils for you, but suggests trying the sandstorm on Route 111.

Much later. After you become Champion, the Fossil Maniac's tunnel leads on to the Desert Underpass (see Part 20).

Lanette's house

Lanette, who looks after the Pokémon Storage System, lives here. She apologises for the mess and tells you she's made the Boxes hold far more Pokémon than before.

Meteor Falls

Meteor Falls is a deep cave of waterfalls. People say a falling meteor carved it out long ago. Its ground floor links Route 114 with Route 115, which leads south to Rustboro City. Somewhere inside, the team is holding Professor Cozmo.

{{tables:HOENN_METEOR_FALLS}}

Lunatone and Solrock both live here, and rare Bagon too. The Aerodactylite glints on the ground on Floor 1. Many more items lie around the falls: a Moon Stone, a Star Piece, TM078 Dragon Claw and a PP Max among them.

Saving Professor Cozmo

Inside, you find the team's Grunts with Professor Cozmo, who begs you to stop them taking his Meteorite. Your rival catches up, heals your team, and wants to take them on with you.

{{trainers:HOENN_METEOR_FALLS:Floor 1}}

Their leader here is an Admin: Tabitha of Team Magma (or Shelly of Team Aqua in Alpha Sapphire).

{{battle:HOENN_METEOR_FALLS:Tabitha}}

With the Admin beaten, Professor Cozmo is free. After the battle, you also run into someone unexpected: Archie, the leader of Team Aqua (or Maxie of Team Magma in Alpha Sapphire), the other team. He tells you the team you just beat made off with the Meteorite and is heading for Mt. Chimney. He leads a team of his own, and warns you to stay out of his plans. Your rival offers to take the Professor home to Fallarbor.

Deeper in the falls

The cave goes on, down and up through more floors. The trainers deeper inside are far stronger than anything you've met so far, so this part is best left for later in your journey.

{{trainers:HOENN_METEOR_FALLS:Floor 2}}

On Floor 2, an old woman gives you a stone her granddaughter loves: the Salamencite.

Much later. After the Delta Episode, the Millennium Comet passes over Hoenn, and its light shines into the deepest part of the falls. Professor Cozmo will tell you more.

Route 115

Route 115 runs south from Meteor Falls to Rustboro City, along the coast. It's a quick way back to Rustboro, and a good place to train on the way.

{{tables:HOENN_ROUTE_115}}

Jigglypuff and Swablu live in the grass. Several kinds of Berry grow along the route, and the Heart Scale is hidden.

{{trainers:HOENN_ROUTE_115}}

A hiker warns that exploring a cave isn't like walking a road: you never know when wild Pokémon will pop out.

Fallarbor Town (again)

Back in Fallarbor, Professor Cozmo is safe at home.

{{journal:hoenn_s14}}

He thanks you for saving him at Meteor Falls, and tells you the team took his Meteorite up Mt. Chimney: the Cable Car on Route 112 goes to the top. As thanks, he gives you TM179 Smack Down. He also hands you a strange stone he says isn't a meteorite at all: it's the Galladite, a Mega Stone.

Outside, your rival is heading back to Mauville City and offers to take you along. Say yes to travel there together, or say you'll walk if you'd rather explore Meteor Falls and Route 115 on the way.
