Route 127

Route 127 is open sea running south from Mossdeep City. It joins Route 128, where Steven told you to dive, and Route 126. Swimmers aren't the only trainers out here: divers, Ace Trainers, Bird Keepers, Fishermen and a Black Belt are waiting too. With Dive, you can go down to the seabed in places.

{{tables:HOENN_ROUTE_127}}

The Heracronite glints on the route. Many items are hidden here, among them three Plates: the Spooky, Dread and Insect Plates.

{{trainers:HOENN_ROUTE_127}}

Route 128

Route 128 is the sea past Route 127. Far below its waves lies the Seafloor Cavern, where the pillar of light burst out. A grunt from Team Magma (or Team Aqua in Alpha Sapphire) patrols the water here. To reach the cavern, use Dive: you need HM Dive from Steven and the Mind Badge.

{{journal:hoenn_m19}} {{tables:HOENN_ROUTE_128}}

Luvdisc are common on a Good or Super Rod, and Corsola bites now and then. Many items are hidden here, among them the Stone, Draco and Pixie Plates and an Adamant Orb.

{{trainers:HOENN_ROUTE_128}}

The way to Ever Grande City also lies past Route 128, but it needs Waterfall, which comes much later (see Part 17).

Seafloor Cavern

The Seafloor Cavern is a cave deep under the sea, sealed since ancient times until the team broke in. Dive down from Route 128 and surface inside it. A grunt on lookout is amazed you got down here without a submarine.

The cavern is a long chain of rooms, eight of them before the deepest room. Golbat swarm the dry rock, and Tentacruel live in the water. Rocks can be broken with Rock Smash, and Graveler sometimes hide inside them. Grunts wait in several rooms, all sure their boss is too far ahead for you to catch.

{{tables:HOENN_SEAFLOOR_CAVERN}}

TM Scald lies in Room 6, washed up past the currents, and TM Earthquake lies in the deepest room. A Heart Scale and an Escape Rope are hidden at the entrance.

{{trainers:HOENN_SEAFLOOR_CAVERN}}

The deepest room

In the deepest room, two grunts cheer their boss on. Beyond them, Groudon (Kyogre in Alpha Sapphire) lies sleeping, and Maxie (Archie in Alpha Sapphire) stands before it with the Red Orb (the Blue Orb). He says you're too late: the orb will wake it, and the team will expand the land (the sea), with nothing to stop them.

{{battle:HOENN_SEAFLOOR_CAVERN:Maxie|His last Pokémon is Camerupt in Omega Ruby, Sharpedo in Alpha Sapphire.}}

Even beaten, he won't stop. He gives you a Master Ball with his prize money, then holds up the orb, and it begins to shine.

The awakening

Groudon (Kyogre) wakes and goes through Primal Reversion. With a roar, Primal Groudon (Primal Kyogre) bursts out of the cavern, up through the sea, and flies toward Sootopolis City. Its weather spreads over all of Hoenn: in Omega Ruby the sunlight turns extremely harsh, and in Alpha Sapphire a heavy rain pours down everywhere.

Hoenn's weather. Until you calm the legend in the Cave of Origin, that harsh sun or heavy rain covers the whole region.

Route 128 (again)

Surface on Route 128 and the team's leader is waiting, shaken. He admits the legend's power is far beyond anything he imagined, and that he was wrong. The leader of the other team, Archie (Maxie in Alpha Sapphire), is here too: for once, the two of them agree to work together and stop it. They say it's heading for the Cave of Origin in Sootopolis City.

Steven arrives as well. He saw the weather over Sootopolis go wild from the sky, and he's going there now. He asks you to come as soon as you can. Sootopolis is reached from Route 126 (see Part 16).

Route 126

Route 126 is the sea beyond Route 127, and the way to Sootopolis City. Its seabed is full of hidden treasure: dive down with Dive to search it.

{{tables:HOENN_ROUTE_126}}

A green Shard lies on the surface. The seabed's hidden items include a Mind Plate, a Big Pearl, and a blue and a yellow Shard for the Treasure Hunter on Route 124.

{{trainers:HOENN_ROUTE_126}}

Sootopolis City, and the Cave of Origin inside it, come next (see Part 16).
