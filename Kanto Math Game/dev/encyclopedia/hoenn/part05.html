Dewford Town

Dewford Town is a tiny island town in the blue sea, south of Route 104. Mr. Briney's boat ties up at its pier. The town has a Pokémon Center, Dewford Hall and a Gym, but no Poké Mart, so stock up before you sail. Route 106 leads north from town, along the beach to Granite Cave, and Route 107 lies out to sea in the east, for when you can Surf.

{{journal:hoenn_m5}} {{tables:DEWFORD_TOWN}}

The water around the island can be fished with the Old Rod you get here. A rod never hooks anything below its own level: Old Rod Lv 5, Good Rod Lv 10, Super Rod Lv 15.

The fishing spot

A fisherman by the water says Dewford is a famous fishing spot and gives you his spare Old Rod. Cast it anywhere there's water.

Dewford's people love whatever is trendy: an islander says anything new catches on here right away, and a trendy boy wants to know if you've heard the latest saying. A Pokéfan in the Pokémon Center has heard of a big cave at the edge of town: that's Granite Cave, where Steven is.

Dewford Hall

Everyone in the Hall is talking about what's in fashion. The trendiest move of all is Sludge Bomb, says a collector, but he only shares TM148 Sludge Bomb with Trainers who have five badges, so come back after Petalburg Gym.

Houses

In the house by the pier, a Brawly fan is sure you came to battle him, and a stylish Ace Trainer shows off a Silk Scarf, sees you like it, and gives it to you: a Pokémon holding it hits harder with Normal moves. In the other house, an island couple are happy with their slow island life and their Zigzagoon.

Dewford Gym

Brawly trains Fighting Pokémon, and his Gym is pitch dark. Each of his trainers you beat makes it a little brighter, and they can see you better than you can see them. Brawly only accepts challengers who already hold a Hoenn badge.

{{trainers:DEWFORD_TOWN:Dewford Gym}} {{battle:DEWFORD_TOWN:Brawly}}

Beat him for the Knuckle Badge, TM064 Bulk Up and three Rare Candies.

You can take on Brawly before or after Granite Cave; the Journal asks for both.

Route 106

Route 106 is a sea route north-west of Dewford Town. From the north side of town, a long sandy beach runs along its south shore to the mouth of Granite Cave. The open water to the west leads to Route 105, but you need Surf for that.

{{tables:HOENN_ROUTE_106}}

A Protein lies on the ground, and a Heart Scale, Stardust and a Poké Ball are hidden. The route's wild Pokémon and most of its trainers are at much higher levels, and most of the trainers are out on the water: they're for when you come back with Surf.

{{trainers:HOENN_ROUTE_106}}

Watch out on the sand near the cave. A grunt of Team Magma (or Team Aqua in Alpha Sapphire) stands on the beach, and his Pokémon are in the mid-20s, far stronger than anything you've met so far.

Granite Cave

Granite Cave is a deep cave at the end of Route 106's beach, with an entrance floor, two basements and a deepest room. Zubat are everywhere, Makuhita and Aron are common, and Abra, Sableye and Mawile hide here too. Steven is somewhere in the deepest room.

{{tables:HOENN_GRANITE_CAVE}}

Some of the cave's items lie in the open: a Repel, a Rare Candy and TM061 Shadow Claw on B2F, and an Escape Rope and a Poké Ball in the deepest room. The rest are hidden. The Steelixite is hidden on 1F. Geodude and Nosepass hide in the breakable rocks, for when you can use Rock Smash.

{{trainers:HOENN_GRANITE_CAVE}}

1F

Just inside, a hiker warns you that it gets awfully dark further in, and gives you HM Flash to light the way. A fellow called Steven went through a while ago, he says.

B1F and B2F

The basements wind on deeper. Check B2F's north side for the Repel and the Rare Candy, and look for TM061 Shadow Claw on the same floor.

The deepest room

In the deepest room, a man is studying the old cave walls. It's Steven, Mr. Stone's son. He thanks you for bringing his father's letter all this way, introduces himself, and tells you he's fond of rare stones. Mr. Briney can take you on to Slateport, he says.

Talk to him again before you go and he gives you TM239 Steel Wing to thank you.

Later visit. After you become Champion, Zinnia waits deep in Granite Cave for a battle (see Part 18).

Dewford Town (again)

With the letter delivered, Mr. Briney's boat at the pier can take you on to Slateport City, where Captain Stern is waiting for the Devon Parts (see Part 6). It can still take you back to Route 104 whenever you like.

Route 105

Route 105 is open sea between the southern end of Route 104 and Route 106. You can't come here until you have Surf, from Wally's father in Petalburg City after the Balance Badge. It's a wide stretch of water with a few sandbars, full of swimmers in their mid-20s.

{{tables:HOENN_ROUTE_105}}

An Iron lies on a sandbar in the south, and a Big Pearl and a Heart Scale are hidden.

{{trainers:HOENN_ROUTE_105}}

A cave on the route, the Island Cave, stays sealed until much later in the story (see Part 19).
