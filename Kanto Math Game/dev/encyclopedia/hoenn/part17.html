Ever Grande City

Ever Grande City lies at the east end of Route 128, the paradise of flowers, the sea and Pokémon. It's the home of Hoenn's Pokémon League. The city has a Pokémon Center, and the League lies beyond Victory Road.

{{tables:EVER_GRANDE_CITY}}

A Mewtwonite Y glints on the ground in town, and TM120 Psychic lies nearby.

Pokémon Center

Scott is here. He always knew you'd make it to Ever Grande, and he tells you to go show the Elite Four what you're made of. A tired trainer advises resting before Victory Road, because it's long and dark.

{{journal:hoenn_m22}}

Victory Road

Victory Road is the last cave before the League, with three floors: 1F, B1F and B2F. The wild Pokémon are strong, and the trainers are some of the best in Hoenn. Keep some healing items handy. On 1F you can pick up a Max Elixir, TM105 X-Scissor, a Full Heal, TM125 Flamethrower, a Full Restore and a PP Up, and four more items are hidden there: a Max Repel, an Elixir, an Ultra Ball and an Iron.

{{tables:HOENN_VICTORY_ROAD}} {{trainers:HOENN_VICTORY_ROAD}}

Wally

On 1F, Wally is waiting. He has come all this way with his Pokémon, and he wants one more battle before the League. His Gallade holds its Mega Stone and Mega Evolves. Ice moves are good against his Altaria; Fire, Flying and Psychic hit Roselia; and Ground moves hit Magneton hard. Against Mega Gallade, use Flying, Ghost or Fairy moves.

{{battle:HOENN_VICTORY_ROAD:Wally}}

The League's guards only let you in once you've beaten Wally here, with all eight Hoenn badges.

{{journal:hoenn_m23}}

The Pokémon League

Past Victory Road, the Pokémon League building waits in Ever Grande City. Inside there's a counter to heal your team. The guards remind you to heal before you go on: the Elite Four battle you one after another. Each room's far door only opens when you've won its battle.

Sidney

Sidney likes the look in your eye and promises a battle you'll both remember. He uses Dark Pokémon: Fighting, Bug and Fairy moves work well.

{{battle:EVER_GRANDE_CITY:Sidney}}

Phoebe

Phoebe trained with the spirits on Mt. Pyre, and she fights with Ghost Pokémon. Ghost and Dark moves are good choices. Her Sableye is part Dark, so it has only one weakness: Fairy moves.

{{battle:EVER_GRANDE_CITY:Phoebe}}

Glacia

Glacia came all the way to Hoenn looking for a real challenge. Her team is Ice types, so bring Fire, Fighting, Rock or Steel moves.

{{battle:EVER_GRANDE_CITY:Glacia}}

Drake

Drake has sailed the roughest seas with his Dragon Pokémon. Ice moves are the strongest choice against most of them, and Dragon and Fairy moves work too. Ice hits his Altaria, Flygon and Salamence four times as hard. His Kingdra is part Water, so use Dragon or Fairy moves on it.

{{battle:EVER_GRANDE_CITY:Drake}}

The Champion: Steven

In the last room, Steven is waiting. He's the Champion of Hoenn, and he knew you'd make it this far. His team is Rock and Steel, with a Mega Metagross at the end. Ground and Water moves hit most of his team hard, and Fire works on Skarmory and Metagross.

{{battle:EVER_GRANDE_CITY:Steven}}

When you win, Professor Birch rushes in. He can hardly believe the kid who just moved to Littleroot is the new Champion. Steven asks you to follow him.

Hall of Fame

Steven leads you into the Hall of Fame, where every Champion of the Hoenn League is honoured. The team that made you Champion is recorded there too. Then you head home to Littleroot Town to share your victory.

You're Hoenn's Champion now. Part 18 tells you what opens up, and the Delta Episode begins.
