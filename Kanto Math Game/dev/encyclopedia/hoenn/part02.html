Oldale Town

Oldale Town sits at the top of Route 101, a small crossroads town with Hoenn's first Pokémon Center and Poké Mart. Route 103 leads north, and Route 102 leads west toward Petalburg City. The sign says it's where things start off scarce.

{{journal:hoenn_s11}} {{tables:OLDALE_TOWN}}

Free sample

As you walk into town, a clerk from the Poké Mart hurries over. The Mart is giving away free samples today, and the clerk hands you ten Potions to heal your Pokémon in a pinch.

The road west

The way west to Route 102 is blocked for now. A boy is drawing footprints right in the middle of the path, and he won't let you walk over his sketch until you've seen Professor Birch. North to Route 103 is open.

Pokémon Center

Heal your team for free at the counter, and use the PC in the corner whenever you like. A Battle Girl says the upstairs is where Trainers link up to battle and trade, but upstairs every corner is closed for now.

Poké Mart

The first Mart on your way. A little boy reminds you to take fainted Pokémon to a Pokémon Center, and an unlucky shopper grumbles that the Poké Balls are sold out today.

Route 103

Route 103 runs north from Oldale Town to the coast. The west side is grass and paths; a wide stretch of water cuts it off from the east side, which leads on to Route 110 and is out of reach until you can Surf.

{{tables:HOENN_ROUTE_103}}

Four Leppa Berries, four Chesto Berries and a Guard Spec. lie on the ground here. The water can be fished once you have a rod and surfed much later. A rod never hooks anything below its own level: Old Rod Lv 5, Good Rod Lv 10, Super Rod Lv 15.

{{trainers:HOENN_ROUTE_103}}

The trainers on the east side, across the water, are much stronger than the ones on this side. Leave them until you come back with Surf.

Rival battle

Walk up the route and your rival spots you. They know you're the new kid next door, and they want to see what your Pokémon can do. Their partner is the one with the type advantage over yours.

{{battle:HOENN_ROUTE_103:rival|On your first visit after Professor Birch thanks you.}}

After the battle, your rival heals your Pokémon and heads back to the Lab, and asks you to come by. A boy nearby wishes he'd packed a Potion, and a man points out that once you can Surf, the water here is a quick way back to Oldale Town.

{{journal:hoenn_m2b}}
Later visit. After you become Champion, your rival is waiting on Route 103 for one more battle. Their partner Mega Evolves now.

Littleroot Town (again)

A Pokédex

Go back to Professor Birch's Lab. When he hears you beat his kid, he hands you a Pokédex, which records every Pokémon you meet, and asks you to fill it for him. Your rival adds ten Poké Balls, since you can't catch anything without them. From now on you can catch wild Pokémon.

The Professor also mentions your dad: he's in Petalburg City, west past Route 102.

{{journal:hoenn_m2c}}

As you head out of town to the north, Mom runs over. She's proud of you for getting a Pokémon of your own, and tells you to come home whenever you need a rest.

The DexNav

On Route 101, your rival catches up with a tip: Pokémon hide in the grass, and if you see a tail poking out, creep up quietly to catch them by surprise. Then they hand you the DexNav from their dad. It shows which Pokémon live wherever you are, and it lives under NAV in your MENU. (The tails it finds only start showing up much later in the story.)

Back in Oldale Town, the boy has finished his sketch, so the road west is clear.

Route 102

Route 102 runs west from Oldale Town to Petalburg City. It's a long stretch of tall grass with a pond in the north-east corner, and the first route where you can fill your Pokédex. Ralts and Surskit hide in the grass here, but they're rare.

{{tables:HOENN_ROUTE_102}}

A Potion lies on the ground, and the two patches of soft soil have Berries ready to pick: Oran Berries in one, Pecha Berries in the other.

{{trainers:HOENN_ROUTE_102}}

Youngster Calvin, Lass Tiana and Bug Catcher Rick stand along the route too, each with one Zigzagoon or Wurmple a little stronger than the wild ones. A boy is going to catch loads of Pokémon, and a little boy finds the tall grass tickles his nose.

Beautifly over the grass

On the west side of the route, your rival catches up again and points: a few Beautifly are fluttering over the tall grass. Petalburg City is just ahead, and your rival knows your dad is the Gym Leader there, then runs off to look around. Keep heading west.
