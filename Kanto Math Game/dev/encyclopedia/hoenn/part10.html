Mt. Chimney

Mt. Chimney is Hoenn's smoking volcano. The Cable Car from Route 112 stops at its peak. When you arrive, the peak is a battlefield: Team Magma and Team Aqua are fighting each other. Grunts of both teams are everywhere, and the other team's leader is trying to hold back the team you've been chasing. The Jagged Pass leads down the mountain's far side.

{{tables:HOENN_MT_CHIMNEY}}

Numel, Machop and Spoink live up here. Two items are hidden, and TM290 Incinerate lies on the peak.

Battle at the peak

The Grunts mutter about each other's teams: one can't believe the other team picked a name so like theirs. Archie (or Maxie in Alpha Sapphire) is too busy holding them off to help, and grumbles at you for turning up again. Fight your way through to the top.

{{trainers:HOENN_MT_CHIMNEY}}

The team's Admin blocks the way first: Tabitha (or Shelly in Alpha Sapphire).

{{battle:HOENN_MT_CHIMNEY:Tabitha}}

At the top stands Maxie (or Archie in Alpha Sapphire), with the Meteorite. He plans to use its power to help his team expand the land (or the sea), and he won't let a child stand in his way.

{{battle:HOENN_MT_CHIMNEY:Maxie}}

Beat him and he gives up the Meteorite, grumbling that there are other ways to awaken Groudon (or Kyogre).

Afterwards, the other team's leader comes over. He can't believe the Meteorite was given up so easily. He thinks something is wrong, and worries the team may have found out where the orbs are hidden. He means to move first.

The peak, afterwards

Once the teams have gone, a lady at the peak sells Lava Cookies, Mt. Chimney's sweet treat.

Jagged Pass

The Jagged Pass is a steep, rocky path down the side of Mt. Chimney. It leads from the peak down to Route 112, right beside Lavaridge Town.

{{tables:HOENN_JAGGED_PASS}}

Two TMs lie on the pass: TM282 Rock Polish in the grass and TM038 Flame Charge among the rocks. The Tyranitarite only turns up after the Cave of Origin.

{{trainers:HOENN_JAGGED_PASS}}

A Grunt is standing lookout on the pass, and nervously tells you this is none of your business. Somewhere on the pass there's a bare rock wall that doesn't look quite natural. You'll find out why later (see Part 20).

Lavaridge Town

Lavaridge Town is a hot-spring town at the foot of Mt. Chimney, famous for its healing waters. People bathe in the hot springs and bury themselves in the warm sand. The town has a Pokémon Center with its own hot spring out back, a Poké Mart, a Herb Shop and Flannery's Gym. Route 112 is just outside.

{{journal:hoenn_m9}} {{tables:LAVARIDGE_TOWN}}

The Ice Heal is hidden. The Houndoominite only turns up after the Cave of Origin.

Around Lavaridge

A Black Belt who dances karate asks if you understand the language of karate and dance. You don't, so he gives you TM088 Swords Dance. A sand bather is buried in the warm sand, enjoying it until a Pokémon nips them. A fan wants to be a strong, beautiful Gym Leader like Flannery.

The Egg lady

A woman in town has an Egg she'd like you to take on your travels: it hatches into Wynaut. Make room in your party first. Walking a lot helps it hatch.

{{journal:hoenn_s_eggs}}

Come back after the Cave of Origin, once the weather has calmed down, and she has a second Egg for you, which hatches into Togepi.

Pokémon Center and the hot spring

Step out the back of the Pokémon Center to the hot spring, and a soak fully refreshes your Pokémon. The Center also sells Moomoo Milk.

Herb Shop

The Herb Shop sells herbal medicine: Energy Powder, Energy Root, Heal Powder and Revival Herb. It works wonders, but it's so bitter your Pokémon won't thank you. The old man here gives you Charcoal, which makes a Pokémon's Fire moves burn hotter.

House

An old man's wife is warming an Egg from the Day Care in the hot springs, and a boy copies the way the old folks of the town talk.

Lavaridge Gym

Flannery trains Fire Pokémon, and she only accepts challengers with three Hoenn badges. Her Gym is full of steam and hidden trainers. Sand pits drop you to the floor below, and vents there shoot you back up, so find the way to Flannery. Water Pokémon are a good choice here.

Beat Flannery for the Heat Badge, plus TM157 Overheat and three Rare Candies. She also gives you a Root Fossil: the fossil lab in Rustboro City can bring it back to life.

{{badges:4}} {{cap}}

The Heat Badge lets your Pokémon use Strength outside battle, so the boulders in the Fiery Path can be pushed now. You also get the Go-Goggles, which keep the sand out of your eyes in Route 111's desert. With them, the desert is open, and the Mirage Tower sometimes appears in the sand (see Part 20).

Your next goal is the Balance Badge, back in Petalburg City (Part 11).
