Sootopolis City

Sootopolis City sits inside the crater of an old undersea volcano, so no road leads in. Dive down on Route 126 and swim through the seabed, and you come up in the round lake in the middle of the city. The houses are built into the crater walls, and the Cave of Origin opens at the north end. The city has a Pokémon Center, a Poké Mart and the last Gym, but when you first arrive the Gym is empty: its Leader has gone to guard the cave.

The water inside the crater is full of Magikarp. With the Super Rod you can hook a Gyarados too.

The crisis in Sootopolis

After the Seafloor Cavern, Primal Groudon (Primal Kyogre in Alpha Sapphire) has flown to the Cave of Origin, and its weather now covers all of Hoenn: blazing sun in Omega Ruby, pouring rain in Alpha Sapphire. Walk toward the cave and the people waiting there come over one by one:

  • Wallace steps away from the cave mouth. He's heard about you from Steven, and his job is to protect the Cave of Origin.
  • Steven warns that if the legend draws on the power inside the cave, Hoenn won't survive it.
  • Maxie (or Archie in Alpha Sapphire) admits his team can't go any further. It's up to you now.
  • Tabitha (or Shelly) hands you the team's suit, the Magma Suit (or Aqua Suit), to protect you down there.
  • Your rival runs up just in time to wish you luck.

Wallace says the Orb you carry will shield you and your Pokémon, and lets you through. Until this scene, he stands in the cave mouth and turns everyone away.

Around Sootopolis

The people here are nervous. A girl wonders where the legends went, a boy asks if the city will be all right, and the legend teller says the land and sea were shaped by a great battle between Pokémon. A boy who has never left the city wonders what the world is like outside their round sky. Something glints on the ground in town: it's the Sablenite, a Mega Stone. A collector in town also gives you TM285 Infestation.

Houses

An old man who has trained here for thirty years gives you TM058 Brick Break, which smashes Reflect and Light Screen. A girl who never gets visitors gives you a big Wailmer Doll. In another house, two fans want to see Pokémon you don't find in Sootopolis: show one a Barboach and the other a Shroomish (in your party), and each gives you a Max Elixir.

The other houses are for chatting. A historian explains how the crater filled with rainwater to become the city, a woman thinks the cave was made to keep something in, and a researcher believes Sootopolis and Mt. Pyre are somehow connected.

Pokémon Center and Poké Mart

Heal at the counter as usual. A Gym hopeful in the Center calls the Gym here the toughest in Hoenn. The link rooms upstairs are closed.

Cave of Origin

The Cave of Origin goes down and down: the Entrance, 1F, B1F, B2F, B3F and finally the deepest chamber. The legend's weather fills the cave too. Golbat, Sableye and Mawile live here, and an X Attack (1F) and a Revive (B1F) are hidden on the way down. Heal up before the last stairs.

{{tables:HOENN_CAVE_OF_ORIGIN}}

The deepest chamber

In the deepest chamber, the legend is drawing power from the great crystal above it. Walk up to it and it undergoes Primal Reversion and attacks.

Primal Groudon (Omega Ruby) or Primal Kyogre (Alpha Sapphire), Lv 45. It reverts to its Primal form as the battle starts. Primal Groudon is a Ground and Fire type, so Water moves are your best weapon. Primal Kyogre is pure Water: use Grass and Electric moves. Weaken it and try to catch it. If you beat it without catching it, it comes back the next day.

When it's over, the skies over Hoenn clear. Maxie (or Archie) is waiting in the chamber. He admits the legend's power was never theirs to control, and gives you the Red Orb (or Blue Orb). If the legend holds it in battle, it undergoes Primal Reversion.

Sootopolis City (again)

Back in town, the crisis is over and a lot opens up:

  • Steven is waiting in town. He thanks you for saving Hoenn and gives you the Eon Flute. Play it outside and Latios (or Latias) flies you high over Hoenn. That opens Soaring in the Sky and the Mirage spots (see Part 19).
  • The Weather Institute on Route 119 has a present: the Director gives you the Weather Dial and one of each weather rock.
  • The stone collector on Route 114 starts selling Mega Stones you can't find anywhere else in Hoenn.
  • The Abandoned Ship on Route 108 has a new surprise in a filing cabinet (see Part 20).

Sootopolis Gym

Wallace is back at the Gym, the eighth and last one. Its floor is made of ice. The Gym Guide explains the puzzle: step on every tile of ice in a section to open the stairs to the next one. Each tile cracks when you step on it, and if you step on a cracked tile again, you fall through to the floor below. There are three sections to clear on the way up to Wallace.

The basement, B1F, is where you land if you fall. The Gym's trainers are all waiting down there.

{{trainers:SOOTOPOLIS_CITY:Sootopolis Gym B1F}}

Wallace, the artist of water, uses Water Pokémon. Electric and Grass moves are your best choices, but watch out: his Whiscash is part Ground, so Electric moves don't touch it, and Grass moves hit it four times as hard. His Milotic can heal itself with Recover.

{{battle:SOOTOPOLIS_CITY:Wallace}}

Beat him to win the Rain Badge, your eighth. He also gives you HM Waterfall, a Claw Fossil for the fossil lab in Rustboro City, and the Eon Ticket, for the ship from Slateport City to Southern Island (see Part 11).

With eight badges, the way to the Pokémon League is open: go east along Route 128 to Ever Grande City (Part 17). Wattson also has a job for you back in Mauville City now (see Part 20).

The western sea

West of Route 128, a long chain of sea routes runs across the south of Hoenn: Route 129, Route 130, Route 131, then Pacifidlog Town, and on through Route 132, Route 133 and Route 134 to Slateport City. None of it is needed for the League, but it's full of trainers, items and secrets.

Route 129

Route 129 is open sea west of Route 128. Two swimmers wait for a battle, and a Big Pearl and a Heart Scale are hidden here. Dive down to the seabed for more hidden treasure: the Lustrous Orb, a Blue Shard and three Plates.

Route 130

Route 130 carries on west. Its seabed hides the Griseous Orb, a Green Shard and four Plates. Some days, a small island appears out here: Mirage Island (see Part 19).

Route 131

Route 131 runs from Route 130 to Pacifidlog Town. To the north stands the tall Sky Pillar, but you can't go in yet (see below).

{{tables:HOENN_ROUTE_131}} {{trainers:HOENN_ROUTE_131}}

Pacifidlog Town

Pacifidlog Town floats on the water: a proud local says the houses float, and another says the whole town rests on a colony of Corsola. There's a Pokémon Center but no Poké Mart. A Kangaskhanite glints on the ground. A Big Pearl, TM273 Frustration and TM274 Return lie about town, a Heart Scale is hidden, and a man gives you TM043 Fling.

{{tables:PACIFIDLOG_TOWN}}

An old fisherman thinks the Sky Pillar is too scary to climb, and a man warns that the sea between here and Slateport runs fast.

Houses

  • The Mirage house. A scholar who studies Mirage Island gives you TM054 Psyshock for crossing all that sea. His friend looks for the island every day.
  • The trade house. A girl wants a Bagon someone else caught. Trade her one and you get her Horsea.
  • The friendship house. Once a week, a man checks how your first Pokémon feels about you. If it likes you, he gives you a TM of Return; if not, a TM of Frustration.
  • The story house. An old man says three Pokémon of Hoenn stand for rock, ice and steel, each hidden in a cave of its own. His grandchild remembers a saying about six dots opening three doors. That's a clue for Part 19.

Pokémon Center

A man here teaches one of your Pokémon Explosion, if it can learn it. Like other tutors in this game, he can also sell you the move as a TM ours.

Route 132

Route 132 heads west from Pacifidlog Town. A Rare Candy has washed up on a rock, someone left a Protein out here, and TM214 Sludge Wave lies on the route too.

Route 133

Route 133 carries on west. Look among its rocks for a Star Piece, a Big Pearl, a Calcium and a Max Revive. A Psychic out here gives you TM203 Psych Up.

{{tables:HOENN_ROUTE_133}} {{trainers:HOENN_ROUTE_133}}

Route 134

Route 134 is the last stretch, ending at Slateport City. A Carbos, a Star Piece, a Heart Scale and TM086 Rock Slide lie on the rocks, and two more Heart Scales are hidden. Deep under this route is the Sealed Chamber, which you can reach after you become Champion (see Part 19).

{{tables:HOENN_ROUTE_134}} {{trainers:HOENN_ROUTE_134}}

Sky Pillar

The Sky Pillar is the tall, ancient tower north of Route 131. Its door stays shut for now. It opens after you become Champion, during the Delta Episode, once Wallace has met you on Route 131 (see Part 18). Inside, the climb goes from the Entrance and the Outside path up through five floors to the top. Some floors have cracked tiles: ride the Bicycle over them, or they crumble and you fall to the floor below. A Dragon Scale, a Full Restore, TM281 Explosion and an Ultra Ball lie near the Entrance.
