Route 119

Route 119 runs north from Route 118 toward Fortree City. It's a long, wild route of tall grass, with water running through it. The grass is so thick that bike tyres get snagged in it, as a stuck cyclist will tell you, so you'll be walking. Tropius live here, and the river is the place to fish for Feebas.

Partway up the route stands the Weather Institute, and two grunts from Team Magma (or Team Aqua in Alpha Sapphire) are on lookout outside, warning you to keep away. Something is going on in there.

{{journal:hoenn_m12}} {{tables:HOENN_ROUTE_119}}

Feebas bites only rarely, on any rod. Lots of items lie along the route, among them TM Acrobatics, a PP Max and a Rare Candy. The Venusaurite appears on the route only after the Cave of Origin (see Part 16). A Full Heal is hidden here.

{{trainers:HOENN_ROUTE_119}}

Along the route

A man asks whether your Pokémon can use Secret Power on a big bush to make a hideout. Another traveller admits he used to think you Fly by grabbing a whole flock of bird Pokémon and holding on tight. There's a small rest stop on the route too, with two Wingull inside.

Two spots on the route are blocked by something you can't see. Come back with the Devon Scope from Route 120: it shows a Kecleon blending into the scenery at each spot, and the startled Kecleon attacks (Lv 30). You can catch them.

Weather Institute

The Weather Institute studies Hoenn's weather. When you arrive, the team has taken it over. The receptionists downstairs are too scared to help: the trouble is upstairs, where the scientists and their Professor are being held.

{{tables:HOENN_WEATHER_INSTITUTE}}

Three grunts guard the ground floor, and another waits upstairs with their admin, Tabitha (or Shelly in Alpha Sapphire).

{{trainers:HOENN_WEATHER_INSTITUTE}} {{battle:HOENN_WEATHER_INSTITUTE:Tabitha}}

Beat the admin and the team clears out of the Institute.

The scientists' thanks

The scientists upstairs have been studying a Pokémon that changes its form with the weather. As thanks for saving them, they give it to you: Castform.

{{journal:hoenn_s15}}

Downstairs, a grateful scientist gives you a Rocky Helmet, which hurts any Pokémon that touches its holder. One of the receptionists now hands out a souvenir every day: a weather rock, starting with an Icy Rock, then a Smooth Rock, a Heat Rock and a Damp Rock on the days after. A sleepy researcher lets your team nap on the beds at the back, which heals them fully.

Much later ours. After the Cave of Origin, the Weather Director upstairs gives you the Weather Dial and one of each weather rock. Set a rock in it on an outdoor route and you choose the weather there until midnight.

Rival battle

Head back onto Route 119 and your rival runs up. They heard you chased the team out of the Institute, and they want to see how strong you've become. Their team depends on the starter you picked.

{{battle:HOENN_ROUTE_119:rival}}

After the battle your rival gives you HM Fly, which carries you to any town you've been to. You need Fortree's Feather Badge before your Pokémon will use it on the map.

Fortree City

Fortree City is built up in the trees, at the end of Route 119. The whole city lives up in the treetops, and the people here live gently alongside nature. Route 120 leads east out of town.

{{tables:FORTREE_CITY}}

TM Grass Knot lies in town.

Something blocking the Gym

When you get to the Gym, something you can't see is standing right in front of the door. Someone who came to challenge Winona is stuck outside too. Walk on through town and Steven passes by: he's looking for a certain Pokémon, and he's heard it's on Route 120. He'll meet you there.

Pokémon Center and Poké Mart

Heal and shop before the Gym. In the Mart, a lady recommends Super Repels for long walks, and a psychic reminds you that a Rare Candy makes a Pokémon grow a level.

Fortree Gym

Winona trains Flying Pokémon. Her Gym is full of turnstile gates: push a gate's arm and it swings round, so you have to work out which way to turn each one to reach her. Winona only takes challengers with five Hoenn badges, and you'll need the Devon Scope from Route 120 to deal with whatever is blocking the door. Use it and you'll see a Kecleon there, which flees.

{{journal:hoenn_m13}} {{trainers:FORTREE_CITY:Fortree Gym}} {{battle:FORTREE_CITY:Winona|Every one of her Pokémon knows Aerial Ace. Altaria also has Earthquake, Dragon Breath and Dragon Dance, and Skarmory has Steel Wing.}}

Win for the Feather Badge. Winona also gives you TM Roost and three Rare Candies. With the Feather Badge, your Pokémon can use Fly outside battle.

Fortree Treetops

Climb up into the trees for the rest of Fortree: its treehouses. A man up here swears he once saw a gigantic Pokémon squirming across the sky, and nobody believes him.

{{tables:FORTREE_CITY_UPPER}}

The coin game

An old woman plays a coin game to keep herself awake. Win three in a row and she gives you TM Hidden Power, a move whose type is different for every Pokémon. Lose and you can simply try again.

The trade house

A little girl wants a Spinda, which you can find on Route 113. Bring her one and she trades you her Skitty.

The Wingull house

A breeder here has a Wingull that carries letters to Mossdeep City and back, but it's off on an errand. Once you've met its friend in Mossdeep (see Part 14), the Wingull flies home with a letter, and the breeder gives you a Mental Herb it found on the way.

Secret Base Guild

This used to be a furniture shop. Now it's the home of the Secret Base Guild, and its leader, Aarune, would like a battle. He's still setting the place up.

Other houses

One man once met a Trainer called Steven while hunting for rare stones. Another family says Fortree is the best town there is for living with Pokémon.

Route 120

Route 120 runs east from Fortree City toward Route 121 and Lilycove City. It has a bridge, water to Surf across and patches of tall grass where Absol sometimes turns up. Steven is here, standing near a bridge that something invisible is blocking.

{{tables:HOENN_ROUTE_120}}

Two Rare Candies, a Nugget, a Full Restore and a Light Ball (Pikachu's own item) lie on the route, and a Revive and a Zinc are hidden. A lone grunt from the team is out here too.

{{trainers:HOENN_ROUTE_120}}

Steven and the Devon Scope

Steven has noticed the invisible thing on the bridge, and the one at the Fortree Gym. He gives you the Devon Scope, which shows hidden Pokémon. Then he says you and your partner have come a long way together, and gives you the Mega Stone for your starter: Sceptilite, Blazikenite or Swampertite.

{{journal:hoenn_m14}} {{journal:hoenn_s20}}

With the Devon Scope, walk up to the invisible blocker on the bridge. It's a Kecleon (Lv 30), and it attacks. More hidden Kecleon stand around the route: six in all, each a Lv 30 battle, and you can catch them. Then go back to Fortree City and take on Winona.

Later on Route 120

A rock face on the route has a doorway carved into it, sealed shut. It's the Ancient Tomb, and it opens much later (see Part 19). A path north leads to the Scorched Slab (also Part 19).
