Introduction

Hoenn is a warm region in the south, with long sea routes, a smoking volcano and islands scattered across the water. You've just moved to Littleroot Town, a little town in the far south. Your dad, Norman, is the new Gym Leader of Petalburg City, and your new neighbour is Professor Birch, who studies Pokémon out in the wild.

There are two ways to get here:

  • A new game. Pick Hoenn from the region list. Choose your look and type your name, then pick your rival, May or Brendan, and give them a name. Your rival is Professor Birch's kid, and lives right next door.
  • From another region. Once you become the Champion of the region you're in, Mom calls about your prize money: it's enough to buy a new home, and she asks where to move. Pick Hoenn (she only offers regions you haven't played yet). Hoenn keeps its own party, PC and Bag, so you arrive with no Pokémon and get your first partner the same way as in a new game, out on Route 101.

Either way, your Journal (in the MENU) always shows your next main mission, and a gold marker on the map points the way.

Omega Ruby or Alpha Sapphire

The first time you arrive in Hoenn, the game asks which story you want to play. Hoenn's story follows Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire. Both are the same journey with a different villain:

  • Omega Ruby: Team Magma and its leader Maxie want to awaken the ancient Pokémon Groudon to expand the land. Their admins are Tabitha and Courtney, the orb at the heart of the story is the Red Orb, and the Eon Pokémon on your side is Latios.
  • Alpha Sapphire: Team Aqua and its leader Archie want to awaken Kyogre to expand the sea. Their admins are Shelly and Matt, the orb is the Blue Orb, and the Eon Pokémon is Latias.

You choose once, and it decides the team you face all the way through, along with their Pokémon: where Team Magma sends out Numel and Camerupt, Team Aqua uses Carvanha and Sharpedo. This guide names both, Omega Ruby first. It says "Team Magma (or Team Aqua in Alpha Sapphire)" the first time in a part, then "the team", and the battle boxes show the villains' Pokémon as "Camerupt / Sharpedo".

How this guide plays

This guide is written for two settings in Options › Gameplay: Battle style: Overworld and Level cap: Story. The cap is on from the start; the battle style you switch on yourself.

Overworld battles

On routes, in caves and in Petalburg Woods, you can see the wild Pokémon moving about, mostly in the tall grass. Walk into one and the battle happens right there on the map: it fights from where it stands, yours steps up beside it, and small HP bars float over both. Trainers on these maps spot you and walk over. Their words come up in the map's text box, they send out their Pokémon in front of them, and you fight on the map too. In the trainer tables, "on the map" marks these.

Every battle still works the same way: pick a move and answer its math question to land it. The buttons and questions are the usual ones.

The important story battles always open the battle screen: Gym Leaders, the team's grunts in story scenes, its admins and its leader, your rival, Wally, the Elite Four and the Champion. So do the trainers inside Gyms and towns and a bite on your rod. Each battle box says which.

Hordes and hidden Pokémon

Now and then, a wild Pokémon you walk into turns out to be a horde: five wild Pokémon at once against your one, on the battle screen. A move that hits every foe, like Surf or Rock Slide, hits all five. You can only throw a Poké Ball once a single one is left standing. The tables list each place's hordes under its wild Pokémon.

Your rival gives you the DexNav early on. Much later in the story it starts to show Pokémon hiding nearby: a tail poking out of the grass, or a ripple on the water while you surf. Creep onto the spot before it slips away and you battle a Pokémon you can't meet any other way there. The tables list these too, and say when they start to appear.

The story level cap

Your Pokémon can't level past the next Gym Leader's best Pokémon plus two. At the cap they stop gaining Exp., and Rare Candies don't work. Beat the Leader and the cap moves up. Each part starts with a box giving the cap for that stretch.

Levels never rise to meet you: wild Pokémon always come at their place's own level, from two below it to one above, and trainers keep their own levels. So the levels in this guide's tables and battle boxes are the ones you'll face, however strong your team is.

Poké Marts and prizes

Every Poké Mart in Hoenn sells from the same counter, and it grows with the Hoenn badges you hold. TMs join the shelf as you earn badges too, and the Pokémon League's shop adds Revives, Rare Candy, Full Restores and Max Potions. Not every town has a Mart: Littleroot Town has none, so your first one is in Oldale Town.

Route trainers don't pay in Poké Balls: beating one gives you a healing prize that grows with your badges.

Littleroot Town

Littleroot Town is a small, quiet town at the southern tip of Hoenn's mainland. Your new house and Professor Birch's house stand side by side in the middle of town, and Professor Birch's Pokémon Lab is to the south. The only road out is Route 101, north toward Oldale Town. The town sign calls it a town that can't be shaded any hue.

A Potion lies on the ground in town. The Mega Stones only turn up long after the main story: see the notes at the end of this part.

Moving day

The moving truck rumbles to a stop in Littleroot Town. Mom welcomes you to your new home in Hoenn and tells you that Professor Birch lives just next door, so you should go and say hello.

Your house

Talk to Mom any time you're home and she heals your team. She has something different to say each time: the boxes are still half packed, your dad's Gym is over in Petalburg City, and she'll keep your prize money safe in a jar on the counter. If you moved here from another region, she tells you your prize money paid for the moving truck and the house. The TV is showing her favourite cooking show.

Upstairs in your room, your notebook's first rule of adventure is to check your Bag often, and Mom has already set the wall clock for you.

Professor Birch's house

Mrs. Birch welcomes you as the new neighbour and sends you upstairs to say hello. Her husband and her kid are both wild about Pokémon, she says.

Upstairs, your rival introduces themselves: they help their dad study Pokémon out in the wild. Then they remember they promised to help him on Route 101, and rush off.

Professor Birch's Lab

The Professor isn't in. An aide tells you he's out doing fieldwork on Route 101 again: he thinks the best research happens in the tall grass, not behind a desk.

Around town, a little girl warns you never to walk into the tall grass without a Pokémon of your own, a boy says the Professor is hardly ever home, and a science fan is amazed that a PC can keep your items and Pokémon safe.

Route 101

Route 101 is a short, grassy path north from Littleroot Town to Oldale Town. As soon as you step onto it, you hear someone shouting for help.

{{tables:HOENN_ROUTE_101}}

You can't catch anything yet: the Pokédex and the Poké Balls come after your first rival battle. The DexNav's hidden Pokémon here only appear after you become Champion.

Professor Birch in trouble

A man is running in circles in the grass with a wild Poochyena snapping at his heels. It's Professor Birch! His bag lies dropped in the grass nearby, and he calls out to you: there are Poké Balls in it, so pick one and help him.

Look at each Pokémon in the bag, then answer the math questions to choose your partner.

Treecko

Grass. Strong against Roxanne's Rock types at the first Gym. A good, easy start.

Torchic

Fire. Weak against Roxanne's Rock types, so the first Gym is the hardest with it.

Mudkip

Water. Strong against Roxanne, and the steadiest all the way through.

Your new partner goes straight into battle against the Poochyena (Lv 2). You can't catch it. Beat it and it runs off into the tall grass.

Professor Birch picks up his bag, still out of breath. He thanks you for saving him, introduces himself, and asks you to come back to his Lab in Littleroot Town.

Littleroot Town (again)

Birch's thanks

As soon as you're back in town, Professor Birch thanks you again. You and that Pokémon worked so well together that he wants you to keep it. Then he tells you his kid is out on Route 103, north of Oldale Town, and suggests you go and say hello.

{{journal:hoenn_m2}}

Head north again through Route 101 to Oldale Town and Route 103 (see Part 2).

Later visits. When you've beaten your dad for the Balance Badge, Mom gives you an Amulet Coin. After you become Champion, Mom has news of a meteor heading for Hoenn, which starts the Delta Episode (see Part 18). Once that's over, she finds a Mega Stone in the yard for you: Latiasite in Omega Ruby, Latiosite in Alpha Sapphire. And after the Cave of Origin, a Mewtwonite X glints on the ground in town.
Route 101 later. Once you're Champion, Professor Birch waits on Route 101 with Pokémon a colleague sent him from Johto: pick Chikorita, Cyndaquil or Totodile (Lv 5). After the Delta Episode he has Pokémon from Unova too: Snivy, Tepig or Oshawott (Lv 5). And when you enter the Hall of Fame a second time, Professor Rowan has sent Turtwig, Chimchar and Piplup from Sinnoh. Make room in your party first.
