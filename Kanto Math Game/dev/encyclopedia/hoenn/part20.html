The Battle Frontier and other places

This last part covers the Battle Frontier, which opens once you're Champion, and the side places around Hoenn that aren't needed for the League. Some of them open during the journey: each section says when.

S.S. Tidal

Once you're Champion, the S.S. Tidal sails from the harbours in Slateport City and Lilycove City. It takes you between the two cities, and a sailor on board takes Champions on to the Battle Frontier. Mr. Briney and Peeko are aboard: the ship has made him its honorary captain.

The ship has a corridor, the cabins and a lower deck. The passengers in the cabins are itching for a battle. TM280 Embargo and a Sharp Beak lie in the corridor, a Blastoisinite glints on the floor of the cabins, and a Leftovers is hidden on the lower deck, where the Cleanup Brothers keep the ship (mostly) spotless.

Gentleman Thomas in the cabins is the toughest passenger. Beat him to finish the S.S. Tidal's mission. His Braviary is part Flying, so Electric, Ice and Rock moves are good choices.

Battle Frontier

The Battle Frontier is a whole island built for battling. It has seven battle facilities, the Battle Maison, a Pokémon Center, a Poké Mart, the Ranking Hall and the Exchange Service Corner. The S.S. Tidal docks here, and its attendant takes you back whenever you like.

{{tables:HOENN_BATTLE_FRONTIER}} {{trainers:HOENN_BATTLE_FRONTIER}}

Arriving

As you step off the ship, Wally runs over. He's come to train at the Frontier, and he asks you to find him by the Battle Maison for a battle. Then a familiar face from the other team nearly bumps into you. That team has given up its plans, and the boss wants you to have a Mega Stone: in Omega Ruby, Matt gives you the Sharpedonite; in Alpha Sapphire, Courtney gives you the Cameruptite.

{{battle:HOENN_BATTLE_FRONTIER:Wally|By the Battle Maison, once you're Champion.}} {{journal:hoenn_s27}}

Around the Frontier

  • The Custodian keeps things people leave lying about. Nobody came back for them, so he gives you a Protein, Zinc, Carbos, Iron and Calcium. A Gengarite glints by his cabin.
  • Two former grunts are making a fresh start. The Magma Grunt gives you his Camerupt (Lv 30) with a Passho Berry, and the Aqua Grunt gives you his Sharpedo (Lv 30) with a Wacan Berry. Make room in your party first.
  • Looker washed up on the shore here. To repay you, he gives you the Audinite.
  • The Whismur show. Once a day, watch the Whismur dance and guess which one holds the Big Mushroom. Guess right and it's yours.
  • The Berry lover gives you one of the Berries that make Pokémon friendlier each day.
  • A little girl gives you a Lansat Berry once you've beaten the Battle Maison's Chatelaine, and a Starf Berry once you've beaten her again.
  • A weird tree stands on the island. Sprinkle it with the Wailmer Pail and it attacks: it's a Sudowoodo (Lv 40).
  • Move tutors teach moves for Battle Points: Thunder Punch, Fire Punch and Ice Punch for 8 BP each, and Superpower for 16 BP.
  • The Judge in the Pokémon Center looks at your first Pokémon and tells you how much potential it has.

The Poké Mart sells Ultra Balls, Hyper Potions, Max Potions, Full Restores, Full Heals, Revives, Max Repels and X Attacks.

Somewhere in the Battle Tower is a floor that doesn't officially exist, with a Game Corner of its own and a crowd of trainers playing the slots ours.

The facilities

The seven facilities are the Battle Tower, Battle Dome, Battle Palace, Battle Arena, Battle Factory, Battle Pike and Battle Pyramid. Talk to the attendant in each to start a streak. Here, every facility plays the same simple way ours, without the special rules each one has in Emerald:

  • You battle Frontier trainers one after another, in single battles. Their Pokémon get stronger as your streak goes on.
  • Your team is healed before every battle. If you lose, the streak ends and your team is healed: there's no blacking out.
  • Win 5 in a row and the facility's Frontier Brain battles you. Beat the Brain to earn its Silver Symbol.
  • After that, win 10 in a row to face the Brain's stronger team for the Gold Symbol. You can keep going for more after that.
  • Each win earns 1 BP (Battle Point). Beating a Brain earns 10 BP for the Silver Symbol and 20 BP for the Gold.

Battle Maison

The Battle Maison comes from Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire's Battle Resort. Here it has moved into the Frontier ours. It works like the other facilities: single battles, your team healed in between. Win 5 in a row and the Chatelaine, Nita, battles you herself in the battle room; then 10 in a row for her stronger team. Your name goes up on the Maison's wall of champions. The Maison gives no Symbol of its own.

Exchange Service Corner

Spend your Battle Points here, on Emerald's prizes:

  • Protein, Calcium, Iron, Zinc, Carbos and HP Up: 1 BP each.
  • Leftovers, White Herb, Quick Claw, Mental Herb and Razor Claw: 48 BP each.
  • Bright Powder, Choice Band, King's Rock, Focus Band and Scope Lens: 64 BP each.

The Razor Claw is Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire's prize, at their price.

Artisan Cave

The Artisan Cave opens at the north of the Battle Frontier. It has two floors, 1F and B1F, and the only wild Pokémon is Smeargle. A Carbos lies on 1F and an HP Up on B1F, and a Zinc, a Calcium, a Protein and an Iron are hidden on B1F.

Desert Underpass

Once you're Champion, the Fossil Maniac's tunnel, behind his house on Route 114, leads on into the Desert Underpass. Ditto, Whismur and Loudred live here. Buried in the sand at the end of the tunnel is the fossil you didn't take in the Mirage Tower: the Claw Fossil if you took the Root Fossil, or the Root Fossil if you took the Claw Fossil.

Side places during the journey

These places open before the League. None of them is needed to finish the story.

Abandoned Ship

The Abandoned Ship is an old wreck out on the water of Route 108. A researcher in the Captain's Office says it's the S.S. Cactus, from long ago. Captain Stern asked him to look it over and find a Scanner somewhere on board.

{{journal:hoenn_s3}}

From the deck, go down into the 1F corridors and cabins, then the B1F corridors and cabins. A Revive is in the 1F west cabins, and a Max Repel, an Escape Rope and a Dive Ball are in the B1F cabins.

{{tables:HOENN_ABANDONED_SHIP}} {{trainers:HOENN_ABANDONED_SHIP}}

The storeroom

The Storage Key lies on the captain's desk in the Captain's Office. It opens the locked "Storage" door on B1F. The storeroom is full of treasure: eight Big Nuggets, four Nuggets, TM135 Ice Beam and the Beedrillite.

The hidden floor

One of the B1F cabins is flooded. With Dive, dive down there to reach the ship's hidden floor, where a White Herb grows in a damp corner. Six cabin doors lead off it, and four of them are locked. Their keys, Rm. 1, Rm. 2, Rm. 4 and Rm. 6, are hidden in the hidden cabins themselves, so search the open ones first. In the cabins you find a Luxury Ball, TM050 Rain Dance, a Water Stone and the Scanner.

Take the Scanner to Captain Stern at his shipyard in Slateport City. He trades you either a Deep Sea Tooth or a Deep Sea Scale for it.

The filing cabinet

After the Cave of Origin (Part 16), look in the old filing cabinet in the 1F cabins. An old report says the crew once worked for Wattson, raising energy from the sea, and lost the Odd Keystone. You feel as though you're being watched...

Spiritomb, Lv 50. In the 1F cabins' filing cabinet, after the Cave of Origin. A Ghost and Dark type, its only weakness is Fairy moves.

Mirage Tower

The Mirage Tower appears in the desert on Route 111 once you have the Heat Badge. Sandshrew and Trapinch live inside its four floors. At the top, on 4F, lie two fossils: the Root Fossil and the Claw Fossil. You can only take one. As soon as you pick it up, the tower starts crumbling into the sand and you scramble out to Route 111. After that, only sand is left where it stood. The fossil you left behind turns up in the Desert Underpass after you're Champion.

{{tables:HOENN_MIRAGE_TOWER}}

The fossil lab in Rustboro City's Devon Corporation can revive the fossil.

New Mauville

New Mauville is an old power plant under Route 110. Its door is at the east end of Mauville City, and it's locked until Wattson gives you a job. After you win the Rain Badge (Part 16), Wattson meets you in Mauville's square: the tower's light is on, which means something's up in New Mauville. Visit him in his flat in Mauville Hills, the building by the Mart. He scans your eyes so the doors will open for you.

Inside, Magnemite and Voltorb crackle about. Run the check on the security computer in the back room: all systems are normal. An Ampharosite glints on the floor, and there's more to pick up: a Metal Coat, a Paralyze Heal, a Full Heal, a Thunder Stone, an Ultra Ball and an Escape Rope. A Max Repel is hidden. Tell Wattson the good news and he gives you TM126 Thunderbolt.

Altering Cave

The Altering Cave is at the north of Route 103, across the water, so you need Surf. It's a single cave, home to Zubat and a mix of Johto Pokémon: Mareep, Pineco, Houndour, Teddiursa, Aipom, Shuckle, Stantler and Smeargle.

{{tables:HOENN_ALTERING_CAVE}}

Magma Hideout

The Magma Hideout is dug into the volcano off Jagged Pass. Its entrance opens once you hold the Feather Badge. It's Team Magma's hideout in both versions: in Alpha Sapphire, it's the other team's base. Grunts guard every floor: 1F, 2F and its far side, 3F with a side room, and 4F, where the magma is.

{{tables:HOENN_MAGMA_HIDEOUT}} {{trainers:HOENN_MAGMA_HIDEOUT}}

On 4F, Tabitha and Courtney are waiting. Beat Courtney to clear the hideout. On the way, look for a Rare Candy on 1F, a Full Restore and a Max Elixir on 2F's far side, a Nugget on 3F, the Cameruptite and a PP Max in 3F's side room, an Escape Rope on the way round, and a Max Revive by the magma on 4F.
