Route 124

Route 124 is open sea between Lilycove City and Mossdeep City. Once the team's Wailmer have left the cove, Surf out from Lilycove and head east. Swimmers are out on the waves, and in places you can Dive down to the seabed.

{{tables:HOENN_ROUTE_124}}

The red, yellow and blue Shards lie around the route, and the green one is down on the seabed, so it needs Dive (see Part 15). Lots of items are hidden on the route, among them a Sky Plate. The Pinsirite glints out on the water.

{{trainers:HOENN_ROUTE_124}}

Treasure Hunter's house

The Treasure Hunter's house stands on the route. He collects Shards, pieces of tools from ancient times. Bring him one and he trades you an evolution stone for it: a Fire Stone for a red Shard, a Thunder Stone for a yellow one, a Water Stone for a blue one and a Leaf Stone for a green one.

Mossdeep City

Mossdeep City is an island town out in the eastern sea, green and bright, with a Gym, a Pokémon Center, a Mart and the Mossdeep Space Center, which launches rockets from the island. Steven lives here too. Route 125 lies to the north, and Route 127 to the south.

{{tables:MOSSDEEP_CITY}}

A Star Piece and an Iron are hidden in town. Steven's gifts are in his house (see below).

Around town

A boy gives you a King's Rock that Steven gave him: some Pokémon evolve while holding one. Someone says Sootopolis City is built inside a crater, and Scott has come to watch the twins' Gym battles. A woman gives you TM Quash.

Fisherman's house

A fisherman thinks a Super Rod really is super: it hooks Pokémon right off the seafloor. He gives you the Super Rod.

The Wingull house

A girl here writes to her friend in Fortree City, and her Wingull carries the letters. Talk to the Wingull and it takes a letter in its beak and flies off toward Fortree. Visit its trainer in Fortree's treetops afterwards for a Mental Herb (see Part 12).

Steven's house

Steven collects rare stones, and his shelves are full of them, every one polished. He has left a Mega Stone on his table for you, the Metagrossite. Steven himself is home: he tells you Mossdeep's Gym Leaders are twins who battle as one, and asks you to come back once you've won.

Other houses

A man in one house can tell which Pokéblocks a Pokémon likes just by looking at your lead Pokémon. In the Hex Maniac sisters' house, one sister reads the past in your Gym Badges, and once a day she quizzes you on them for a reward. A little explorer has found a hidden spot in the bushes on the route and wants you to find one too.

Pokémon Center and Poké Mart

Heal and shop before the Gym. A sailor in the Mart says Net Balls and Dive Balls are made right here in Mossdeep.

Mossdeep Gym

Tate and Liza are twins who train Psychic Pokémon, and they battle together. Their Gym is a puzzle of coloured switches and arrow blocks: step on a switch and every block of that colour turns, so you have to change the blocks' arrows to clear a path. They only take challengers with six Hoenn badges. When you reach them, Liza tells you to talk to Tate when you're ready.

Win for the Mind Badge. Tate and Liza also give you TM Calm Mind. With the Mind Badge, your Pokémon can use Dive outside battle.

A pillar of light

Back out in town after your win, the ground shakes. Far to the south, a pillar of light bursts up out of the sea. Steven comes running: the team has broken into the Seafloor Cavern, a cave that has been sealed since ancient times, and they mean to wake the super-ancient Pokémon sleeping inside. He can't stop them alone.

Visit him in his house and he gives you HM Dive, which takes your Pokémon down into the deep sea. He tells you to dive down from Route 128, south of Mossdeep. Before you go, though, the team has another target right here in Mossdeep (see the Space Center, below).

Route 125

Route 125 is a stretch of sea north of Mossdeep City. At its northern end is Shoal Cave, a cave that floods and drains with the tide.

{{tables:HOENN_ROUTE_125}} {{trainers:HOENN_ROUTE_125}}

Shoal Cave

Shoal Cave is a cold sea cave, and the tide changes it. The tide comes in from 3:00 to 9:00 and from 15:00 to 21:00, by the real clock, and goes out in between. At low tide you can walk into rooms that are underwater at high tide, and at high tide you can Surf to places you can't reach on foot. Deep inside there's an ice room, where Snorunt live. Spheal and Sealeo are common.

{{journal:hoenn_s4}} {{tables:HOENN_SHOAL_CAVE}}

The Slowbronite is only reachable at high tide. The Focus Band comes from a Black Belt in the lower cavern.

{{trainers:HOENN_SHOAL_CAVE}}

The Shell Bell

An old man at the entrance makes Shell Bells. Bring him four Shoal Salt and four Shoal Shells and he makes you a Shell Bell. Shoal Salt is left in the cave at low tide, and Shoal Shells wash in at high tide. Both come back every day, so you can collect a few each day until you have enough.

The Black Belt

In the lower cavern, a Black Belt is training in the cold. He gives you a Focus Band, which can let a Pokémon hang on when it would otherwise faint.

The ice room

The ice room holds TM Hail, a lump of Never-Melt Ice and the Glalitite. TM Frost Breath lies by a sandy slope in the stairs room.

Mossdeep Space Center

The Space Center launches rockets from Mossdeep. Every launch so far has gone up safely, and the staff are proud of it. A sailor on 1F gives you a Sun Stone he found on the beach.

Once you have the Mind Badge, a notice turns up on 1F: the team has written that they're coming for the rocket fuel. Then they storm the building. Grunts fill both floors, and Steven is upstairs: he asks you to help him stop them.

Then their leader, Maxie (or Archie in Alpha Sapphire), turns up himself with another admin. Steven fights beside you: it's a double battle, two against two, with Steven's Metang (Lv 42), Skarmory (Lv 43) and Aggron (Lv 44) on your side. Maxie's partner is Tabitha in Omega Ruby; Archie's is Matt in Alpha Sapphire.

Beaten, the team gives up on the rocket fuel and leaves the Space Center. Now dive for the Seafloor Cavern from Route 128 (see Part 15).
