Take the Elevator Key to the Pyrite Building. A trainer now stands guard by the giant lift on the ground floor: Cipher's orders are that nobody gets on.

The Under

The Under is an old city buried beneath Pyrite Town, with its own shops, hotel, subway and Colosseum. Cipher Admin Venus runs it from her TV studio, and her face is on every screen. A floating platform stands in the middle of town; its slot takes disks, and each disk sends it a different way.

{{tables:THE_UNDER}}

The Rare Candies are under the newsreader's desk in the TV studio, and the Ultra Balls are on the subway stairway's landing. Ein File F, the Subway Key, the Maingate Key and the U-Disk all turn up as you chase Venus (see below). The Power Up Part goes straight to Nett.

{{trainers:THE_UNDER}}

Spies in the Under

As soon as you arrive, every screen in the Under lights up. Venus, all pink and smiles, warns the town that there are spies about and tells everyone to turn them in within thirty seconds. A kid nearby just laughs: nobody down here would tell Venus a thing. Welcome to the Under.

The Junk Shop

In the Junk Shop, a boy can tell you're not from down here, so you can't be a friend of Venus. He gives you the Power Up Part and tells you to take it to Nett, whose house has the dish on the roof. On the shop's TV, a man in a suit called Nascour is talking with Venus and a scientist, Ein. Nascour tells Ein his Shadow Pokémon research must go on, and tells Venus to keep the Under in line. Then the screen goes dark.

The Kids Grid

Nett won't talk to strangers, unless they've got the Power Up Part. When you show it to him, he lets you in: he and his friends are the Kids Grid, and they hate the way Venus runs the town on TV. With the part, their network is back up. Megg, who keeps the Kids Grid's address book, takes your P*DA number so they can send you anything they hear, and Bitt laughs that Venus thinks everyone in the Under loves her.

Then there's a shout from outside: they've caught the spy! It's Silva, and the Under's Cipher peons have locked him in a cage.

{{journal:orre_m8b}}

Silva in a cage

Silva calls through the bars: deal with the two peons guarding him first, then come back.

{{battle:THE_UNDER:Cipher Peon Kloak|By Silva's cage.}} {{battle:THE_UNDER:Cipher Peon Dagur|Right after Kloak.}}

The cage's door is locked and the peons have the key, but Silva says not to worry about him. He snatched something off a peon before they grabbed him: the R-Disk. With the F-Disk Dakim dropped on Mt. Battle, F sends the floating platform forward and R sends it right, and right is Venus's studio. Go!

{{journal:orre_m8c}}

Venus's studio

Slot the disks in and ride the floating platform to the TV studio. The studio lights are blazing, and Venus is about to go on air. She soon works out that you're the spies, on her big day of all days. Very well: let all of Orre watch her win.

{{battle:THE_UNDER:Cipher Admin Venus|In the TV studio · a story battle.}}

This is not how her show ends! Venus spins round and runs down the studio's back stairs, dropping a file as she goes: Ein File F. Before you follow, look under the newsreader's desk for 3 Rare Candies.

{{journal:orre_m8d}}

After Venus: the old subway

Down the stairs, Venus shouts that you'll never catch her, because the subway is hers. The subway stairway is full of trainers, and four of them have Shadow Pokémon: Frena's Gligar, Liaks's Stantler, Lonia's Piloswine and Nelis's Sneasel. There are 4 Ultra Balls on the landing.

At the subway station, a man in dark glasses says a woman in pink just tore past with two of those uniforms and took the tracks on foot. The train runs to the old depot, but its cab needs a key. Venus has dropped it outside the station: the Subway Key. You catch up with her by the tracks, but she just says goodbye and runs off over the tracks with her peons.

Turn the Subway Key in the train's cab and ride to the far station. Two Cipher Peons there are setting a charge on a door to stop you, but it goes off too soon and blows the door wide open. In the rubble lies the Maingate Key, the key to the Shadow Pokémon Lab's main gate. Behind the crates is a disk marked U: the U-Disk. The train can take you back to the station.

Up to the windmill

Slot the U-Disk into the floating platform and it rises up and up, through a hatch, and stops inside Pyrite's windmill. On a ledge lies another Time Flute. Then the platform sinks back down to the Under.

{{journal:orre_m9}}

Under Colosseum

The Under has its own Colosseum, with a Pokémon Center and a Poké Mart at its door. Its tournament opens to you once you have three badges and have driven Dakim off Mt. Battle. Sign up at the desk: three rounds, then the Under Colosseum's champion, Carben. There's no badge here, and no game before the final. Win it and the Under's crown is yours, and the way to Cipher's lab opens. Lose, and your Pokémon are healed and you start over.

{{trainers:UNDER_COLOSSEUM:Gym}}

The three rounds, in order: Piliot, Nistil, Hileon.

Shadow Pokémon Lab

Cipher makes its Shadow Pokémon in a secret lab, reached from The Under once you've won the Under Colosseum. Its main gate opens with the Maingate Key. Inside are three floors of researchers and Cipher Peons, and Admin Ein at the bottom.

{{tables:SHADOW_PKMN_LAB}}

The Full Heals are by the fence and the Revives round the back of the lab. The PP Ups are hidden behind the 1F PC's wall. Bulbasaur, Charmander and Squirtle aren't part of this visit.

{{trainers:SHADOW_PKMN_LAB}}

1F: the Card Key and the shutter

The lift down won't move without a Card Key. Beat Researcher Myron and he bolts, dropping his Card Key. A shutter is down over one of the doors: one of the computers has a big switch marked SHUTTER that raises it. Look for a sealed case too: it's a DNA sample, labelled Aipom.

B1F: the Down St. Key

A second DNA sample here is labelled Murkrow. The Down St. Key lies on a table: it unlocks the lift down to B2F. There are 2 Elixirs on this floor.

B2F: the analyzer

The third DNA sample is labelled Forretress. Aipom, Murkrow, Forretress: that's the password for the analyzer's door, which asks for three Pokémon. When you beat Researcher Kotan, he shouts for the alarm, and sirens wail through the lab. Skrub, from the Relic Cave, is down here too, and ready this time.

{{battle:SHADOW_PKMN_LAB:Cipher Peon Skrub|On B2F. If you snagged his Hitmontop in the Relic Cave, he has a Meditite in its place.}}

Behind the analyzer door, Ein stands among his machines. His Shadow Pokémon are perfect fighting machines, he says, and you've been setting them free.

{{battle:SHADOW_PKMN_LAB:Cipher Admin Ein|Behind the analyzer door · a story battle.}}

Ein can't believe it, but he says this changes nothing: Cipher's plan is already in motion. He flees through a back door. On his desk is a Data ROM, and a Full Restore is on this floor too.

The Under (again): the Data ROM

Take the Data ROM to Nett. He cracks it: it's a list of every Shadow Pokémon Cipher made! Someone has erased most of it, but what's left points to one place, Realgam Tower. Your bike can now take you there from Phenac City or the Desert Trail.

Snagem Hideout

Team Snagem's old hideout stands in the desert, in ruins since the night you ran off with the Snag Machine. Snagem left its cold halls behind, and Gym Leader Pryce has made them a Gym. He waits in the back hall, and he only takes challengers with three badges once Nett has Ein's Data ROM.

{{tables:SNAGEM_HIDEOUT}}

The items lie around the old hideout: TM120 Psychic behind the stairs, a PP Up in the old jail cell, Hyper Potions behind a broken vending machine, and Full Heals in a corner of the back hall. The locked box needs a key you get five years on. The Shadow Pokémon here all belong to later visits.

Pryce

Pryce wants to see a trainer's warmth. As at every Gym, he has a game for you first; win it, then the battle begins.

{{battle:SNAGEM_HIDEOUT:Pryce|In the back hall, after his game.}}

Pryce admits he shouldn't judge a book by its cover. The Snagem Badge, your fourth, is yours, with TM143 Blizzard.

The Tower Halls

The four keys open the giant door to the Tower Halls. Trainers line the way, and the Mystery Troop are back: Verde, Rosso and Bluno each battle you again. The one you beat in Phenac has lost his Shadow Pokémon to you; the other two still have theirs.

The right-hand lift's doors slide open, and out steps a familiar face: Gonzap, the boss of Team Snagem. Evice pays better than you'd think, he says. Nothing personal!

Cipher's Colosseum

The lift at the end of the Tower Halls goes up to the roof, to Cipher's Colosseum. Once the matches start there is no way to heal, so stock up first. Sign up with the host and take your place: four matches, then Nascour, then Cipher's leader, one after another.

Nascour, Cipher's director, has failed his master. Then the floor of the arena splits open, and up rises... Phenac's mayor, Es Cade! He calls Nascour a fool and tears away the kindly mayor's face. Evice can't believe it: his Cipher, his tower. Rui is overjoyed. Cipher is finished, and every Shadow Pokémon can be saved now.

Five years later

The roof of Realgam Tower falls silent. Evice is led away, and without its leader, Cipher falls apart. Rui is going home to Agate to help her grandfather at the Relic Stone, and she makes you promise to come and see them.

Then the story jumps five years ahead. Orre has grown quiet. Phenac has a new mayor, the kids of the Under run a TV station in Pyrite, and Realgam Tower's lights are dark. But a message arrives on your P*DA from Professor Krane at the Pokémon HQ Lab: please come at once. You arrive at the HQ Lab, and the second half of the story begins (see Part 5).
