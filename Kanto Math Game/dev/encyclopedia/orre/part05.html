Five years after Evice fell on Realgam Tower's roof (Part 4), Professor Krane's message brings you to the Pokémon HQ Lab. The second half of the journey, the story of XD: Gale of Darkness, starts at the lab's front door.

What carries over. Your team, your four badges and your Snag Machine all come with you. Cipher is making Shadow Pokémon again, and your Journal opens new pages for this half of the story.

Pokémon HQ Lab

The Pokémon HQ Lab is Professor Krane's research centre near Gateon Port; you can ride there by bike from Gateon Port or Phenac City. Its team is working on a way to purify Shadow Pokémon. The lab has its own Pokémon Center counter, a battle hall downstairs where the staff test new machines, and a Battle CD player.

Battle CD 33 sits on a bookshelf, but only after the story's end on Citadark Isle (Part 8).

Professor Krane is taken

As you ride up, three men in Cipher uniforms burst out of the lab dragging an old man in a white coat: Professor Krane. Their boss wants to know about his Purify Chamber. Lily, who runs the lab with him, comes running and begs you to stop them.

{{battle:POKEMON_HQ_LAB:Spy Naps|Outside the lab. His Teddiursa is a Shadow Pokémon: snag it.}}

You win, and Naps loses his Teddiursa to your Snag Machine, but the men still shove the Professor into a car and race off down the pipe road.

Lily and the Purify Chamber

Inside, Lily thanks you for trying. Professor Krane was close to finishing the Purify Chamber, a machine that can open a Shadow Pokémon's heart, and Cipher mustn't get it. The chamber still needs one Machine Part, and Gateon Port's Parts Shop should have one: ask for Perr. Jovi, a little girl who's always helping at the lab, decides she's coming along.

{{journal:orre_m10c}}

Around the lab, a Lab Assistant explains the battle hall downstairs, and Supertrainer Aferd tests the lab's training data with a single battle (a Lv 35 Sentret). The Battle CD player plays the Battle CDs you find all over Orre: each one is a battle puzzle, with a team lent to you for it.

The Purify Chamber. Once it's working, it stands on the lab's lower floor. It has nine sets: a Shadow Pokémon goes in the middle of a set and up to four of your other Pokémon in a ring round it. The better the ring's Pokémon work together, the faster the Shadow Pokémon's heart opens as you travel. See the Shadow Pokémon page for the details.

Gateon Port

Gateon Port is Orre's harbour town. Ships come and go from its harbour, and out on the islands stands the lighthouse. It has a Pokémon Center, a Poké Mart, the Parts Shop and the sailors' Krabby Club. The movable bridges out to the islands are stuck in the raised position when you first arrive.

{{journal:orre_s1}} {{tables:GATEON_PORT}}

Battle CD 06 lies on a desk in the Krabby Club.

Gateon's trainers battle you two against two when you talk to them:

  • Navigator Berk, at the sailors' Krabby Club, wants a battle before he lets you in: Wingull and Lotad, both Lv 37.
  • Chaser Laken races the ferries along the pier: Swablu and Feebas, both Lv 37.
  • Sailor Bost: Whismur and Marill, both Lv 37. Beat him for TM240 Attract.
  • Casual Guy Cyle waits to be the first across the bridges once they work: a Lv 37 Taillow and a Lv 38 Shadow Ledyba.
  • Bodybuilder Kilen is up on the lighthouse roof once the bridges work: a Lv 37 Zubat and a Lv 38 Shadow Poochyena.

Cyle and Kilen each have a Shadow Pokémon, so have Poké Balls ready to snag them.

A bully on the pier

As you and Jovi walk into town, Jovi runs straight into a big man, Thug Zook, who threatens her with his Zangoose. A gentleman, Mr. Verich, steps in, and his companion Ardos calmly tells Zook his Zangoose is in no state to fight. Zook storms off, promising this isn't over. Mr. Verich warns that Orre is full of surprises and leaves with Ardos. Jovi shrugs it off and heads for the Parts Shop.

The Parts Shop

Perr runs the Parts Shop. There's a Machine Part in the stockroom, but his dad, Makan, has the key, and Makan has gone to Kaminko's House up the road north of town, where he looks after the old inventor's machines.

Around the port

A sailor has heard that Cipher is back, with new Shadow Pokémon and a ship nobody can find. A woman says the harbour has been busy since the big ships started coming in again. An old man in one house used to row over to the shops every morning, and an old woman in another doesn't like the big grey ships she sees sailing out past the rocks. The herbalist lives in the small house; his Munchlax has a habit of getting loose (see Part 6).

Kaminko's House

Kaminko's House belongs to Dr. Kaminko, an inventor whose house is full of half-finished machines. You reach it by bike from Gateon Port. Inside are his workshop, a crane floor where a giant mechanical Kyogre stands half built, and a storeroom underground that Jovi has claimed as her secret base.

{{tables:KAMINKO_HOUSE}}

Six Battle CDs are tucked around the house: two in the workshop, three on the crane floor's hook platforms and one in the storeroom.

Chobin at the door

Dr. Kaminko's assistant, Chobin, guards the front door and takes you for a burglar.

{{battle:KAMINKO_HOUSE:Chobin|Outside the house, the first time.}}

Beaten, he realises you're just a kid and lets you in. Makan is in the workshop. When he hears that Perr sent you for Lily's Machine Part, he heads back to the shop with the key, and says he'll fix Gateon's movable bridges on the way.

Go back to the Parts Shop and Makan hands you the Machine Part, free of charge. The bridges to the islands are down at last.

The Pokémon HQ Lab again

Bring the Machine Part to Lily and Jovi runs it down to the chamber. Then Datan rushes in: he traced Cipher's car to the old Shadow Pokémon Lab out in the desert, the lab Cipher abandoned five years ago. Cipher has opened it again, and Lily asks you to bring the Professor home.

{{journal:orre_m11}}
One thing first. The road to the Cipher Lab leaves from Gateon Port, but you can only ride out there once you hold the Gateon Badge. The Gym is in the lighthouse.

The Gateon lighthouse

With the bridges down, you can walk out to the islands and the lighthouse. Jasmine keeps it now, and its rooms are her Gym. A Steel-type Leader, as the keeper says.

{{journal:orre_m10d}}

Three of the lighthouse's own Pokémon guard the stairs, one on each floor: an Electabuzz (Lv 36) on the ground floor, an Electrode (Lv 37) on 2F and an Ampharos (Lv 38) on 3F. Battle each one to get past it. Jasmine is up on the roof and won't battle you until all three have been beaten. As in every Orre Gym, you play the Gym's math game first, then battle the Leader.

{{battle:GATEON_PORT:Jasmine|On the lighthouse roof, after the three guardians.}}

Jasmine gives you the Gateon Badge, your fifth, and TM265 Iron Tail. The road to the Cipher Lab is open.

A side page: the wishing star. Now that five years have passed, the Journal has a side page about a wishing star seen near Agate Village's Relic Stone. Visit the Relic Forest when you're nearby: something is waiting there.
