Cipher Lab

The Cipher Lab is the old Shadow Pokémon Lab in the desert, the one Cipher left behind five years ago. Cipher has opened it again, and Professor Krane is somewhere inside. You ride there from Gateon Port once you hold the Gateon Badge. Inside are three floors: 1F, B1F and B2F.

The Hexagon Brothers

Six Cipher Peons guard the grounds outside: Resix, Browsix, Yellosix, Greesix, Blusix and Purpsix, the Hexagon Brothers, each named for a colour. Every one of them carries a Shadow Pokémon (a Houndour, a Baltoy, a Mareep, a Seedot, a Spheal and a Gulpin), so bring plenty of Poké Balls.

Down through the lab

On 1F, a Revive and an Ether lie among the peons and researchers. On B1F, Cipher Peons Nexir and Solox have a Shadow Spinarak and a Shadow Numel. The lift down to B2F is locked and reads an ID Card: the card is lying on a table on B1F, with three Super Potions and a Great Ball nearby. On B2F, Cipher Peon Cabol has a Shadow Carvanha and R&D Klots a Shadow Shroomish.

Professor Krane and Lovrina

Professor Krane is tied to a chair on B2F, with a Cipher Peon standing guard. It's Naps, the spy from outside the HQ Lab, and this time he says he has a real team.

{{battle:XD_CIPHER_LAB:Naps|On B2F, guarding the Professor.}}

With Naps out of the way, the Professor is free. Cipher wanted the Purify Chamber's secrets, he tells you, and they kept talking about "XD001", a Shadow Pokémon unlike any other. As you turn to leave, a woman with long pink hair blocks the way, R&D Tekot behind her: Cipher Admin Lovrina. She invites you to join Cipher and help her with XD001. When you say no, she battles you instead.

{{battle:XD_CIPHER_LAB:Lovrina|On B2F. Her Delcatty is a Shadow Pokémon.}}

Lovrina storms off, saying XD001 doesn't need the Professor any more. Battle CD 42 and a Leaf Stone are left on the table by Krane's chair.

The Pokémon HQ Lab: the Professor is home

Back at the HQ Lab, Lily is overjoyed to have the Professor back. The Purify Chamber is nearly done, and he'll finish it. He has one more favour to ask: take his research data, the Data ROM, to Nett at the ONBS building in Pyrite Town, and keep it out of Cipher's hands.

Talk to Professor Krane too: he gives you an Eevee, a good partner that can become many things.

Pyrite Town, five years on

Pyrite Town has a TV station now, ONBS, run by the kids who once fought Cipher from The Under. Its duel square is busy again, and seven trainers around town now want a two-against-two battle:

  • Rogue Cail, who still calls Pyrite his town: Ralts, Snubbull, Seedot and Houndour, all Lv 41.
  • Chaser Labet: Swinub, Bagon, Murkrow and Clamperl, all Lv 41.
  • Bodybuilder Doby: Machop and Tentacool, Lv 40.
  • Hunter Raling: Zigzagoon, Whismur, Aipom and Dunsparce, Lv 40.
  • Worker Dobit, on his break: Spoink, Magnemite and Numel, Lv 40.
  • Chaser Finol: Shuppet, Ledyba, Remoraid and Togepi, Lv 40.
  • Rider Dert: Slugma, Natu and Oddish, Lv 40.

ONBS Building

ONBS is Orre's news station, a building in Pyrite Town with a lobby, 2F, 3F and a satellite dish on the roof. You can go in once Professor Krane is safe. Secc on 2F tells you Nett is up on the roof with the dish.

{{tables:XD_ONBS}}

Battle CD 24 is on the reporter's bookshelf in the lobby and Battle CD 21 on Secc's bookshelf on 2F. A Revive sits behind the make-up table on 3F. The Ether on 2F and the HP Up on 3F only turn up once Cipher has moved in.

Kandee

A little girl called Kandee is wandering about 2F, looking for her mother, who works in the building. She takes your hand and comes with you. Her mother is on 3F, and gives you a Soothe Bell for bringing her daughter back.

Nett and Commander Exol

On the roof, Nett has heard all about you. He takes the Data ROM: decoding it will take a while. In the meantime, he says, Duking has found something called Poké Spots, places where wild Pokémon appear right here in Orre. Duking is at the Rock Poké Spot, off the Desert Trail.

Then Cipher storms the building. Peons now stand on every floor, and a tall man in a Cipher coat has Nett and Bitt cornered on the roof: Commander Exol. The Data ROM is Cipher's now, he says, and so is ONBS.

{{journal:orre_m12}} {{trainers:XD_ONBS}} {{battle:XD_ONBS:Exol|On the roof. His Mawile is a Shadow Pokémon.}}

Exol shrugs off the loss: Cipher already has what it needs from this place. He storms off with the Data ROM. Nett has found something, though: the S.S. Libra, the ship that vanished, was taken by Cipher. And Cipher is planning something in Phenac City. He'll warn the mayor, and asks you to help a family on the roof first.

Rock Poké Spot

Poké Spots are where wild Pokémon turn up in Orre. There are three, and Duking found them. The first, the Rock Poké Spot, is off the Desert Trail. Duking is here with Pofty, a reporter from ONBS, telling the story of how he found them: fifteen years of looking. Put out Poké Snacks, and wild Pokémon come. He'll meet you at the second one, an oasis near Pyrite Town.

{{journal:orre_m11c}} {{tables:ORRE_POKE_SPOTS}}

These are the Pokémon that come to eat at this spot. Nothing appears unless there are snacks on the tray.

Poké Snacks

Each spot has a snack tray that holds up to ten Poké Snacks. The first time you use a tray, Duking has left a note on it and ten snacks to start you off. Fill the tray, then go somewhere else. After you've walked a while, a wild Pokémon comes to eat, and your P*DA's Spot Monitor beeps: slow beeps for a common Pokémon, fast beeps for a rare one. Go back to the spot to battle it and try to catch it. It keeps eating as long as there are snacks, and leaves when they're gone.

Sometimes a Munchlax comes instead and gobbles the whole tray. It belongs to the herbalist in Gateon Port: tell him and he gives you ten Poké Snacks and a herb. Trainers you beat around Orre now and then give you a Poké Snack too.

Oasis Poké Spot

The Oasis Poké Spot is a pool of water in the desert, a short ride from Pyrite Town. Duking is waiting: Pokémon love the water here. The last spot is a cave beyond Agate Village.

{{tables:OASIS_POKE_SPOT}}

Duking's trades

Duking would love a Trapinch, a Surskit and a Wooper, and gives you something rare for each: a Meditite for the Trapinch (it knows Dynamic Punch, Confusion, Shadow Ball and Detect), a Shuckle for the Surskit (Toxic, Substitute, Sludge Bomb and Encore) and a Larvitar for the Wooper (Sandstorm, Dragon Dance, Bite and Outrage). Trapinch comes to the Rock Poké Spot, Surskit to this one and Wooper to the Cave Poké Spot.

Cave Poké Spot

The Cave Poké Spot is in a cave, a ride from Agate Village. As you arrive, two familiar faces are already there: Folly and Trudly, Miror B.'s old peons. Folly has a Miror B. Radar, a gadget that finds Miror B. anywhere, and it's beeping. They spot you and run.

{{tables:CAVE_POKE_SPOT}}

Miror B.

Then Miror B. himself dances in. The cave has groove, he says, and you look like trouble.

{{battle:CAVE_POKE_SPOT:Miror B.|In the cave. His Voltorb is a Shadow Pokémon.}}

He loses his groove and dances off with Folly and Trudly, and drops something: the Miror B. Radar.

The Miror B. Radar. From now on, any Shadow Pokémon you beat without snagging it goes to Miror B. It leaves the place where you met it and joins his team. Each day he dances at one of the three Poké Spots; use the radar from your Bag to find out which. Battle him there and snag them back.

S.S. Libra

This is a side trip. The S.S. Libra is the ship Cipher took, now stranded in the deep desert sand. On the ONBS roof, after Exol, an old man asks for your help: his granddaughter's Bonsly was on board. No scooter can cross the deep sand, they say.

{{journal:orre_m12b}}

Gateon's Parts Shop fixes anything. Perr says his grandpa could do it, since he rebuilds engines, and he's at Kaminko's House, down on the crane floor. Chobin guards the way down.

{{battle:KAMINKO_HOUSE:Chobin@2|Outside Kaminko's House, the second time.}}

Chobin gives you a Sun Stone and lets you through. Perr's grandfather fits your scooter with wide tyres and a stronger engine: now it can cross any sand in Orre. Ride to the S.S. Libra from Gateon Port.

{{tables:SS_LIBRA}}

Battle CD 18 and an Iron are on the deck. Two PP Ups are in the first hold, a Max Ether in the second and a Fire Stone in an item box in the third.

{{trainers:SS_LIBRA}}

On the bridge, a huge man in a Cipher coat is shouting at two peons for losing a Pokémon on a ship stuck in the sand: Cipher Admin Gorigan. He leaves for the Key Lair, and Peon Snidle sends Smarton after you. There's a bed on the bridge where you can rest.

Deep in the third hold, something small is hiding behind the crates: the girl's Bonsly. It hops into your arms. Take it back to her on the ONBS roof, and her grandfather thanks you.

Once the S.S. Libra is done, a Bonsly may also come to eat at your Poké Spots, until you have one.
