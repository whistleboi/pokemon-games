Both of the games behind Orre's story have their own after-stories, and in this game they're side stories you can play during the second half of your journey, before or after the League. Colosseum's begins as soon as the five years have passed, with a tip from Nett, and ends deep under the Under. XD's begins once Greevil is beaten. None of them is needed for the League, and the Journal keeps a side page for each.

Colosseum's after-story

A rumour in Pyrite

Visit Nett at the Kids Grid in the Under. He calls you champ and passes on a tip: a kid in Pyrite Town saw Cail battle someone with a Shadow Pokémon. The kid is hiding behind a bookcase in one of the houses up the hill.

{{journal:orre_s7}}

Find the kid behind the bookcase. He whispers that he saw Cail battle a man with a scary Pokémon, all dark and glowing, and that Cail hangs about at the bottom of town. Rogue Cail won't talk until you beat him. Win, and he admits it: the man was one of Team Snagem's, and Snagem has moved back into its old hideout in the desert, along with half the trainers you let get away.

The Snagem raid

Head back to the Snagem Hideout. It's full again: Team Snagem's members are back in business, and among them are Cipher's old trainers with the Colosseum Shadow Pokémon you missed. Each of those trainers is there only while their Shadow Pokémon is still unsnagged, so this is your chance to catch up. One of them may be a Cipher Peon in a coloured suit, with a Shadow Bayleef, Quilava or Croconaw you haven't snagged yet.

{{journal:orre_s7b}}

Then go back to Nett. Snagem's return doesn't surprise him, and he sends you to Gurks, a man who knows things. Gurks hangs out on the balcony of the Junk Shop's upstairs in the Under. He wants a battle first, with his two bored Wailord. Beat him and he tells you he battled a peon in a bright suit, with a Shadow Pokémon, who was heading for the old lab out past Phenac. If you still haven't snagged all three of Bayleef, Quilava and Croconaw, a coloured Cipher Peon is waiting on the lowest floor of the Shadow Pokémon Lab with the next one.

Once you've battled Gurks, Gonzap himself is waiting in the hideout's Back Hall. Snagem is his again, and he's not giving it up.

Deep Colosseum

Take the D-Disk to the floating platform in the Under and slot it in. The platform shudders and sinks, down past the Under's floor, down and down, to the Deep Colosseum. Its way out leads up to Realgam Tower.

{{journal:orre_s7c}} {{tables:DEEP_COLOSSEUM}}

Talk to the desk to pick a round. Each round is four double battles in a row, and your team is healed between battles. Lose one and the round is over: you're healed and back at the desk, and next time the round starts from its first battle. Win all four and you get the round's prize, and the next round opens. You can play a round you've won again, just for the battles.

Fein, the master of disguise

Once you've snagged every other Shadow Pokémon from Colosseum's story, a shady man turns up at the Outskirt Stand. He knows you think you've snagged them all. Then he throws off his coat: he's Cipher Peon Fein, master of disguise, and he kept one last Shadow Pokémon hidden, a Shadow Togetic. It's a double battle, and Togetic leads his team of six.

The Wishing Star and Ho-Oh

Two side pages lead to the Relic Forest by Agate Village. Once the five years have passed, visit the Relic Stone there: the Shadow Pokémon you saved gather round it, a shooting star falls into the forest, and Jirachi, the Wish Pokémon, wakes up.

{{journal:orre_s3}}

And once every Shadow Pokémon in Orre has been snagged, and every kind of Shadow Pokémon has been purified at least once, a rainbow arcs over the Relic Stone and Ho-Oh comes down, drawn by all the hearts you opened. You may keep some of your Shadow Pokémon as they are: it's enough that each kind has been purified once.

XD's after-story

Eagun's challenge

Once Greevil is beaten, Eagun is waiting outside the Pokémon HQ Lab. He heard you beat Greevil himself, so you've earned one more battle, with him. His Pichu, Pikachu and Raichu haven't lost their spark.

The Orre Colosseum's seven rounds

Back at the Orre Colosseum, a new desk in the lobby runs seven rounds of four double battles, against the toughest trainers in Orre. They work like the Deep Colosseum's: your team is healed between battles, and a loss ends the round. The last battle of each round is someone you know from the second half of your journey.

Silent Pokémon

When you win your first round at the Orre Colosseum, your P*DA beeps with an e-mail from Beluh: something is wrong with Agate Village's Pokémon. Some of them have stopped making any sound at all, starting with Senilor's Wobbuffet. Senilor lives just down the hill from the elder's house. His son put a little machine on Wobbuffet, a "Pokémon Translator", and it hasn't made a sound since. Follow the trail of the translators, which leads out to Dr. Kaminko, and help the village's Pokémon find their voices. When they're all chattering again, Beluh thanks you with a Lucky Egg: a Pokémon holding it learns more from every battle.

{{journal:orre_s6d}}

XD001's heart

The Shadow Lugia you snagged from Greevil, XD001, can't be purified at the Relic Stone, and not even a Time Flute reaches it. Only the HQ Lab's Purify Chamber can: all nine sets must be at Max Tempo at once before it will take XD001. Once purified, it can learn Feather Dance and Psycho Boost too. See Shadow Pokémon for how the chamber works.

Miror B.'s last Shadow Pokémon

Once you hold Miror B.'s radar, every XD Shadow Pokémon you beat without snagging it goes to Miror B., who dances at a different Poké Spot each day with the ones you missed. Snag every one of XD's Shadow Pokémon, from him or from their trainers, and after Greevil's defeat Miror B. is waiting at the door of Gateon Port's lighthouse. He has one more: in the Key Lair, while you were busy, he took the best one for himself, a Shadow Dragonite. It's the last Shadow Pokémon there is, and you'll have to out-dance him for it.
