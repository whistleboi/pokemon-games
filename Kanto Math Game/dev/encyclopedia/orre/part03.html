Agate Village

Agate Village is a green, quiet place by the falls in the north of Orre. Rui's grandparents live here: her grandfather Eagun is the village elder. The village has a Pokémon Center, a Poké Mart and a Pokémon Day Care, and the Agate Gym. The Relic Cave is here too, with the Relic Stone, and the bike can take you on to the Relic Forest.

{{tables:AGATE_VILLAGE}}

The Quick Claw lies on the ground in the village. Ein File C turns up by the Relic Stone after the Relic Cave (see below). Battle CD 14 is in the house in the middle of the village, under its Wobbuffet; Battle CDs are played at the Pokémon HQ Lab, five years on.

The elder's house

Ride into the village and visit the elder's house. Rui runs to her grandparents, Eagun and Beluh. Eagun notices you straight away: a trainer with a sharp look in the eye. Rui tells them all about the Shadow Pokémon and their shut hearts. Then a man bursts in: men in strange outfits have pushed right past him and into the Relic Cave! Eagun won't have anybody touching the Relic Stone while he's around, and he rushes off.

Until now, the man at the cave's mouth has kept everyone out on the elder's orders. Now he lets you through.

Relic Cave

The Relic Cave opens off the village. Three Cipher Peons have come through it, and at the back Eagun lies beaten on the floor. He says that peon's Shadow Pokémon is no ordinary Pokémon.

Skrub

The peon who beat Eagun is Skrub, and now it's your turn. Rui spots the dark aura on his Hitmontop.

Skrub runs, swearing that Cipher always gets its way, and drops a file on his way out. Eagun gets back on his feet. You've got real strength, he says, and asks you home: there's something you should know about the Relic Stone.

Agate Village (again)

The Relic Stone

Back at the elder's house, Beluh thanks you both for saving her husband. She explains that the Relic Stone can open a Shadow Pokémon's heart, so bring your Shadow Pokémon there. She also has news for Rui: an e-mail came, saying something about Mt. Battle being attacked.

From now on, at the Relic Cave's doorway you can purify at the Relic Stone. Eagun says the stone can open a Shadow Pokémon's heart if you wish it, though some trainers keep their Shadow Pokémon as they are. Choose one to purify, or none: the stone purifies the one you choose, however full its gauge still is, and it becomes an ordinary Pokémon with its own moves back.

Skrub dropped something by the Relic Stone: Ein File C, one of a Cipher scientist's files.

The Agate Gym

Agate Village has a Gym, and it has two Leaders: Blaine and Erika, who battle side by side. Erika tends the garden and Blaine tends the fire. The Gym is only open to you after the Relic Cave.

The hall of gardens

The first room is a hall with three little gardens in it, each with six flower beds and a number to make: 10, 18 and 27. Every bed has a number of its own. Walk over the beds: each one you step on blooms and adds its number to the garden's total. Make the garden's number exactly and it stays in bloom. Go over, and the vines grow back and you start that garden again.

The first garden's beds are 2, 7, 4, 6, 3 and 5; the second's are 8, 3, 6, 4, 9 and 5; the third's are 9, 5, 12, 7, 8 and 6. There's more than one way to make each number. For example: 7 + 3 = 10, 9 + 4 + 5 = 18, and 12 + 9 + 6 = 27.

When all three gardens are in bloom, the vines over the far door burn away, and you can walk through to the Leaders' garden.

Blaine and Erika

Blaine is waiting, ready when Erika is. Before you battle, the Leaders have a game for you; win it, then the battle begins.

{{battle:AGATE_VILLAGE:Blaine|In the Leaders' garden, after the hall of gardens and their game.}}

Blaine says you've got fire in you after all. The Agate Badge is yours, with TM168 Solar Beam and TM141 Fire Blast.

The Day Care

Agate has a Pokémon Day Care. A Shadow Pokémon left there opens its heart a little as it stays.

Relic Forest

The Relic Forest is a quiet stretch of trees near Agate Village, reached by bike. A Relic Stone stands in the forest too, glowing softly, and the forest feels as if it's waiting for something. Come back five years on: Cipher's files talk about a wishing star falling near the Relic Stone. And once every Shadow Pokémon in Orre has been set free, a rainbow Pokémon may come down here.

{{tables:RELIC_FOREST}}

Mt. Battle

Mt. Battle is a mountain next to Agate Village, built for battling: ten areas, one above the other, with ten trainers in each. Beat every trainer in an area and the staff let you go up. Each area has a rest point at its top that heals your team, and from then on the lobby can send you straight to the next area. There's a reception desk and a nurse in the lobby.

{{tables:MT_BATTLE}}

Dakim attacks

Ride to Mt. Battle and go up to Area 1. A huge man in a Cipher uniform stands over a beaten trainer, Area Leader Vander. He's Dakim, one of Cipher's Admins, and he laughs that this is the best Mt. Battle has. Then he turns on you, and sends out Entei.

{{battle:MT_BATTLE:Cipher Admin Dakim|In Area 1, in a story scene.}}

As Dakim is beaten, two figures drop down from a ledge: Skrub, and a woman in a Cipher uniform. Skrub tells Dakim they're pulling out. Dakim promises to crush you next time, leaps clean off the platform and is gone. Something clatters where he stood: a disk marked F, the F-Disk. Pick it up.

{{journal:orre_m7b}}

Vander's thanks

Vander thanks you for driving Dakim off. He can tell that Cipher's Pokémon have shut hearts, so he gives you the Time Flute: its song opens a Shadow Pokémon's heart in an instant, but it can only be played once. Once he's caught his breath, Vander will be back at his post as Area 1's leader. Come and climb!

{{journal:orre_s2a}}

The climb

Climbing Mt. Battle is a side challenge. Its first areas match where you are now, but the higher areas are far stronger: Areas 7 to 10 have trainers at Lv 41 to 50, and Mt. Battle Master Battlus waits at the top of Area 10. Come back when your team is ready. The lobby's Start over from Area 1 lets you climb the whole mountain again, with every trainer ready for another round.

{{trainers:MT_BATTLE}}

Vander, master of the peak, waits above Battlus only after you become Orre's Champion.

Pyrite Town (again): the Elevator Key

When you're done at Mt. Battle, your Journal sends you back to Pyrite Town. Duking has caught two of Miror B.'s peons, and they're locked up at the sheriff's office.

{{journal:orre_m7c}}

The peons are Reath and Ferma. Ferma grumbles that Duking caught them fair and square; she says she'll wait for Miror B. Reath is fast asleep, with a key on her belt. If you haven't already, take the Jail Key from the sheriff's desk and unlock the cell. Hanging on Reath's belt is a key marked ELEVATOR: ease it off. It's the Elevator Key, and it must work the Pyrite Building's giant lift (see Part 4).
