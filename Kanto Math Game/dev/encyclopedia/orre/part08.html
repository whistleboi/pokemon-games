Cipher Key Lair

The Cipher Key Lair is Cipher's base in Gateon Port. You can ride there from the port once you're the Orre Colosseum's champion. Inside are a maze of corridors (the Labyrinth), the Box Rooms full of crates, the Control Room, and at the top, the hall of Cipher Admin Gorigan.

{{journal:orre_m17}} {{tables:XD_CIPHER_KEY_LAIR}}

The Max Revive in the Box Rooms is behind a steel door marked with a crescent moon. Push one of the crates onto the floor switch and the moon door slides open.

The thug at the door

Thug Zook, the bully from Gateon's pier, is standing in the doorway: the place is Cipher's, he says, and nobody gets by him.

{{battle:XD_CIPHER_KEY_LAIR:Zook|At the door. His Zangoose is a Shadow Pokémon.}}

Zook runs off, but Cipher's guards still block the door. Then Team Snagem turns up. Biden says Gonzap reckons Snagem owes you one, and they'll deal with the guards, once he's seen you battle up close.

{{battle:XD_CIPHER_KEY_LAIR:Biden|Outside the Key Lair. A one-on-one battle.}}

Snagem's grunts rush the guards, and the door is clear. Biden and his friends hold it for you.

{{journal:orre_m17b}}

The Labyrinth

The first floor is a maze of corridors with seven Cipher Peons in it. Lok has a Shadow Beedrill and a Shadow Pidgeotto, Humah a Shadow Paras and a Shadow Growlithe, and Gorog a Shadow Shellder. Boxes along the way hold 3 Ultra Balls, TM126 Thunderbolt, a PP Up, 3 Hyper Potions, 2 Revives and a Rare Candy.

{{trainers:XD_CIPHER_KEY_LAIR:Labyrinth}}

The Box Rooms

The Box Rooms are packed with crates you push around to clear the way. A panel by the stairs marked "Crate Control" slides the crates by the doorway aside, so the way back stays clear, and a "Crate Reset" panel puts every crate back where it started if you get stuck. Snidle has a Shadow Magneton here, and Targ a Shadow Tangela and a Shadow Butterfree.

On a table lies the System Lever, a heavy lever with a plug on the end. Take it. The rooms also hold a Full Restore, an Elixir, 3 Full Heals, an HP Up and, behind the moon door, a Max Revive.

{{trainers:XD_CIPHER_KEY_LAIR:Box Rooms}}

The Control Room

The Key Lair's power panel has an empty slot shaped like a lever. Put the System Lever in and push it all the way up: the voltage needle swings into the red and the whole building shakes. Somewhere nearby, a door bangs open, and out stumbles an old acquaintance from the S.S. Libra, his hair on end.

Three more peons guard the Control Room; Angic has a Shadow Venomoth and a Shadow Weepinbell.

{{trainers:XD_CIPHER_KEY_LAIR:Control Room}} {{journal:orre_m17c}} {{battle:XD_CIPHER_KEY_LAIR:Smarton|In the Control Room. His Arbok is a Shadow Pokémon.}}

Smarton opens the shutter to the stairs up to Gorigan's hall.

Gorigan's hall

Gorigan waits in the middle of his hall, cracking his knuckles.

{{battle:XD_CIPHER_KEY_LAIR:Gorigan|In his hall at the top of the Key Lair. His Primeape and Hypno are Shadow Pokémon.}}

The moment Gorigan is beaten, the big screen on the wall flickers on. A large man in a feathered cloak looks out: Greevil, Grand Master of Cipher. Cipher's true base, he says, is Citadark Isle, behind waves no ship can cross, and there waits XD001, his masterpiece. He dares you to come, and the screen goes dark.

On Gorigan's desk is Cipher's full list of the Shadow Pokémon they made. You copy it into your P*DA, so every last one of them can be tracked down now. A locked door by the lair's entrance now opens from the inside, a shortcut back to the hall.

{{journal:orre_s5}}

Norman's hall

With Gorigan gone, his hall has a new master: Gym Leader Norman. Cipher is gone from the hall, he says, and it's a Gym now. He needs you to hold five badges. Win the Gym's math game, then battle him.

{{journal:orre_m17d}} {{battle:XD_CIPHER_KEY_LAIR:Norman|In Gorigan's old hall.}}

Norman, reminded of his own son, gives you the Key Lair Badge, your sixth, and TM025 Facade.

The Pokémon HQ Lab: the Master Ball

Professor Krane has something for you. He takes you down a staircase even Lily rarely uses. Cipher has gathered on Citadark Isle, he explains, and Greevil's XD001 is a Shadow Pokémon unlike any other. He gives you a Master Ball, the only one there is: it catches any Pokémon without fail. Save it for XD001. Makan and Perr have a robot at Gateon Port that can cross the sea.

That robot is the giant mechanical Kyogre from Kaminko's House. Once you have the Master Ball and the Key Lair Badge, it carries you from Gateon Port's harbour to Citadark Isle. Stock up before you go. On the island, Cipher's own healing machines in the fortress and the Dome heal your team.

Citadark Isle

Citadark Isle is Greevil's island, wrapped in a storm that never lets up. The way up leads through a fortress, then across the volcano's platforms, then through the Hook Hall, and at the top stands the Dome, where Greevil waits with XD001. A navigator at the dock can hardly believe you came all this way on a Kyogre robot, and gives you Battle CD 31.

{{journal:orre_s5b}} {{tables:XD_CITADARK_ISLE}}

A PP Max is tucked among the rocks below the fortress. Battle CD 46 turns up on Greevil's empty seat once he's beaten. The Full Restores and Elixirs in the Terra Cave belong to the post-game, when a ladder on the island opens.

{{trainers:XD_CITADARK_ISLE:Citadark Isle}}

Navigator Abson, outside the fortress, has a Shadow Golduck and a Shadow Sableye.

The fortress

The fortress is the longest stretch, full of trainers and items, with a healing machine. Grupel has a Shadow Magmar and a Shadow Pinsir, Leden a Shadow Scyther and a Shadow Chansey, Litnar a Shadow Kangaskhan and a Shadow Banette, Furgy a Shadow Dodrio and a Shadow Raticate, and Geftal a Shadow Lickitung. Items: 2 Revives and 2 White Herbs behind the crates, 2 Full Restores by the way up, 2 Hyper Potions in the box a Hunter guards, a PP Up, an Elixir, a Max Elixir, 2 more Full Restores at the end of Grason's room, 5 Ultra Balls on the bridge and 3 Hyper Potions.

{{trainers:XD_CITADARK_ISLE:Fortress}}

Lovrina is here too. Greevil scolded her because of you, but she says it doesn't matter: XD001 is complete, and nothing can purify it.

{{battle:XD_CITADARK_ISLE:Lovrina|In the fortress. Her Farfetch'd and Altaria are Shadow Pokémon.}}

The volcano

The way spirals up the volcano. Kolest has a Shadow Rapidash and a Shadow Magcargo, Karbon a Shadow Hitmonchan and Petro a Shadow Hitmonlee. 2 Max Potions wait on a hidden ledge, and 3 Rare Candies at the heart of the spiral.

{{trainers:XD_CITADARK_ISLE:Volcano}}

The Hook Hall

Two more peons stand in the Hook Hall. It holds 3 Max Ethers, 4 Full Heals and 3 Timer Balls, a Max Revive in one cut-off corner and 2 Revives in the other.

{{trainers:XD_CITADARK_ISLE:Hook Hall}}

Outside the Dome

Kolax, with a Shadow Dugtrio, and Kimly stand outside the Dome. Then Cipher's Admins come for you, one after another.

{{trainers:XD_CITADARK_ISLE:Outside the Dome}}

Snattle steps out of the left lift, purple in the face: you humiliated him on live television, and Greevil promised him all of Orre.

{{battle:XD_CITADARK_ISLE:Snattle|Outside the Dome. His Solrock and Starmie are Shadow Pokémon.}}

Next is Ardos, Greevil's guard, the man you saw with Mr. Verich in Gateon Port. The Dome is closed to you, he says.

{{battle:XD_CITADARK_ISLE:Ardos|Outside the Dome. His Swellow, Electabuzz and Snorlax are Shadow Pokémon.}}

Beaten, Ardos lets you in: Father has prepared for you. Last, Gorigan stomps out of the lift for round two.

{{battle:XD_CITADARK_ISLE:Gorigan|Outside the Dome. His Poliwrath and Mr. Mime are Shadow Pokémon.}}

Gorigan runs off down the lift, and the way into the Dome is open.

The Dome's lift down has its own landing, beside the ladder to the Terra Cave; it stays locked until Ardos is beaten. Eldes waits behind the glass wall, and the way to him is Gorigan's lift, open once Gorigan is beaten.

Dome 1F: Eldes

The Dome's first floor has a healing machine, a Max Revive by the wall and a big screen showing the storm round the island, with one word flashing on it: XD001. Two Cipher researchers stand guard. Behind a thick glass wall, Greevil watches you.

{{trainers:XD_CITADARK_ISLE:Dome 1F}}

Eldes, Greevil's other son, was sent by his father to stop you. He apologises, but he must.

{{battle:XD_CITADARK_ISLE:Eldes|On Dome 1F. His Manectric, Salamence, Marowak and Lapras are Shadow Pokémon.}}

Your Pokémon's hearts are strong, Eldes admits; perhaps his father is wrong. He lowers the glass wall.

{{journal:orre_m19}}

Dome 2F: Drake

At the top of the Dome, Gym Leader Drake stands before Greevil's arena. You've climbed all the way to Greevil's door, he says, but first: do you dare challenge an ancient force? He needs six badges. Win the Gym's math game, then battle him.

{{battle:XD_CITADARK_ISLE:Drake|On Dome 2F.}}

Drake gives you the Citadark Badge, your seventh, and TM078 Dragon Claw. Now Greevil will face you.

Greevil and XD001

Greevil says you've ruined everything and shown Cipher to the whole world, but it no longer matters. He calls down XD001, a Shadow Pokémon whose heart, he says, can never be purified, the first of his new army. Something enormous and dark descends from the roof of the Dome: a Shadow Lugia.

{{battle:XD_CITADARK_ISLE:Greevil@1|XD001. Snag it: this is what the Master Ball is for.}}

With XD001 taken, Greevil is furious, and battles you himself with the rest of his Shadow Pokémon.

{{battle:XD_CITADARK_ISLE:Greevil@2|Every one of them is a Shadow Pokémon, the three legendary birds among them.}}

Greevil collapses. Ardos tells his father the helicopter is ready: they'll leave the island and blow it up, with you inside. But Eldes stops him. It's over, he says: your pure heart won out over XD001, and nothing is left of Cipher. He and his father will give themselves up. Greevil agrees. Ardos leaves without a word, and outside, the storm round Citadark Isle breaks and the sun comes out over Orre.

Purifying XD001. XD001's heart is shut tight, and walking alone won't open it. Only the HQ Lab's Purify Chamber can reach it: fill all nine sets at their best (four Pokémon in each ring, each strong against the next), then put XD001 in. The Journal has a side page for it.

Realgam Tower

Realgam Tower is a short ride from Phenac City. It's where the first half of the story ended, with Evice beaten on its roof (see Part 4), and its lights have been dark for five years. With Cipher finished, the Journal points you back here: the tower's Colosseum in the sky has a Gym Leader again.

{{journal:orre_m20}} {{tables:REALGAM_TOWER}}

The Poké Flute is on the table in Mr. Fuji's house.

The tower's square has a Pokémon Center and a Poké Mart. Visitors come from all over Orre to watch a battle here, and a woman's Cubone loves the crowds. Inside, the Realgam Colosseum's desk runs four rounds of four battles, every team at Lv 47, each round paying a cash prize. The Realgam Tower Dome leads into the tower itself.

Realgam Tower Dome

The Realgam Tower Dome is the inside of the tower, where Cipher's Admins held the four coloured keys and the lifts climb to the roof (see Part 4). Cipher's files are still in the tower yard. One note mentions a wishing star near Agate's Relic Stone; another says Project XD's new Shadow Pokémon were to be shipped through Gateon Port.
