Phenac City, taken over

Nett was right: Cipher has come to Phenac City, and this time they're wearing the townsfolk's faces. Everyone you meet insists everything is perfectly normal. The lady at the gate even hands you Battle CDs 01, 07 and 20, perfectly normally. Phenac Stadium is shut, and the Pre Gym is full of Cipher Peons.

{{journal:orre_m13}}

The mayor's secretary

In the mayor's house, the secretary says the mayor is far too busy to see anyone and upstairs is off limits. She's bored, though, and wishes she had some music. A music lover in the house in the north-east of town gives you a Music Disc for her (Battle CD 10 is on his bookshelf too).

Give the secretary the disc and she starts dancing, and you slip upstairs. The mayor's office is empty. On the desk lies an unfinished note to Justy: Cipher means to take Phenac by wearing its people's faces. The secretary catches you snooping, and she's no secretary.

Cipher Peon Pellim, in disguise, battles you with a Lv 41 Sudowoodo and Clamperl, then calls Cipher Peon Fenton, who has Goldeen, Carvanha, Koffing and Mightyena (all Lv 41). With both beaten, look around upstairs: Battle CDs 27, 12 and 32 are under the desk, on the bookshelf and in the cabinet.

Six Justys

Fenton said the Justys would get you, and he meant it: all over town there are men who say they're Justy of the Pre Gym. Talk to each one and he battles you. Beaten, each turns out to be one of the Hexagon Brothers you met at the Cipher Lab.

  • Browsix: Noctowl and Vigoroth, Lv 41. He drops Battle CD 16.
  • Resix: Slugma and Numel, Lv 41. He drops Battle CD 19.
  • Blusix: Horsea and Goldeen (Lv 41) and Beldum (Lv 40). He drops Battle CD 08.
  • Yellosix: Electrike, Chinchou and Magnemite, Lv 41. He drops Battle CD 28.
  • Purpsix: Koffing, Grimer, Tentacool and Zubat, Lv 41.
  • Greesix: Oddish, Cacnea, Shroomish and Lotad (Lv 40) and Pineco (Lv 41).

Prestige Precept Center

The Prestige Precept Center, the Pre Gym, is Phenac's training hall, where every trainer in town practises. Justy is its leader. Now Cipher Peons hold the training floor, and a lift leads down to a basement.

{{tables:PRESTIGE_PRECEPT_CENTER}}

The Shadow Pokémon with no level here belong to the peons in Phenac Stadium (see below). Battle CD 35 is half buried in the basement's sand once Snattle is beaten.

{{trainers:PRESTIGE_PRECEPT_CENTER}}

Cipher Peons Forgs, Kapen, Ezoor and Greck hold the training floor; Greck has a Shadow Swinub. Cipher Peon Fostin is waiting for you too.

{{battle:PRESTIGE_PRECEPT_CENTER:Fostin|On the training floor. His Meowth is a Shadow Pokémon.}}

Beaten, Fostin admits that the real townsfolk are locked in the basement. The lift down needs an Elevator Key, and Cipher Admin Snattle has it.

Phenac Stadium and Snattle

Outside the stadium, Cipher Peon Ertlig says it's closed on Snattle's orders, then battles you (Poochyena, Magnemite, Staryu and Absol, all Lv 41). Inside, the frightened receptionist tells you Cipher has taken over and their boss is down in the arena, and begs you to get them out of Phenac; talk to her and she sends you through. In the arena, five more peons wait, each with a Shadow Pokémon:

  • Cipher Peon Exinn: Seviper, Golbat and Mightyena (Lv 41) and a Lv 42 Shadow Snorunt.
  • Cipher Peon Gonrag: Murkrow and Ariados (Lv 41) and a Lv 42 Shadow Pineco.
  • Cipher Peon Faltly: Chimecho and Stantler (Lv 42) and a Lv 42 Shadow Grimer.
  • Cipher Peon Ezin: Pelipper and Electrike (Lv 42) and a Lv 42 Shadow Spearow.
  • Cipher Peon Egrog, sent by Snattle personally: Hoothoot, Graveler and Gulpin (Lv 42) and a Lv 43 Shadow Seel.

Once Egrog is beaten, Snattle has the stadium to himself, with Marcia cornered. He's thrilled to battle a little hero on live TV.

{{battle:PHENAC_CITY:Snattle|In Phenac Stadium. His Lunatone is a Shadow Pokémon.}}

Embarrassed on national television, Snattle hurries off, and drops the Pre Gym's Elevator Key. Battle CD 44 is left on the empty stadium floor.

{{journal:orre_m13b}}

The Pre Gym's basement

Take the lift down. Two more peons are in the basement: Eloin, with a Shadow Natu, and Fasin, with a Shadow Roselia. Beyond them, the townsfolk Cipher locked up pour out, blinking at the light. Justy thanks you: they were kept down here while the peons wore their faces. The mayor wasn't locked up with them, he says, yet he's been seen back in his office without a scratch. Odd. He gives you his P*DA number and warns you that shady types have been heading out into the desert.

Back on the training floor, Justy wants to thank you the only way he knows.

{{battle:PRESTIGE_PRECEPT_CENTER:Justy|In the Pre Gym, after the townsfolk are free.}}

He gives you TM275 Double Team and a Lum Berry. The Pre Gym's own trainers are back too: Lady Gwin, Rich Boy Dugo and Cooltrainers Botan and Liqui are still up for practice battles.

Phenac, back to normal

With the real townsfolk home, Phenac's trainers want to celebrate with a battle: Fun Old Man Eroll (Hoothoot, Sentret and Zubat, Lv 42), Cooltrainer Equin (Sneasel, Spoink and Spinda, Lv 42), Cooltrainer Quelor (Ledyba, Spoink and Bellossom, Lv 41), Cooltrainer Teslor (Sunflora, Furret and Linoone, Lv 41), Supertrainer Nopel (Kecleon, Azumarill and Yanma, Lv 42) and Matron Kalus (Plusle, Minun and Absol, Lv 42). A runner's Castform has Battle CD 15 stuck to it, and he lets you keep it.

Outskirt Stand, again

Back at the Outskirt Stand, where your journey began, someone is waiting for you.

{{journal:orre_m14b}}

Hordel

Hordel is a researcher who worked for Cipher until he saw what they do. He ran, and took one of their Pokémon with him: a Togepi from their factory, its heart shut tight. He gives it to you to open: a Lv 42 Shadow Togepi, yours to purify. He also tells you Team Snagem is back in its old hideout in the desert, and marks the way on your map.

Miror B. dances in

Folly spots you, and Miror B. is right behind him, ready to see if you can keep up with his rhythm.

{{battle:OUTSKIRT_STAND:Miror B.|At the Outskirt Stand. His Nosepass is a Shadow Pokémon.}}

Once he's gone, Rider Willie, the first trainer you ever battled in Orre, is back with four Pokémon this time: Zigzagoon and Zigzagoon (Lv 41) and Linoone and Linoone (Lv 42).

The Snagem Hideout, again

Hordel's directions get you across the sand to Team Snagem's old hideout in the desert. Snagem is back in business, and its boss, Gonzap, has a score to settle with the kid who walked off with his Snag Machine. (Pryce's Gym in these halls is closed during this half of the story; it opens again once you're Champion.)

{{journal:orre_m15}} {{trainers:SNAGEM_HIDEOUT}}

Grunts Agrev and Jedo watch the way in, and seven more fill the hideout. Inside you'll find 3 Ultra Balls, 2 Hyper Potions behind a broken vending machine, 2 Revives, a Rare Candy, a PP Up in the old jail cell and TM120 Psychic behind the stairs. Gonzap waits in his office in the back hall, with 2 Full Heals in the corner.

{{battle:SNAGEM_HIDEOUT:SNAGEM HIDEOUT: GONZAP|In his office in the back hall.}}

Gonzap admits you've grown, and calls it square. He gives you Gonzap's Key, the key to a locked box downstairs: inside is TM114 Shadow Ball.

Orre Colosseum

The Orre Colosseum is the biggest tournament in Orre, out past the Desert Trail. The way there stays closed until the tournament opens, which it does once Gonzap is beaten. Inside are a Pokémon Center and a Poké Mart, and the receptionist signs you up.

{{journal:orre_m16}} {{tables:ORRE_COLOSSEUM}} {{trainers:ORRE_COLOSSEUM}}

Sign up at the desk and you're taken out to the arena. The challengers walk in one at a time: Hunter Greel, then Rider Herlam, then Navigator Lestor. Then the champion takes the field for the final. Lose a battle and your Pokémon are healed, but you're back in the lobby and the tournament starts over.

{{battle:ORRE_COLOSSEUM:Justy|The final.}}

Win the final and the crowd roars: the Orre Colosseum's crown is yours. Cipher has a base in Gateon Port, and now you're ready to go after it.
