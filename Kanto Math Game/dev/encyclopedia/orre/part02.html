Pyrite Town

Pyrite Town is a rough town of outlaws, built on a hillside with a windmill and a Colosseum. Its duel square is never quiet, and Cipher's people come and go as they please. At the bottom of the hill a bridge leads past the tall Pyrite Building. The Pokémon Center is inside the Colosseum's lobby, and there's a Poké Mart, a hotel, the sheriff's office and Duking's house.

{{tables:PYRITE_TOWN}}

HM Fly lies by the Colosseum's steps. The Jail Key is on the sheriff's desk, and the prisoner gives you TM018 Thief once you let him out. Battle CD 22 is on Duking's bookshelf; Battle CD 40 turns up five years on. They're played at the Pokémon HQ Lab (Part 5 onwards).

A rough welcome

As you ride in, a police officer stops a man by a stall: did he just take something? The man shows his empty hands, and the officer lets him go. As soon as the officer has gone, the thief chuckles at how easy that was. A local warns you to keep your Poké Balls close in this town, and a shopper grumbles that the Mart's prices went up the day the uniforms moved in.

By the Colosseum's steps, someone has dropped a disc: HM Fly. Once you win the Pyrite Badge, your Pokémon can fly you to any town you've been to.

The duel square

Nobody walks through Pyrite's duel square without a fight. Several of the trainers here say a stranger handed them a strong new Pokémon, and Rui can see why: they're Shadow Pokémon. Cipher is giving them away. Beat each trainer and snag the Shadow Pokémon.

Rogue Cail

A thug near the Colosseum tells you the real prize in Pyrite is whatever Rogue Cail has: beat Cail and he hands out an awesome Pokémon. Cail hangs around the bottom of town. Talk to him before you've heard of him and he won't battle a nobody; once the thug has told you about him, he's happy to show you his prize up close. It's a Shadow Furret.

The missing Gear

After Cail, walk past Duking's door and a man in a lab coat stumbles out and falls flat. Silva has taken the Gear right out of the windmill! Without the windmill there's no power, and the Colosseum is dark. Rui thinks Silva must have gone off somewhere with it.

Construction Lot

The Construction Lot is a building site out past Pyrite, with a locked shed at the back. Silva is nowhere to be seen, but something heavy is wedged against the fence: the windmill's Gear. Rui says to take it straight back to Pyrite.

{{tables:CONSTRUCTION_LOT}}

The shed's door stays locked, though someone is moving about inside. It opens long after the story, once you're Orre's Champion.

Pyrite Town (again): the Pyrite Colosseum

Duking's house

Take the Gear to Duking, who looks after the town's kids. He's sad to hear Silva really did take it; he trusted that boy. He asks you to help him set it back in the windmill. The windmill creaks, then turns, and the Pyrite Colosseum has power again. Duking tells you to go up and show the town what a real trainer looks like.

In his house, a boy says Duking used to keep the town in line but hardly comes out now, and Marcia asks you to leave him be: he has a lot on his mind. Before the power went out, Silva was here too, angry that nobody stands up to Miror B. A Battle CD sits on Duking's bookshelf.

{{journal:orre_m4c}}

The tournament

Sign up at the Colosseum's lobby desk. It works like Phenac Stadium: four rounds in a row, a challenge before the final, then the Colosseum's champion, Gym Leader Duking. Lose, and your Pokémon are healed and the tournament starts over.

{{trainers:PYRITE_TOWN:Gym}}

The four rounds, in order: Hoks, Tisler, Vilch, Mirez. Mirez, the fourth, gives you TM175 Toxic as well.

{{battle:PYRITE_TOWN:Duking|The final of the Pyrite Colosseum, after its challenge.}}

Duking says you've given the old town its courage back. The Pyrite Badge is yours, with TM064 Bulk Up. Now HM Fly works too.

Duking's Plusle

Once you have the badge, a man in a Cipher uniform walks up to you in town. He offers a special prize for winners like you, then realises you're the one who's been giving Miror B. so much trouble. His Yanma has the dark aura.

Around Pyrite

The sheriff's office

The sheriff says Pyrite is a rough town, with outlaws on every corner, and lately Cipher has been running things from The Under, the old city below. The Jail Key is lying on his desk. Take it and let the prisoner out of his cell: he gives you TM018 Thief, since he won't be needing it where he's going. Keep the key: you'll need it again (Part 3).

The houses, the fortune teller and the hotel

In the house up the hill, an old couple moved here for the quiet and haven't found any; folk keep their doors shut these days. The fortune teller sees sand, a long ride and a Pokémon with a closed heart. The hotel is fully booked.

Pyrite Building

The Pyrite Building stands by the bridge at the bottom of Pyrite Town. It's Cipher's hideout, full of trainers who work for Miror B. A giant lift on the ground floor goes down to The Under, but it needs a key you don't have yet (Part 3).

{{tables:PYRITE_BUILDING}}

The Shadow Pokémon table lists Pyrite's duel square too: the ones you missed in town can be snagged again here once the building is cleared. Nosepass belongs to the story five years on.

{{trainers:PYRITE_BUILDING}}

Chaser Kai on the ground floor is a later battle: he guards the giant lift once you have the Elevator Key (Part 4).

Silva upstairs

Upstairs, Silva lies beaten on the floor, with two of Miror B.'s peons standing over him. They've been warned about you.

{{battle:PYRITE_BUILDING:Miror B. Peon Reath|Upstairs, in a story scene.}} {{battle:PYRITE_BUILDING:Miror B. Peon Ferma|Right after Reath.}}

Silva asks you to tell Duking he's sorry: he only took the Gear to save Plusle. Then Miror B. appears on a TV screen, laughing. Plusle was the bait, and you took it! If you want her back, come and find him, if your feet can keep up.

The guard

Back on the ground floor, Hunter Doken stands in the way. Before you've beaten Reath and Ferma he won't even talk; now he says nobody gets to Pyrite Cave past him.

{{battle:PYRITE_BUILDING:Hunter Doken|On the ground floor, after the peons upstairs.}}

Beaten, Doken lets you go on to the cave, and says you'll regret it. Your bike can now take you to Pyrite Cave.

Pyrite Cave

Pyrite Cave goes three floors down, and Miror B. has made it his hideaway. Trainers wait on every floor, and some of them carry Shadow Pokémon. At the bottom, music is playing.

{{tables:PYRITE_CAVE}}

TM299 Snatch is in a box in Miror B.'s room. It stays locked while his dancers guard it, and opens once he's beaten.

{{trainers:PYRITE_CAVE}}

Miror B.

In the deepest room, a man with a huge afro is dancing with four Ludicolo. Miror B. stops the music: so this is the famous snag-happy kid. If you want Plusle back, you'll have to dance with him.

{{battle:PYRITE_CAVE:Cipher Admin Miror B.|In Miror B.'s room, at the bottom of the cave.}}

His rhythm broken, Miror B. dances away with his Ludicolo. Behind where they stood, Plusle sits shivering. Silva and Duking burst in, and Duking scoops Plusle up and takes her home to Pyrite.

Once Miror B. is gone, a peon called Mirakle B. steps up. He's Miror B.'s one true successor, he says, and one day he'll have an afro just like his.

{{battle:PYRITE_CAVE:Mirakle B.|In Miror B.'s room, after Miror B.}}

Plusle's thanks

Go and see Duking at his house. He says he owes you everything, and Plusle wants to go with you: he gives you Plusle (Lv 13).

As you head out of the bottom of town, a fortune teller called Fateen stops you. She sees trouble coming to a village by the falls in the north. Rui knows the place: it's Agate Village, where her grandparents live. She wants to make sure they're all right.
