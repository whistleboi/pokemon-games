Realgam Tower (again)

Greevil is beaten and Cipher is finished for good. Seven badges shine in your case, and the last Gym of Orre is waiting where Part 1 of your journey ended: on the roof of Realgam Tower. Ride your bike from Phenac City to Realgam, walk into the tower, and take the lift up to its Colosseum in the sky, the open-air arena where you once faced Evice.

Five years on, the arena belongs to the Orre League. Its host stands at the side of the arena, and beside the arena waits Professor Birch, who is also the Gym Leader here.

Gym Leader Birch

Talk to Birch once you hold seven badges and have beaten Greevil. With Cipher gone, he can go back to his other job, as this Gym's Leader, and he gives you the final test of your journey. If you come too early, he tells you to bring seven badges first. As at every Gym, he has his Gym's math game for you first; win it, then the battle begins.

Birch's team is all about the weather. Three of his six are Castform: one calls up sunshine, one rain and one hail, and each uses Weather Ball, a move that changes its type with the weather. Delibird and Slowbro back them up, and his strongest is Meganium. Like nearly every battle in Orre, it's a double battle.

{{battle:REALGAM_TOWER:Gym Leader Birch}}

Win, and Birch hands you the Realgam Badge, your eighth, with TM193 Weather Ball. With it you may challenge the Elite Four.

The Orre League

With all eight badges, talk to the host in the arena: Realgam Tower: Orre League. The Elite Four are four of Orre's strongest trainers, and you'll know every one of them from your travels. They come out one after another, and after the fourth, the Champion. Every battle here is a double battle.

No rest between battles. The five battles come straight after each other, and your team is not healed in between. If you lose one, a receptionist heals your Pokémon and you start again from the first battle. Stock up on healing items at Realgam's Poké Mart before you sign up.

Elite Four Justy

The first to step into the arena is Justy, the Pre Gym's Leader, who taught you your first steps in battle. Now he teaches you the last lesson before the Champion. His team is a mix of Ground and Rock Pokémon with Azumarill and Cacturne, and it loves a sandstorm and Double Team. Water, Grass and Ice moves do well here.

{{battle:REALGAM_TOWER:Elite Four Justy}}

Elite Four Eagun

Next comes an old friend: Eagun, Rui's grandfather and the elder of Agate Village. Old as he is, the League asked for him, and Rui sends her love. His team is all Pikachu and Raichu, so Ground Pokémon and Ground moves are your best bet.

{{battle:REALGAM_TOWER:Elite Four Eagun}}

Elite Four Miror B.

Miror B. has given up Shadow Pokémon. These days he just dances with his Ludicolo, four of them, plus a Dragonite to close the show, and they all call down the rain. Ludicolo are Water and Grass at once, so Flying, Poison and Bug moves hit them hard. Ice moves are the answer to Dragonite.

{{battle:REALGAM_TOWER:Elite Four Miror B.}}

Elite Four Vander

The last of the Elite Four keeps the peak of Mt. Battle, and today he brings the mountain to you. Vander's team is a heavy one: Blissey, Kingdra, Metagross, Salamence and Tyranitar. Each needs a different answer, so a team with many types of move is best.

{{battle:REALGAM_TOWER:Elite Four Vander}}

Champion Eldes

The Champion of Orre is Eldes. He was Greevil's son and one of Cipher's Admins, but he left all of that behind to guard Orre instead. His team is the strongest in Orre: Tauros, Snorlax, Gengar and Metagross, with Latias and Latios last. Latias and Latios are Dragon and Psychic, so Ice and Dragon moves work well on them.

{{battle:REALGAM_TOWER:Champion Eldes}}

The Hall of Fame

Beat Eldes and you are the Champion of Orre, and your name goes into the Hall of Fame. Your team appears one by one under the spotlight, then all together with you. Then the music stops, your phone rings, and it's Mom: she saw it all on TV, and it's time to pack your bags for a move! After the call, you're back at the Outskirt Stand, where your journey in Orre began.

When you're ready, Mom can move you to a region you haven't played yet, just as when you became Champion anywhere else.

League rematches

Come back to the arena any time and the host signs you up again. In a rematch the Elite Four and Eldes bring their teams back much stronger, with every Pokémon at least Lv 70. Your team is healed between battles in a rematch, but a loss still starts you over from Justy.

What's new for the Champion

Becoming Champion opens a lot of Orre. Part 10 takes you through it:

  • Team Rocket has a tower behind Pyrite's Construction Lot.
  • Mr. Fuji in Realgam's square can send you to the homes of rare legendary Pokémon.
  • A Battle Frontier opens, reached by bike from Realgam or Pyrite.
  • Vander waits at the very top of Mt. Battle.
  • Every Gym Leader in Orre battles you again with a new team.

And whenever you like, Part 11 covers the side stories of the second half of your journey: Colosseum's and XD's own after-stories, the Deep Colosseum, and the last Shadow Pokémon.
