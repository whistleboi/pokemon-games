You're the Champion of Orre, but the region isn't quiet yet. Team Rocket has come all the way from Kanto and set up a hideout, two other villains have joined them, and Mr. Fuji in Realgam knows where rare Pokémon gather. This part follows the Journal's side pages for all of it. You can do them in any order, except that the Marine Cave, the Terra Cave and New Island open only once Team Rocket's tower is cleared.

Team Rocket's tower

Ride from Pyrite Town to the Construction Lot, the building site where you found the windmill's gear long ago. The shed's door, locked all this time, now opens, and someone is moving about inside. Behind it rises Team Rocket's tower.

{{journal:orre_s9}}

1F: Jessie and James

Team Rocket's own healing machine stands on the first floor: use it as often as you like. Jessie, James and Meowth came to Orre for a quiet life, they say, but since you barged in, it's battle time. It's a double battle against the pair of them.

{{battle:CONSTRUCTION_LOT:Team Rocket Jessie & James}}

Blasting off, they tell you the boss is upstairs.

2F: the alliance and Giovanni

Upstairs, Archie of Team Aqua and Maxie of Team Magma are waiting. They work with Giovanni now, and they mean to make Orre's land and sea their own. They battle you together.

{{battle:CONSTRUCTION_LOT:Team Leaders Archie & Maxie}}

Beaten, Maxie tells Archie to fall back to the caves. Then Giovanni himself steps up. Orre's Shadow Pokémon gave him an idea, he says: his ultimate Pokémon, in armour.

{{battle:CONSTRUCTION_LOT:Boss Giovanni}}

Giovanni admits defeat and promises Team Rocket won't trouble Orre again. But then the armoured Pokémon behind him speaks. It's Mewtwo, and it has had enough of humans using Pokémon as tools. It tears free of its armour and teleports away. Archie and Maxie have slipped off too: word is that Maxie went under Citadark Isle, and Archie to a sea cave near Agate.

The Terra Cave

Sail from Gateon Port's harbour to Citadark Isle again. On the island, the ladder that was roped off is open now, and it leads down into the Terra Cave, where Maxie is hiding. On the first floor are 10 Full Restores and 10 Elixirs, there for the taking.

{{journal:orre_s9b}} {{trainers:XD_CITADARK_ISLE:Terra Cave}} {{trainers:XD_CITADARK_ISLE:Terra Cave B2}}

At the bottom, Maxie says you're too late: Groudon sleeps here, and he'll shape the land as he likes. It's a double battle.

{{battle:XD_CITADARK_ISLE:Team Magma Leader Maxie}}

Beaten, Maxie says Groudon is stirring and even he can't hold it now. The ground shakes, and Groudon wakes up, burning with anger, at Lv 70. This is your chance to catch it.

Marine Cave

Archie went to a sea cave near Agate Village, and Eagun's Alakazam can take you there. Find it in Eagun and Beluh's house in Agate: after Team Rocket's tower is cleared, it teleports you to the Marine Cave, and takes you back to Agate when you're done.

Two Team Aqua grunts guard the cave. At the end, Archie says you're too late: Kyogre's heart will be theirs. It's a double battle.

{{battle:MARINE_CAVE:Team Aqua Leader Archie}}

Beaten, Archie grumbles that the sea doesn't want him and leaves Kyogre to you. The water swells and Kyogre rises from the deep, at Lv 70. Catch it if you can.

Mr. Fuji's Alakazam

Mr. Fuji lives in a house in Realgam's square. He's a friend of Eagun's, and Eagun has told him all about you. His Alakazam can sense where rare Pokémon gather, and once you're Champion it will teleport you to any of the places below. At each place, Alakazam waits to take you back to Mr. Fuji's.

If you haven't already, pick up the Poké Flute from Mr. Fuji's table.

Catching them. Knock one of these Pokémon out and you're asked whether to catch it, and the catch never fails. Say no to a legendary Pokémon and it's back the next day.

Sky Pillar

At the top of the Sky Pillar, Rayquaza coils and roars at the sky. It's Lv 70.

Birth Island

On Birth Island a triangle glows, and something from space takes shape: Deoxys, at Lv 70.

Lake of Rage

The lake churns, and a furious red Gyarados rises out of it. This one is shiny, and it's Lv 60.

New Island

New Island is where Mewtwo fled after it broke out of its armour, so Alakazam offers it only once you've seen Mewtwo escape from Team Rocket's tower. Mewtwo is waiting, and it isn't pleased that you followed it all this way. It's Lv 70.

Mewtwo's Castle

In the castle, three glass pods hold sleeping clones: a Venusaur, a Charizard and a Blastoise. Walk up to a pod and its clone wakes, looks at you, and joins your team at Lv 50. All three can come with you.

Battle Frontier

A Battle Frontier has opened in Orre. Ride there by bike from Realgam Tower or from Pyrite Town. It has a Pokémon Center, a BP Exchange and a Battle Tower.

{{journal:orre_s11}}

The Battle Tower

The tower has three challenges, and none of them has an end: you battle trainer after trainer until you lose. Your team is healed before every battle, and losing doesn't send you to a Pokémon Center: your streak ends, your team is healed, and you can start again. The record board keeps your best streak in each.

The Battle Tower's challenges

Every win earns 1 BP (Battle Point). The tower has its own nurse, and a seller who has Little Cup Pokémon: eight to choose from on each visit, Lv 5, for 3 BP each.

Spending BP

The BP Exchange has two counters. One sells held items and vitamins for BP. The other sells TMs, and the Move Tutor in the tower sells the same TMs.

Mt. Battle's master

Now that you're Champion, climb Mt. Battle once more. Clear all ten areas, past Battlus in Area 10, and Vander waits at the very top as the master of the mountain, with six of his strongest: Slaking, Tyranitar, Salamence, Metagross, Kingdra and Blissey.

{{journal:orre_s2}} {{battle:MT_BATTLE:Mt. Battle Master Vander}}

The mountain's "start over" option sends you back to Area 1 to climb again, and the master battles you again at the top.

Gym Leader rematches

Every Gym Leader in Orre has a new team for the Champion, and each battles you once a day. Talk to them where they stand: Misty in Phenac Stadium's lobby, Duking in the Pyrite Colosseum's lobby, Blaine and Erika in their garden in the Agate Gym, Pryce in his hall in the Snagem Hideout, Jasmine on the Gateon lighthouse's roof, Norman in his hall in the Cipher Key Lair, Drake in Citadark's Dome, and Birch in Realgam's Colosseum in the sky. Each win pays 10 Poké Balls.
