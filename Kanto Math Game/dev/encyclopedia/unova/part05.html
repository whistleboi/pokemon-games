Route 4

Route 4 runs north from Castelia City to Join Avenue and Nimbasa City. It's a sandy road through a half-built town: a row of new houses stands along it, and a sign marks a planned route-expansion area. A side road to the west leads to the Desert Resort. A girl in the sand points out that Pokémon hide in the darker patches of sand.

{{journal:b2_s_r4_trade}} {{tables:UNOVA_ROUTE_4}}

TM055 Dig is by the road, a Stardust in the sand, a Grip Claw in the expansion area and a Mystic Water by the water. A Red Shard, Hyper Potion, Stardust, Yellow Shard and Green Shard are hidden. The Wide Lens is upstairs in one of the houses, and a Black Belt upstairs in another gives you three Sitrus Berries.

Your friend's challenge ours

The first time you walk onto Route 4 with the Insect Badge, the friend you picked when your journey began, Cheren or Bianca, is there to test what you've learned in Castelia. Cheren uses a Lv 20 Pidove, Liepard and Pansage, Pansear or Panpour, and a Lv 22 Pignite, Dewott or Servine. Bianca uses a Lv 18 Herdier, Munna and Pansage, Pansear or Panpour, and a Lv 20 Pignite, Dewott or Servine. Which ones you face depends on the Pokémon you chose first.

Colress and the Crustle

Further up the road, a row of huge boulders blocks the way. A worker says they lined up across the road out of nowhere, and even Strength won't budge them. Colress is waiting in front of them. They aren't rocks at all, he says, but worn-out Crustle, and the device he's carrying gives Pokémon energy. Team Plasma said Pokémon should be freed from people; Colress thinks people should bring out the hidden power in Pokémon instead. He wants to see whether you're a Trainer who can do that. He battles you with a Lv 21 Magnemite and a Lv 23 Klink.

When he's beaten, he decides you bring out your Pokémon's power the way the Gym Leaders and the Champion do, by being kind to them. He gives you a Protein to thank you and leaves, wishing he could talk with Pokémon. The Crustle move off, and the road north is open. The worker suggests the Desert Resort if you like Crustle.

The houses

In one house, an Ace Trainer who loves Speed names a number each day. Show her a Pokémon whose Speed matches it exactly and she gives you five Health Wings; one with a higher Speed earns you one. In the trade house, a girl will swap her Petilil for a Cottonee. In another house, a maid imitates the cry of one of your Pokémon: guess which one and the first time she gives you a Poké Toy. The woman in the rest house heals your team. A boy in the empty house says some Pokémon fly in on Thursdays.

Mandibuzz and Braviary

On Thursdays a Lv 25 Mandibuzz lands on Route 4, and on Mondays a Lv 25 Braviary. Battle one and you can try to catch it.

A vending machine by the road sells Fresh Water, Soda Pop and Lemonade.

Desert Resort entrance

West of Route 4, through a gate, the sand begins. The sign says the Desert Resort is nicer than the average desert. A sandstorm blows here all the time. Wild Pokémon can appear almost anywhere in the sand, but a schoolkid in the gate tells you they never hide in the pale sand, so walk there to avoid them. A hiker says to keep straight on for the Relic Castle.

Desert Resort

The Desert Resort is a wide sea of sand in a constant sandstorm, with the Relic Castle sticking out of the dunes at the north end. It's a side trip, not part of the main story. A hiker says the castle was a city built long ago by a hero and the dragon that went with them.

{{tables:DESERT_RESORT}}

Poké Balls hold a Heart Scale by the castle, a Nugget in the north-west, a Max Ether in the south-west and a Stardust near the nurse, plus a Shard of each colour. A Ground Gem, a Red and a Blue Shard, a Star Piece, a Fire Stone and a Super Repel are hidden. A Backpacker training out here, who wishes for a Water type, is so pleased that someone came to talk that you get Soft Sand.

{{trainers:DESERT_RESORT}}

Nurse Mariah battles you the first time you meet her (a Lv 22 Cleffa). After that she heals your team whenever you ask.

Relic Castle

The Relic Castle is an ancient ruin, slowly being buried by the sand. Sand pours through its halls, and quicksand pits drop you down to B1F below. A hiker in the ruins warns that walking through the middle of quicksand makes you fall in, and so does running through it.

{{tables:RELIC_CASTLE}}

TM036 Rock Tomb is on B1F, with a Heart Scale hidden there too.

{{trainers:RELIC_CASTLE}}

Psychic Perry waits on the first floor; Psychics Low and Dua are down on B1F.

Relic Castle depths

The deepest halls of the Relic Castle can't be reached from the desert. Come back later through the Relic Passage, which joins them. The wild Pokémon here are much stronger (Lv 27 to 30), and an Ultra Ball lies on the sand, with a Sun Stone hidden.

{{tables:RELIC_CASTLE_DEPTHS}}

Volcarona

In the innermost room, Volcarona sleeps. It's Lv 35. Battle it and you can try to catch it.

Join Avenue

Join Avenue is a brand-new shopping street between Route 4 and Nimbasa City. Its eight lots are empty, and signs say a shop opens on one whenever a visitor's dream comes true. The owner spots you as you walk in and calls you over.

{{journal:b2_s_join}}

The new manager

The owner runs several avenues and is far too busy for this one, so he asks you to be its manager. He asks how you'd greet everyone and what you'd say when something really moves you (type anything you like), and then hands the avenue over to you. His assistants Jacci and Future stay in the manager's office at the south end to help.

Three visitors come each day, and each has a dream: to open a shop of a certain kind. Invite a visitor to open their shop on a free lot. You can also recommend a shop to a visitor: if it's the kind they like, the shop and the avenue grow more popular; if not, they leave. A shop's rank (1 to 10) decides how many of its goods it sells, and the avenue's rank (1 to 100) brings rewards along the way, from a Potion at rank 2 to a Master Ball at rank 20 and a Comet Shard at rank 100. The shops are a Market, a Flower Shop, a Cafe, a Dojo, an Antique Shop, a Raffle Shop and a Beauty Salon. A Nursery can open too, but only after you've entered the Hall of Fame and the avenue reaches rank 15.
