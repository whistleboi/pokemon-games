Castelia City

Castelia City is Unova's biggest city: a maze of tall office towers, busy streets and piers that stick out into the bay. Its sign calls it a city of grandeur. Pop Roxie's ship from Virbank City ties up at Prime Pier. Five streets run up from the waterfront, each with a pier at its foot: Mode Street and Prime Pier, Castelia Street and the Cruise Dock, Narrow Street and Unity Pier, Gym Street and Liberty Pier, and Thumb Pier at the east end. Central Plaza is at the top of the city, and Route 4 starts just past it.

{{journal:b2_m14}} {{tables:CASTELIA_CITY}}

A Heart Scale lies at the end of Prime Pier, and the Battle Company and Game Freak's office each have a Poké Ball or two inside. Almost everything else is a gift from someone in town: see "Around Castelia" below. The Miracle Seed, Charcoal and Mystic Water by the Battle Company window are one gift, the one that matches your first Pokémon (bring it along). The Pokémon fanatic's Eviolite needs 40 kinds of Pokémon seen in your Pokédex.

Arriving at Prime Pier

As you step off the boat, Pop Roxie calls out that you've reached Castelia City. Your rival is amazed at how big it is, but he hasn't forgotten Team Plasma: he'll find them wherever they run. Before he dashes off, the two of you register each other on your Xtransceivers. Back in Aspertia you never needed to, since you could just walk round to each other's houses.

Pop Roxie stays on Prime Pier, and from now on he'll sail you back to Virbank City whenever you like. A woman on the pier wonders about the old sailing ship she can see out in the bay.

The Castelia Harlequin Hunt

A Harlequin out in the city welcomes you to Castelia with a gift: a Bicycle, to get around the city in style. Then he explains his game. Three more Harlequins are hiding in the city: one in the Medal Office, one in Passerby Analytics HQ and one in the Battle Company lobby. Find all three, come back to him, and he gives you a Rare Candy.

Burgh is out

The Gym is on Gym Street. Walk up to it and Clyde, the Gym's guide, stops you: Burgh said there might be trouble somewhere and wandered off. Just then a girl runs up: it's Iris. Burgh is always vanishing, she says: he claims to have artist's block and goes off for walks. When she hears you're after Team Plasma, she's surprised, since Team Plasma broke up two years ago. Still, she wants to help, and she has an idea where shady people might hide. She runs off round the corner. Clyde sighs that Burgh and Iris are very alike, and tells you to follow the street down to the sea and then go east.

Until the trouble is sorted out, Clyde won't let anyone into the Gym.

{{journal:b2_m15}}

Thumb Pier

Head east along the waterfront. Iris waits on the way and waves you on: Thumb Pier is just past here, and it's the only suspicious place she can think of. On Thumb Pier she shows you a door into the Castelia Sewers. Your rival comes running, hasn't found Team Plasma anywhere else, and charges straight into the sewers, calling for you to help. Iris agrees the sewers make a perfect hiding place and sends you after him.

A worker on the pier says that because of the tides, the sewers can't always be entered the same way: it changes with the season.

{{journal:b2_m16}}

Castelia Sewers

The Castelia Sewers carry the city's rainwater out to sea. Walkways run beside deep channels, with side rooms off the tunnels. The way in is the door on Thumb Pier, at the east end.

{{tables:CASTELIA_SEWERS}}

The Heart Scale is by the north stairs and the TwistedSpoon near the Plasma Grunts. An Ultra Ball, a Poison Gem, a Nugget, a Max Repel and an HP Up are hidden around the channels. The Black Glasses and the Repel are in Back Street.

{{trainers:CASTELIA_SEWERS}}

Janitor Brady is only down here in autumn and winter, when the water is low, and the three in Back Street only in spring and summer (see below).

Your rival joins you

Just inside, your rival is waiting. The sewer isn't as smelly as he expected. He's worried you need to get tougher, so he joins you for the walk: he battles wild Pokémon beside you, so they come out two at a time, and he heals your whole team after every battle. If you step back outside, he waits for you.

Team Plasma

Climb the north stairs. Two Team Plasma Grunts are standing on the walkway at the top. They say they're "liberating" Pokémon; your rival calls them thieves who use stolen Pokémon as tools, and they try to take yours. It's a Double Battle: you and your rival against both Grunts at once, a Lv 16 Sandile and a Lv 16 Scraggy. Your rival fights with his first partner, now evolved (the one strong against yours: a Lv 20 Pignite, Dewott or Servine), and a Lv 18 Pidove.

Beaten, the Grunts say they already have the Pokémon they need, and run off up the tunnel. Your rival wants to chase them further in, but a voice stops him: it's Burgh, the Gym Leader. He has already searched the back of the sewers and found nobody. Your rival thanks you for your help and gives you HM04 Strength, then dashes off alone to hunt the rest of the gang.

Before you leave, a man in a white coat steps out of the tunnel the Grunts fled into. He praises how you drew out your Pokémon's power in that battle, then walks away again without saying who he is. Burgh has no idea either. He heads back to his Gym and tells you to come and challenge him when you're ready.

High water and low water

The water level changes with the season. In spring and summer the channels are full, and the walkways on the west side lead to Back Street and to a scientist's room. In autumn and winter the water is low: you can walk along the channel floors, Janitor Brady comes down to clean them, and a manhole room on the east side opens instead, but the west walkways are out of reach.

Two scientists down here are turning the sewer's toxins into medicine, and each hands you what today's experiment made, once a day. One's gift is an Antidote, a Full Heal or sometimes a Full Restore; the other's is a Potion, a Super Potion or sometimes a Revive.

A tunnel at the north end of the sewers leads on into the Relic Passage, and stairs at the west end climb up to the Empty Lot.

Back Street

Back Street is a scruffy hideout reached from the west walkways in spring and summer. Its three Trainers are much stronger than anything else down here (Lv 32 and 33), so it's a place to come back to later. Roughneck Ricky and Dancer Jean-Paul are annoyed that a stranger has found their secret spot; once you've beaten Jean-Paul, their boss, Guitarist Tina, will battle you too. Beat her and she declares you the new boss of the lot.

Empty Lot

Stairs at the west end of the sewers come up in the Empty Lot, a quiet little park of tall grass tucked among the towers. A man here has heard that this is where Castelia City first began. Eevee lives in the grass, though it's rare.

Castelia Gym

With the sewers cleared, Clyde lets you into the Gym. He knows you must be tired after chasing Team Plasma and the Gym Leader round the crowded streets, so he gives you a Fresh Water. The Gym's theme is cocoons: step inside one and ride its sticky thread up to the next level. The threads all lead to Burgh in the end. Harlequins wait along the way, and one of them, Harlequin Jack, hides inside a cocoon and pops out to battle you as you pass (a Lv 20 Combee and Swadloon).

{{trainers:CASTELIA_CITY:Castelia Gym}}

At the top is Burgh's studio. He's glad you came through the sewers safely, and he's proud of his ace, Leavanny, which sews clothes out of leaves for other Pokémon.

{{battle:CASTELIA_CITY:Burgh}}

Beat him for the Insect Badge and TM015 Struggle Bug, which lowers the Sp. Atk of whatever it hits. With three Badges, Pokémon up to Lv 40 obey you, even traded ones.

Central Plaza

{{journal:b2_m18}}

Walk up to Central Plaza at the top of the city, on the way to Route 4. The man in the white coat from the sewers walks over and asks to look at your Pokémon. Your Pokémon seem more confident than others of their kind, he says, which is interesting for a Trainer with only three Badges. He introduces himself: he's Colress, a scientist, and his research is how to bring out the power of Pokémon. Is it the bond with their Trainer, or something else? He'd like to test his theory in a battle, and goes off to wait for you on Route 4.

{{journal:b2_m19}}

Castelia Gate

The gate north of the plaza leads to Route 4. As you walk in, Bianca hurries over: she saw you in the city and wanted to catch up. She gives you the Dowsing Machine, which shows where items are hidden, wishes you luck and leaves. In the gate, a woman warns that the Pokémon Breeder on Route 4 challenges every Trainer she sees.

The gate to Route 4 only lets you through once you have the Insect Badge.

Around Castelia

Pokémon Center

The Pokémon Center has the Poké Mart counters inside: everyday items on one side, special Poké Balls on the other. Mr. Medal is here for the Medal Rally. A visitor says Professor Fennel has moved to Castelia.

Gym Street

The building on the west side of Gym Street holds the Medal Office (with Mr. Medal and one of the Hunt's Harlequins) and Professor Fennel's lab. In the lab, Amanita, who looks after the PC Boxes, gives you an Eevee at Lv 10, a gift from a friend in Kanto. Fennel herself studies Trainers and the dreams of sleeping Pokémon. Downstairs, the Name Rater will rate a nickname, and a groomer gives one of your Pokémon a massage once a day, which makes it friendlier with you.

Passerby Analytics HQ, with the second hidden Harlequin, counts and surveys the people you pass. It has no work for you yet.

The Battle Company

The Battle Company welcomes visiting Trainers, and its lobby hides the third Harlequin. By the window, an Ace Trainer gives you a held item that matches your first Pokémon: a Miracle Seed for Snivy, Charcoal for Tepig, Mystic Water for Oshawott (it has to be in your team). The janitor reminds you that Exp. Share can be switched on in the Options. Take the lift up to 47F, where the clerks battle you between their desks. Clerk Clemens gives you two Quick Balls and Clerk Warren five Timer Balls when you beat them, and a developer hands you a Scope Lens for taking the company tour.

{{trainers:CASTELIA_CITY:Battle Company 47F}}

On 55F the company president remembers you from the entrance and battles you himself, with a Lv 17 Eevee and Herdier. His dream is a device that lets people talk with Pokémon. An Ether lies in the corner.

Castelia Street

Game Freak's office is on 22F of a tower on Castelia Street. Morimoto (a Lv 14 Pansage, Pansear and Panpour) and Nishino (a Lv 16 Clefairy) each battle you once a day. The Game Director wants you to show him your Pokédex once it's full, and a Revive sits by the server, which is why the room is so cold.

In the tower on the west side of the street, a guitarist on 11F worries you aren't sleeping enough and gives you TM085 Rest.

Mode Street and Narrow Street

On Mode Street there's a long queue for Castelia's famous Casteliacone, an ice cream that sells for $100. You can buy one a day, except in winter, when the stand is closed. Studio Castelia, a little gallery on the same street, shows paintings by young artists; its painter asks to paint one of your Pokémon and gives you a Berry for it, once a day.

Narrow Street is a dark alley. A man by the dumpsters finds his sunglasses and decides you need more light to see by: he gives you TM291 Flash. Cafe Sonata is here too, and on your first visit the manager gives you a Moomoo Milk for your Pokémon.

The piers

On Unity Pier, a girl talks about the red threads of destiny and gives you a Destiny Knot. The ship to Unity Tower only sails for Trainers who have traded with someone far away, so it won't take you. From Liberty Pier, a sailor runs a boat to Liberty Garden whenever you like. The Cruise Dock is where the Royal Unova leaves each evening.

Street to Route 4

The towers along the street up to Route 4 have more gifts. In the west building, an old man gives a travelling Trainer an Amulet Coin. In the north building, a Pokémon fanatic gives you an Eviolite once you've seen 40 kinds of Pokémon, and a Charcoal lies by a window on 47F. In the south building, the lift guard pats you down before you go up to a party on 47F, where a girl who loves cute Pokémon gives you TM240 Attract.

Bridge Gate

In the Bridge Gate, a woman rushes over to give you a Quick Claw. The Skyarrow Bridge beyond leads to Pinwheel Forest and Nacrene City, but workers are inspecting it, so you can't cross.

Liberty Garden

Liberty Garden is a small island with a lighthouse, a boat ride from Liberty Pier. A sign says a rich family bought it two hundred years ago as a place where people and Pokémon could live freely. The islanders remember a story: long ago, the rich man hid a special Pokémon called Victini in the lighthouse to keep it safe from bad people. Hardly any tourists come now, and one boy finds it boring, but he'd still rather have boring than Team Plasma. The sailor takes you back to Castelia whenever you're ready.

{{tables:LIBERTY_GARDEN}}

Victini in the lighthouse

Come back after you've become Unova's Champion, and Victini is waiting in the room at the top of the lighthouse. It's Lv 15, and you can battle it and try to catch it.

{{journal:b2_s_victini}}

If you visit with Victini in your team, Professor Juniper is on the island. She explains that the Unova Pokédex gives Victini the special number zero, in the hope that its knack for victory passes to whoever carries the Pokédex. In the lighthouse room, Victini wriggles out of its Poké Ball for a little victory dance.

Royal Unova

The Royal Unova is a cruise ship that sails from Castelia's Cruise Dock once a day, in the evening, up the river past the Skyarrow Bridge and back. A ticket costs $1,000. Boarding hours change with the season: talk to the attendant on the Cruise Dock and she tells you this season's times.

{{journal:b2_s_royal}}

Aboard, the voyage lasts as long as five blasts of the ship's whistle, one as she sets off and one about every 45 seconds after that; the fifth means you're back in Castelia. The clock stops while you're talking or battling, but a few passengers chat for so long that the next whistle blows as soon as they finish. Trainers wait in the cabins, a different group each day of the week (three to seven of them). A sailor by the stairs tells you how many are aboard and how many you've beaten, and will let you off early. One sailor tells you how many are on the port side (on your left as you face the front) and how many on the starboard side; another whispers how many are behind blue doors.

Beat every Trainer aboard before the fifth whistle, and the captain gives you a prize back on the dock: a Lava Cookie on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, a Berry Juice on Tuesday, an Old Gateau on Thursday, a RageCandyBar on Saturday and a Rare Candy on Sunday.

{{trainers:ROYAL_UNOVA}}

Each passenger is in the table twice: the first team before you become Champion, the much stronger one after.
