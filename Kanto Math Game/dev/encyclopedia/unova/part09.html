Mistralton City

Mistralton City, "Strewn with Windblown Leaves", sits on the far side of Chargestone Cave. A long runway runs through the middle of town, with vegetable gardens and a greenhouse all around it, and the Cargo Service flies the town's vegetables all over the world. Skyla, the Gym Leader, is also its best pilot. A worker in the gardens says Mistralton was once a desolate patch of land that people and Pokémon built up together. Route 7 leaves town to the north.

TM278 Sky Drop is in a Poké Ball by the greenhouse and the Max Repel by the south gardens. A Flying Gem is hidden in town and an Iron in the TM fan's house. TM027 Aerial Ace is the runway kids' treasure (see below).

Professor Juniper and Skyla

As you walk into town, Professor Juniper comes up to meet you in person at last. She checks your Pokédex, is pleased with how you're doing, and thanks you with a Master Ball, which catches any Pokémon without fail. Then Skyla runs up: the professor asked her for a lift in her plane over Twist Mountain, since there's no way to Opelucid City on foot. Juniper laughs, but first she wants a look at Celestial Tower, at the end of Route 7, and heads off. Skyla shakes her head at the professor, then asks what you'll do next: come to her Gym, or follow the professor and train on the way. Either is fine with her, as long as she gets to battle a strong Trainer.

You can go up Route 7 to Celestial Tower now, or take on the Gym first. Both orders work.

Mistralton Gym

Skyla's Gym is an aircraft hangar turned into a wind tunnel. The Gym Guide bumps into you at the door and gives you a Fresh Water to say sorry. His tips: when the turbines at the back spin up, the wind blows anyone out in the open right back to the door, so hide behind a wall until it stops; and Flying types are weak to Rock, Electric and Ice moves.

The turbines warn you before each gust. Wait for the calm, dash to the next wall, and stand where a wall, a pillar or a person is between you and the turbines. Five Pilots stand along the way.

Beat her for the Jet Badge and TM014 Acrobatics. She says it's the first time in about two years that she and her Pokémon have fought at full strength.

Mistralton Cargo Service

After the Jet Badge, Skyla is in the Cargo Service getting her plane ready. She asks you to go and fetch Professor Juniper from Celestial Tower, at the end of Route 7. If you've already met the professor there and she's gone back, Skyla is ready to fly. Juniper is waiting too, and Bianca comes running in: she wants to fly with you and research Reversal Mountain. Everyone climbs aboard, and Skyla flies you all to Lentimas Town (Part 10).

Afterwards, the receptionist will fly you to Lentimas Town whenever you ask. A worker loading the cargo needs a Flying- or Psychic-type Pokémon to help lift the boxes: have one in your team and he gives you 5 Net Balls. A maid wants Sweet Hearts and would trade a Heart Scale for them, but she has no deal for you yet. The workers say Skyla's grandfather was a legendary pilot, and she's even better than he was.

Around Mistralton

In the kids' house, a boy is still shaking from the wind in Skyla's Gym. One of the runway kids promises something cool if you have a Badge from Mistralton. Come back with the Jet Badge and he tells you their secret: they left their treasure at the south end of the runway. Go and look, and you find TM027 Aerial Ace, a move that never misses. Their Ducklett already knows it, so you can keep it.

In the TM fan's house, a woman is very fussy about TMs. Each time your collection reaches 10, 20, 35, 50 and 70 TMs, she gives you an HP Up, and one more when you've collected all 95.

Out in town, a pilot on the runway says the planes take passengers now, since not everyone's Pokémon can use Fly, and a Pokémon Ranger is amazed that even a tiny Pokémon can carry a person with Fly. A Pokémon Breeder would pay well for Mulch, though there's none to sell him yet. A woman by the Center is still angry that Team Plasma stole Pokémon like Purrloin.

Pokémon Center

Heal your team for free. The counter on the left sells everyday items, and another clerk sells specialty Poké Balls. Mr. Medal is here too. A pilot by the counter wonders about the Entralink, which some say is the source of Unova's special powers.

Route 7

Route 7 runs north from Mistralton City through tall grass, some of it dark grass. Raised wooden walkways cross the grass, and a hiker jokes that you'll fall off if you stand still on them. At the north end, one path leads to Celestial Tower and another climbs to Twist Mountain. The sign at the tower calls it a place of rest for innocent spirits.

{{tables:UNOVA_ROUTE_7}}

The Elixir is by the walkway, TM105 X-Scissor on the east walkway, the Leaf Stone in the west grass and the Zinc by the south walkway. A Tiny Mushroom and a Revive are hidden. Shadows of flying Pokémon cross the route: step into one for the Pokémon in the Flying shadows rows. Cubchoo appear only in winter.

{{trainers:UNOVA_ROUTE_7}}

Partway up the route, your Xtransceiver rings. It's Mom: she used to work at a Pokémon Center's reception, so she knows to take hurt Pokémon to a Center right away.

The houses

In the west house, a hiker wants an Emolga and will trade his Gigalith for one; it comes holding a Leppa Berry. In the rest house, a girl heals your team whenever you drop in. An old farmer there grumbles about Thundurus, who brings thunder, and Tornadus, who brings wind and rain, and ruin his crops. Long ago, he says, Landorus taught those two a lesson right around here. He shows you all three, and they go into your Pokédex as seen. There's a shrine to Landorus on Route 14, he adds. A woman by the window says she's seen them, though they looked different from the old stories.

Hidden Grotto

Route 7 has a Hidden Grotto, right of the house. It can hold a Cubchoo, a Watchog, a Zangoose or a Seviper (Lv 30 to 34), or an item instead.

Tornadus ours

Once you've become Champion, a fierce wind tears across Route 7 and Tornadus swoops down out of the storm, at Lv 40. Battle it, and you can try to catch it.

Twist Mountain

The path at the top of Route 7 leads to the mouth of Twist Mountain. As you walk up, Marshal of the Elite Four stops you. The inside of the mountain has collapsed, he says, so there's no way through to Icirrus City or across to Opelucid: go and challenge Mistralton's Gym instead. He stands in the doorway until you've become Champion.

Celestial Tower

Celestial Tower is a giant memorial where Pokémon are laid to rest. Its floors are full of graves, and Ghost- and Psychic-type Pokémon live among them. Each floor is a spiral: walk up the ramp at one end to reach the next. A man by the stairs says there's a big bell at the very top, and that ringing it pleases the spirits.

{{tables:CELESTIAL_TOWER}}

The two Lucky Egg rows are the same gift: Professor Juniper gives it once, whichever way you come.

Professor Juniper

Professor Juniper is studying the tower on the ground floor. If you come before the Jet Badge, she gives you a Lucky Egg and stays to carry on her field work: a Pokémon holding it gets more Exp. Points from battles. She wonders whether the Ghost and Psychic types gathered here because of the tower, or the tower was built where they lived. If you come with the Jet Badge, she's glad the plane must be ready, gives you the Lucky Egg as thanks, and goes back to wait at the Cargo Service. Either way, you get one Lucky Egg.

Celestial Tower 2F

A woman says her Litwick's light helps her find her way through the tower, and a boy by the graves remembers someone he wanted to battle beside.

Celestial Tower roof

The top of the tower is open to the sky, with the great bell in the middle. Walk up to it and ring it, and its sound carries far around.

A Ghost Gem is hidden on the roof.

The Black Fog

The Black Fog ours. On the roof, the air turns ice-cold and a black fog pours across the stones. A giant Haunter rises out of it: people once worshipped it as a god, and it feeds on dreams. It's always as strong as your strongest Pokémon, and it can't be caught. Beat it for 10 Poké Balls and 3 Rare Candies. It vanishes in a burst of black mist, whispering that it will rise again in another haunted place.
