Undella Town

Out of Reversal Mountain's east chamber, you come down into Undella Town, a summer town of holiday villas on a sandy bay. The beach is busy: an Ace Trainer shouts the town's name at the sea, and a girl wonders whether Cynthia will enter the Pokémon World Tournament. Your way on is north, through the Undella Gate onto Route 13, toward Lacunosa Town, where Professor Juniper and Bianca are waiting.

{{journal:b2_m35}} {{tables:UNDELLA_TOWN}}

The Berry Juice is in a Poké Ball by the west villa. A Shiny Stone, a Heart Scale and two Pearls are hidden around town. The water here is the edge of Undella Bay: you can Surf and fish off the beach.

What's still closed

A worker stands by the Marine Tube, the tunnel under the bay to Humilau City: they're still cleaning it, and it isn't open yet. The beach road south to Route 14 is closed off until the summer crowds go home. Both open once you've become Champion.

Around Undella

Pokémon Center

Heal your team for free. The counter on the left sells everyday items, and another clerk sells specialty Poké Balls. Mr. Medal is here too. A woman by the PC says the walls of the Abyssal Ruins under Undella Bay are carved with messages, and a man in sunglasses jokes about paying $1,000 for a plain Sitrus Berry.

The house

A boy can't wait to visit the shops on Join Avenue, and a woman wonders why only the father of the rich family stayed on in Undella.

The villa

The big villa is empty for now. Come back after you've become Champion: Cynthia is staying here at a friend's villa to research the Abyssal Ruins, and she'd like to battle you one day.

The Undella Gate

The gate at the north end of town leads to Route 13. A maid has heard that Route 13 is the longest road in Unova, and a hiker loves clearing his way with Hidden Moves.

Undella Bay

East of town lies Undella Bay, open sea with rocks and sandbars. You need Surf to explore it: push off from Undella's beach, or from the shore of Route 13. Six Trainers are out on the water, most of them swimmers. Some of the bay's Pokémon change with the seasons: in winter Spheal, Sealeo and Walrein take the place of Mantyke, Mantine and Jellicent.

{{tables:UNDELLA_BAY}}

The Draco Plate is in a Poké Ball on the rocks and the Splash Plate on a sandbar. A Heart Scale is hidden.

{{trainers:UNDELLA_BAY}}

Jellicent

On one day of the week, a Jellicent drifts up out of the bay: on Mondays in Black 2, on Thursdays in White 2. It's Lv 40. Battle it, and you can try to catch it.

The cave mouth

At the north of the bay is the mouth of a cave, Seaside Cave, but the waves are crashing into it and it's too rough to go in. Come back after you've become Champion.

Route 13

Route 13 is a long coastal road that climbs north from Undella Town to Lacunosa Town. It winds up cliffs and stairs, across sand dunes and along beaches, with tall grass, dark grass and plenty of water to Surf on. The way north at the top, to the Giant Chasm, is closed until you've become Champion. Lacunosa Town is in the north-west corner of the route.

{{tables:UNOVA_ROUTE_13}}

The items are spread all over the route: the Hyper Potion on a cliff top, the Max Ether by the stairs, the Blue Shard past the small trees, the Star Piece on the beach, TM120 Psychic by the dunes, the Red Shard in the sand, the Luxury Ball by the water, the Ultra Ball on the shore and the Max Repel by the gate. A Big Pearl, a Zinc, a Pearl and two Heart Scales are hidden.

{{trainers:UNOVA_ROUTE_13}}

The treasure hunter

A man in the dunes is hunting for treasure: things get buried in the sand, he says. Talk to him once a day and he gives you the thing he dug up that day, a Flute (Blue, Red, Yellow, Black or White). If you talk to him again the same day, he's got nothing more and says he'll be hunting here tomorrow too.

The houses

The west house

A woman's Pokémon used to deliver Mail to everyone, and she gives you what it brought her: a Power Lens in Black 2, a Power Band in White 2. A woman by the window wants to go to Pokéstar Studios, and a boy hopes for a new PWT winner.

The east house

A Chatot chatters away. Its owner took Team Plasma's old words to heart and keeps his Pokémon out of their Poké Balls, but his Panpour doesn't listen to him at all. A woman says Poké Balls made it possible for everyone to be with Pokémon.

Hidden Grottoes

Route 13 has two Hidden Grottoes. The one at the top of the stairs can hold a Tangela, a Skorupi or a Foongus; the one south-west of the small trees you Cut can hold a Drifloon, a Spheal or a Foongus. The Pokémon are Lv 35 to 39, Foongus is the most common and Skorupi and Spheal are the rarest. Either grotto may hold an item instead, sometimes an evolution stone.

Lacunosa Town

Lacunosa Town is a small town surrounded by high walls. Its people live by strict rules, from when they wake up to when they go to sleep, and the biggest rule of all is to be home before dark: the walls keep out something that comes at night. A girl in town is sure the scary monster from the big hole is really a Pokémon. The road west, Route 12, leads toward Opelucid City.

{{tables:LACUNOSA_TOWN}}

The Full Heal is in a Poké Ball by the west wall. The Metronome is in the north-east house.

Professor Juniper and Bianca

As you come into town, Professor Juniper is waiting. The road to Opelucid goes straight past Lacunosa, she says, but first there's something she wants you and Bianca to hear. Bianca flew here ahead of you and thanks you for your help in Reversal Mountain. Then the professor sends you both to the house up north.

The old tale

In the house up north, an old woman invites you in and tells the town's old tale. Behind Lacunosa is a huge hole, the Giant Chasm, made long ago by a falling meteorite, and a terrible Pokémon was hidden inside. When darkness falls it comes with a freezing wind, freezing everything and eating people and Pokémon. So the town's ancestors built the walls and made the rule that nobody goes out after dark. Bianca adds that Cheren says the temperature around the Chasm has dropped to minus 58 degrees. Professor Juniper wonders whether a power to freeze everything could rival the legendary dragons, since the same minerals have been found in Dragonspiral Tower and the Giant Chasm.

Team Plasma at the gate

When you head for the west gate, your rival runs up: he heard Team Plasma was around. Zinzolin of Team Plasma steps out with a Grunt, annoyed to see the children from the frigate again. The creature of the Giant Chasm, he boasts, will freeze Unova solid and Team Plasma will rule it. Your rival won't let them get away with it, and you battle them together, two against two.

{{battle:LACUNOSA_TOWN:Zinzolin|A double battle: you and your rival against Zinzolin and a Team Plasma Grunt}}

The Grunt has a Golbat and a Garbodor, at Lv 39. Your rival fights at your side with the final form of the starter that's strong against yours at Lv 43, plus an Unfezant and one of the elemental monkeys at Lv 41. Once they're beaten, Zinzolin says they've learned what they came for and pulls out. Your rival chases after them.

Around Lacunosa

Pokémon Center

Heal your team for free. The counter on the left sells everyday items, and another clerk sells specialty Poké Balls. Mr. Medal is here too, and a Psychic reminds you to treasure your Gym Badges.

The houses

In the south-west house, an Ace Trainer says her Pokémon is much stronger than she is, and a girl asks about the monster from the big hole. In the north house, a woman thinks going out is dangerous even in the afternoon. In the north-east house, a businessman is always home at night, a Parasol Lady would love to meet all the people in the world, and a Metronome sits in a Poké Ball by the table.
