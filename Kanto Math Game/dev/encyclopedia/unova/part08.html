Route 6

Route 6 leaves Driftveil City to the west and winds north to Chargestone Cave. It's a green route of grass, rivers and bridges, with the Season Research Lab, a rest house and two Hidden Grottoes along the way. A sign by the lab says it studies the year's seasons.

{{journal:b2_m30}} {{tables:UNOVA_ROUTE_6}}

TM043 Fling is north of the lab and the Moon Stone by the lab's fence. The PP Up is across the river, the Ultra Ball on the west bank, the Elixir by the east river and the Heart Scale by the south bridge. A Tiny Mushroom, a Big Mushroom and a Protein are hidden. The rivers can be surfed and fished once you have Surf. Deerling's coat changes with the season, like the route itself.

{{trainers:UNOVA_ROUTE_6}}

The Season Research Lab

Cheren is waiting outside the lab and asks you to come inside with him: it's about the weather. The records show a sudden drop in temperature near Lacunosa Town, just for a moment, all the way down to minus 58 degrees, and the same thing around Castelia City and Driftveil City. Together with Team Plasma's return, he wants it looked into before it gets worse (even though he hates cold weather). He gives you HM03 Surf and asks you to look around different places and see what's going on.

Your Quake Badge lets your Pokémon use Surf outside battle, so you can cross water now.

The lab studies Deerling from all four seasons. One scientist has found a rather rare Deerling and asks you to raise it: you get a Deerling (Lv 30) with its Hidden Ability, Serene Grace, which doubles the chance of a move's extra effect. A Parasol Lady gives a Pokémon weather forecast: rain makes Water moves stronger and lets Thunder and Hurricane always hit; sun makes Fire moves stronger and lets SolarBeam fire without charging.

Cobalion on the bridge

On the west bridge, a Pokémon leaps down from the cliff above: the legendary Cobalion. Rood and an elderly man are there to see it. The old man tells the story of Cobalion, Virizion and Terrakion, who fought people to protect Pokémon from a war those people started, and wonders why it shows itself now. Then Cobalion leaps back up the cliff and is gone. Rood wonders whether it came to ask you for help, and says that if you can befriend Cobalion, it will be a great ally. You'll meet it again much later, in Mistralton Cave.

The rest house

A woman in the rest house heals your team whenever you drop in. A girl gives you a Shiny Stone (her Minccino evolved when she gave it one).

Hidden Grottoes

Route 6 has two Hidden Grottoes. The one north of Pokémon Breeder April can hold a Dunsparce, a Woobat or a Foongus. The other is by the entrance to Mistralton Cave, across the water, so you need Surf to reach it; it can hold a Nosepass, a Woobat or a Foongus. Either may hold an item instead.

Chargestone Cave entrance

At the north end of Route 6, a short path leads to the mouth of Chargestone Cave. The sign reads Route 6 to Mistralton City: the cave is the way through.

Chargestone Cave

Chargestone Cave is full of electricity. Glowing stones float in the air, charged so strongly that they hover off the ground, and Electric and Steel Pokémon live among them. The first floor has the way out to Mistralton City at its far end; stairs lead down to B1F and B2F.

{{journal:b2_m31}} {{tables:CHARGESTONE_CAVE}}

The Full Heal is by the first stone, the Hyper Potion in the north room, the Carbos by Hiker Otto, the Max Repel south of Scientist Ronald and the Magnet past Ace Trainer Corky. A Yellow Shard, a Hyper Potion and a Carbos are hidden.

{{trainers:CHARGESTONE_CAVE}}

As in other caves, clouds of dust puff up from the floor: step in for a Shard or a wild Pokémon (the Dust clouds rows in the tables).

Bianca and the floating stones

At the foot of the first floor, Bianca comes running up. She tells you the floating stones move if you push them, though not all of them, and that the charge jumping from stone to stone is what keeps them in the air. Then she remembers she came here to research something, and hurries off.

Each glowing stone fills a 3 x 3 block. Walk into one from the side and it slides the other way until it hits something, and it stays where it stops, even after you leave. B1F and B2F have stones too.

A voice across the bridge

Further on, by a bridge, a voice echoes from the far side. It says it loves this cave, where formulas express the forces behind electricity and the bond between people and Pokémon, and that it has to go to save Pokémon and to stop a friend. By the time you get there, whoever it was has gone.

Chargestone Cave B1F

The first basement floor has stronger Pokémon. Bianca is down here, researching a Pokémon called Tynamo for Professor Juniper; she says there aren't many of them and they don't look very strong.

{{tables:CHARGESTONE_CAVE_B1F}}

The Revive is west of the blue rocks and the Iron on the east ledge. An Ultra Ball is hidden.

{{trainers:CHARGESTONE_CAVE_B1F}}

The Nugget Man and the Nugget Boy are down here too. The boy gives you a Big Nugget, and the man a Nugget.

Chargestone Cave B2F

The deepest floor is a dead end with more trainers and items, and Tynamo appear more often here than on the floors above.

{{tables:CHARGESTONE_CAVE_B2F}}

The Thunder Stone is north of Ace Trainer Shaye, the Escape Rope south of Guitarist Beverly and the Metal Coat on the lower level. An Electric Gem is hidden.

{{trainers:CHARGESTONE_CAVE_B2F}}

Mistralton Cave

Mistralton Cave opens off Route 6, on a patch of land you can only reach by Surf. It's a side trip. Inside it's dark, and you can see only a little way around you. Have a Pokémon in your team that knows Flash (TM291, from Narrow Street in Castelia City) and the whole cave lights up. Boulders sit on holes in the floor; push them in with Strength to open the way on. A worker by a tunnel on the east side says it's Clay Tunnel, still being dug, so you can't go through yet. He's heard there's a legendary Pokémon in the cave.

{{tables:MISTRALTON_CAVE}}

The Yellow Shard is north of the entrance and the Dusk Ball in the north-east corner. A Super Repel is hidden.

{{trainers:MISTRALTON_CAVE}}

Mistralton Cave 2F

The second floor has more boulders to push and leads up to the Guidance Chamber.

{{tables:MISTRALTON_CAVE_2F}}

The Blue Shard is on the north ledge and the Hyper Potion by the south wall. A Green Shard and an Ultra Ball are hidden.

{{trainers:MISTRALTON_CAVE_2F}}

Guidance Chamber

The Guidance Chamber is the top of Mistralton Cave. An old man has been searching for Cobalion here for decades. He tells how Cobalion, Terrakion and Virizion once saved Pokémon from a fire started by a war between people, and wonders if Cobalion still hates people.

{{tables:GUIDANCE_CHAMBER}}

TM086 Rock Slide is in the north-east corner and the Escape Rope on the rock platform. A Full Heal and a Dragon Gem are hidden.

Cobalion

Come back after you've become Champion, and Cobalion is waiting in the chamber, at Lv 45. Battle it, and you can try to catch it. Afterwards, the old man understands: you showed it that people and Pokémon can trust one another.
