Floccesy Town

Floccesy Town is a quiet little town east of Route 19, with an old clock tower in the middle. Alder lives here, in a house just past the clock tower. The town has a Pokémon Center with a Poké Mart counter, and two houses to visit. Route 20 leads east, and a path behind Alder's house goes to Pledge Grove.

{{tables:FLOCCESY_TOWN}}

The X Speed is in a Poké Ball west of Alder's house, and a Potion is hidden elsewhere in town: step on its spot to find it. An old man in the west house gives you the other Potion.

Alder's welcome

Alder is waiting as you walk into town. Your Pokémon always give their all for you, he says, so you must always be kind to them. His house is a little farther on: stop by the Pokémon Center first if you like.

When you reach his house, Alder calls you over to start training... then wonders why you're carrying two Town Maps. When you explain, he laughs: your friend, the one who raised his partner from an Egg, was training on Route 20 just now. Give him his Town Map first! Alder will wait for you here.

Around Floccesy

A girl by the steps passes on something Alder taught her: the Pokémon at the top of your party goes out first, so put the one you're raising there. A woman warns that wild Pokémon at Floccesy Ranch can take you by surprise. A boy saves a record of his journey all the time, a man's Pokémon hog the whole bed at night, and a woman by the tower says the best time with Pokémon is right now.

Pokémon Center

Healing is free, and the Mart counter is on the left. A man says Alder could live anywhere but likes quiet Floccesy, and a woman tells you to carry lots of Potions and Poké Balls.

The houses

In the west house, an old man loves how a Bag sorts everything by itself, and gives you a Potion. A woman there dreams of taking the boat from Virbank to Castelia, the biggest city in Unova. In the east house, a man says the ranch was just a grassy meadow once, and a boy raises one Pokémon and charges ahead with it, the hard way.

Route 20

Route 20 runs east from Floccesy Town toward Virbank City. It's a long route of tall grass, with a river along the south and a patch of dark grass. A side path leads north to Floccesy Ranch. Halfway along, steps climb up toward Virbank, but a hiker stands at the top and won't let a Trainer with no badges past.

{{tables:UNOVA_ROUTE_20}}

The Great Ball lies in the dark grass, and the Heart Scale and Big Mushroom are hidden: step on their spots. The river can be surfed and fished much later. The dark grass holds stronger Pokémon, and Venipede lives only there. Rustling grass can hide Audino or Dunsparce. In summer, more Sunkern come out and fewer Pidove (see the season columns).

{{trainers:UNOVA_ROUTE_20}}

Twins Lia and Lily battle you together, two against one.

Hiker Jerome

Hiker Jerome blocks the steps. A kid without a single Gym Badge going on past here? Battle the Trainers and Pokémon around here first, he says, then battle some more. Come back once you've beaten Cheren (later in this part), and he'll battle you before he lets you through.

{{battle:UNOVA_ROUTE_20:Hiker Jerome|After the Basic Badge. Beat him to go up the steps.}}

A man by the sign reminds you that battles start when Trainers' eyes meet.

Route 20 in autumn

In autumn, fallen leaves pile up on Route 20 and the route looks different. Four Pokémon Rangers come to the route, and they're much stronger than anyone else around here, so battle them later in the story:

  • Ranger Bret: Stoutland (Lv 33). He gives you a Leppa Berry when you win.
  • Ranger Malory: Arcanine (Lv 33). A Leppa Berry too.
  • Ranger Naoko: Foongus and Leavanny (both Lv 32). She gives you a Sitrus Berry.
  • Ranger Leaf: Deerling and Scolipede (both Lv 32), once you've chased Team Plasma off Virbank's pier (Part 3). A Sitrus Berry too.

Four more items appear in autumn too: a Poké Ball, a Rare Candy by the south water, a Max Revive by the south bank, and a Sticky Barb below the bridge. These aren't in the Items table above, since they're only there in autumn.

Floccesy Ranch: the way in

A short path leads north from Route 20 to the gates of Floccesy Ranch. The sign says the ranch is a place to meet lots of Pokémon. There's no tall grass here, only water.

The Poké Ball is hidden: step on its spot to find it. The water can be surfed and fished much later.

Floccesy Ranch

Floccesy Ranch is a big farm of fenced pastures, with Mareep grazing and woods all around. The owner and his wife look after the ranch's Pokémon, including a pair of Herdier who are always together. The tall grass is full of Lillipup, Azurill, Mareep and more, and you might even meet a Riolu.

{{tables:FLOCCESY_RANCH}}

The three Poké Balls lie in the forest, south of the youngster, and between the pastures. The water can be surfed and fished much later. Rustling grass here can hide Audino or Dunsparce.

The missing Herdier

The battle brings the ranch's owner and his wife over. She gives you each a Potion. Then the owner asks if you've seen a Herdier: one of theirs has wandered off. He's only a little worried, but your rival is furious that he isn't more upset. A Pokémon could be lost forever! He storms off to look for it, and asks you to help.

{{journal:b2_m5}}

Head north. Your rival catches up and shares a Paralyze Heal with you. Pokémon don't just wander off on their own, he says: it might be mixed up in some kind of trouble. A little farther on, you both hear a cry. Your rival checks nearby and sends you deeper into the woods.

In the woods you find the Herdier, and someone else: a stranger who's been hiding there. He's from Team Plasma, the group that tried to take over Unova two years ago, "to free Pokémon". He got lost chasing the Herdier, and now a nosy kid has found him. He throws something at you and runs: it's TM273 Frustration.

Your rival arrives with the owner, who is just glad to have Herdier back. Your rival tells him off: he should take better care of his Pokémon. The owner wonders if your rival is afraid of losing Pokémon himself, then leads Herdier home.

{{journal:b2_m6}}

Afterwards, the owner asks if Alder trained you, and tells you the ranch began when a fence was put up to keep Pokémon safe. His wife says to rest if your Pokémon get hurt out here.

The Hidden Grotto

In the north-east of the ranch, across the water, is a Hidden Grotto: a little clearing reached through a gap in the trees. You need to Surf to get there, so come back much later. Each time you visit, the grotto holds one thing: a Pokémon with its Hidden Ability (Marill, Herdier, or rarely Dunsparce, Lv 10–14), an item on the ground, or an item hidden in the grass. Once you've taken it, the grotto is empty, but as you walk around Unova it may fill up again.

Mr. Medal

As you step outside, a man runs up, clapping. He's Mr. Medal, and he gives Medals to Trainers who do great things on their travels: catching, battling, exploring. He gives you a Medal Box to keep them in. Visit him at any Pokémon Center and he'll hand over the Medals you've earned.

Alder has news too: the new Gym in Aspertia City has its Leader at last! If you want to get stronger, go and challenge it. He also mentions Pledge Grove, a quiet place behind his house that something special is said to visit.

Pledge Grove

Pledge Grove is a small, quiet clearing in the woods behind Alder's house. Three sharp marks are cut deep into a rock. There are no wild Pokémon, trainers or items here.

{{tables:PLEDGE_GROVE}}

Come back after you become the Unova Champion: a proud little Pokémon is waiting in the grove. It's Keldeo, the Colt Pokémon, and it will battle you.

Route 19 (again)

As you head back west along Route 19, Alder catches up with you. He forgot to give you something: 5 Oran Berries. Give one to a Pokémon to restore its HP, or better, let a Pokémon hold one, and it eats the Berry by itself when it's hurt in battle. Aspertia's Gym Leader is strong, he says, but think about what your Pokémon can do and you'll be fine.

Aspertia City (again)

Aspertia Gym

The Gym is behind the Trainers' School, and its back door is open now. As you walk in, a young man welcomes you: he's Cheren, and he only just became the Gym Leader. You're his first challenger! Beat his two Gym Trainers first, then him.

Clyde, the guide at every Gym, gives you a Fresh Water to celebrate your debut, and reminds you that you lose if all your Pokémon can't fight, so bring a few.

{{trainers:ASPERTIA_CITY:Gym}}

Beat both of them to challenge Cheren.

It's Cheren's first battle as a Gym Leader as well as your first Gym challenge. Win and he gives you the Basic Badge and TM230 Work Up, a move that raises Attack and Sp. Atk. There are eight Gyms in Unova, he tells you: collect them all, and fill that Pokédex as you go.

Bianca's present

When you step back out into town, Bianca is waiting to congratulate you. She gives you TM274 Return, a move that gets stronger the more your Pokémon likes you. The next Gym is in Virbank City: back through Floccesy, then east along Route 20.

{{journal:b2_m8}}

In the north-east house, the girl with the Lillipup is amazed you beat Cheren. Maybe she and her Pokémon can be brave too. She gives you an Ultra Ball. Mr. Medal is in Aspertia's Pokémon Center now too.

Floccesy Town and Route 20 (again)

The Habitat List

Back in Floccesy, Bianca catches up with you. She forgot to upgrade your Pokédex! She adds the Habitat List: open the Pokédex and pick a place to see which Pokémon live there. Her other tip: sometimes you'll see grass rustling. Go there and see what happens.

Cheren on Route 20

On Route 20, your rival calls out to you, and Cheren is with him. Cheren shows you both the dark grass, where the Pokémon hiding are a little stronger. He gives you 3 Pecha Berries, which cure poison, handy for the Virbank Gym ahead. Your rival has decided: he's going to get stronger than Cheren, and you're to fill up that Pokédex and watch his back.

Now Hiker Jerome spots your Basic Badge and battles you (see above). Beat him, climb the steps and follow Route 20 east to Virbank City (see Part 3).
