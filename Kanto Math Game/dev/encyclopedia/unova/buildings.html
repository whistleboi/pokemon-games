Your home in Aspertia City. Your Unova story starts here: Mom tells you Professor Juniper wants you to have a Pokémon and a Pokédex, and that her assistant Bianca has come to find you. Talk to Mom any time you're home. See Part 1.

Your rival's house. His mom would rather he stayed home, and upstairs his little sister and her Purrloin wait for news of Bianca. See Part 1.

A girl here worries her Lillipup will get hurt in battle. Once you've beaten Cheren, she's braver, and gives you an Ultra Ball (Part 2).

A house where an old couple and their grandson chat about setting out with your first Pokémon.

Your first Pokémon Center in Unova, and where Bianca gives you the tour. In Unova the Poké Mart counter is inside the Center, and here Bianca gives you 10 Poké Balls. After your first badge, Mr. Medal waits here too. See Part 1.

Aspertia's brand-new Trainers' School, with the Gym right behind it. A student gives you an X Defense, and the blackboard and the other students teach easy lessons about types, evolution and status problems. The back door to the Gym stays locked until Cheren takes over as Leader. See Part 1.

The gate at the north end of town, onto Route 19. The guard won't let you through until Bianca has shown you the Pokémon Center and Mom has seen you off; then he gives you a Potion for the road.

Cheren has only just become the Gym Leader, and you're his very first challenger. The Gym opens once you've trained with Alder in Floccesy Town; come in through the back of the Trainers' School. Two Gym Trainers stand before Cheren, and Clyde, the guide at every Gym, gives you a Fresh Water. Cheren uses Normal types. Win the Basic Badge and TM230 Work Up. See Part 2 and Gyms.

Alder's house, just past the clock tower. After you've given your rival his Town Map, Alder trains you here: his students Seymour and Cassie battle you one after the other, each with an elemental monkey, and Alder heals you between them. As you leave, Mr. Medal gives you a Medal Box. See Part 2.

An old man who loves how the Bag sorts itself gives you a Potion.

A man here remembers when Floccesy Ranch was just a grassy meadow.

A Hidden Grotto across the water in the north-east of Floccesy Ranch, so you need Surf to reach it. It holds one thing at a time: a Pokémon with its Hidden Ability (Marill, Herdier or, rarely, Dunsparce) or an item. Once you've taken it, it can fill up again as you walk around Unova. See Part 2.

Roxie's Poison-type Gym is a Pokémon Gym and a rock club at the same time. Clyde gives you a Fresh Water and warns that Grass and Bug moves don't work well here. The band on the stage is far too loud for Roxie to hear you, so stop the music by beating her two bandmates first. Win the Toxic Badge and TM045 Venoshock. See Part 3 and Gyms.

The stage at the back of the Virbank Gym, where Roxie and her band play. Beat the roughneck and the guitarist to stop the music, then battle Roxie. Right after, a scout from Pokéstar Studios invites you to the studios. See Part 3.

The ferry terminal on Virbank's pier. Ships sail for Castelia City, but only once Roxie's pop, the captain, is back from his film audition and the chase after Team Plasma is on. After that he sails you to Castelia whenever you ask. See Part 3.

Virbank's Pokémon Center has two shop clerks, one for everyday items and one for specialty Poké Balls, and Mr. Medal is here.

A nursery for little children, not a Pokémon Day Care. The aide gives you a Burn Heal, and the children share simple tips about Repels and party order.

A woman here dreams of her Audino starring in a movie. A Poké Toy sits in a Poké Ball.

The gate from Route 20. The attendant thinks the more Pokémon you have, the more fun your journey gets, and gives you 2 Great Balls.

The gate to Pokéstar Studios. Its guard won't let you through while the auditions are on; after your Gym battle, the studios' scout is waiting for you on the other side.

The soundstage, where you make your films. Mr. Stu Deeoh hands you a script, and as the scene plays you pick your lines: the right line every time gives a happy ending, which raises your Star Rank, brings a present from a fan and opens the next script. After your first film, the assistant director here lets you shoot another any time. See Part 3.

The back rooms of the studios, full of the people behind the camera: a cameraman, a screenwriter, a prop maker, a sound engineer and suit actors.

The theater where every film is shown. On your tour you watch Roxie's pop in Riolu-Man!, and after each of your own films the audience gives three reviews. Fans wait in the lobby to tell you how much they loved your work. See Part 3.

Where Pop Roxie's ship from Virbank ties up. He stays here and sails you back to Virbank whenever you like. A Heart Scale lies at the end of the pier. See Part 4.

The pier at the east end of the waterfront, with the door into the Castelia Sewers. Iris shows you the way here while Burgh is missing, and your rival charges straight in after Team Plasma. See Part 4.

A girl here gives you a Destiny Knot. The ship to Unity Tower only takes Trainers who have traded with someone far away, so it won't take you.

A sailor here runs a boat to Liberty Garden whenever you like.

Where the Royal Unova cruise ship sets off each evening. The attendant tells you this season's boarding times. See Part 4.

Central Plaza, at the top of the city on the way to Route 4. After the Insect Badge, Colress introduces himself here and goes to wait for you on Route 4. See Part 4.

The street that leads up to Burgh's Gym. When you first arrive, Clyde won't let anyone into the Gym because Burgh has wandered off, and Iris runs up to help you look for Team Plasma. See Part 4.

Burgh's Bug-type Gym is built around cocoons: step inside one and ride its sticky thread up to the next level. The threads all lead to Burgh in the end, with Harlequins waiting along the way, and one of them hides inside a cocoon and pops out as you pass. Clyde lets you in once the Castelia Sewers are cleared, and gives you a Fresh Water. See Part 4 and Gyms.

Burgh's studio at the top of the Castelia Gym. His ace is Leavanny, which sews clothes from leaves. Win the Insect Badge and TM015 Struggle Bug. With three badges, Pokémon up to Lv 40 obey you. See Part 4.

Castelia's Pokémon Center has both Mart counters, everyday items and special Poké Balls, and Mr. Medal for the Medal Rally.

The Battle Company welcomes visiting Trainers, and its lobby hides one of the Harlequin Hunt's Harlequins. By the window, an Ace Trainer gives you the held item that matches your first Pokémon: a Miracle Seed, Charcoal or Mystic Water. On 47F the clerks battle you between their desks, and a developer gives you a Scope Lens for taking the tour. On 55F the company president battles you himself. See Part 4.

Passerby Analytics HQ counts and surveys the people you pass on your travels. It has no work for you yet, but one of the Harlequin Hunt's Harlequins hides here.

The Medal Office, home of Mr. Medal's Medal Rally. One of the Harlequin Hunt's Harlequins hides here too. See Part 4.

Professor Fennel's lab. Fennel studies Trainers and the dreams of sleeping Pokémon. Her friend Amanita, who looks after the PC Boxes, gives you an Eevee (Lv 10), a gift from a friend in Kanto. See Part 4.

The building on the west side of Gym Street. Downstairs, the Name Rater rates and changes nicknames, and a groomer gives one of your Pokémon a massage once a day, which makes it friendlier with you. The Medal Office and Fennel's lab are upstairs.

Game Freak's office, on 22F of a tower on Castelia Street. Morimoto and Nishino each battle you once a day, and the Game Director wants to see your Pokédex once it's complete. A Revive sits by the server. See Part 4.

The lobby of the Castelia Street tower that holds Game Freak's office.

The street to Prime Pier, with a long queue for Castelia's famous Casteliacone ice cream: $100, one a day, except in winter when the stand is closed. Studio Castelia is here too.

Studio Castelia, a little gallery of paintings by young artists. Once a day its painter paints one of your Pokémon and gives you a Berry for it.

Gear Station, home of the Battle Subway. Two exchange counters trade Battle Points for items and TMs. One platform is for the Brown Line, a quiet local train to Anville Town. See Part 6 and Battle facilities.

The Subway Bosses wait as the 21st Trainer, and beating one opens that line's Super train. See Battle facilities.
