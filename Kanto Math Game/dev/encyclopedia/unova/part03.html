Virbank City

Virbank City is a foggy port town at the east end of Route 20. Its sign calls it the city of falling fog and rising stars. It has a Pokémon Center, the Poison-type Gym (which is a rock club too), a nursery, and a ferry terminal where ships sail for Castelia City. Pokéstar Studios, where movies are made, is through the gate to the north, and the Virbank Complex, a big industrial site, lies to the south.

{{tables:VIRBANK_CITY}}

The five Poké Balls come from a roughneck by the Gym, and the Antidote is hidden in the west of town: step on its spot. The Great Balls are from the attendant in the gate from Route 20, the Poké Toy is in a Poké Ball in the north-east house, the Burn Heal is from the nursery aide, and the Fresh Water is from Clyde in the Gym. The water can be surfed and fished much later.

Arriving in Virbank

In the gate from Route 20, the attendant hurries over: you're travelling with Pokémon, and the more you have, the more fun your journey gets. Take 2 Great Balls and catch some more! A girl in the gate says Audino hide in rustling grass, and give lots of Exp. Points.

As you walk into town, your Xtransceiver rings. It's Mom, trying hers out. She says you have to see the Virbank Complex lit up at night, and reminds you not to skip meals: your Pokémon get hungry too.

A little farther on, a man is arguing with a young woman. He's off to Pokéstar Studios to be a ship's captain AND a movie star! She's Roxie, the Gym Leader, and he's her pop. He's the captain already, she shouts, and if his ship doesn't sail, people can't get anywhere. He heads off to his audition anyway. Roxie apologises that you had to see that: if you want a battle, come to her Gym.

{{journal:b2_m9}}

The guard at the north gate won't let you through to Pokéstar Studios yet: auditions are on, and no one gets in without a pass.

Virbank Gym

Roxie's Gym is south of the Pokémon Center. Inside, Clyde explains that it's a Pokémon Gym and a rock club, and the Leader and her band are practising. He gives you a Fresh Water to stay hydrated. His tip: everyone here uses Poison types. Grass and Bug moves don't work well on them, and a poisoned Pokémon keeps losing HP, so bring Antidotes (and your Pecha Berries).

On the stage, the band is way too loud: Roxie can't hear a thing. Stop the music first by beating her two bandmates. The sound engineer reminds you to balance your team's types the way he balances the sound.

Beat her and she says she gave it everything and feels refreshed. She gives you the Toxic Badge and TM045 Venoshock, a move that does double damage if the target is already poisoned.

Right after the battle, a scout from Pokéstar Studios rushes up: he could tell you were special! He wants you to come to the studios. Roxie suddenly remembers her pop, and dashes off to drag him back to work. The scout will wait for you at the studios: just go north through the gate.

Around Virbank

A roughneck by the Gym says Roxie's poison stings, but the Magnemite he caught in the Virbank Complex was fine: Steel types don't get poisoned. He gives you 5 Poké Balls to go and catch one. A guitarist says Roxie's band tickets sell out fast, a boy counts out the six Poké Balls that fit on your belt, and an old man tells you to think about which six you take before a big battle. A woman with a Watchog says its Keen Eye has saved them many times.

Pokémon Center

Healing is free. Virbank's Pokémon Center has two shop clerks: one for everyday items, and one with specialty Poké Balls. Mr. Medal is here too. A boy says more Gym Badges mean more things for sale, and another says Roxie told him to fight Poison types with Poison types.

The houses and the nursery

In the north-east house there's a Poké Toy in a Poké Ball, and a woman can't wait for her Audino to be in a movie. In the south house, an old woman's grandson goes round the Gyms collecting Badges too, and a boy wants to make friends with a legendary Pokémon. At the nursery, the aide gives you a Burn Heal, and the children share their tips: a Repel keeps wild Pokémon away, and the Pokémon at the top of your list goes out first.

The terminal

The ship to Castelia City leaves from the terminal on the pier. For now, the sailor at the counter says the captain is off at Pokéstar Studios.

Pokéstar Studios

Pokéstar Studios is a whole little town built for making movies, north of Virbank. There are fake buildings so detailed they look real, a scale model of the Skyarrow Bridge and the Royal Unova ship for filming, a theater, a soundstage and a props store. Directors shout at their assistants, actors rehearse, and fans wander about looking at the posters.

{{tables:POKESTAR_STUDIOS}}

The tour

The scout meets you at the entrance and introduces his boss, Mr. Stu Deeoh. Movies move people all over the world, he says, and while he prepares, the scout shows you around. First stop is the theater, where Virbank's captain has just shot his first film.

Inside, you all watch it: Riolu-Man!, starring Roxie's pop. He forgets his line, the audience yawns, and someone snores in the back row. It's not how he pictured it, and he heads back to Virbank. Not every film's a hit, the scout says: now it's your turn! The soundstage is west across the plaza.

Your first film

At the soundstage, Mr. Stu Deeoh is waiting with today's script: Brycen-Man Strikes Back. Brycen-Man has frozen the city, and you and your rental Pokémon, a Dewott, must save it.

As the scene plays, your co-star says a line and you pick one of three answers: the line the script wants, a wrong one, or an odd one. Pick the right line every time for a happy ending. Two or more odd lines make a strange ending, and anything else is a sad one. Then the film is shown in the theater, with three reviews.

  • Every film raises your Star Rank (up to ten stars): a happy ending by two stars, a strange one by one.
  • A happy ending also gets you a present from a fan, better as your Star Rank grows: Lemonade at first, up to a Rare Candy.
  • A happy ending opens the next script: Full Metal Cop (with Klink), then The Mystery Door (with Yamask).

After your first film, talk to the assistant director at the soundstage any time to shoot another. Back in the theater lobby, fans want to tell you how much they loved your work.

Around the studios

In the soundstage, a guide explains that the lines you choose change how your co-stars act and how the story ends, and a boy with a Stunfisk says that after a happy ending with a rental Pokémon, you can act with your own. The back rooms are full of the people behind the camera: a cameraman, a screenwriter, a prop maker and a sound engineer. Outside, a portrait seller says Brycen's picture is the hot ticket, and a child actress only speaks Spanish, so her manager translates.

Virbank Complex: the docks

South of Virbank City, the road runs down to the Virbank Complex. Its sign calls it the place where fire meets steel. This first stretch runs along the water, with a little tall grass where Magnemite, Magby and Elekid live alongside Patrat and Pidove. Farther south is the complex itself. It's a side trip: you don't need it for the story, but it's a good place to train and catch a Steel type for Roxie's Gym.

The Silk Scarf is in a Poké Ball behind the roughneck, the X Accuracy in the grass, and the Pearl is hidden: step on its spot. The water can be surfed and fished much later.

A roughneck by the crane offers a deal: your Poké Ball for his Great Ball. You can swap with him once a day.

Virbank Complex

The Virbank Complex is a sprawling industrial site of tanks, smokestacks and a tall distillation tower, with patches of tall grass and dark grass between them. Magnemite, Growlithe, Koffing, Magby and Elekid live here, and workers walk around with their Pokémon.

{{tables:VIRBANK_COMPLEX}}

The Super Potion is in a Poké Ball past a small tree, and TM018 Thief in one by the south-east tanks. One Great Ball and the Tiny Mushroom are hidden: step on their spots. The five Great Balls are from a girl in the south, once your Habitat List for the complex is full (see below).

The foreman's favour

The foreman likes your Pokémon's energy and asks a favour: three new Workers need firing up. Battle them for him! They're in different parts of the complex. Before you talk to the foreman, they just wonder what to do now that their dream job has come true.

Beat all three and go back to the foreman. He gives you TM294 Rock Smash as thanks.

Your complex buddy

A scientist by the distillation tower can't stop explaining how it works, then apologises for showing off. Find him again by a smokestack, and he explains those too. The third time, in a clearing, he's delighted you're actually interested: you're his complex buddy! He gives you an Ether.

A girl in the south of the complex asks you to show her your Habitat List once the complex's page is full. Meet every Pokémon that lives here, and she gives you 5 Great Balls.

Virbank City (again)

Trouble at the docks

After your first film, head back to Virbank. Out on the pier, east of the Gym, Roxie and your rival have cornered three Team Plasma Grunts. Two years ago, the Grunts say, the one they called their king betrayed them and their plan to rule Unova failed. But they'll never give up: they'll free every Pokémon from its Trainer! Your rival shouts that they're nothing but thieves, and Roxie asks you to help. Take the one in the middle!

{{battle:VIRBANK_CITY:Plasma Grunt|On Virbank's pier, with Roxie and your rival, after your first film.}}

The Grunts run for it. Roxie thanks you both: if she'd lost, her Pokémon would have been taken. She gives you HM01 Cut: a Pokémon that knows Cut can cut down small trees, even outside battle. The Grunts split up, so she'll search the town, and you and your rival should check Route 20.

Route 20 (again)

Go back west through the gate. Climb the steps in the middle of Route 20, then look south in a small clearing: one of the Grunts is hiding there. She can't believe you followed her all the way out here.

{{battle:UNOVA_ROUTE_20:Plasma Grunt|In a clearing south of the steps, after the fight at the docks.}}

She grumbles that she should have run to the coast, not inland. Your rival runs up: people in town saw Team Plasma heading out to sea on a ship. If they're on a ship, you need one too! Back to Virbank.

Virbank City: the ship to Castelia

On Virbank's pier, Roxie is talking with her pop. She thought his movie was pretty good, but he knows it wasn't: he never really became Riolu-Man. Roxie tells him he can keep trying while he captains the ship, as long as he doesn't leave people stuck. Pop Roxie cheers up. He'll thrill everyone by barreling through rough waves instead, and the ship sails for Castelia City whenever you're ready.

Go to the terminal. Your rival is waiting there, set on finding Team Plasma. Talk to Pop Roxie at the counter to set sail for Castelia City (see Part 4). After that, he'll sail you to Castelia again any time you're back in Virbank.
