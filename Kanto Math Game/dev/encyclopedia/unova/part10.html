Lentimas Town

Lentimas Town is a quiet town of potters at the foot of a volcano, Reversal Mountain. Its pottery is made from the volcano's clay and is famous all over Unova. What the town doesn't have, Skyla flies in, and her plane lands on the town's runway. Reversal Mountain is just to the east.

{{tables:LENTIMAS_TOWN}}

The Fire Stone is in a Poké Ball by the runway.

Professor Juniper's errand

When you land, Professor Juniper tells you why she brought you along. Cheren told her that Team Plasma plans to use legendary Pokémon to take over Unova. Unova's two legendary Dragon types, Reshiram and Zekrom, each chose a Trainer as a hero two years ago and follow them, so Team Plasma shouldn't be able to use them... but there's so much nobody knows. Drayden, the Gym Leader of Opelucid City, uses Dragon types and might know something. She asks you to go to Opelucid and hear what he has to say, though as a Gym Leader he probably won't talk until you've beaten him. Bianca is off to the volcano just beyond town, chasing rumours of a rare Fire-type Pokémon inside Reversal Mountain.

Around Lentimas

The pilot by the runway flies you back to Mistralton City whenever you like, and the Mistralton Cargo Service flies you here again. A maid by the runway has heard that a rare Pokémon lives deep inside Reversal Mountain.

The Move Tutor's house

A Move Tutor here loves Blue Shards. Bring him some and he teaches your Pokémon a move: Last Resort and Iron Defense for 2 Blue Shards; Magnet Rise and Magic Coat for 4; Block, Hyper Voice, Electroweb, Icy Wind, Iron Tail and Zen Headbutt for 6; Aqua Tail, Earth Power and Foul Play for 8; and Superpower, Gravity, Dragon Pulse and Dark Pulse for 10. A boy in the house says you can find Blue Shards in the dust clouds, and a woman recommends a move of the day.

The TM house

Talk to the old man by the table and he likes you straight away: he gives you TM023 Charge Beam, an Electric move that sometimes raises the user's Sp. Atk.

Pokémon Center

Heal your team for free. The counter on the left sells everyday items, and another clerk sells specialty Poké Balls. Mr. Medal is here too. A hiker says the volcano is the same kind as Stark Mountain in Sinnoh.

Reversal Mountain

East of Lentimas, a rocky path of grass and dark grass climbs the slopes of Reversal Mountain to the mouth of the cave. The cave is the way on: Reversal Mountain's tunnels come out at Undella Town on the far side. The mountainside's eastern cliffs, and the Strange House beside them, are reached from inside, through the south passage.

{{tables:REVERSAL_MOUNTAIN}}

The Full Heal is by the cave mouth. TM114 Shadow Ball is on the east cliff, and the Escape Rope and the Smoke Ball on the south cliff.

{{trainers:REVERSAL_MOUNTAIN}}

Reversal Mountain: the entrance chamber

Inside the volcano it's hot, and clouds of dust puff up from the floor: step into one for a Shard or a wild Excadrill (the Dust clouds rows in the tables). The tunnels are laid out differently in Black 2 and White 2, and each version has its own set of chambers, but they lead to the same places.

{{tables:REVERSAL_MOUNTAIN_ENTRY}}

The Max Repel is in the entrance chamber.

Reversal Mountain: the main chamber

The main chamber is the heart of the mountain, with tunnels running off in every direction and plenty of trainers.

{{tables:REVERSAL_MOUNTAIN_INSIDE}}

The PP Up is in the north tunnel, the Nugget in the west tunnel, the Revive in the east tunnel, the Hyper Potion by Backpacker Kiyo and TM282 Rock Polish in a dead end. A Green Shard, an Elixir, a Star Piece, a Calcium and a Blue Shard are hidden. In White 2 the balls sit in other spots, and the hidden things are an Elixir, a Star Piece, a Calcium, a Yellow Shard and a Red Shard.

{{trainers:REVERSAL_MOUNTAIN_INSIDE}}

The two Pokémon Rangers swap places between the versions. In Black 2, Ranger Eliza is out in the chamber and Ranger Lewis waits by the lake, with a Watchog and a Grumpig. In White 2, Lewis is out in the chamber (Watchog and Camerupt) and Eliza waits by the lake, with a Krookodile and an Arcanine, both at Lv 38.

Bianca

Not far in, Bianca is waiting for you. The mountain is full of wild Pokémon, she says, so let's go through together. She walks with you through the main chamber, battles wild Pokémon beside you, and heals your team after every battle. Her Pokémon are a Musharna and the final form of the starter that's strong against yours, at Lv 36.

The scientist and the Habitat List

A scientist in the mountain asks to see a completed Habitat List for Reversal Mountain. Once you've seen every Pokémon that lives here, he gives you TM057 False Swipe, a move that always leaves its target with at least 1 HP.

The lake

One side chamber holds an underground lake, guarded by a Pokémon Ranger who patrols the volcano. In Black 2 a Toxic Orb lies by the lake and a Water Gem is hidden; in White 2 it's a Flame Orb and a hidden Fire Gem.

Heatran

Deep in the mountain is a chamber over the magma. Come back after you've become Champion, and Heatran crawls up out of the magma, at Lv 68. Battle it, and you can try to catch it.

The way out

The east chamber leads out to Undella Town. As you go in, Bianca says goodbye: she wants to stay and research Heatran some more, and wishes you luck on the rest of your journey.

Reversal Mountain: the south passage

The south passage runs from the main chamber back out onto the mountainside, on the far side of the cliffs. That's the way to the cliffs' items and to the Strange House.

{{tables:REVERSAL_MOUNTAIN_SOUTH}}

Strange House

An old, empty house stands on the mountainside, and wild Pokémon wander its halls. Its portraits are covered in scratches, some have fallen, and one now hangs upside down though nobody saw it change. The shelves are full of old books about sleep and dreams: about Cresselia, whose wings keep nightmares away, and Darkrai, who brings them; about Drowzee and Hypno, the move Dream Eater and the Forewarn Ability.

{{tables:STRANGE_HOUSE}}

The Spell Tag is between the shelves of the first library and the Dusk Stone by the east shelves of the second. The Full Heal and the Rare Candy are in the side rooms. The Lunar Wing is in the attic.

Through the house

Each time you go through a door, the house seems to have changed around you. From the first library you come into a hall with a room on each side, then a second library, then a second hall with two more rooms and the stairs to the attic. In the side rooms, Backpacker Boone thinks a haunted house is perfect for a battle, and Psychic Lynette senses a presence; afterwards she says the girl is still waiting.

The girl and the Lunar Wing

In the first hall, a girl stands in the gloom. She speaks of an endless dark dream and calls for her dad, her mom and her Abra, then fades away. In the second hall she appears again: in the dark dream she heard her dad's voice, and she begs you to forget the Lunar Wing and stay with her. Then she's gone.

Up in the attic lies the Lunar Wing. As you pick it up, her voice says she can't take it now, but it will be all right. She asks you to return the wing to the Pokémon it belongs to: she was waiting on the bridge so she could return it herself.
