Introduction

Unova is a big region of busy cities and wild country. Two years ago a group called Team Plasma tried to take it over, and now they're back. You live in Aspertia City, in the far south-west corner of Unova, under the mountains. Your neighbour is a boy who's itching for a battle, and Professor Juniper, who studies Pokémon, has sent her assistant Bianca all the way to Aspertia to find you.

There are two ways to get here:

  • A new game. Pick Unova from the region list. Choose your look and type your name. Your rival is a boy from your town who's itching for a battle, and you give him his name. Then you pick a second rival, Cheren or Bianca, the friends who set out from Nuvema Town together two years ago. Whichever you pick promises to battle you whenever you meet on the road.
  • From another region. Once you become the Champion of the region you're in, Mom wants to spend your prize money on a new home, and asks where to move. Pick Unova (she only offers regions you haven't played yet). You arrive in Aspertia City, and pick and name your rivals there. Unova keeps its own party, PC and Bag, so you arrive with no Pokémon and get your first partner the same way as in a new game, from Bianca.

Either way, your Journal (in the MENU) always shows your next main mission, and a gold marker on the map points the way.

Black 2 or White 2

The first time you arrive in Unova, the game asks which story you want to play: Black 2 or White 2. Unova's story follows Black 2 and White 2, with places and events from Black and White where they fit. The choice decides which legendary dragon watches over your story: Zekrom in Black 2, Reshiram in White 2. It changes the story only. The wild Pokémon are the same in both, so every Pokémon in this guide's tables can be met whichever you pick.

How this guide plays

This guide is written for two settings in Options › Gameplay: Battle style: Overworld and Level cap: Story. The cap is on from the start; the battle style you switch on yourself.

Overworld battles

On routes and in places like Floccesy Ranch and the Virbank Complex, you can see the wild Pokémon moving about, mostly in the tall grass. Walk into one and the battle happens right there on the map: it fights from where it stands, yours steps up beside it, and small HP bars float over both. Trainers on these maps spot you and walk over. Their words come up in the map's text box, they send out their Pokémon in front of them, and you fight on the map too. In the trainer tables, "on the map" marks these.

Every battle still works the same way: pick a move and answer its math question to land it. The buttons and questions are the usual ones.

The important story battles always open the battle screen: Gym Leaders, your rivals, Team Plasma in story scenes, the Elite Four and the Champion. So do the trainers inside Gyms and towns, people who stop you for a battle in a scene, and a bite on your rod. Each battle box says which.

Rustling grass and dark grass

Now and then, as you walk through a place with tall grass, a patch of grass near you starts to shake. Step into it and a Pokémon jumps out that you'd rarely meet in the normal grass, like Audino. Ripples on the water work the same way once you can surf, and a rod cast into the ripples hooks different Pokémon too. Walk too far away and it stops. In this guide's tables these are the Rustling grass, Rippling water and Fishing (ripples) rows. In caves, the Dust clouds rows show what bursts out of the dust clouds, and on the Driftveil Drawbridge the Flying shadows row shows the birds whose shadows cross the deck.

Some routes also have patches of dark grass, taller and thicker than the usual kind. The Pokémon hiding in it are stronger than the ones in the usual grass: they're the Dark grass rows in the tables.

The seasons

Unova has four seasons, and the season changes every month: January is spring, February summer, March autumn, April winter, and round again from May. A few places change with the season, and the weather on the routes does too. Where it matters, this guide says so.

The story level cap

Your Pokémon can't level past the next Gym Leader's best Pokémon plus two. At the cap they stop gaining Exp., and Rare Candies don't work. Beat the Leader and the cap moves up. Each part starts with a box giving the cap for that stretch.

Levels never rise to meet you: wild Pokémon always come at their place's own levels, and trainers keep their own levels. So the levels in this guide's tables and battle boxes are the ones you'll face, however strong your team is.

Poké Marts and prizes

Every Poké Mart in Unova sells from the same counter, and it grows with the Unova badges you hold. TMs join the shelf as you earn badges too, and the Pokémon League's shop adds Revives, Rare Candy, Full Restores and Max Potions. In Unova the Mart counter is inside the Pokémon Center, so your first one is right in Aspertia City.

Route trainers don't pay in Poké Balls: beating one gives you a healing prize that grows with your badges.

Aspertia City

Aspertia City sits in Unova's south-west corner, under the mountains. Its sign says the sky feels close here. At the top of the city is the Aspertia Outlook, a lookout with a view over Route 19 and all of Unova. The city has a Pokémon Center, a brand-new Trainers' School with a Gym behind it, and a pond where a Poké Ball is waiting. The only way out is the gate at the north end, onto Route 19.

{{tables:ASPERTIA_CITY}}

The pond can be surfed and fished much later. The Fresh Water is Clyde's welcome gift in the Gym, and the Ultra Ball comes from a girl in the north-east house once you've beaten Cheren (see Part 2).

Mom's news

Your story starts at home. As you head for the door, Mom stops you: Professor Juniper called. She and Mom go way back, and she'd like you to have a Pokémon of your own, and a Pokédex to help with her research. Mom already said yes for you! The Professor's assistant, Bianca, has come to Aspertia to find you, but it's her first time here, so she's probably lost. Look for a big green hat.

{{journal:b2_m1}}

Talk to Mom any time you're home. Before you have a partner she reminds you to look for that hat; after, she tells you the world's waiting.

Your rival

Step outside and your rival runs up. Nobody in town battles, and he's bored stiff. When he hears that someone called Bianca is giving you a Pokémon, he can hardly believe it. His little sister comes along too, and asks you to take good care of your Pokémon when you get it. Your rival has something he needs to do out in the world, and he wants someone he can count on with him: you. He bets Bianca is up at the Outlook, the best spot in town, and dashes off.

If you picked Cheren as your second rival, he's standing in town too. He tells you he set out from Nuvema Town two years ago with his friends, and now he's going to teach here, as Aspertia's new Gym Leader, though the Gym isn't open yet. You remind him of himself back then, so he makes you a promise: whenever you meet on the road, you battle.

Your rival's house

Your rival's mom would rather her son stayed home, but she wants to see him grow. A man visiting thinks it's a shame you don't have a Pokémon yet, or you could see who's the better Trainer. Upstairs, his sister hopes you find Bianca soon, and her Purrloin mews at you.

The Outlook and your first partner

Climb to the Aspertia Outlook at the top of town. A girl in a big green hat is there, admiring the view. It's Bianca. She's been looking for a Trainer called... you! She asks a big favour: will you help fill in the Pokédex? Then she opens her case. Inside is the Pokémon who'll be your partner.

Look at each Pokémon, then answer the math questions to choose.

Snivy

Grass. Cheren's Normal types at the first Gym have no weak spot for any partner, but the Virbank Gym's Poison types shrug off Grass moves, so the second Gym is the hardest with Snivy.

Tepig

Fire. A steady pick for the first two Gyms.

Oshawott

Water. A steady pick for the first two Gyms too.

Bianca says you two are a perfect match, and hands you the Pokédex: it records every Pokémon you meet. Just then your rival comes running. He wants a Pokédex too, and Bianca happens to have a spare. Then she has an idea: you both have Pokémon now, so why not battle? Your rival's partner hatched from an Egg he raised himself, and it's the one that has the type advantage over yours.

Bianca's tour

Inside the Pokémon Center, Bianca shows you around: the counter where they heal your Pokémon for free, the PC where any Trainer can keep their Pokémon, and the Poké Mart counter. There she gives you 10 Poké Balls to start you off, and your partner is healed.

In the Center, a man who practically lives in Pokémon Centers says to heal whenever HP gets low, a woman says every journey in Aspertia begins here, and a Trainer by the door tells you Cheren travelled all over Unova a few years back.

The send-off

Step back outside and Mom is there to see you off. She gives you Running Shoes: hold the run button to run. Then your rival's sister gives you a Town Map... and a second one, for her big brother. Could you take it to him? Mom wishes you a wonderful trip, and Bianca says to meet her on Route 19, through the gate at the north end of town. She'll show you how to catch a Pokémon.

Until this send-off, the gate's guard won't let you through: Bianca wants to meet you at the Pokémon Center first.

Around Aspertia

Before you go, look around. There's a Poké Ball by the pond with TM119 Energy Ball in it. A boy says people grow up when they go on journeys, and another that Gym Leaders are the strongest Trainers of all. A girl says the Pokémon you befriend stay right by your side, and a woman tells you your mom is wonderful with Pokémon. A boy at the school door has heard the Trainers' School won't open until a teacher arrives, or maybe a Gym Leader.

Trainers' School

The students are already studying. One gives you an X Defense and explains that X items only last while that Pokémon stays in battle. Others tell you that Normal types are weak to Fighting types, that pressing B stops a Pokémon from evolving, and that the blackboard explains status problems. The teacher says they asked Alder of Floccesy Town to teach here, but he says it's the young people's turn now. The back door to the Gym is locked: it isn't open yet.

The houses

In the south-west house, an old man says that once you've got a Pokémon, you can head out of town, and an old woman says your partner will show you a whole new world. Upstairs, a boy can't wait for his first battle. In the north-east house, a girl's Lillipup hasn't battled yet, and she's scared it'll get hurt. Upstairs, a girl's fortune-telling says you'll meet a Pokémon soon.

The gate

On your way out, the gate guard gives you a Potion for the road, and a girl loves reading the news on the big board by the wall.

Route 19

Route 19 runs north from Aspertia City and then east to Floccesy Town. It's a short, grassy road with a pond and a ledge or two. Patrat and Purrloin live in the tall grass.

{{tables:UNOVA_ROUTE_19}}

The pond can be surfed and fished much later. The Expert Belt is hidden on the east side of the route: step on its spot to pick it up.

A catching lesson

Bianca is waiting just past the gate. Your Pokédex fills in whenever you meet a Pokémon, she explains, and catching one teaches you even more. Then she shows you: a wild Purrloin jumps out of the grass, her Pokémon wears it down, and she throws a Poké Ball. Gotcha! Her tip: weaken a Pokémon first, then throw. A sleeping or paralysed one is even easier to catch. Floccesy Town is straight east.

{{journal:b2_m3}}

If you picked Bianca as your second rival, she runs back with one more thing. She went on a journey two years ago too, with her best friends, and they battled each other all the time. So, from now on, you're rivals: whenever you meet on the road, you battle.

Ledges and Alder

Farther along, a youngster asks if you know about ledge jumping, and shows you anyway: you can hop down a ledge as a shortcut, but never climb back up. A boy in the grass tells you to stay out of it if you'd rather not meet wild Pokémon. A Trainer Tips sign says to talk to everyone you meet.

At the east end of the route, a man calls out to you. He's Alder, a Trainer who loves seeing the world, and his goal is to show people how wonderful it is to walk toward the future with Pokémon. He likes the look of your partner, but you're not a seasoned Trainer yet, so he'll train you a little. Follow him to Floccesy Town (see Part 2).
