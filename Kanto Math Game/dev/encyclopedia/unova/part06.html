Nimbasa City

Nimbasa City is Unova's city of fun, and its sign says it's lit by flashes of lightning. It has the Pokémon Musical Theater, Big Stadium and Small Court for sports, the Battle Institute, and Gear Station, where the Battle Subway's trains leave. The amusement park, with its Ferris wheel, roller coaster and the Gym, is through the gate on the east side. Join Avenue is to the south, Route 5 to the west, and Route 16 to the east.

A Thunder Stone is hidden in town and a Revive in the amusement park. A gentleman in the Pokémon Center has a pile of Red Shards from cave dust clouds and shares ten of them with you. The Soothe Bell is only for a Trainer whose lead Pokémon (the one at the front of the team) is very fond of them.

Your friend in Nimbasa ours

As you come into town, the friend you picked at the start meets you. Cheren remembers passing through here two years ago, wanting to be strong without knowing why; now he teaches in Aspertia. Bianca remembers her father coming all this way to take her home, and how Elesa helped them talk it over. Either way, they point you toward Elesa's Gym in the amusement park.

The Pokémon Musical

The first time you enter the Musical Theater, the owner hurries over. You have quite an aura, he says, like a superstar who drew crowds here two years ago and left a Prop Case behind. Until they come back, he gives it to you: the Prop Case, with a Top Hat, Blue Barrettes, Square Glasses, a Maraca, the Umber Belt and a Tie inside.

In the Musical, you dress a Pokémon up in Props and it dances on stage. Talk to the receptionist at the middle counter to join a show. Every Prop has its own look (cool, cute, elegant or quirky), and the people in the lobby say matching the show's theme is what wins the audience over. The owner gives you more Props as you go: a Toy Cake after your first show, the Crown after five wins and the Winner's Belt after ten. Keep performing and fans start waiting for you in the lobby with Props of their own. A rival stylist in the lobby gives you notes on your last show.

A woman by the posters has no Props to give, so she gives you TM277 Echoed Voice instead.

The Leader is out

Go through the east gate into the amusement park and walk up to the Gym. Its guide stops you at the door: Elesa is out, and she should be in the roller coaster building by the park's entrance.

The Shining Roller Coaster

The Shining Roller Coaster used to be Nimbasa's Gym. Step into a car and it carries you along the track to another platform. Switches on the platforms change where the cars go, and Trainers wait along the way. Beat them and ride on toward the back of the building.

At the back, a woman tells you that you've just missed Elesa: she's gone back to her Gym. She gives you a Paralyze Heal for coming all this way.

Nimbasa Gym

Elesa's Gym is a fashion show. The guide welcomes you to the runway and gives you a Fresh Water, with a tip: Electric types don't like Ground-type moves, but watch out, because Emolga floats above them. Beauties wait on the catwalk, and as you beat your way along it, spotlights click on one after another further down.

Beat her for the Bolt Badge and TM048 Volt Switch, which hits and then swaps the user out for another of your Pokémon.

Trouble at Big Stadium

Leaving the park with your new Badge, you find Team Plasma Grunts loitering outside Big Stadium, in the west of the city, and your rival shouting at them.

{{journal:b2_m23}}

The Grunts claim they're just standing there, but your rival will never forgive Pokémon thieves, and he calls on you to cover him. It's a Double Battle again: you and your rival against a Lv 27 Trubbish and a Lv 27 Watchog. Your rival has his evolved starter at Lv 27 and a Lv 25 Tranquill.

The beaten Grunts boast that Team Plasma is searching for something, and that when they find it their secret weapon will show its true power. Then they run. Your rival tells you why he hates them: five years ago, Team Plasma stole the Purrloin he'd given his little sister as a present, and he was too young to do anything. He heals your team and heads off, asking you to keep filling your Pokédex and getting stronger. The road to Driftveil City runs west along Route 5.

Gear Station and the Battle Subway

Gear Station is home to the Battle Subway. Each train carries seven Trainers, one after another, in a row of cars; you're healed after every battle, and losing doesn't send you to a Pokémon Center, it just ends your run. After each win you choose to continue to the next car or get off. At first the Single, Double and Multi Trains run (on the Multi Train, Rosa or Nate battles beside you). The Subway Bosses wait as the 21st Trainer: beat one and that line's Super train opens. Wins earn Battle Points, which you can spend at the exchange counters on items and TMs.

Outside, Subway Bosses Ingo and Emmet are taking a break, but they'll only battle two on two.

{{journal:b2_s_subway}}

A Trainer who came to try the Battle Subway asks to team up with you: Rosa if you're a boy, Nate if you're a girl. Together you take on Ingo's Lv 26 Boldore and Emmet's Lv 26 Gurdurr in a Double Battle. Your partner uses two Pokémon that depend on your starter.

{{journal:b2_s_subway_boss}}

Big Stadium and Small Court

Big Stadium plays baseball, soccer and football, and Small Court tennis and basketball: which sport depends on the day of the week. At set times a game is on, and ushers keep you off the field while you watch from the stands. The rest of the time the players are practising, and a warp panel in the lobby takes you out onto the field. Talk to the athletes there and they'll battle you once a day; their teams get stronger as you earn more Badges, and most give you a medicine after a win.

The Ferris wheel

By the Ferris wheel in the amusement park, a Trainer is waiting to ride it. Who it is depends on the season, and on whether you're a boy or a girl. They battle you (one Lv 24 Pokémon), then you ride the wheel together and hear a little about their life. Come back on another day and they'll battle and ride with you a second time.

Near the rides, something dropped on the ground is ringing: an Xtransceiver. Pick it up and its owner answers. They're too busy at work to come for it, and ask you to hold on to it. They'll call you on it now and then.

Around Nimbasa

Pokémon Center

The Poké Mart counters are inside, along with Mr. Medal. A girl met her Minccino on Route 5 and wants to put it in the Musical.

Houses

In the west house, people talk about the Wonder Launcher and about Pokémon that Team Plasma stole and never gave back. Upstairs, a Socialite gives you a Soothe Bell if the Pokémon at the front of your team is very fond of you. In the east house, people talk about Shiny Pokémon, and an Ace Trainer upstairs, inspired by Nimbasa's glow, gives you a Sun Stone.

Battle Institute

The Battle Institute's tests run over the wireless network, which doesn't reach this far, so it's closed to you.

The gates

In the east gate, an infielder gives you a Macho Brace to get your Pokémon into shape, and a woman says a Zoroark made its lair in Lostlorn Forest two years ago.

Gear Station's Brown Line

One platform at Gear Station is for the Brown Line, a quiet local train with no battles, out to Anville Town. Board it any time.

A Depot Agent outside the Musical Theater has found a Pansage that got off a train all on its own. If you've heard about a lost Pansage in Anville Town, he asks you to take it home.

Route 16

Route 16 is a short road east of Nimbasa City, leading to the Marvelous Bridge. The lift up to the bridge isn't working, and the woman in the Bridge Gate won't let you past until you've become Champion. Lostlorn Forest is to the north.

{{tables:UNOVA_ROUTE_16}}

A Heal Ball is in the grove and TM247 Payback past a boulder, with a Tiny Mushroom hidden. In the Bridge Gate, a Roughneck got a free drink from the vending machine and shares a Fresh Water with you.

{{trainers:UNOVA_ROUTE_16}}

Lostlorn Forest

Lostlorn Forest is a quiet wood north of Route 16, with a pond you can surf and fish. It's a side trip, not part of the main story.

{{tables:LOSTLORN_FOREST}}

A Net Ball, an Ultra Ball and a Rare Candy lie in the forest, and a Leaf Stone and a Grass Gem are hidden.

{{trainers:LOSTLORN_FOREST}}

Veteran Murphy is far stronger than anyone else around here: five Pokémon at Lv 51. Be ready before you go near him.

The odd Backpacker

A Backpacker asks if you've heard of Zoroark: they say one settled here two years ago and changed how the forest looked with its illusions. The Backpacker hands you TM030 Snarl as a present, then growls, turns into a Zoroark and runs off.

Hidden Grotto

In the north-east of the forest, a gap in the trees leads to a Hidden Grotto. It holds one thing at a time: an item, or a Pokémon with its Hidden Ability (Lv 20 to 24). The Pokémon is most often a Combee, sometimes a Leavanny, and rarely a Heracross or a Pinsir. Once you've taken what's there, it's empty, but as you walk around Unova it has a chance to fill up again.

Anville Town

Anville Town is a small, quiet town at the end of the Brown Line, which you board at Gear Station in Nimbasa. Its sign says it's rolling out the steel rails, and everyone here loves trains. A turntable by the bridge turns the train cars round, and a worker tells you which car is resting on it today: a different one each day of the month. A Depot Agent takes you back to Nimbasa.

The lost Pansage

A girl on the platform has lost her Pansage. After a day at the Musical in Nimbasa, it got on the wrong train home. Find it with the Depot Agent outside Nimbasa's Musical Theater and bring it back, and she thanks you with a BrightPowder.

Weekends

On Saturdays and Sundays, crowds come to watch the trains, and some trade items. A traveller gives a Revive for two Escape Ropes, a collector a PP Up for a Star Piece, and a lady a Full Restore for twenty Poké Balls. On the 8th, 18th and 28th of the month no train comes in, and the traders stay away.

Lost and found

The Depot Agent in the lost-and-found house gives you a Rare Candy the first time you visit. After that, each time you win a seven-Trainer run on the Battle Subway, he finds something left on the train for you.
