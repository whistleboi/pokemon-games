Route 5

Route 5 runs west out of Nimbasa City's gate to the Driftveil Drawbridge. It's a lively road: performers have parked their trailers here, a clown tells you to keep smiling, and a big crowd has gathered at the far end. A sign by the trailer explains Hidden Grottoes, the big shadows under the trees.

{{journal:b2_m24}} {{tables:UNOVA_ROUTE_5}}

The Super Repel is on a ledge and the Hyper Potion behind a fence. Another Hyper Potion is hidden.

{{trainers:UNOVA_ROUTE_5}}

Your friend's battle

As you come onto the route, the friend you picked at the start of your Unova journey, Cheren or Bianca, catches up with you and wants a battle. Cheren is still trying to work out what it means to be strong; Bianca wants to see how far you've both come before the drawbridge. Only the one you picked battles you here.

{{battle:UNOVA_ROUTE_5:Cheren|If Cheren is your friend.}} {{battle:UNOVA_ROUTE_5:Bianca|If Bianca is your friend.}}

HM02 Fly and a Hidden Grotto

A little further on, Bianca comes running to congratulate you on the Bolt Badge. Professor Juniper asked her to give you HM02 Fly, which zips you back to any town you've already been to. Your Bolt Badge lets your Pokémon use it outside battle.

Then she hears rustling by the trees and leads you over to a big shadow: a Hidden Grotto. She explains that a grotto sometimes holds a Pokémon with a rare Ability, sometimes an item, and that they fill up again after a while. She thinks this one has a Minccino in it, and leaves it to you.

Hidden Grottoes. Step into the grotto right of the trailer and you're in a small clearing. Each grotto holds one thing at a time: a Pokémon with its Hidden Ability, an item on the ground, or an item hidden in the grass. Once you've taken it, the grotto is empty, but as you walk around Unova it has a small chance to fill again. This one can hold a Minccino, a Liepard or a Foongus.

The trailer and the crowd

A Maid in the trailer is always hungry for new ingredients: she'll buy food from your Bag, and Berries too, at her own prices. A Berry seller sells one bunch of five Berries a day for $200; the kind changes every day. Musician Preston wants to battle, but only Trainers with a Prop Case: once you have one, beat his Lv 25 Maractus and he gives you an Electric Guitar prop for the Musical Theater.

The crowd at the west end are all stuck: nobody can get past to the drawbridge. An Ace Trainer asks if you can help.

Motorcyclist Charles

The reason is Charles, a motorcyclist who calls himself a heartbreaker. He's mastered a new style of battling to win a girl's heart, and he won't let anyone onto the bridge until someone beats him. His battle is a Double Battle. His Pokémon are the same in both versions; only the order changes.

{{battle:UNOVA_ROUTE_5:Charles@1|Black 2's order.}} {{battle:UNOVA_ROUTE_5:Charles@2|White 2's order.}}

Beaten, he admits you've got a good attitude and lets you through. The crowd thins out, and the drawbridge is open.

Driftveil Drawbridge

The Driftveil Drawbridge crosses the river from Route 5 to Driftveil City. People call it the Charizard Bridge, because when it's raised it looks like a Charizard.

{{journal:b2_m25}} {{tables:DRIFTVEIL_DRAWBRIDGE}}

The youngster on the bridge has never seen a real Charizard. Bring one in your team to show him and he gives you a Heal Powder.

Bird Pokémon fly over the bridge, and their shadows cross the deck. Step on a shadow and either a feather drifts down (a Wing) or the bird itself swoops in: that's where the Ducklett in the table come from (its Flying shadows row). A runner on the bridge is gasping for a drink; he says you met on Skyarrow Bridge, and if you have a Fresh Water you can hand him one.

Driftveil City

Driftveil City is a port, "a City of Billowing Sails". It used to be a shabby old harbour town; now tall hotels line the streets, and there's a Pokémon Center, a market, Clay's Gym and a house on the hill. The Driftveil Drawbridge is to the east, Route 6 to the west, and a gate in the south leads to the Pokémon World Tournament. A worker says they've started digging a tunnel toward Twist Mountain, but the rock is hard going.

{{tables:DRIFTVEIL_CITY}}

The Calcium is by the stone bridge, and an Ultra Ball and a Rare Bone are hidden in town. The other items are gifts from people in the buildings, below.

Team Plasma, old and new

As you step off the bridge, a Team Plasma Grunt is trying to talk someone into stealing Pokémon "like old times". The other one refuses: they used to be in Team Plasma, but they've learned that stealing is wrong, and their lord N would be sad. The Grunt calls N a traitor who ran off and left them.

Your rival charges in and demands answers. The Grunt grumbles that they're not supposed to make trouble and runs off, with your rival hot on their heels. The former Plasma member explains that two years ago Team Plasma split: some followed N, who only wanted to save Pokémon, and some followed Ghetsis, who wanted to rule the world. If you want to understand them, come to their house on the hill, north of the Gym.

{{journal:b2_m26}}

If your friend is Bianca, she finds you in town before you've won Clay's badge. Her dad wanted her to come home, but she's decided to keep travelling, and she wants a battle to prove it.

{{battle:DRIFTVEIL_CITY:Bianca}}

Until you've visited the house on the hill, a worker outside the Gym says Clay is busy and asks you to come back later.

Rood and the house on the hill

Outside the house, an old man named Rood stops you. He's glad you want to know about Team Plasma, but first he'd like to see what kind of person you are, in a Pokémon battle.

{{battle:DRIFTVEIL_CITY:Rood}}

He sees that you care for your Pokémon, and apologises for testing you: as former members of Team Plasma, they have to be careful. Your rival comes back, having lost the Grunt, and demands to know what makes these people any different. Rood explains that since two years ago they've looked after Pokémon who lost their Trainers, to make up for what they did. Your rival tells him that five years ago Team Plasma stole his little sister's Purrloin. It isn't here, and Rood fears Team Plasma still has it. An apology won't bring it back, your rival says, and he storms off to the Gym to get stronger.

Rood is sad at how Team Plasma made Trainers like him suffer. Then he asks you a favour: inside, there's a Pokémon he'd like you to look after.

The former Plasma base

Inside, talk to Rood and he gives you Zorua (Lv 25), a Pokémon that travelled with N. The former members tell you about N's past: he was an orphan who lived in the woods with Darmanitan and Zorua until Ghetsis took him in, raised him and built Team Plasma around him to take over Unova. They care for Pokémon whose Trainers can't be found, and one of them is waiting for N to come home, since N can talk to Pokémon.

Driftveil Gym

{{journal:b2_m27}}

Clay is the Underground Boss, and his Gym is a dark mine. Take the lift down from the lobby to the Gym floor. The Gym Guide gives you a Fresh Water and two tips: Clay uses Ground types, which don't like Water, Grass or Ice attacks; and the conveyor belts carry you between the platforms.

The floor is a grid of platforms in the dark, joined by belts. Step off a platform's edge onto the end of a belt and it carries you across to the next one. Each platform lights up once you've landed on it, so you can see where you've been. Workers stand on the platforms; work your way across to Clay.

{{trainers:DRIFTVEIL_CITY:Driftveil Gym}} {{battle:DRIFTVEIL_CITY:Clay}}

Beat him for the Quake Badge and TM028 Bulldoze, which hits and lowers the target's Speed. His advice: decide your own limits. The Quake Badge also lets your Pokémon use Surf outside battle, once you have it.

Clay's invitation

Back outside, Clay is waiting with your rival. Your rival asks why Clay has forgiven Team Plasma. Clay says there's always room for people to grow and change, and that chasing only what you think is right is mighty dangerous. Your rival doesn't buy it. Then Clay asks if you've heard of the Pokémon World Tournament: Trainers from all over the world come to see who's toughest, and he'll be waiting for you both at the south end of town.

{{journal:b2_m28}}

The gate to the tournament is in the south of the city. Before Clay's invitation, its guard turns you away.

Around Driftveil

A sailor on the pier worries about the ex-Plasma people on the hill. A man on the bridge had his Pokémon stolen by Team Plasma once (they gave it back later), and a boy by the Center says he saw the old Cold Storage every day and has already forgotten what it looked like. A girl by the hotel loves the old story of a Pokémon of the earth that punished a Pokémon of thunder and one of wind.

Once the drawbridge is open, Charles hangs around town too. Once a day he'll have another Double Battle with you: Triple Battles in Black 2, Rotation Battles in White 2, he says, and for more of those he points you to the Pokémon World Tournament.

Pokémon Center

Heal your team for free. The counter on the left sells everyday items, and another clerk sells specialty Poké Balls. Mr. Medal is here to tell you about your Medals. Upstairs, a girl gives you three Luxury Balls: Pokémon caught in them warm up to you faster.

The hotels

Four big hotels stand in Driftveil: the Chateau Hotel, the Grand Hotel Driftveil, the Driftveil Luxury Suites and the Driftveil Continental Hotel. They're all fully booked, but you can wander the lobbies and ride each lift to a couple of floors.

  • Chateau Hotel. A move tutor in the lobby loves Red Shards: bring him some and he teaches your Pokémon one of fifteen moves, from Covet and Bug Bite (2 Red Shards) up to Fire Punch, Thunder Punch and Ice Punch (10). A man beside him recommends a different move each day of the week. On 25F, a gentleman who calls himself a balloon man gives you an Air Balloon: a Pokémon holding one floats, and Ground moves miss it until it's hit.
  • Grand Hotel Driftveil. An old man in the lobby gives you a Shell Bell once your Pokédex has seen 70 Pokémon. On 19F, an Axew has a Haban Berry in its mouth for you.
  • Driftveil Luxury Suites. A lady in the lobby loves moves: each day she asks to see a Pokémon that knows one particular move, picked from the TMs you own. Show her one and she gives you a Heart Scale (once a day). On 23F, a waiter gives an unexpected guest an Everstone.
  • Driftveil Continental Hotel. On 25F, a veteran whose Pokémon aren't with him gives you a Big Root instead of a battle.

Driftveil Market

The market sells things you can't buy anywhere else in Unova. One seller has Moomoo Milk; another sells herbs from another region (Heal Powder, Energy Powder, Energy Root and Revival Herb); and a former Team Plasma member now sells nine kinds of incense. A man in sunglasses gives you an Expert Belt once you have a Pokémon at Lv 30 or more. TM280 Embargo is in a Poké Ball in the corner.

Pokémon World Tournament

The Pokémon World Tournament, the PWT, stands where Driftveil's old Cold Storage used to be. Clay built it. Its grounds have a fountain, a row of move tutors and a pier, and the big hall holds the registration desks and the stadium. A worker says the Relic Passage, a cave nobody knew about until they dug here, opens off the grounds too.

{{tables:PWT}}

A worker by the fountain gives you a Rocky Helmet from the old site. The PP Up is on the pier, and a Zinc and an Ether are hidden.

The Driftveil Tournament

Clay and your rival meet you on the plaza. Clay welcomes you to the PWT, and your rival is in at once. Follow them into the hall. Clay has roped in Cheren as well, and Colress is entering too, to watch how Pokémon bring out their power. Register at the left desk: Clay has already entered you in the Driftveil Tournament.

It's three rounds, one after another, as Single Battles: win all three to take the title. Your team is healed between rounds, and if you lose a round you don't black out: you go back to the hall with a Shard as a consolation prize, and you can register again. This first time, your opponents are always the same three, all at Lv 25, and your own Pokémon battle at their own levels.

  • Round 1: your rival. Liepard, Unfezant, and the last form of the first partner that beats yours: Emboar if you chose Snivy, Samurott if you chose Tepig, Serperior if you chose Oshawott.
  • The semifinal: Cheren. Stoutland, Cinccino and Watchog.
  • The final: Colress. Klink, Elgyem and Magneton.

Win the final and you're the champion of the Driftveil Tournament, with BP for the win. Back in the hall, Roxie is all fired up and says she's entering the next one. Clay is delighted: battles like that will bring people to Driftveil from all over. From now on the PWT is open to you any time.

Tournaments and BP

After your first win, the left desk offers the Driftveil Tournament again, plus the Rental and Mix Tournaments, and you choose Single or Double Battles. The Driftveil Tournament now draws a regular entrant and two of Unova's Gym Leaders. From now on every tournament battle is at the tournament's own level (Lv 25 for the Driftveil Tournament, Lv 50 for the rest), and your Pokémon above it battle at that level. The right desk runs the special tournaments, which open later: the Unova Leaders Tournament once you've beaten the Pokémon League, then the other regions' Leaders and more. Every title earns BP to trade for items. A girl in the hall reminds you that you can't use items during a tournament, so give your Pokémon something to hold first.

The tutors' booths

On the grounds, one tutor teaches the Pledge moves (Grass Pledge, Fire Pledge and Water Pledge) to the Pokémon you start your journey with. Another teaches their ultimate moves (Frenzy Plant, Blast Burn and Hydro Cannon), but only to a fully evolved one that shares the strongest bond with you. A man says he can see a Pokémon's hidden power and suggests teaching it Hidden Power to find out its type. The Move Reminder remembers every move your Pokémon ever forgot, and can't stop remembering them; the Move Deleter seems to have deleted what he was going to say.

Plasma Frigate

As you leave the hall after your win, Team Plasma Grunts rush past toward a ship at the pier. Your rival goes after them. Colress tells you rushing in alone isn't wise, though he knows you won't listen, and says he'll be watching. Your rival calls you to the pier.

{{journal:b2_m29}}

A backpacker on the pier says a strange big ship was moored there. That's the Plasma Frigate, Team Plasma's ship. Board it by the gangway, where your rival and Cheren are already on deck.

{{tables:PLASMA_FRIGATE}}

The Revive is on the deck and TM257 Retaliate at the stern.

On the deck

Your rival demands his sister's Purrloin back, and Cheren warns that the Grunts are coming from both ends of the deck. Grunts come at you one after another: two on their own, then two pairs. For the first pair, Cheren battles beside you; for the second, your rival does.

{{trainers:PLASMA_FRIGATE}}

The table lists one Grunt from each battle. In the two Double Battles, a second Grunt joins in: with Golbat and Liepard beside Cheren's opponent, and with Koffing and Watchog beside your rival's, all Lv 29. Cheren's team is Stoutland, Watchog and Cinccino.

Zinzolin

When the last Grunt falls, Zinzolin steps onto the deck. He's one of the Seven Sages, and he says Team Plasma will soon rule Unova and freeze it solid. Your rival demands the stolen Purrloin, but Zinzolin just calls for the Shadow Triad. They seize you, and you're thrown back onto the pier.

Back on the pier

The ship has sailed. Your rival swears he'll find it again. Cheren says Team Plasma's return, with a ship like that, is serious. He's heading to Route 6, and tells you Mistralton's Gym is past Chargestone Cave: he'll meet you there. Route 6 leaves Driftveil to the west (Part 8).

Relic Passage

The Relic Passage is a long cave the workers found while digging under the old Cold Storage. It runs all the way from the Castelia Sewers to the PWT's grounds, in three parts: the south chamber off the Sewers, the middle around an underground lake (with a side way into the deepest part of Relic Castle), and the north end, which comes out at the PWT. It's a side trip: the story doesn't send you through it.

Clouds of dust puff up from the cave floor. Step into one and you may find a Shard buried there, or a wild Pokémon may burst out: those are the Dust clouds rows in the tables below.

South chamber

The chamber off the Castelia Sewers has weaker Pokémon and trainers than the rest of the passage. A boulder sits on a hole in the floor: push it in with Strength to open the way on.

{{tables:RELIC_PASSAGE}}

The Hard Stone is by Scientist Lumina, an Escape Rope by Scientist Terrance and the Max Repel between the ledges. Another Escape Rope is hidden.

{{trainers:RELIC_PASSAGE}}

Relic Passage (middle)

The middle of the passage wraps around an underground lake, which you can surf and fish. A door on the west side leads into Relic Castle's deepest floors. The room on the east side, with its Rocky Helmet and Rare Candy, is reached only by the long tunnel from the south chamber.

{{tables:RELIC_PASSAGE_MIDDLE}}

The Big Pearl is by the lake, the Rocky Helmet and Rare Candy in the east room and the Ultra Ball by the east door. A Max Repel, a Rock Gem and one Shard of each colour are hidden.

Relic Passage (north)

The north end of the passage comes out on the PWT's grounds. A worker here says the passage goes all the way to Castelia, and warns you that something is always hiding in the dust clouds.

The Protein is by the north wall.
