Voltorb Flip is the puzzle game from HeartGold and SoulSilver's Game Corners. A board of 25 face-down tiles hides numbers and Voltorb. Use the hints at the end of each row and column to work out which tiles are safe, flip them, and multiply your coins. Flip a Voltorb and the round's coins are gone. It's one of the games of the Game Corners, so you need a Coin Case to play, but you don't bet anything: a round costs no coins.

The board

The board is 5 tiles across and 5 down. Every tile is a 1, a 2, a 3 or a Voltorb. Beside each row and under each column is a little panel with two hints:

  • the total of the points in that line (the Voltorb count as 0), and
  • how many Voltorb hide in it.

So a line with 0 Voltorb is safe all the way along, and a line whose points add up to the number of tiles with no Voltorb (5 points and 0 Voltorb, say) is all 1s, with nothing worth flipping.

How to play

  • Flip a tile: tap it, or move the cursor with the arrow keys and press Enter or Space.
  • Coins: the first tile you flip sets your coins for the round to its number. Every tile after that multiplies them: a 1 keeps them the same, a 2 doubles them, a 3 triples them. The left of the board shows "This round".
  • Clear the level: flip every 2 and 3 on the board. The round's coins go into your Coin Case.
  • Voltorb: flip one and it explodes. You lose the coins of this round, and the whole board turns over so you can see where everything was.
  • QUIT (or Esc) keeps the coins you've found so far this round and takes you away from the machine. The level stays where it is, and a new board is dealt when you come back.

After a round ends, tap (or press Enter) for the next board.

Memo

Press MEMO (or M) to switch to memo mode; the button changes to FLIP to switch back. In memo mode, tapping a face-down tile marks it instead of flipping it. Each tap moves the mark on: Voltorb, then 1, 2, 3, then no mark again. Marks are just notes for you; they don't change anything.

Levels

The board gets harder over 8 levels. Your level is saved, so you pick up where you left off at any machine, in any region.

  • Moving up: clear a level and the next board is one level higher, up to level 8.
  • Moving down: when a Voltorb catches you, count the safe tiles you flipped that round. If that's fewer than your level, you drop to that number (never below level 1). If you'd flipped at least as many tiles as your level, you stay where you are.

Each level has five possible boards, and one is picked at random. Higher levels hide more Voltorb and more 2s and 3s, so the coins grow much faster.

Where you can play

Voltorb Flip is on every Game Corner clerk's menu. In three Game Corners you can also walk up to the rows of machines and play it right there (with a Coin Case in your Bag):

  • Johto: Goldenrod City's Game Corner, as in HeartGold and SoulSilver.
  • Hoenn: Mauville City's Game Corner.
  • Sinnoh: Veilstone City's Game Corner.

Through the clerk's menu it's also in Celadon Game Corner, Two Island's Joyful Game Corner and the hidden Game Corner on the Battle Tower's secret floor. In the Sevii Islands, Hoenn and Sinnoh, Voltorb Flip is our addition: those regions' own games don't have it. ours
