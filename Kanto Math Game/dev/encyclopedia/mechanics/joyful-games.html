Two Island's Joyful Game Corner has an attendant who runs FireRed's two Joyful Games: Pokémon Jump and Dodrio Berry Picking. In FireRed they're played together with friends; here you play them on your own, with sums to answer along the way. ours Neither game costs anything. Every game pays a Berry by your score, and your best score in each is kept.

Playing

Talk to the attendant (Joyful Games in Two Island's Game Corner) and pick a game. The sums are at your own math level, as in Math Battles: three answers to tap, or press 1, 2 or 3.

Pokémon Jump

The first Pokémon in your party that can still battle stands on a swinging vine. Jump as the vine swings under it.

  • Jump: tap, or press Space or Up, as the vine comes round to the bottom.
  • A good jump scores 10 points. A jump timed right at the bottom is a PERFECT, worth 20.
  • Jump too early, or not at all, and your Pokémon trips. Three trips and the game is over.
  • Every 10 jumps, the vine swings a little faster.
  • Every fifth swing a sum comes up. Answer it right before the jump, and that jump's points are doubled.

Dodrio Berry Picking

You need a Dodrio in your party, or the attendant asks you to bring one.

  • Berries fall down five columns. Dodrio stands under three of them at once, one head over each, and catches whatever lands above its heads.
  • Move: tap the left or right side of the screen, or press Left or Right, to slide Dodrio over one column.
  • Each Berry is worth points: a Cheri or Oran Berry 1, a Pecha Berry 2, a Sitrus Berry 3.
  • Now and then a golden Berry falls, worth 5, and a sum comes up with it. Answer the sum right before you catch it and it's worth double.
  • The Berries fall faster as you catch more. Ten dropped Berries and the game is over.

Prizes

When a game ends, the attendant tells you your score and hands over one Berry, by how well you did:
