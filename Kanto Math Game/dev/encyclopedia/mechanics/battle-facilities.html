Battle facilities are places built for battling and nothing else: you take on trainer after trainer in a row (a streak), and every win earns Battle Points (BP) to trade for strong items and TMs. Losing at a facility is never a blackout: your team is healed and the streak simply ends.

Here each facility keeps the shape of its own game, but the special rules of the main games' facilities (rental Pokémon, the Pike's rooms, the Arcade's board and so on) aren't built: every one is a streak of ordinary battles, fought with math as always. Streaks are shorter than in the main games, so a win pays more.

Battle Points

  • You hold one BP total, up to 9,999, and it goes with you to every region.
  • Each win in a Battle Frontier streak or on the Battle Subway: 1 BP.
  • Beating a Frontier Brain: 10 BP the first time (the Silver battle), 20 BP for the Gold battle. A Subway Boss pays 10 BP on top of the win.
  • Winning a Pokémon World Tournament: its own BP (see Unova below).

You spend BP at each region's Exchange Service Corner, which sells what its own game sells, at its own prices. Each TM can be bought once.

Frontier streaks and Brains

The Battle Frontiers in Hoenn, Johto and Sinnoh all work the same way:

  1. Talk to a facility's attendant to start. Your team is healed before every battle.
  2. Each opponent is a random Frontier trainer with three Pokémon. The further your streak goes, the stronger their Pokémon get.
  3. Win 5 in a row and the facility's Frontier Brain battles you. Beat them for the Silver Symbol (Hoenn) or Silver Print (Johto, Sinnoh).
  4. After that, win 10 in a row to face the Brain's real team, for the Gold Symbol or Print. You can keep challenging the Gold battle after that.
  5. A loss ends the streak: your team is healed and you're back in the lobby.

Region by region

Kanto and the Sevii Islands: the Trainer Tower

The Trainer Tower on Seven Island is FireRed and LeafGreen's tower, with FireRed's own trainers. Sign up at the desk for one of four climbs: Single, Double, Knockout or Mixed (Double and Mixed need two Pokémon). Then climb eight floors and the roof.

  • On each floor, beat its trainers to open the stairs up: one trainer in a Single floor, a pair in a Double floor, three trainers with one Pokémon each in a Knockout floor. A Mixed climb mixes the three.
  • Every tower Pokémon comes at your party's highest level.
  • A clock runs from sign-up until you reach the owner on the roof. He keeps your best time for each climb.
  • The first time you finish a climb, he gives its prize: an Up-Grade (Single), a Dragon Scale (Double), a Metal Coat (Knockout) or a King's Rock (Mixed). The Up-Grade waits until you have the National Pokédex.
  • Lose, and you're healed and back in the lobby; that climb is over.

The Trainer Tower doesn't give Battle Points.

Every floor of every climb, with its trainers and their Pokémon: Trainer Tower Floors.

Johto: the Battle Frontier

Once you're Champion, Frontier Access leads to the Battle Frontier of HeartGold and SoulSilver. Its five facilities draw their trainers from Platinum, HeartGold and SoulSilver.

Orange Islands

The Orange Islands have no battle facility.

Sinnoh: the Battle Frontier

The Battle Frontier beside the Fight Area is Platinum's: the same five facilities and Brains as Johto's (HeartGold and SoulSilver copied Platinum's Frontier), with their own streaks and Prints. Its stalls sell Platinum's lists, and it has a Scratch-Off clerk too.

Unova: the Battle Subway

Black 2 and White 2's Battle Subway runs from Gear Station in Nimbasa City. Its Depot Agents board you onto a train at its platform, and each Subway Trainer walks into your car from the next one.

  • Lines: the Single Train, Double Train and Multi Train. In the Multi Train, Nate or Rosa (whichever you aren't) battles beside you, with a team style you pick: attack, defence or balance.
  • Every Pokémon battles at Lv 50: yours above it battle at 50.
  • A train has seven cars. After each win you choose CONTINUE (the next trainer walks in) or RETIRE. After the seventh you get off at the line's end. A loss ends the streak and puts you back on the platform. You're healed after every battle.
  • The Subway Bosses, Ingo (Single), Emmet (Double) or both (Multi), are the 21st trainer of a line. Beating them opens that line's Super train, where they wait as the 49th.
  • 1 BP for each win, and 10 more for a Boss.

Who boards at each stage of a streak, Ingo and Emmet's teams and every Subway Trainer: Battle Subway Lines.

Gear Station's two Exchange Service Corner counters sell Black 2 and White 2's items and TMs.

Unova: the Pokémon World Tournament

The Pokémon World Tournament stands beyond its gate in Driftveil City. Pick a tournament at one of the two desks in the hall, then Single or Double. Eight trainers enter; you win three rounds in the stadium, healed between them, and battle at the tournament's level (your Pokémon above it battle at it). Win all three for its BP and title; lose, and you get a Shard as a consolation prize. Triple and Rotation Battles from the main games are played as double battles.

Your first visit is the story's Driftveil Tournament: your rival, Cheren and Colress in turn, with your Pokémon at their own levels.

Orre: the Battle CDs

Orre's Battle CDs aren't a streak and give no BP: each is one puzzle battle with a team you're lent, played at the Pokémon HQ Lab, and the first time you clear one it gives you XD's prize for it (an item, or a TM for CDs 21, 49 and 50). All fifty, with their prizes: Orre's Battle CDs.
