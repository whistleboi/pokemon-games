The Royal Unova is a cruise ship that sails from the Cruise Dock in Castelia City. It goes out once a day, in the evening. Its cabins are full of Trainers who want to battle, and you only have until the ship comes home to find them all.

Boarding

Talk to the attendant at the Cruise Dock. A ticket costs $1,000, and you can sail once a day. Boarding is only open in the evening, and the hours change with the season (by the real clock):

The voyage

A voyage lasts about three minutes, marked by the ship's whistle. It blows once as the ship sets off, then every 45 seconds; the fifth whistle means the ship is back in Castelia and you're taken back to the dock. The clock only runs while you're walking around: it waits while you're in a menu, a battle or a conversation. But watch out for one passenger who talks and talks: when you finish listening, the next whistle blows straight away.

The crew help you find them:

  • The sailor by the stairs tells you how many Trainers are aboard and how many you've battled. He can also let you get off early.
  • One sailor tells you how many are on the port side (your left as you face the front of the ship) and how many on the starboard side.
  • Another whispers how many are behind blue doors.

Beat every Trainer aboard before the voyage ends, and the captain gives you that day's prize when you get back to Castelia. Every new voyage brings the day's Trainers back, ready to battle again.
