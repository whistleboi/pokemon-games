The Poké Mart is where you spend your prize money. Most towns have one. Its counter sells Poké Balls, medicine and travel goods, and it stocks more as you win badges. In Unova the Mart's counter is inside the Pokémon Center (see Pokémon Centers).

How it works

  • BUY and SELL. Two tabs at the top. The shelf is split into tabs too: BALLS, MEDICINE, BATTLE, EVOLUTION, TMs and OTHER. Pick an item, choose how many (-1, +1, +10 or MAX), then BUY. Point at an item to read what it does.
  • Poké Balls come in packs of 5 for $1000. Buy two packs or more and you get a Premier Ball free for every two packs.
  • Selling. The Mart buys things back for half of what it sells them for. It never takes key items, gifts or Mega Stones.
  • Money. You can't buy more than you can pay for; the clerk tells you how much more you need.

The counter grows with your badges

The ordinary counter is the same in every Mart of a region, and it depends on how many of that region's badges you hold, like the Poké Mart in the later games. Once you're the region's Champion it counts as all eight. Orre is the one exception at the start: hardly anyone there sells Poké Balls, so its Marts keep no Poké Balls of any kind until you've beaten one of Phenac's Mystery Troop guards (Part 1).

A few more things turn up on the counter:

  • TMs. Every TM has a tier from 0 to 8, and the Mart sells each one once you hold that many badges. Weak TMs come first and strong ones last, and by the League every TM is on sale. Only TMs you don't have yet are shown, and the TMs tab can be sorted by number, power, type or kind. Prices go by tier: $1000, $1500, $2000, $2500, then $3000 for the strongest. A TM whose move a tutor in this region teaches is sold only by that tutor. See TMs and tutors. ours
  • At the Pokémon League the Mart also sells Revives, Max Potions, Full Restores and Rare Candy ($10000) ours.
  • Weather rocks ($2000) for each kind of weather the region has at this time of year: a Damp Rock for rain, a Heat Rock for sun, a Smooth Rock for sandstorms and an Icy Rock for snow (see Weather and time).
  • Pokéblocks in towns with a Contest Hall (see Contests).

The Up-Grade is never on an ordinary shelf: it is sold in Veilstone City.

Department stores and special shops

Big cities have a department store, with a floor for each kind of goods: see Department stores. Around the regions, other people sell one kind of thing from their own shelf. They don't change with your badges.

Region by region

Kanto, Sevii, Johto, Hoenn and Sinnoh

Every Poké Mart has the counter above, counted by that region's badges. Kanto's evolution stones are sold only at the Celadon Dept. Store, and only for Pokémon that can evolve in Kanto.

Orange Islands

The Orange Islands' Marts work differently: each island's shop has its own fixed shelf, from the fan game, that doesn't change with badges and has no TMs. The first islands sell the basics; the far islands sell Ultra Balls, Hyper Potions and Max Repels, and Pummelo Island's shop has Full Restores and Max Potions. For TMs, visit the Trovito Dept. Store.

Unova

Unova's counter stands inside each Pokémon Center, as in Black 2 and White 2. It works like every other region's counter.
