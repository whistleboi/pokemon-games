Around Unova, people in houses and on the roadside have little jobs, quizzes and trades for you. Many of them change every day (by the real clock), so they're worth a visit now and then.

Route 4

The stat fan

A Trainer in the Speed House on Route 4 is very fussy about one stat. Each day they name a number, based on how strong your Pokémon are, and want to see a Pokémon in your party with that much or more:

  • a Pokémon whose stat is exactly the number earns you five Wings;
  • a Pokémon whose stat is higher earns you one.

You can do this once a day. In Black 2 the stat is Speed and the Wings are Health Wings; in White 2 it's Attack and Swift Wings (see Black 2 and White 2).

The cry quiz

In the Cry House, a Maid imitates the cry of one of the Pokémon in your party, and you guess which one it was. You need at least two Pokémon with you. Your first right answer wins a Poké Toy; after that she's happy to keep playing just for fun.

The Berry seller

A Berry seller on Route 5 sells one bunch a day: five Berries for $200. Today's kind is one of Pomeg, Kelpsy, Qualot, Hondew or Grepa Berries, and it changes from day to day.

Anville Town

Anville Town, at the end of the Battle Subway's line from Nimbasa City, is a town of train lovers.

The turntable

A worker on the bridge tells you about the train car resting on the turntable. It's a different car each day of the month, in a cycle of ten: a Single Train, a Double Train, a Multi Train, a Super Double Train, a Super Single Train, a Super Multi Train, the Anville local, the Hyperspeed Train, a Wi-Fi Train and an old train car. The Hyperspeed Train's days (the 8th, 18th and 28th) are different: no train comes to town at all, and the worker can only tell you about it.

The lost-and-found

A Depot Agent of the Battle Subway lives in the house with the lost-and-found. The first time you visit, he gives you a Rare Candy that nobody claimed. After that, every time you win a whole seven-Trainer train on the Battle Subway, he finds another lost item. It's one of Rare Candy, Full Restore, Max Revive, Elixir, or a vitamin, and each train you win adds one more of it, up to ten. Visit him to collect the lot.
