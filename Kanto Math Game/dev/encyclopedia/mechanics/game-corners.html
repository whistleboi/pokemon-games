A Game Corner is a hall of games played with coins. You buy coins at the counter or win them at the games, then trade them at the prize counter for Pokémon and items. Each Game Corner has its own prizes.

Coins

  • You need a Coin Case to play. Without one, the staff turn you away.
  • The coin counter sells packs of 50, 100, 500 or 1,000 coins at $20 a coin ($1,000 for 50).
  • You can hold up to 9,999 coins. Your coins are kept with your save, so they go with you to every region.

The games

Each game has a page of its own:

  • Slots: bet 1 to 3 coins and stop the reels.
  • Voltorb Flip: HeartGold and SoulSilver's tile puzzle, levels 1 to 8.
  • Blackjack: cards against Meowth.
  • PokéMath Infinite: the endless math game, paid in money.
  • Gym Games: the Gym Leaders' games you've won, again for coins.
  • Joyful Games: Two Island's Pokémon Jump and Dodrio Berry Picking, for Berries.
  • Scratch-Off: the Battle Frontiers' lucky cards, paid for with Battle Points.

At the counter

Talk to the Game Corner clerk to open its menu: the games on one side, the coin counter on the other, and PRIZES at the bottom.

Slots

Bet 1, 2 or 3 coins, press START, then stop each of the three reels (tap its button, or press 1, 2 or 3; Space stops the next one). A bigger bet plays more lines: 1 coin plays the middle row, 2 coins all three rows, 3 coins the rows and both diagonals. More on the Slots page.

Voltorb Flip

HeartGold and SoulSilver's puzzle game. The board is 5 × 5 tiles. Each row and column shows the total of its points and how many Voltorb hide in it. Flip tiles: a 1, 2 or 3 multiplies the coins you've found this round. Find every 2 and 3 to clear the level and keep the coins, and the next board is a level higher (up to level 8). Flip a Voltorb and you lose this round's coins, and you may drop back a level. MEMO lets you mark tiles you're sure of, and QUIT keeps the coins found so far. More on the Voltorb Flip page.

Blackjack

Play cards against Meowth: get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Bet 1, 5, 10 or 25 coins at a time. A win pays your bet back doubled, a Blackjack (an Ace and a 10 or picture card) pays 3 to 2, and a tie gives your bet back. The dealer draws until 17. DOUBLE doubles your bet for exactly one more card. More on the Blackjack page. ours

PokéMath and Gym Games

POKéMATH opens PokéMath Infinite, the endless math game, which pays $50 a point. GYM GAMES lists every Gym challenge game you've won so far: play one again, as hard as your badges in the region, and win 50 coins. ours

Kanto and the Sevii Islands

In Celadon City, the Game Corner is a floor you walk around: Celadon Game Corner. Talk to the clerk across the counter for the menu, or walk up beside one of the slot machines to play the slots right there. Prizes are traded at the Prize Corner next door. A poster on the Game Corner's wall hides the stairs down to the Rocket Hideout.

A man in Celadon's restaurant who lost everything at the slots gives you his Coin Case.

On Two Island, the Joyful Game Corner has the Game Corner counter (its games and coins, but no prizes of its own: as in FireRed, the prizes here are the Joyful Games' Berries), and an attendant with FireRed's two Joyful Games, played alone and with sums: Pokémon Jump (your lead Pokémon jumps a swinging vine) and Dodrio Berry Picking (you need a Dodrio with you). Each game gives a Berry by your score and keeps your best score.

Johto

Goldenrod City's Game Corner is a room you walk in: talk to the clerk for the menu, or walk up to one of its machines to play Voltorb Flip. A man in Goldenrod gives you a Coin Case. Porygon and its Up-Grade are both prizes, so Porygon2 is within reach in Johto.

Outside, the Battle Frontier has a Scratch-Off clerk: three lucky cards for 1 BP (see Scratch-Off).

Orange Islands

The Orange Islands have no Game Corner.

Hoenn

Mauville City's Game Corner comes from Emerald. Its clerk opens the Game Corner menu, or walk up to one of its rows of machines to play Voltorb Flip there, as in Goldenrod. The prizes are swapped at its counter. The Coin Case man inside gives you a Coin Case, once.

The Battle Frontier also hides a small Game Corner on a floor of the Battle Tower that doesn't officially exist, with its own prizes. ours

Sinnoh

Veilstone City's Game Corner is a room you walk in, with a clerk for the Game Corner menu and Eevee among its prizes. Walk up to one of its rows of machines to play Voltorb Flip there, as in Goldenrod and Mauville.

The Battle Frontier has a Scratch-Off clerk too (see Scratch-Off).

Unova

Unova has no Game Corner.
