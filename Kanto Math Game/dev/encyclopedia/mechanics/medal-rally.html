Mr. Medal gives Medals to Trainers who do great things on their travels. You earn a Medal just by playing: catching Pokémon, beating Trainers, finding hidden items and so on. Then Mr. Medal hands it over the next time you see him.

The Medal Box

You meet Mr. Medal in Floccesy Town, right after your training battle with Alder. He gives you the Medal Box, a key item that keeps your Medals. Open it from the Bag to see how many you have, their names, and how many more you've earned but not collected yet.

Collecting your Medals

Earning a Medal and receiving it are two steps. When you've done what a Medal asks, talk to Mr. Medal and he hands over every Medal you've earned so far, saying what each one was for. He waits in the Pokémon Centers of Floccesy Town, Virbank City, Castelia City, Nimbasa City, Driftveil City, Mistralton City and Aspertia City, and in the Medal Office in Castelia City.

The Medals

There are 21 Medals. Most count everything you've done on your whole journey, in every region; the badge Medals count only Unova's badges.
