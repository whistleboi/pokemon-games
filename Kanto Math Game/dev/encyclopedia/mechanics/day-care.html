At the Pokémon Day Care you can leave up to two of your Pokémon. If the two get along, then after a while the Day Care couple find an Egg, and you can take it with you to hatch. Each region you walk has its own Day Care.

Using the Day Care

Talk to the Day Care to open its screen. It shows the two spaces, and for each Pokémon left there its gender, level, Egg Group and Ability.

  • LEAVE A POKÉMON opens your party so you can choose one. When one Pokémon is already in the Day Care, each card in your party says COMPATIBLE or NOT COMPATIBLE with it before you choose. A Pokémon you leave is healed.
  • TAKE BACK returns a Pokémon to your party (you need a free space).
  • COLLECT EGG appears once an Egg is waiting (you need a free space for it too).

In Orre, a Shadow Pokémon left at Agate Village's Day Care opens its heart while it's there: a little more every 100 steps you walk. See Shadow Pokémon.

You can't leave an Egg, or your last Pokémon. Yellow's rowdy Pikachu won't leave your side either.

Which Pokémon get along

Two Pokémon can have an Egg when:

  • one is male and one is female, and they share an Egg Group; or
  • one of them is Ditto (and the other can have Eggs at all).

They can't when one is in the Undiscovered group (legendary Pokémon and babies, for example), when both are Ditto, when they're the same gender, or when a Pokémon with no gender is paired with anything but Ditto. The Day Care screen tells you why they "prefer to play apart".

Finding and hatching the Egg

Time at the Day Care is counted in trips: each time you travel to another place, that's one trip.

  • A pair that gets along finds an Egg after 20 trips. A message tells you when, and the screen counts down the trips left.
  • An Egg in your party hatches after 30 trips. A Pokémon with Flame Body, Magma Armor or Steam Engine in your party makes every trip count twice.
  • An Egg can't battle. Its summary gives a hint of how close it is to hatching, from "a long time" to "sounds can be heard coming from inside".

What hatches

  • The species: the first stage of the mother's evolution line (or of the parent that isn't Ditto). If the mother is a regional form, so is the baby, and Pokémon that only evolve from a regional form (such as Sirfetch'd) hatch as that form.
  • Orange Islands colours: a Pinkan or Valencia colour comes from either parent, on the lowest stage of the line that has that colour.
  • Stats: three of the baby's six IVs come from the parents (five if a parent holds a Destiny Knot).
  • Ability: if the mother has her Hidden Ability, the baby gets it 6 times in 10.
  • Gender is rolled from the species' usual ratio, and the baby starts at level 1.

The Odd Egg (Johto)

The Day-Care Man on Route 34 has a surprise: an Egg that nobody can explain. He gives you an Odd Egg seven times, a different baby Pokémon each time: Pichu, Cleffa, Igglybuff, Smoochum, Elekid, Magby and Happiny, in a random order. (HeartGold and SoulSilver's Odd Egg can be Tyrogue; here Happiny takes its place, since Karate King Kiyo already gives you a Tyrogue.)

  • You need a free space in your party to take one.
  • The next Odd Egg is ready the day after the last one hatched (by the calendar), and a Journal side mission, "The Odd Egg", starts by itself that day.
  • Once all seven have been given, there are no more.
