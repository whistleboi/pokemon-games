The Ruins of Alph hold four small stone chambers, each with a broken panel to put back together. Every panel you fix calls a new set of Unown letters into the ruins, and opens the way to a hidden room. The puzzles are HeartGold and SoulSilver's, drawn with SoulSilver's own pieces.

The panel puzzle

In each chamber, step up to the stone panel. Its picture is cut into 16 pieces (4 by 4), and they start in the slots around the edge of the board. Tap a piece, then a free spot (or another piece, to swap them), until the picture is whole. With keys, the arrows move the cursor and Enter picks a piece up and puts it down. There's no time limit and no way to fail. A solved panel stays solved for good.

When you solve a panel, the floor gives way and you drop into the chamber's writing room below, where Unown letters on the floor spell out a message from the people who built the ruins. The first time, a researcher hurries over and gives you the Unown Report, to keep a record of every form you find.

Which Unown each panel calls

Before any panel is solved, no Unown appear in the ruins' inner hall. Each solved panel adds its own letters to the ones you can meet there:

The hidden rooms

Inside each chamber, Unown letters on the far wall spell a word. Walk up to it carrying what the word asks for (see the table) and the wall slides open onto a hidden room with items to pick up. Without it, nothing happens. The chambers and their rooms each have a page: Kabuto, Omanyte, Aerodactyl and Ho-Oh.

Collecting every Unown

The researcher in the Ruins Research Center keeps count of the forms you've caught. When you've documented all 28, he has one more thing to tell you, about the Unown and Arceus: it's the key to the Sinjoh Ruins, which you reach from the ruins' entrance much later.
