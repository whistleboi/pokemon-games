Once Hoenn is safe again, you can climb onto the back of your Mega Latios or Latias and fly. From up in the sky you can land in towns, find Mirage spots that can't be reached any other way, and meet legendary Pokémon. It's Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire's Soaring in the Sky, drawn as a 3D view from behind your Eon Pokémon.

The Eon Flute

Steven gives you the Eon Flute in Sootopolis City after the Cave of Origin (see Part 16). Play it from the Bag anywhere with open sky above you: not indoors and not in a cave. Your Eon Pokémon swoops down and carries you up. You ride the Latios or Latias in your party if you have one with you, otherwise the one from your version (see Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire).

The flute works in any region, not just Hoenn, and you come back down in the region you took off from.

Flying

  • Turn with Left and Right, climb and dive with Up and Down, and hold Space (or the FAST button) to go faster. With a finger or the mouse, hold where you want to go.
  • LAND (or Esc) sets you down in the nearest town you've visited. As with Fly, it has to be a town with a Pokémon Center that you've been to, or a region's home town.
  • The ground below is one big world: Hoenn, Johto, Kanto, the Sevii Islands and the Orange Islands, laid out as on the world map. Only the parts you've already visited are clear. Everywhere else is hidden under clouds. ours
  • Wild Pokémon fly over each region: flocks of three to five, and now and then one strong flyer on its own. Fly into one to battle it. Each region has its own birds: Wingull, Taillow and Swablu over Hoenn, Pidgey and Spearow over Kanto, and so on.

Mirage spots

Fly over a Mirage spot to land on it. A red beacon shines over any spot where a legend is still waiting for you. Each time you land, a legend that's still there may or may not show itself: one visit in five, it stays away. When one does, it's one you haven't caught yet, picked at random.

Legends in the sky

Once you're Hoenn's Champion, two places in the sky hold legends of their own. Fly into them to battle:

  • A huge gap in the sky south of Dewford Town: Dialga, then Palkia, then Giratina.
  • A black storm cloud north of Fortree City: Tornadus, then Thundurus, then Landorus.

One legend comes each time you fly in, in that order. A Journal side mission, "Soaring in the Sky", keeps track of them. A legend that gets away comes back for another try. For a full tour of every spot, see Part 19.
