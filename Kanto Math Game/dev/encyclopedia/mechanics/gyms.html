Each region has eight Gyms (the Orange Islands have four), and each Gym is led by a Gym Leader who loves one type of Pokémon. Beat the Leader to win their badge. Badges open the way forward: they let you use field moves, make the Poké Marts stock more, and with all of them you can take on the region's Pokémon League.

How a Gym works

  1. Walk in. Every Gym is a building you walk through, built like the one in the region's own games, puzzles and all (see the tables below). The Gym's trainers stand in its rooms and battle you as you make your way through. Many Gyms have a Gym Guide who gives advice.
  2. The Leader's game. When you reach the Leader, they want to play a game with you before they battle ours. It is a short minigame with a goal. Most Gyms pick one at random the first time (more often one that suits the Gym's type) and keep it, so trying again gives the same game until you win it. A few Gyms always have their own: Lt. Surge's trash cans, Koga's walls, Sabrina's warp tiles, Blaine's quiz machine and Cissy's challenge. Win the game and the battle starts. Gym games you've won can be played again at the Game Corner (see Game Corners).
  3. The battle. Win, and you get the badge and prizes: a stack of Poké Balls, often Rare Candy, sometimes a key item, and a TM. Most Leaders give the TM they give in their own game; a Leader who has none picks one of their type for you.

Once you hold a badge, talking to its Leader again just gets a word of praise. After you become Champion, some Leaders take you on again in a rematch with a stronger team.

What badges unlock

  • Field moves. In Kanto, Johto and Hoenn, an HM works outside battle only once you have the right badge, as in those regions' games. In Sinnoh the Icicle Badge also hands you HM Rock Climb and the Beacon Badge HM Waterfall; in Unova the Insect, Bolt and Quake Badges hand you HM Strength, HM Fly and HM Surf. Johto's Rising Badge also gives HM Rock Climb, so you can climb to Mt. Silver ours. The full list is on Field moves.
  • Key items. Some badges come with a key item, shown in the tables below.
  • Shopping. The Poké Mart counter adds new items, and new TMs, with each badge (see Poké Marts).
  • The level cap. Your Pokémon can't grow past the next Leader's strongest Pokémon + 2 until you win that badge ours. After the last badge, the cap is set by the Champion. The tables show the cap before each Gym; see Level cap for the other settings.
  • Obedience. If you pick the obedience setting instead, high-level Pokémon may ignore you: Pokémon up to Lv 20 always obey, 10 more levels with each badge, and all of them with eight.
