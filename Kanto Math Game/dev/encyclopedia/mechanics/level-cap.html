The level cap keeps your journey a fair challenge. Before each Gym, your Pokémon can grow only up to a set level: the level of the next Gym Leader's strongest Pokémon (their ace), plus 2. Beat the Gym, and the cap moves up to the next Leader's. After the last Gym the cap is set by the Champion's ace. Once you're Champion of a region, the cap is lifted there. ours

How it works

  • A Pokémon at the cap earns no Exp. from battles. The game tells you it can't grow past that level until you beat the next Gym.
  • Rare Candies stop working at the cap too.
  • Effort points still come in at the cap, so your Pokémon keep getting stronger in their stats.
  • The cap counts the badges of the region you're in. A badge from another region doesn't raise it.
  • The cap never drops: if a later Leader's ace is lower than an earlier one's (Pryce's is below Jasmine's), the cap stays where it was.

Example: Brock's ace is Lv 14, so before the Boulder Badge your Pokémon can reach Lv 16.

Three cap settings

In Options > GAMEPLAY, Level cap has three choices:

Level cap settings
SettingWhat it does
StoryThe cap on this page: the next Leader's ace + 2. This is where every new game starts.
ObedienceNo cap, but Pokémon above a certain level may ignore you, as in the main games: Lv 20 with no badges, 10 more for each badge in this region, and every Pokémon obeys once you have all eight. The higher above that line a Pokémon is, the more often it loafs around.
OffNo cap at all, like the original games.

Kanto and the Sevii Islands

FireRed and LeafGreen's levels. The Sevii Islands belong to Kanto, so they follow Kanto's badges and Kanto's title.

Johto

HeartGold and SoulSilver's levels. Jasmine's ace is above Pryce's, so the cap stays at 37 for both of those badges.

Orange Islands

The Orange League has four Gyms, using the fan game's levels: the cap is 24 before Cissy, then 37, 42 and 48, and 54 with all four badges, when Drake is next.

Hoenn

Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire's levels.

Sinnoh

Brilliant Diamond and Shining Pearl's levels.

Unova

Black 2 and White 2's levels, in their Gym order.

Orre

Orre's eight Gyms set its cap, in the order of the story: 16 with no badges (before Misty), then 23, 26, 31, 43, 47, 50 and 52 (before Birch), and 65 with all eight badges, when the Elite Four and Champion Eldes are next, until you beat the Champion at the Orre League. After the title, the cap follows your own team and never drops below Lv 50. Orre's League rematches bring every Pokémon up to at least Lv 70 (see Part 9).

Wild Pokémon and trainers

  • Wild Pokémon never scale. They always come at their place's own level, before and after the title.
  • Route trainers also stay at their place's level, until your lead Pokémon reaches the next Leader's ace. Then trainers in the lower-level places come from 3 levels under that ace to 1 over it (ace 25: Lv 22 to 26).
  • Gym Leaders, the League and story trainers always keep their own levels.
  • Legendary Pokémon you meet come at the next Leader's ace + 3 before the region's last badge, and at Lv 70 from the last badge on.

After the title

Once you're Champion of a region, the cap stops blocking Exp. there. The cap the game shows is your team's highest level, but never lower than that region's League rematch floor. Places that are part of a region (the Sevii Islands with Kanto) follow it.

  • Route trainers now keep up with you: they come near your team's average level (your lowest-levelled Pokémon left out), or their place's own level if that is higher.
  • Wild Pokémon still keep their own levels. Legendary Pokémon are Lv 70.
  • League rematches never rise to your level. They use the main games' rematch teams where there are some, and otherwise come back about 17 levels stronger than the first time.
