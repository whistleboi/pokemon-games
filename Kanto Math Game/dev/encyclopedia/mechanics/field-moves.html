Trees, rocks, boulders, water and dark caves stand in your way on the maps until you can deal with them. Each one needs a field move, which you get as an HM: an item in your Bag that is never used up. In some regions you also need a certain Gym badge before the HM works outside battle, as in that region's games.

Walk up to something in your way and the game tells you what move would clear it. Once you have the move (and the badge, where one is needed), the way is simply open.

Orange Islands

The fan game has no badges for field moves; you just need the item.

  • Surf: on Tangelo Island.
  • Fly: in Mandarin Island South.
  • Cut: on Butwal Island.
  • The Climb Kit (from Mandarin Island North) climbs the same walls as Rock Climb.
  • The Pickaxe (from Fukuhara Island) breaks cracked rocks like Rock Smash.
  • Waterfall has no HM here: a Pokémon on your team that knows the move climbs the falls.
  • The Scuba Gear dives like Dive.

Unova

In Black 2 and White 2 the HMs are gifts from people in the story:

  • Cut: Roxie, at the docks in Virbank City.
  • Strength: your rival, in the Castelia Sewers.
  • Fly: Bianca, on Route 5.
  • Surf: Cheren, in the Season Research Lab on Route 6.

Flash isn't an HM in Unova: a Pokémon on your team that knows Flash lights up a dark cave.

The Itemfinder and the Dowsing Machine

Hidden items are buried all over the maps. You find one by stepping on it (or walking into it), with or without a finder. A finder helps you know where to look:

  • The Itemfinder: with it in your Bag, a hidden item on the screen (up to 7 tiles to either side and 5 up or down) makes it beep once and tells you which way the item lies. In Kanto, Professor Oak's aide on Route 11 gives it to you; in Johto, someone in Ecruteak City; in the Orange Islands, on Pinkan Island.
  • The Dowsing Machine (Unova): Bianca gives it to you at the gate by Castelia City. On its own it says how many steps away a buried item is. Switch it on from the Bag and a panel on the map shows two rods that swing toward the nearest buried item within 9 steps, their tips glowing brighter as you get closer.

The VS Seeker

The VS Seeker lets you battle trainers again. Use it from the Bag on a route: each trainer you've beaten today who stands within 9 steps of you has a chance to want a rematch (7 in 10; in Sinnoh, half). They come back with a "!" and battle you when you meet them. Others nearby show a "!" (not beaten yet) or an "X" (not ready yet). After each use it needs 100 steps to charge.

  • Where you get it: in Kanto and the Sevii Islands, in Vermilion City; in Sinnoh, on Route 207; in the Orange Islands, on Kinnow Island. Everywhere else, someone gives it to you at the next place you arrive after your second badge in that region ours.
  • Who rematches: in Kanto, the Sevii Islands, Johto, Hoenn, Sinnoh and the Orange Islands, only trainers who have a rematch team in the games do, with those teams (Johto's are SoulSilver's phone rematches; Hoenn's are Emerald's, which mostly wait until you have five badges). In Unova every route trainer can, with stronger teams made for this game ours: five steps up, opened at a quarter, half, three-quarters and all of the region's badges, and the last after you become Champion.
  • Route trainers you've beaten also come back by themselves the next day.

Bikes

A Bicycle lets you ride where the map is made for it, and gets you past things you can't cross on foot:

  • Cycling roads (like Kanto's Route 17): you can't go in without a Bicycle. On the long downhill you roll south by yourself; hold the BRAKE button to stop, and pedalling back uphill takes twice as long a step.
  • Muddy slopes: ride up them.
  • Round stones in a row (like those on the Jagged Pass): hop across them.
  • Bike ramps: ride at one for a short hop, or hold Shift for top gear and a long jump.
