Join Avenue is a new shopping street between Route 4 and Nimbasa City. Its owner is far too busy to run it, so he asks you to be its manager. Every day a few visitors come along, each with a dream of the shop they'd like to open. Your job is to fill the eight empty lots with shops and make the avenue popular.

Becoming the manager

Talk to the owner in the Manager's Office at the south end. He asks you two things in your own words: how you greet everyone, and what you say when something truly amazes you. You type both. From then on the avenue is yours, and visitors greet you with your own words. His assistants, Jacci and Future, stay in the office: talk to them to hear the avenue's rank, how many lots have shops, and the rank of the next reward.

Visitors

Three visitors come each day. Each one tells you their dream, which is one kind of shop. Talk to a visitor and choose what to do (each visitor only once a day):

  • Invite them to open the shop of their dream on the next free lot. The new shop starts with a little popularity, and so does the avenue.
  • Recommend a shop that's already open. Every visitor secretly likes one kind of shop. Recommend that kind and they hurry off to it: the shop and the avenue both grow more popular. Get it wrong and they leave, but they tell you which kind they wanted, and the avenue still gains a tiny bit.

When all three visitors have gone, new ones come the next day.

Shops and their goods

Each shop has its own rank, from 1 to 10, that rises with its popularity. A rank-1 shop sells one thing; every rank adds the next item on its list. Visit a shop to buy from it like a Poké Mart.

The avenue's rank and rewards

Join Avenue itself has a rank from 1 to 100 that rises with all the popularity you earn. Opening a shop helps a little, and a visitor who loves your recommendation helps most. As the avenue climbs, Jacci hands you a reward at these ranks:
