Hoenn can be played as either of its two versions, Omega Ruby or Alpha Sapphire. The first time you arrive in Hoenn, before anything else, the game asks which story you want to play. The choice decides which villain team you fight and which ancient Pokémon wakes up. It's made once and kept for that save.

  • Villains. Every Grunt, Admin and the team's leader takes the chosen team's name, look and Pokémon. Where the two versions gave a villain different teams (Tabitha's Koffing and Numel against Shelly's Grimer and Carvanha at Mt. Chimney, for one), you face your version's team. The leader Mega Evolves his ace: Maxie's Camerupt or Archie's Sharpedo.
  • The legend. After Seafloor Cavern, your version's legend undergoes Primal Reversion and flies off, and its weather covers every outdoor place in Hoenn until you face it in the Cave of Origin. Afterwards the team's leader gives you its Orb: hold it while the legend is in your party and it Primal Reverts in battle.
  • The Eon Pokémon. Steven takes you to Southern Island, where your version's Eon Pokémon joins you, with its Mega Stone and the Mega Bracelet. The other one waits on the island too, for you to find and catch.
  • Words. People's lines, the Journal and signs all use your version's names.

Catching both

You don't miss out on the other version's legends. Later in your journey a Journal mission tells you that the other legend, Kyogre or Groudon, rests in a cave that has opened since the Delta Episode: Marine Cave off Route 127, or Terra Cave by Route 114.
