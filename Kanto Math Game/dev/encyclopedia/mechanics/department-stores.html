A department store is a Poké Mart many floors tall. Each floor sells one kind of goods, and some floors have people with something special to offer. There is one in Celadon City, Goldenrod City, Lilycove City, Trovitopolis and Veilstone City.

How it works

Some clerks have a shelf of their own (only vitamins, only battle items, only TMs...). Others run the department counter: the same counter as a Poké Mart, which grows with your badges, plus things an ordinary Mart doesn't sell:

  • the battle items: X Attack, X Defense, X Sp. Atk, X Sp. Def, X Speed, X Accuracy, Dire Hit and Guard Spec.;
  • drinks: Fresh Water, Soda Pop and Lemonade;
  • evolution items, but only those that evolve a Pokémon that can evolve in this region (Kanto and Johto keep later regions' items until you play those regions);
  • Rare Candy and the Everstone ours.

Buying and selling work as in any Mart. Several stores have a lift to ride between floors.

Johto: Goldenrod Dept. Store

The Goldenrod Dept. Store in Goldenrod City has the department counter on every floor, and its shelves also carry extra TMs such as Protect, Light Screen, Reflect, Solar Beam, Fire Blast, Thunder, Blizzard, Hyper Beam and Focus Blast. The elevator attendant takes you to any floor from 1F to 6F, or down to B1F and the Goldenrod Underground.
