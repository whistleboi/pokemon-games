A Safari Zone is a big nature park full of Pokémon that are hard to find anywhere else. Each visit is a Safari Game: you're given your own Safari Balls and a number of steps, and you catch what you can before either runs out.

How it works

  • Safari Balls only. Each visit starts with its own Safari Balls (30 everywhere). Your own Poké Balls stay in your Bag. A Safari Ball is 1.5 times as good as a Poké Ball.
  • No battling. When you meet a wild Pokémon you can only catch it with MATH: answer the question right to throw a Safari Ball. There's no BATTLE choice in a Safari Zone.
  • Steps. Every step you take uses one up. The steps left show on screen.
  • Game over. When your steps or your Safari Balls run out, a bell rings, the game is over, and you're walked back out to the gate. Leftover Safari Balls don't come with you.
  • Areas. Each Safari Zone is split into areas, and each area has its own Pokémon. A gate line on the ground marks where one area ends and the next begins; cross it and the area's name shows on the bar.

Every Safari Zone also has its own Safari Explorer Feat in the Quest Book, counting the Pokémon you catch there with Safari Balls.

Region by region

Kanto

The way in is the Safari Zone Entrance in Fuchsia City. Step up to the desk and the attendant takes $500 for a game, as in FireRed; if you don't have enough money you're turned back. You get FireRed's 30 Safari Balls and 600 steps. The fee is spent once you come back out.

The Safari Zone has four areas: Center, East, North and West, each with its own Pokémon, and a rest house in each. Hidden away is the Secret House, where you get HM Surf, and somewhere in the park are the Warden's lost Gold Teeth: take them back to his house for HM Strength. You can fish and surf here too.

Johto

HeartGold and SoulSilver's Safari Zone, reached through the Safari Zone Gate. It has twelve kinds of area: Plains, Meadow, Savannah, Peak, Rocky Beach, Wetland, Forest, Swamp, Marshland, Wasteland, Mountain and Desert. Only six are laid out at a time. At the gate you can pick one of twelve layouts with Area Setup, and the park is arranged that way when you go in. Each area has its own Pokémon. You get 30 Safari Balls and 500 steps, and you can fish and surf.

Hoenn

The gate is on Route 121, where an attendant explains the game: 30 Safari Balls and 500 steps. The Safari Zone has six areas (Area 1 to Area 6), each with its own Pokémon. With the DexNav you can find its hidden Pokémon here too, and you can surf and fish. There's a rest house inside.

Sinnoh

Platinum's Great Marsh, entered through the Observatory Gate in Pastoria City. The attendant tells you the rules: 30 Safari Balls, and the game ends when they run out or after 500 steps. It has six areas, three side by side on each floor. Its Pokémon are Lv 20 to 26 on land, some only come out at night, and you can surf across its water. Upstairs, the Great Marsh Observatory looks out over the marsh. While Team Galactic's bomb plot is on, your rival guards the gate and nobody can go in.
