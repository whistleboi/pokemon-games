PokéMath Infinite is the game's own endless math challenge, played at the Game Corner counter. Three Pokémon appear with a math question above them and an answer under each one. Throw a Poké Ball at the Pokémon with the right answer. Keep going as long as your Poké Balls last: the more you get right, the more money you take home. It's all our own game. ours

How to play

  • You start with 5 Poké Balls and a score of 0.
  • Each round shows a question and three Pokémon, picked at random from the first 150 in the Pokédex. Tap the answer under one of them, and a Poké Ball flies at that Pokémon.
  • Every throw uses up one Poké Ball, right or wrong. A right answer adds 1 to your score.
  • Every 10 points you get a Poké Ball back.
  • When your last Poké Ball is thrown, the game is over.

There's no clock: take as long as you need over each question. The score and the Poké Balls left show in the top corner.

How hard the math is

The questions come from the same math levels as Math Battles. The game starts at your own math level (the one in Options, or the one your badges give you on AUTO), then climbs one level for every 10 points you score, up to the top level, Master Ball.

What you win

When the game ends, the clerk pays you $50 for every point: a score of 12 earns $600. PokéMath pays in money, not coins. You can then spend the money at the coin counter to buy coins for the other games, or anywhere else.

Where you can play

Talk to the clerk at any Game Corner counter with a Coin Case in your Bag. The clerk first asks if you'd like a game of PokéMath Infinite: say YES to play right away, or NO to see the counter's menu, where the POKEMATH button starts it too. That's at Celadon Game Corner, Two Island's Joyful Game Corner, Goldenrod City, Mauville City, the Battle Tower's secret floor and Veilstone City. See Game Corners.
