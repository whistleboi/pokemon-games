Pokéstar Studios, just past Virbank City, is where movies are made, and you get to be the star. You act in short films with a rental Pokémon at your side. Each film is a few scenes long; in each scene your co-star says a line and you answer with one of three. Your answers decide how the movie ends.

Your first film

When you first come to Pokéstar Studios you get a tour, and then the director is waiting on the soundstage (the Studio) with your first script: Brycen-Man Strikes Back, with Brycen himself as the villain. After that, the assistant director in the Studio lets you shoot a film whenever you like.

How a film works

The film starts with the clapperboard and a short opening. Then come three scenes. In each one your co-star speaks, and three lines appear for you to choose from:

  • the right line, the one the script wants;
  • a wrong line, which spoils the scene;
  • a strange line, which takes the story somewhere nobody expected.

The three lines are mixed up, so the right one isn't always first. When the scenes are done the film is shown in the theater, with three reviews from the audience.

Star Rank and fans

Your Star Rank goes from 0 up to 10 stars and never goes down. After a happy ending a fan brings you a present. The higher your Star Rank, the better the present:

Medals and Feats

Films count toward four medals in the Medal Rally (your first film, your first happy ending, ten films, and the top Star Rank), and toward a Feat in the Quest Book.
