Hardly any wild Pokémon live in Orre, so its trainers battle in big stadiums called Colosseums. Some Colosseums run a tournament, where challengers walk out to face you one after another; win them all and you're the champion. In two of them, the tournament leads to a Gym Leader and a badge. Others have a desk where you pick a round of four battles. And next to Agate Village stands Mt. Battle, a whole mountain built for battling.

Tournaments

  1. Sign up. Talk to the receptionist in the lobby. You're taken out to your spot in the arena.
  2. The rounds. The referee calls the first challenger, who walks in from the far side and battles you. Win, and the next walks in. Each battle opens the battle screen, and most of them are double battles.
  3. The final. Where the tournament ends with a Gym Leader, the referee first sets you the Leader's math game. Win it and the Leader takes the field. Lose it and the referee lets you try again when you're ready, without starting the tournament over. See Gyms for the Leaders' games.
  4. Losing. A loss never sends you to a Pokémon Center. Your Pokémon are healed, you're back in the lobby, and next time the tournament starts again from the first round.

Your team isn't healed between rounds, so heal up before you sign up.

Desk challenges

Three Colosseums have a desk where you choose a round. A round is four double battles in a row, and your team is healed between battles. Lose one and the round is over: you're healed and back at the desk, and next time the round starts from its first battle. Win all four for the round's prize, and the next round opens. A round you've already won can be played again, just for the battles.

Mt. Battle

Mt. Battle, next to Agate Village, is a mountain of ten areas, one above the other, with ten trainers in each. Beat the trainers in an area to go up.

  • Rest points. At the top of each area, the staff heal your team. Once you've rested at an area's top, the reception desk at the bottom can send you straight to the next area.
  • The lobby. The reception desk and a nurse wait at the foot of the mountain.
  • The top. Mt. Battle Master Battlus waits at the top of Area 10.
  • Starting over. Once Dakim has been driven off the mountain, the lobby's Start over from Area 1 sends you back to the bottom with every trainer ready for another round.
  • After the title. Once you're Champion of Orre and have beaten Battlus, Vander waits at the very top as the master of the mountain (see Part 10).
