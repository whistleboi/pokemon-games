Before a Gym Leader battles you, they want you to win a game first (see Gyms). Each game you win is saved, and you can play it again at any Game Corner's counter under GYM GAMES. Every win there pays 50 coins. The Gym games are our own idea. ours

Playing them again

  • At a Game Corner, talk to the clerk and pick GYM GAMES on the counter menu. Until you've won a Gym's game, the button says to win one first.
  • The list shows each game you've won once, with the Gym where you first won it.
  • A game is played with that Gym Leader's team, and your lead Pokémon as your partner.
  • It's as hard as the number of badges you hold in the region you're standing in: each badge makes it a little harder, up to the seventh (the eighth adds nothing more).
  • Win: 50 coins go into your Coin Case. Lose: nothing is lost; TRY AGAIN, or LEAVE to go back to the list.

How the games work

Every game opens with a card that shows its goal, how to play and its controls; GOT IT! (or Enter) starts it. A bar at the top shows how close you are to the goal, and LEAVE (or Esc) stops the game. In a Gym, losing means TRY AGAIN: it's the same game until you win it.

A few Gyms always have their own game. Every other Gym picks one of the twelve shared games at random the first time you reach the Leader, more often one that suits the Gym's type, and keeps it.

Where you can play

GYM GAMES is on the counter menu of every Game Corner: Celadon Game Corner, Two Island's Joyful Game Corner, Goldenrod City, Mauville City, the Battle Tower's secret floor and Veilstone City. The list is the same everywhere, so a game won in one region can be played in another, Cissy's Challenge too, though the Orange Islands have no Game Corner of their own. See Game Corners.
