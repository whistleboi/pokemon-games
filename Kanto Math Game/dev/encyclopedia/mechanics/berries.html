Berries are small fruits your Pokémon can hold or eat, and the main ingredient for Pokéblocks and Poffins. You find them in three ways: you grow them, you pick them from trees along the routes, or someone gives you one. Each region does it the way its own games do.

Growing Berries (Hoenn)

Hoenn's routes have patches of soft soil, in the same spots as Emerald's Berry trees. Each patch starts with a ripe tree of the Berry Emerald puts there.

  • Pick: walk up to a ripe tree to pick its Berries. They go into your Bag, and the soil is empty again.
  • Plant: walk up to empty soil and choose a Berry from your Bag to plant.
  • Grow: a planted Berry grows on the real clock, through five stages: planted, sprouted, taller, flowering, and ripe with Berries. Each kind takes its own number of hours per stage, so the game tells you roughly when it will be ready.
  • Water: with the Wailmer Pail, water the plant once in each of its growing stages. Every stage you water raises the harvest, from the kind's smallest crop toward its biggest.

Soft soil is on Routes 102, 103, 104, 110, 111, 112, 114, 115, 116, 117, 118, 119, 120, 121 and 123 (Route 123 has the most, 18 patches), and on Mirage Island.

The Pretty Petal Flower Shop on Route 104 is where it starts: the eldest sister explains how Berries grow, the middle sister gives you the Wailmer Pail, and the youngest gives you one Berry every day (one of Cheri, Chesto, Pecha, Rawst, Aspear, Leppa, Oran or Persim).

The Pokéblock Kit. Lisia gives you the Pokéblock Kit with your Contest Pass. Choose up to four different Berries and each becomes a Pokéblock in the colour of its strongest flavour (red for Spicy, blue for Dry, pink for Sweet, green for Bitter, yellow for Sour). A Berry with a strong flavour makes two. See Pokémon Contests for what Pokéblocks and Poffins are for.

Picking Berry trees (Sinnoh)

Sinnoh's routes have Berry trees you can pick once you have the Berry Pouch. The florist at Pick a Peck of Colors in Floaroma Town gives it to you on your first visit, with three each of Cheri, Chesto, Pecha, Rawst and Aspear Berries, and after that runs a Berry stand (more kinds once you have three Sinnoh badges). If you go to the Poffin House in Hearthome City first, you get the Pouch there instead.

On a route with Berry trees, PICK BERRIES gathers 2 to 4 Berries from the kinds that grow there, once a day per route. The season and the weather change the crop ours:

  • Spring and autumn: one more Berry. Winter: one fewer.
  • Rain on the route today: one more. Harsh sun: one fewer.

Routes 201 to 210, 212 to 215, 218 and 222 have Berry trees. Cook your Berries into Poffins at the Poffin House.

A Berry a day

Some people give you a Berry (or a few) once a day. Come back the next day for another.
