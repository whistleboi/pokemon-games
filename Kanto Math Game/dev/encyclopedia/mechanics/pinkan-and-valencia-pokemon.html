Some Pokémon in the Orange Islands come in colours you won't find anywhere else. On Pinkan Island many of them are pink, from eating the island's Pinkan Berries. In Valencia Park on Valencia Island, a few kinds wear Valencia's own colours. These are the fan game's colour forms of ordinary Pokémon: a pink Butterfree is still a Butterfree, just pink.

Where to find them

Pinkan Island

Pinkan Island's grass has 36 pink Pokémon, mixed in with the ordinary ones: the Caterpie, Weedle, Pidgey, Rattata, Nidoran, Oddish, Paras, Venonat, Diglett, Mankey, Bellsprout, Exeggcute and Rhyhorn families, and Scyther. Each pink kind is rare (most turn up about 1 time in 100), and the fully evolved ones are there as well as the babies.

Valencia Park

Valencia Park has 17 Valencia forms in all. Twelve can be met in the park's grass, among them Butterfree, Raticate, Nidoran, Nidorina, Nidorino, Vileplume, Paras, Poliwhirl, Weepinbell, Cloyster and Gyarados. They're rare too: most turn up about 1 or 2 times in 100. The rest (Nidoqueen, Nidoking, Parasect, Poliwrath and Victreebel) you get by evolving.

Evolving

A Pinkan or Valencia Pokémon keeps its colour when it evolves, as long as the next stage has that colour too. A pink Caterpie becomes a pink Metapod, then a pink Butterfree.

Eggs

Leave one at the Day Care and the colour can pass to its Egg. It can come from either parent (the mother's comes first). If the bottom of the family has no form in that colour, the Egg hatches into the lowest stage that does: there's no Valencia Magikarp, so an Egg from a Valencia Gyarados hatches into a Valencia Gyarados.

Mega Evolution

A Pinkan or Valencia Pokémon whose species can Mega Evolve can do it too, with the same Mega Stone, and it stays in its own colours as a Mega: a pink Beedrill becomes a pink Mega Beedrill. See Mega Evolution.
