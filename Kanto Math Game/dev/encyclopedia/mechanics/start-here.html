This is a Pokémon journey with one big twist: every time your Pokémon uses a move in battle, you answer a math question. A right answer makes the move work at full strength, and a quick one can power it up. You catch Pokémon with math too. Everything else is a Pokémon adventure: you pick a first partner, walk from town to town, battle trainers, earn Gym badges, stop the villains and take on the Pokémon League. Then a new region is waiting. ours

This page is the place to begin. For how the questions work, see The math.

Starting a new game

A new game takes you through a few screens, one after another:

  1. You. Pick how you look, then type your name.
  2. Your region. Pick the region your journey starts in. Each card shows a picture of the region's first town.
  3. Your rival. Each region has its own rival. Where the region's game had two to choose from, you pick one, then name them (or keep their usual name). Orre skips this screen.
  4. Your math level. Pick how hard the questions are, or leave it on AUTO so they grow with you. You can change this any time in Options (see The math).

Then your journey begins in the region's first town. When you meet your first partner on the starter screen, you pick one and answer two math questions to make friends with it.

The regions you can start in

The region list follows the order of the games: Kanto, the Orange Islands, Johto, Hoenn, Orre, Sinnoh, Unova, then Kalos, Alola, Galar and Paldea. Those last four are on the list, but this encyclopedia doesn't cover them yet. Each region you can start in has its own first town, professor and first partners:

Going from region to region

Mom's move

Once you're Champion of a region, Mom calls you. Your prize money is enough for a new home, and she asks where to move. The list shows only regions you haven't played yet, so every move is a brand-new adventure. You can say you're not ready and pick later: visit Mom at home and she'll ask again. When you move, you start in the new region's first town and meet its professor and rival. Once you've played every region, there's nowhere new left to move.

Kanto and Johto are next door to each other, so Mom doesn't move you between them: she tells you how to get there yourself.

Kanto and Johto

Becoming Champion of Kanto or Johto opens the road between them. From the Pokémon League's Reception Gate, Route 26 and Route 27 lead east to New Bark Town, and Route 22 leads west to Viridian City. You also get an S.S. Ticket for the S.S. Aqua, which sails between Vermilion City and Olivine City. Later, with the Pass and the Power Plant fixed, the Magnet Train runs between Saffron City and Goldenrod City.

The Sevii Islands

The Seagallop ferry links Kanto with the Sevii Islands. Bill takes you from Cinnabar Island to One Island once you have the Volcano Badge or the Tri-Pass. With the Tri-Pass it also sails from Vermilion City to One Island, and with the Rainbow Pass to Four Island. From every island a Seagallop goes back to Vermilion City.

The Orange Islands

When you win Kanto's title, a letter from Professor Ivy invites you to the Orange Islands. When you move there, a Rocket Blimp carries you over the sea to Valencia Island's airport. Once you've lived there and won the Orange League, the blimp flies from Vermilion City's harbour to Valencia whenever you like. A Champion of both Kanto and Johto can also take a ship from Olivine City.

Long trips heal

Long rides between regions (the ships, the Seagallop to and from Kanto, the Magnet Train) heal your whole team on the way.

Each region keeps its own things

Each region has its own party, its own PC and its own Bag, so a new region starts fresh, key items too. When you first step into a region where you have no Pokémon yet, you're sent to its professor for a new partner. The Sevii Islands share Kanto's.

  • Money is shared everywhere.
  • Travel tickets come with you from Bag to Bag: the S.S. Ticket, the Mystic Ticket, the Aurora Ticket, and the GS Ball too.
  • Linking. When you become Champion of a second region, the professor calls: the PCs and Bags of the regions you've won are joined into one. Pokémon from any of them can join your team, in every region you've conquered. Each title after that joins the link too.

The Journal and the gold marker

Open the Journal from your MENU to see what to do next. Its STORY tab tells your adventure in chapters, SIDE lists errands, and FEATS keeps your achievements. Press GUIDE ME on a chapter or errand and a gold marker bobs over the spot to go to, with a gold arrow at the edge of the screen when it's further away. Between places, the way to go next is lit in gold. See The Journal, and every region's book on the Journal pages.

Options worth knowing

  • MATH: pick your math level, or AUTO, and the Answer timer (Off, Normal, or 50% more time) for questions with a countdown. See The math.
  • Battle style (GAMEPLAY): Classic battles on their own screen, or Overworld battles right on the map. See Math Battles.
  • Level cap (GAMEPLAY): Story (your Pokémon can't grow past the next Gym Leader's strongest Pokémon + 2), Obedience (strong Pokémon may not listen until you earn badges) or Off. See Level Cap.
  • Also on GAMEPLAY: Difficulty (EXP Mode gives 30% more Exp.), Game speed (1x or 2x), Exp Share and Autosave.
