In Johto your Pokégear can play the radio, as in HeartGold and SoulSilver. Some stations are just for listening, but others change which wild Pokémon you meet, and one gives you a password to trade for prizes.

Getting the Radio Card

You need two things: the Pokégear, which your Mom gives you in Johto, and the Radio Card. The Radio Card is the prize for the quiz on the first floor of the Radio Tower in Goldenrod City. The quiz shows you sums ours like "12 + 30 = 42", and you answer YES if it's right or NO if it isn't. Get five right in a row and the card is yours; one wrong answer ends that try, but you can try again as often as you like.

With both, a RADIO button appears in your menu. Pick it and choose a station.

The stations

A station you tune in to stays on until you pick another, so the March or the Lullaby keeps working as you walk.

Buena's Password

Each day has its own password. Listen to Buena's show, then go up to the second floor of the Radio Tower and talk to Buena. Tell her the password and she gives you a Blue Card point. You can do this once a day, and only after hearing that day's show. Some point totals bring a prize:
