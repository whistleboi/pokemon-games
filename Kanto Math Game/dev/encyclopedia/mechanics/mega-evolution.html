Mega Evolution is a power-up that lasts one battle. A Pokémon that has a Mega form changes into it, with new stats, sometimes a new type and a new Ability, and changes back when the battle is over. Primal Reversion works the same way for Groudon and Kyogre. The rules follow Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire.

What you need

  • A Key Stone: the Mega Bracelet (or a Mega Ring).
  • The Pokémon's own Mega Stone in your Bag, such as Scizorite for Scizor. Charizard and Mewtwo each have two (X and Y).
  • Rayquaza needs no Mega Stone, only the Key Stone.
  • For Primal Reversion: the Red Orb for Groudon or the Blue Orb for Kyogre, in your Bag. No Key Stone is needed.

In battle

When the Pokémon you have out can change, a MEGA (or PRIMAL) button shows in the battle menu. Press it, then pick a move and answer its math question as usual. When the turn plays out, the change happens first, before anyone moves (if both sides change, the faster Pokémon goes first). It doesn't use up your turn. Press BACK on the move list to take it back.

  • Only one Pokémon on each side can Mega Evolve or Primal Revert in a battle.
  • Primal Groudon brings extremely harsh sunlight, Primal Kyogre heavy rain, and Mega Rayquaza strong winds. The weather ends when that Pokémon leaves the field.
  • Some trainers Mega Evolve their own Pokémon the moment it comes out. In Hoenn, Steven's Metagross and Wally's Gallade do.

Mega Evolution on the map

On a walked map, a MEGA button shows when the Pokémon following you can Mega Evolve. Press it and your Pokémon stays Mega Evolved for a minute of walking (the minute waits while you battle), and goes into battles that way: that counts as the battle's one Mega Evolution. Afterwards it changes back and the Key Stone needs 30 seconds before the next one. This comes from Legends: Z-A. ours

Region by region

Among the regions in this guide, Hoenn is where Mega Evolution comes into the story, as in Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire. Each region keeps its own Bag, so the other regions have no Key Stone or Mega Stones of their own. In the Orange Islands, Pinkan and Valencia Pokémon Mega Evolve like their ordinary species, in their own colours.

Hoenn

After you win the Balance Badge, Steven meets you on Route 118 and takes you to Southern Island. There Latios or Latias joins you, and Steven gives you its Mega Stone and the Mega Bracelet. Later, once the Weather Institute is cleared, Steven finds you on Route 120 and gives you the Devon Scope and the Mega Stone for your starter (Sceptilite, Blazikenite or Swampertite).

After the Cave of Origin, the villain team's leader hands over the orb they stole. Keep it in your Bag while that legendary Pokémon is in your party, and it can Primal Revert in battle.

Once the crisis is over, a stone collector on Route 114 sells the Mega Stones found nowhere else in Hoenn: the other two starters' stones, Gengarite, Audinite, Garchompite, Lucarionite and Diancite.
