A TM (Technical Machine) holds a move. In this game a TM never runs out, and you don't even have to use it: while it's in your Bag, every Pokémon you own that can learn its move can pick that move. Move tutors work the same way once you've paid them.

The TM list

Every region uses the same list of 348 TMs, numbered like Scarlet and Violet's ours:

  • TM001 to TM229 are Scarlet and Violet's own TMs, in their order.
  • TM230 to TM329 are the other moves that have been TMs in the main series games.
  • TM330 to TM348 are the TMs only Legends: Z-A has.

So a TM has the same number wherever you find it. Hoenn's Rock Tomb is TM036, the same TM036 Brock gives in Kanto.

How TMs work

  • They never run out. You can only hold one of each TM.
  • A TM unlocks its move. With the TM in your Bag, its move joins the move list of every Pokémon that can learn it, in your party and in the PC. Pick it from the MOVES page of the Pokémon's Summary.
  • Using a TM from the Bag teaches the move straight away if the Pokémon knows fewer than four moves. If it already knows four, the move is ready to pick in its Summary.

Where TMs come from

  • Poké Marts sell every TM, a few at a time: each TM has a tier from 0 to 8 and goes on sale once you hold that many badges in the region. Prices go up with the tier, from $1000 to $3000. See Poké Marts.
  • Gym Leaders, the Elite Four and the Champion each give one when you beat them (see Gyms). Most give the TM from their own game.
  • The story: TMs lying on the maps and TMs people give you, as in each region's games.
  • Department stores have TM counters (see Department stores).
  • Move tutors sell the TMs of the moves they teach (below).

Move tutors

Move tutors teach a move to one of your Pokémon ours. When their move is also a TM, they offer you a choice: LEARN IT NOW, or BUY THE TM from them. Either way, the first time you pay a tutor the move is yours for good: its TM goes in your Bag, so every Pokémon of yours that can learn it has it, and the tutor teaches it again for free.

  • A TM that a tutor in a region teaches is sold only by that tutor, not by that region's shops.
  • Tutors are paid in different ways, as in their games: money (a TM's price), Battle Points from a Battle Frontier, coloured Shards, Game Corner coins, or nothing at all.
  • Some tutors only teach a Pokémon that loves you very much, such as the tutors of Draco Meteor and of the first partners' ultimate moves (Frenzy Plant, Blast Burn and Hydro Cannon).
  • Only a Pokémon in your party that can learn the move, and doesn't have it yet, can be picked.

The Move Reminder and the Move Deleter

Your Pokémon never forget a move. Everything a Pokémon has learned stays on its list, and you choose its four moves on the MOVES page of its Summary, any time, for free ours. So the Move Reminders and Move Deleters of the regions are out of work. They're still at home, and each time you talk to them they have a new grumble about it.
