Each region gives you a gadget or two, like the one its own games have. They show up as buttons in your MENU once you have them: PHONE, RADIO, NAV and so on.

The Pokégear phone

In Johto, Mom gives you the Pokégear in New Bark Town after you get your first Pokémon, as in HeartGold and SoulSilver. The phone also works in Kanto when the Pokégear is in your Bag there.

  • Getting numbers. Some route Trainers ask for your number after you beat them, if you carry the Pokégear. Some people give you theirs in the story, like Whitney after the Plain Badge.
  • Rematch calls. Each of these Trainers has stronger teams waiting, from SoulSilver's own rematches. When the next one is ready, which happens as your story moves on, they ring you the next time you arrive somewhere. Once per team.
  • Calling them. Open PHONE and tap a name: they tell you whether they're ready to battle and where to find them. Meet them again and they battle with their best ready team. Some give you a gift with the win, once.
  • Story calls. Professor Elm rings when something terrible happens at his Lab, and again about the Egg after your first badge.

The Pokégear radio

Win the Radio Card by answering five yes-or-no sums in a row at the Radio Tower quiz in Goldenrod City. Then RADIO appears in your MENU. The stations, the March and the Lullaby, and Buena's Password are all on the Pokégear Radio.

The DexNav (Hoenn)

Your rival gives you the DexNav on Route 101, and NAV appears in your MENU. Open it on a route to see the Pokémon that live there, how often each one turns up, and which ones you've caught.

Out on the routes, the DexNav also spots hidden Pokémon: now and then a tail pokes out of the grass, the sand or a cave floor, or a ripple shows on the water while you surf. Creep up to it and you battle one of the area's hidden Pokémon. Take too long and it slips away. Hidden Pokémon from the first three generations turn up after the trouble with Groudon and Kyogre is over, and those from later generations once you're Hoenn's Champion. More on Wild Pokémon.

The Poké Radar (Sinnoh)

As in Platinum, Roseanne gives you the Poké Radar once you have the National Pokédex. It charges as you walk (50 steps). Use it in tall grass and four patches of grass nearby shake. Step into one to meet a wild Pokémon. After that, the grass shakes again by itself: meet the same kind of Pokémon again and your chain grows, which makes that Pokémon, and a shiny one, more likely. Step anywhere else, or leave, and the chain breaks. More on Wild Pokémon.

The Xtransceiver (Unova)

In Black 2 and White 2 your wrist carries an Xtransceiver, a video phone. When a call comes in, the screen dims, the Xtransceiver rings and opens up with the caller's face on it, and their words type out as they talk. You can't call anyone yourself. Mom calls when you reach Virbank City and on Route 7, and your rival swaps numbers with you in Castelia City.
