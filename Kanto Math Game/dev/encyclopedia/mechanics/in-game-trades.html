Here and there you meet people who want a certain Pokémon and will give you one of theirs for it. Bring the Pokémon they ask for in your party, talk to them, and the swap is made.

How it works

  • The Pokémon they want must be in your party, not in the PC, and not an Egg. If you have more than one, the first one in your party is traded.
  • The Pokémon you get is the same level as the one you gave, with full HP.
  • Many come with a nickname from their first Trainer, and many hold an item. You also get a copy of a holdable item in your Bag, so you can take it off and give it back.
  • A traded Pokémon keeps its first Trainer's name for it: the Name Rater won't rename it, as in the games.
  • Each person trades only once, and thanks you if you talk to them again afterwards. If you don't have what they want, they simply ask again next time.
  • Your rowdy Pikachu from Kanto is never traded away.

Some people swap items instead of Pokémon, like Anville Town's weekend traders in Unova (see Unova errands).
