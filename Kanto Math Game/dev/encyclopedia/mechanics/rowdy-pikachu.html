Kanto has a little piece of Pokémon Yellow in it: a rowdy Pikachu that refuses to live in a Poké Ball. Professor Oak gives it to you partway through your journey. It walks with you everywhere, it never evolves, and it has a surfing game all of its own at the beach house on Route 19.

Getting Pikachu

Once you beat Lt. Surge in Vermilion City and win the Thunder Badge, a Journal side mission, "A Rowdy Pikachu", sends you back to Professor Oak's Lab in Pallet Town. He hands you the Pikachu he caught at the edge of town. It joins your team at Lv 5, with the best possible individual strengths (IVs), and knows Volt Tackle, Thunder Shock and Growl. If your party is already full, it goes home to your Mom instead.

What makes it different

  • It never evolves. Not by level, and not with a Thunder Stone either.
  • It always follows you. While it's in your party, Pikachu is the Pokémon walking behind you on the map, whoever leads your team.
  • It surfs its own way. When you Surf with Pikachu in your party, you stand on its surfboard instead of riding your usual Pokémon.
  • It won't leave you. It refuses to go into the PC, to be released, to stay at the Day Care or to be traded.

Leaving Pikachu with Mom

Your Mom is the only one Pikachu will stay with. When she heals your team at home, she asks if you'd like to leave Pikachu with her, or take it back if it's already there. She keeps it in one spot shared by every region's Mom, so you can leave it in one region and pick it up at home in another. You can't leave it if it's the only Pokémon you have with you, and you need a free space in your party to take it back.

How much it likes you

The old woman in the Celadon Mansion, once she has given you some Tea, will look at your Pikachu and tell you how it feels about you. There are five answers, from not trusting you yet to adoring you, and they follow its friendship.

Pikachu's Beach

The Surfin' Dude runs the Summer Beach House on Route 19. Bring the rowdy Pikachu in your party and he lets you play his game: surf Pikachu all the way to the shore. Without it, he just asks you to come back with a Pikachu that can ride the waves.

How to play

  • Waves keep throwing Pikachu up into the air. While it's up, tap the left or right side of the screen (or use the arrow keys) to flip that way.
  • Land the right way up and you score Radness. A flip that isn't finished when Pikachu lands is a wipeout: no points, the ride slows right down for two seconds, and Pikachu loses any extra speed it had built up.
  • Clean flips make Pikachu ride faster: the more flips in a landing, the bigger the boost.
  • The HP counter starts at 6,000 and runs down all the time, about a hundred seconds in all. If it reaches zero before you reach the shore, the ride is over.
  • With every wave a quick sum appears with three answers to pick from (tap one, or press 1, 2 or 3). Answer it right before Pikachu lands and that landing's points are doubled. A wrong answer, or none, costs you nothing. ours
