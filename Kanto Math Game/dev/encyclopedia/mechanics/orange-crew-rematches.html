In the Orange Islands, each member of the Orange Crew battles you once on your way to the Orange League. After you become Orange League Champion, they're all ready to battle you again, as many times as you like, with their teams grown much stronger. ours

Rudy, Luana and Danny

Rudy, Luana and Danny are Orange Crew Gym Leaders whose badge battles happen away from a Gym hall: Rudy up on his cliff, Luana in the Kumquat Hotel's battle arena, and Danny at the top of Mt. Navel. Each battles you once during the story. Before you're Champion, talking to them again just gets a friendly word. After you beat Drake, talk to them and they offer a rematch instead.

Each rematch uses the same Pokémon and moves as their first battle, but every Pokémon's level is raised by the same amount the game adds for League rematches in regions whose games have none of their own. There's no badge or TM this time: win, and you get five Poké Balls. Lose, and they tell you to train up and come back.

Cissy and Drake

Cissy, in her Gym on Mikan Island, and Drake, in Pummelo Stadium, battle again too. After the title, a Gym you've cleared can be taken on again from its first trainer, with every level raised by the same amount, and no badge a second time. See Gyms.
