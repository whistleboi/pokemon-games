Unova follows the story of Pokémon Black 2 or White 2. The very first time you arrive in Unova, before anything else happens, the game asks which story you want to play. You pick once, and that choice stays for the whole region.

Choosing your story

The choice screen shows both versions side by side: Black 2 with Zekrom and Black City, and White 2 with Reshiram and White Forest. Tap the one you want. Like Hoenn's Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire choice, it comes up the moment you first step into the region.

What changes

The version changes the story only. People's words name your version's dragon, Kyurem and city, and a few people, items, Trainers and places belong to one version.

  • Route 4. In Black 2 the route is a dig site with ruins; in White 2 it is lined with new buildings, and its map is different. The Trainers there are different too: Policemen, Roughnecks and Hooligans in Black 2; Backpackers, a Lady, a Rich Boy and Pokéfans in White 2.
  • Route 4's stat fans. One house wants to see a Pokémon's Speed in Black 2 and its Attack in White 2, and they give different Wings (see Unova errands).
  • Motorcyclist Charles on Route 5 and later in Driftveil City: he offers a Triple Battle in Black 2 and a Rotation Battle in White 2, with his three Pokémon in a different order.
  • Hidden Grottoes use your version's own list of Pokémon (see Hidden Grottoes).
  • Small things: the old man in Route 6's rest house talks about his Cottonee in Black 2 and his Petilil in White 2.

What stays the same

The wild Pokémon never change. Both versions' wild Pokémon are in the same tables, so you can catch every species in either story, whichever version you picked.

Before you choose. Until a version is picked, people speak about "the legendary dragon" without naming it.
