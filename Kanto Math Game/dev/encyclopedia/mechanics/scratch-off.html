The Scratch-Off is a little game of luck at a Battle Frontier. Instead of coins, you pay with Battle Points (BP), the points you win at the battle facilities (see Battle facilities). 1 BP buys three cards. On each card you scratch three of its nine squares; three of the same Pokémon win that Pokémon's prize.

Where you get a card

Talk to the Scratch-Off clerk outside, at:

  • the Johto Battle Frontier, and
  • the Sinnoh Battle Frontier.

Talk to the clerk and 1 BP buys your three cards. If you leave before scratching a single square, the clerk gives your BP back. If you have no BP, the clerk tells you to go and win some at the facilities first.

How a card works

  • A card has nine silver squares, three by three. Tap a square to scratch it; it flips over to show a Pokémon.
  • Under the silver are the four prize Pokémon, Pikachu, Togepi, Munchlax and Psyduck, and one Ditto. On each card, one of the four is there three times, two are there twice each, and the last one once.
  • You may scratch only three squares. If all three show the same Pokémon, you win that Pokémon's prize.
  • Ditto counts as whichever Pokémon the other two are. So two of a Pokémon plus the Ditto wins too.
  • After the third square, NEXT CARD brings up your next card, until all three are used. Once you've started scratching, cards you leave unplayed are lost.

The prizes

The prize list is on the right of the screen, beside each Pokémon. Every day one of the four Pokémon wins a Nugget, and each of the other three wins three of a Berry. Which Pokémon wins what, and which three Berries are on offer, change from one day to the next (by your device's calendar date). The Berries are picked from these 23:

Babiri, Charti, Chilan, Chople, Coba, Colbur, Grepa, Haban, Hondew, Kasib, Kebia, Kelpsy, Occa, Passho, Payapa, Pomeg, Qualot, Rindo, Shuca, Tamato, Tanga, Wacan and Yache Berry.

The prizes go straight into your Bag.
