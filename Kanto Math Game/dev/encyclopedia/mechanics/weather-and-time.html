The game runs on your computer's real clock and calendar. The time of day decides which wild Pokémon are out, the month decides the season, and in some regions each route has its own weather every day. Weather also carries into battles.

  • Wild Pokémon: Johto has HeartGold and SoulSilver's morning, day and night tables, and Sinnoh has day and night tables. See Wild Pokémon.
  • Evolution: some Pokémon only evolve by day (6 AM to 8 PM) or by night (8 PM to 6 AM), such as Umbreon.
  • Tides: in Hoenn's Shoal Cave the tide is in from 3:00 to 9:00 and from 15:00 to 21:00, as in the games.
  • Night trainers: a few trainers only stand out at night, such as Platinum's night patrol on Route 212 and Route 222.
  • Once a day: daily things (a Berry from someone, a swarm, a rematch) follow the calendar date and start fresh at midnight.

The season changes every month, as in Black and White:

A region with seasons shows the season and today's weather under the MENU button, and tells you when a new season starts. Of the regions you can walk, Hoenn, Sinnoh and Unova have seasons; Kanto, Johto and the Orange Islands don't.

  • Wild Pokémon that like the season show up more often ours: Grass, Bug and Fairy in spring; Fire, Bug and Water in summer; Ghost, Dark and Grass in autumn; Ice in winter (Bug and Grass less). Unova's places use Black 2 and White 2's own tables for each season instead, where the game has them.
  • Deerling and Sawsbuck wear the current season's coat.
  • Battle backgrounds change with the season where a place has seasonal pictures (Unova's).

Weather

There are four kinds of weather: rain, harsh sun, sandstorm and snow. Weather comes from three places:

  • Fixed weather: some places always have the same weather, as in their games (see the table below).
  • Daily weather ours: in a region with seasons, each outdoor route rolls its weather once a day from that season's chances, so a route keeps the same weather all day. Towns and caves stay clear.
  • Story weather: a story can bring weather for a while, like the storm over Shamouti Island during the legend of the three islands.

What weather does

  • In battle: a battle in a place with weather starts with it, for 5 turns (8 if you carry the matching weather rock: Damp Rock for rain, Heat Rock for sun, Smooth Rock for sandstorm, Icy Rock for snow). You can also use a weather rock from the Bag once a battle to call its weather.
    • Rain: Water moves are 1.5 times as strong, Fire moves half as strong.
    • Harsh sun: Fire moves are 1.5 times as strong, Water moves half as strong.
    • Sandstorm: every Pokémon that isn't Rock, Ground or Steel loses a little HP each turn, and Rock types are tougher against special moves.
    • Snow: Ice types are tougher against physical moves.
  • Wild Pokémon ours: rain brings out more Water and Electric Pokémon (fewer Fire); sun more Fire and Grass (fewer Ice); sandstorm more Rock, Ground and Steel; snow more Ice (fewer Fire).
  • Evolution: a Pokémon that needs rain to evolve only does so where it's raining.
  • Shops: Poké Marts sell the weather rocks for the weather that happens in their region this season.

Region by region

Kanto, Johto and the Orange Islands

No seasons and no daily weather. Johto keeps its morning, day and night Pokémon. The Orange Islands have their few places with fixed weather, and the storm and snow of the Shamouti Island story.

Hoenn

Seasons and daily weather on the routes, with Routes 111, 119 and 120 keeping their own. Between clearing the Seafloor Cavern and calming the legend at the Cave of Origin, Groudon's extremely harsh sun (Omega Ruby) or Kyogre's heavy rain (Alpha Sapphire) covers every outdoor place in Hoenn. Water moves don't work at all in that sun, and Fire moves don't work in that rain.

The Weather Dial ours. Once the skies are calm, the director of the Weather Institute gives you the Weather Dial and one of each weather rock. Use it from the Bag on an outdoor route, once a day: set a weather rock in it (the rock is used up) to give that route its weather until midnight, or clear the skies for free. It works on any outdoor route in a region with weather, but it can't change the season. The receptionist at the Institute also hands out one weather rock a day, after you've saved the Institute.

Sinnoh

Seasons and daily weather, snowy routes in the north, and day and night Pokémon.

Unova

Black 2 and White 2's own seasons: many places have a table of Pokémon for each season, and the battle backgrounds change too. Daily weather on the routes, sandstorms in the desert, and snow on Route 7 in winter.
