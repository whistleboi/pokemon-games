The Journal is your Quest Book. Open it from your MENU (JOURNAL). It has three tabs: STORY tells your adventure as a book of arcs and chapters, SIDE lists errands you can do along the way, and FEATS keeps track of your achievements. Pick any row on the left to read it in full on the right. ours

Each region has its own book. To read every arc, chapter, errand and feat, see that region's Journal page:

STORY: arcs and chapters

The main story is split into arcs (ARC 1, ARC 2...), and each arc into titled chapters (CH. 1, CH. 2...). Each chapter is one step on the way to the Pokémon League.

  • Only what you've reached. Arcs still to come aren't in the book yet. The arc you're playing is open, with its chapters listed under it.
  • ??? A chapter you haven't reached yet shows only "???", and its page says that part of the story hasn't happened yet. It gets its title when the story gets there, so you never read ahead.
  • NOW. One chapter at a time is the one you're on. It is marked in gold, and its page tells you what to do next and where.
  • DONE. Finished chapters are ticked off. A finished arc sums up its chapters as a short "Previously...".
  • OPEN. Now and then a chapter can already be started while another one is shown.

The Journal shows one main story step at a time, the newest one, and the story of the region you're in comes first.

SIDE: errands

The SIDE tab lists errands and events that aren't needed to reach the League: people who want something, places worth a visit, Pokémon to find. Errands you can do now are at the top; finished ones are greyed out below them. An errand's page says what to do and, if there is one, what you get for it.

FEATS: achievements

Feats are goals you work toward just by playing: catching different Pokémon, beating Trainers, fishing, finding hidden things, evolving Pokémon, picking Berries, walking, getting sums right in a row, visiting places and more. Each region has its own list, and the FEATS tab shows the one for the region you're in.

  • Tiers. A feat has up to five tiers, each a bigger goal than the last: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum and Master. The tab shows your count next to the goal of the next tier.
  • Prizes. Every tier pays out once, as soon as you reach it: a TM, an item, money, or a mix, and more for the harder tiers. A message tells you which feat and tier you've reached.
  • Counted where you are. Things you do count toward the feats of the region you're in.

In Unova there are also Medals: Mr. Medal hands them out for things you do on your journey (see Medal Rally).

GUIDE ME: finding the way

Pick a chapter or an errand you can do now and press GUIDE ME. The game then points you to it until it's done or you press GUIDING to turn it off. The bar at the bottom of the Journal says which mission is being guided. If you haven't picked one, the game guides you to your current story chapter.

  • On the map. A gold marker bobs over the spot to go to: a tile, a person to talk to, or a door. If it's off the screen, a gold arrow at the edge points the way. When the goal is somewhere else, the marker shows the way out toward it, even through the rooms of a building.
  • Between places. The way to go next is lit in gold, and the screen shows your GOAL and the NEXT place on the way.
  • On the Town Map the goal is marked.

Some missions don't point to one place. Then the Journal tells you to explore to find it.
