A Hidden Grotto is a small, quiet clearing tucked away off a route, reached through a gap in the trees. Each grotto holds just one thing at a time: a Pokémon, an item lying in the open, or an item buried in the grass. Once you've taken it, the grotto is empty, but it can fill up again later.

Your first grotto

After you win the Bolt Badge, Bianca catches up with you on Route 5 to give you HM02 Fly. Then she hears rustling and shows you the big shadow under the trees: Route 5's grotto. This first time a Minccino is waiting inside, a level 22 one with its Hidden Ability, Skill Link.

What a grotto can hold

  • A Pokémon, standing in the middle of the clearing. Walk up to it to battle. A grotto Pokémon always has its Hidden Ability, which makes grottoes the place to look for one. Once you've met it (caught it, beaten it, or run away), it's gone.
  • An item in a Poké Ball, lying in the clearing: usually Poké Balls, Potions or Repels, sometimes better ones, and now and then an evolution stone (most grottoes have the Fire Stone; a few have other stones).
  • A buried item, shown as something in the grass: Mulches, Tiny or Big Mushrooms, colored Shards, and very rarely a Rare Candy, PP Up or PP Max.

The Pokémon in a grotto depend on your version (Black 2 or White 2). Each grotto has its own list in three groups: common ones, uncommon ones and rare ones. The grotto pages below show who can turn up in each one, and wild Pokémon covers the other ways to meet Pokémon.

Refilling

An empty grotto can fill up again as you explore. Every 256 steps you walk anywhere in Unova, each empty grotto has a 5% chance to refill. When it does:

What a refilled grotto holds
ChanceWhat
20%a Pokémon (rare 1%, uncommon 4%, common 15%)
40%an item in the open
40%a buried item

So it pays to come back after a long walk. The first time you look into a grotto, it's already filled with something.
