Almost every town has a Pokémon Center. Walk in and talk to the nurse at the counter: your whole team is healed straight away, and it never costs a thing. A short jingle plays while your Pokémon get better.

How it works

  • Healing. Every Pokémon in your party gets all its HP back and is cured, fainted ones too.
  • The last Center you used. The game remembers it. If all your Pokémon faint, you wake up at the last Center you used in the region you're in. If you haven't used one there yet, you go back to the town where you started that region.
  • The PC. People in the Centers point you to the PC in the corner, but in this game you can open the PC from your MENU anywhere, in towns and on routes ours. It stores the Pokémon that don't fit in your party of six: catch a Pokémon with a full party and it goes straight to the PC.
  • Pokémon Centers on routes. A few routes have a Center of their own, like Route 3's (with a man selling a Magikarp) and Route 10's.

The PC

The PC shows your party on the left and one Box on the right. A Box holds 30 Pokémon in 6 rows of 5, as in the games, and you turn from Box to Box with the arrows. Tap a Pokémon for a small menu: MOVE picks it up (tap a spot to put it down, or to swap), SUMMARY shows its details, and RELEASE lets it go. Each Box can be sorted by Pokédex number or from A to Z, and you can search and filter to find one Pokémon among many.

Each region keeps its own Boxes, and the PC is named after the person who runs it there, as in that region's games.

Upstairs and downstairs

Most Centers have a second floor where, in the games, Trainers meet to link up, trade and battle. The rooms are here and their attendants are at their desks, but everything is closed: they tell you to come back another time. Which desks you find depends on the region (see below).

Region by region

Kanto and the Sevii Islands

Kanto's Centers are built like FireRed's. Upstairs are four attendants: a Mystery Gift lady (nothing has arrived for you), and the Union Room, Trade Center and Colosseum desks, all closed (see Viridian City's Center). On One Island the Center is the Pokémon Network Center, where Celio works on his machine; it has a shop counter of its own.

Johto

Johto's Centers follow HeartGold and SoulSilver. Upstairs are the Wi-Fi Club, the Union Room counter and a link desk, all shut for now. Some Centers downstairs have helpful people, like the fishing guru and the Apricorn fan in Route 32's Center.

Orange Islands

A Chansey stands by the counter in every Orange Islands Center. Upstairs there are link, trade and Colosseum attendants, all closed.

Hoenn

Upstairs in Hoenn's Centers you find a gift attendant, a link attendant, a trade attendant and a record attendant. None of them have anything for you yet.

Sinnoh

Sinnoh's Centers have two extra floors. Upstairs are the Wi-Fi Club, the Union Room and the Colosseum. Downstairs (B1F) is the Wi-Fi Plaza with its guide and a link desk. All are closed for now. See Jubilife City's Center.

Unova

From Unova on, the Pokémon Center and the Poké Mart are one building, as in Black 2 and White 2: the shop counter is inside the Center (see Poké Marts). Mr. Medal waits in Unova's Centers to hand over the Medals you've earned (see Medal Rally). See Aspertia City's Center.
