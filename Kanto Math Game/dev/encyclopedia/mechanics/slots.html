The slot machine is the classic Game Corner game. You put in 1, 2 or 3 coins, start the three reels spinning, then stop them one at a time. If the pictures on a lit pay line match, the machine pays out coins. It's one of the games of the Game Corners, so you need a Coin Case and some coins first.

How to play

  1. Bet. Press BET 1, BET 2 or BET 3 (or the keys 1, 2 and 3). You can change your mind before you spin: the coins of your old bet come back first.
  2. Start. Press START (or Space or Enter). If you haven't bet yet, START bets as many coins as it can, up to 3.
  3. Stop the reels. Tap the round STOP button under a reel, or press 1, 2 or 3 to stop that reel. Space or Enter (or a tap on the reels) stops the next reel still spinning. If you don't stop them, they stop by themselves after about six seconds.

When the last reel stops, any winning lines flash and your winnings are counted into your Coin Case. EXIT (or Esc) takes you back.

Pay lines

Each reel shows three pictures, so the machine has three rows. The more you bet, the more lines count:

Your bet and its lines
BetLines that pay
1 coinThe middle row
2 coinsAll three rows
3 coinsAll three rows and both diagonals (5 lines)

The pay table

Each line is checked from the left reel to the right. A machine can pay on several lines in one spin, and the coins add up.

A Replay line gives you a free spin with the same bet: press START again without paying. A win of 90 coins or more lights up the machine's bonus sign.

With only one red 7 and one blue 7 on each reel, the big wins are rare. Cherries are the most common win.

Where you can play

Every Game Corner's clerk has SLOTS on the counter menu, so you can play anywhere there's a Game Corner:

  • Kanto: Celadon Game Corner in Celadon City. This is the one place where you can also walk up beside one of the slot machines on the floor and play it right there (you still need a Coin Case).
  • Sevii Islands: the counter in Two Island's Joyful Game Corner.
  • Johto: Goldenrod City's Game Corner.
  • Hoenn: Mauville City's Game Corner, and the hidden Game Corner on the Battle Tower's secret floor in the Battle Frontier.
  • Sinnoh: Veilstone City's Game Corner.

The machines you walk up to in Goldenrod, Mauville and Veilstone play Voltorb Flip instead; there, the slots are on the clerk's menu.
