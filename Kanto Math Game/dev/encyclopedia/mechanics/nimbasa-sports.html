Nimbasa City has two sports arenas: the Big Stadium and the Small Court. Each plays a different sport depending on the day of the week (by the real clock). The athletes who practise there are also Pokémon Trainers, and they're happy to battle you.

The day's sport

In each arena's lobby, a warp panel takes you down onto the field for that day's sport.

Game time

While a game is on, ushers stand in front of the warp panel and you can't go onto the field, but the crowd in the stands cheers. Come back when the game is over.

The athletes

On the field, the athletes of that day's sport are practising. Each one battles you once a day. Their Pokémon get stronger as you earn more Unova badges, and some only show up later:

Visitors after the League

Once you're Unova's Champion, three visiting Trainers come to watch each day. They all stand on the field of one of the two arenas, picked by the date, and each one battles you. There are 52 different visitors in all, so the faces change from day to day. Their Pokémon are level 57 to 63.
