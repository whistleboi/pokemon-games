Blackjack is a card game you play against a Meowth dealer. You both get cards, and the aim is to end up closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. It's one of the games on the counter menu of the Game Corners, played for coins. None of the regions' own games have it. ours

The cards

  • Number cards count their number. The 10, Jack, Queen and King count 10.
  • An Ace counts 11, unless that would take the hand over 21; then it counts 1. When an Ace is counting 11, the table shows "(an Ace as 11)" under your total.
  • The cards come from one shuffled deck of 52. When fewer than 15 cards are left, a fresh deck is shuffled.

How to play

  1. Bet. Tap +1, +5, +10 or +25 to add coins to your bet; tap as often as you like to build it up. CLEAR gives your bet back.
  2. DEAL. You and Meowth get two cards each. One of Meowth's cards stays face down. If you haven't bet, DEAL bets 1 coin for you.
  3. Your turn. HIT for another card, or STAND to keep what you have. Go over 21 and you bust: you lose at once. Reach exactly 21 and you stand automatically.
  4. DOUBLE (only on your first two cards, and only if you have the coins): your bet doubles, you get exactly one more card, and then you stand.
  5. Meowth's turn. Meowth turns over its hidden card and keeps drawing until its total is 17 or more.

Keys: H hit, S stand, D double, Enter or Space deal, Up (or +) adds 1 coin to the bet, Esc leaves. You can only leave between hands; a bet you haven't played yet comes back to you.

If either of you is dealt a Blackjack, the hand ends right after the deal. There's no splitting pairs and no insurance: just HIT, STAND and DOUBLE.

Where you can play

BLACKJACK is on the clerk's menu in every Game Corner: Celadon Game Corner (Kanto), Two Island's Joyful Game Corner (Sevii Islands), Goldenrod City (Johto), Mauville City and the Battle Tower's secret floor (Hoenn), and Veilstone City (Sinnoh).
