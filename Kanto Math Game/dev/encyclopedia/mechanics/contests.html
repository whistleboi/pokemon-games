A Pokémon Contest isn't a battle. Your Pokémon goes on stage against three others and shows off one of five qualities: Cool, Beauty, Cute, Smart or Tough. The judges and the crowd score it with hearts, and the most hearts wins a ribbon. As in every part of this game, math powers each appeal.

How a contest plays

At a Contest Hall, choose ENTER CONTEST, then a category, a rank and one of your Pokémon. A contest has two rounds, as in Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald.

Round 1: condition

The judges look over each Pokémon's condition in the contest's category, and every Pokémon starts with hearts for it: the better the condition, the more hearts. Raise condition beforehand with Pokéblocks or Poffins (below).

Round 2: five appeals

Five turns. Each turn, pick one of four moves, then answer a math question with four choices.

  • A right answer makes the appeal: 2 hearts.
  • A move that suits the contest (by its type) pleases the crowd: 1 more heart, and the crowd meter fills a little.
  • A right answer within 6 seconds is perfect timing: 1 more heart.
  • Using the same move as last turn bores the judges: 1 heart less.
  • The crowd meter is shared by everyone on stage. The appeal that fills it up (the fifth pleasing one) is a Spectacular Talent: 4 more hearts for that Pokémon, and the meter empties.
  • A wrong answer: your Pokémon gets nervous and the appeal falls flat, with no hearts.

Each move's category comes from its type:

After the five turns, the Pokémon with the most hearts wins.

Ranks, ribbons and prizes

Each category has four ranks. To enter a rank you need enough badges from the region you're in, and a win in the rank below in that same category.

  • A win gives the prize money and that rank's ribbon (the Cool Normal Rank Ribbon, and so on) to the Pokémon that won it. The rival Pokémon get better at each rank.
  • The RIBBONS button at the hall shows each Pokémon's ribbon case.
  • Your first Master Rank win gets you 3 Rare Candies from Lisia, once.

Condition: Pokéblocks and Poffins

Each quality runs from 0 to 255. Treats raise it, and every treat also adds Sheen. Once Sheen is full, a Pokémon won't eat any more, so think about which treats you feed.

  • Pokéblocks: each colour raises one quality by 20: red Cool, blue Beauty, pink Cute, green Smart and yellow Tough. Feed them from the Bag. The Contest Hall's POKéBLOCKS stand sells all five.
  • Poffins (Sinnoh): cooked from Berries at the Poffin House. Each flavour raises one quality: Spicy for Cool, Dry for Beauty, Sweet for Cute, Bitter for Smart and Sour for Tough. A smooth Poffin adds less Sheen, so a Pokémon can eat more of them.
  • Contest scarves (Hoenn): a Pokémon holding the scarf of the contest's colour has its condition counted a tenth higher.
  • Feebas evolves into Milotic only when its Beauty is 170 or more.

Region by region

Hoenn

Contest Halls stand in Slateport City, Verdanturf Town, Fallarbor Town and Lilycove City, where the Contest Hall has its own stages. On your first visit Lisia, the Contest Idol, gives you a Contest Pass, a Pokéblock Case with three of each Pokéblock, and a Pokéblock Kit. Use the Kit from the Bag to turn up to four different Berries into Pokéblocks: each Berry makes the Pokéblock of its strongest flavour (two of them for a strongly flavoured Berry). After your first Master Rank win, the Lilycove Museum wants a painting of your Pokémon.

Sinnoh

The Contest Hall in Hearthome City runs the same two-round contest as Hoenn's. Your first visit brings a Contest Pass and a Pokéblock Case with a few Pokéblocks. ours

Next door, the Poffin House gives you a Poffin Case (and a Berry Pouch with a few Berries if you don't have one). To cook, pick up to three different Berries, then stir the batter through three stages by answering math questions before the timer runs out: right answers stir, wrong ones spill and slow ones burn. The better you stir, the stronger and smoother the Poffin. The same Berry twice in one batch makes a Foul Poffin.

Johto: the Bug-Catching Contest

Johto has no Contest Hall, but it has HeartGold and SoulSilver's Bug-Catching Contest in National Park. The officer at the gate signs you up once a day, on any day (the main games hold it only three days a week).

  • You get 20 Sport Balls and 150 turns: each step (or wait) uses one.
  • Bug Pokémon roam the contest's park. There's no battling: you catch with math.
  • You keep only your best catch. Catch a better one, and the other is let go.
  • When time or Sport Balls run out (or you leave by the gate), the judges score your catch against five other contestants'. The score counts how rare the Pokémon is (Scyther and Pinsir score the most), its level, its strength and a little luck.
  • Prizes: 1st place a Sun Stone, 2nd an Everstone, 3rd a Rare Candy, and a Potion for everyone else. Whatever your place, the Bug Pokémon you kept is yours.

Kanto, the Sevii Islands and the Orange Islands

No contests are held here.

Unova

Unova has no contests. Its stage show is the Pokémon Musical in Nimbasa City.
