Trovita Island

Trovita Island is a small, rocky island ringed by steep cliffs. An islander tells you they rose up millions of years ago, along with the island itself. The Trovita River flows across it. Route 65 is back to the east, and Route 66 leads north.

{{journal:orange_m11a}} {{tables:TROVITA_ISLAND}}

The Hyper Potion is hidden: step on its spot. The TM for Protect is tucked away in the trees.

Fisherman's house

A fisherman takes one look at you and knows you're a trainer. He gives you the Super Rod, the best rod there is. The deeper you fish, the stronger the Pokémon you'll hook.

Other buildings

Heal at the Pokémon Center. A Cooltrainer there has heard that Rudy battles on top of a cliff. The Move Tutor lives in a house nearby, but since Pokémon don't forget their moves any more, he has nothing to remind them of.

Trovita Gym

Rudy's little sister, Mahri, greets you at the door and says he's training somewhere inside. Two Cooltrainers watch the hall, and they come over to battle when they spot you.

{{journal:orange_m11b}} {{trainers:TROVITA_ISLAND:Trovita Gym}}

Rudy waits at the far end of the Gym. He's glad you're taking on the Orange League, but he knows a much better place for a battle than in here. He heads outside.

The cliff-top battle

Step out of the Gym and Rudy is waiting by his big red hot-air balloon. He wants to battle on top of a cliff. Misty and Tracey come running too, because they want to watch you earn your third badge. Everyone climbs in, and the balloon drifts up over the cliffs.

At the top, talk to Rudy. His three Pokémon are very different from each other. Ground moves work well against Electabuzz, and Bug and Dark moves hit Exeggutor and Starmie hard.

{{battle:TROVITA_ISLAND:Rudy}}

Win and you get the Spike Shell Badge. Rudy also gives you a TM for Psychic.

{{badges:3}} {{cap}}

Talk to Rudy again when you're ready, and the balloon floats back down to the Gym. Misty and Tracey say goodbye: they're heading north, where the fourth Gym should be.

The Whirlpuller

Before you beat Rudy, an islander says anyone who can do it deserves something handy. Now that you have the Spike Shell Badge, a man in town has something for you: the Whirlpuller. With it you can cross whirlpools, which you'll need to do to reach the north of the Orange Islands.

Route 66 is open sea running north from Trovita Island, then east into Route 56.

Route 56 (again)

Back on Route 56, the whirlpools around Fairchild Island's south beach are no problem now: the Whirlpuller pulls them apart so you can cross.

Fairchild Island

Fairchild Island has no town, just grass, trees and cliffs. A notice warns that the Pokémon here grow very big and strong, so take care. The wild Pokémon are only Pidgeot and Rhydon, and they're tough. A path winds through the island from the south beach to the north beach.

{{journal:orange_m12b}} {{tables:FAIRCHILD_ISLAND}}

The Ether, the X Sp. Atk and the Elixir are hidden: step on their spots.

Team Rocket on the run

Partway along the path, Jessie, James and Meowth race straight past you, scared stiff. Whatever they're running from, it's somewhere on this island.

The giant nest

Deeper in, you come to a huge pile of branches. It looks like the nest of some gigantic bird. A shadow sweeps over you, and a Pidgeot lands right beside the nest, glaring. It means to defend its home.

The north beach

The path drops down to Fairchild's north beach. Route 67 lies across the sea to the north.

Route 67

Route 67 is a wide stretch of sea with sandbars, north of Route 56 and Fairchild Island. Shamouti Island is to the west, and Route 68 leads east towards Cleopatra Island.

{{journal:orange_m12c}} {{tables:ORANGE_ROUTE_67}}

The Star Piece and the Rare Candy are hidden: step on their spots.

{{trainers:ORANGE_ROUTE_67}}

Team Rocket

Out at sea, the Meowth balloon drops out of the sky onto a sandbar ahead of you. James explains what scared them on Fairchild: a giant bird grabbed Meowth, and it took both of them to get him free. Now they're back to business, and they want your Pokémon. Psychic and Ground moves work well against Arbok, Weezing and Victreebel, and Fighting moves against Lickitung.
