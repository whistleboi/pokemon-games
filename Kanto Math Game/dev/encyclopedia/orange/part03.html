Mikan Island

Mikan Island is a quiet little island with a Pokémon Center, a Poké Mart and the first Gym of the Orange Crew. Routes 50 and 51 come in from the south, and Route 53 leads on across the sea towards Mandarin Island North. A man admits there's not much to do here: trainers only come for the Gym.

{{journal:orange_m2a}} {{tables:MIKAN_ISLAND}}

A fighter on Mikan

{{journal:orange_s_tyrogue}}

An old man in one of the houses worries that his Ground and Rock Pokémon don't stand a chance against Cissy's Water types. He has one that fights with its fists, though, and gives it to you: Tyrogue at Lv 8, holding a Black Belt, which powers up its Fighting moves.

Around the island

Cissy's little brother hangs about outside, sure his big sister never loses. A woman warns you he's a real pest. In the Pokémon Center, a boy who lost to Cissy is looking for Pokémon that beat Water types, and a trainer has heard the Orange League is tougher than the Indigo League. Upstairs, the Union Room, the Trade Center and the Colosseum are closed.

Poké Mart

Mikan's Mart adds Super Potions to the shelf: Poké Balls, Super Potions, Potions, Antidotes, Paralyze Heals, Awakenings, Burn Heals, Escape Ropes and Repels.

Mikan Gym

Cissy trains Water Pokémon. Four trainers wait in her Gym. You don't have to beat them to reach Cissy, but they're good practice.

{{trainers:MIKAN_ISLAND:Mikan Gym}}

Cissy's tests

Before she'll battle you, Cissy sets you two tests, played as one game:

  • Round 1, the Water Gun test. Targets pop up from the pool and sink again. Tap one while it's up (or move the sight with the arrow keys and press Space) to hit it with Water Gun. Hit eight before six of them get away.
  • Round 2, the Lapras race. Your Lapras races Cissy's Seadra across the bay. Tap (or press Space) to swim on, and switch lanes (tap above or below Lapras, or use the arrow keys) to dodge the rocks, which slow you down. Reach the buoy first.

Win both rounds and the battle is next. Lose, and you can try the tests again.

{{battle:MIKAN_ISLAND:Cissy|Seadra knows Water Gun, Smokescreen, Twister and Surf; Blastoise knows Water Gun, Withdraw, Skull Bash and Hydro Pump. Each holds a Cheri Berry.}}

Beat her for the Coral-Eye Badge, your first Orange Crew badge. She also gives you TM011 Water Pulse. Outside, her brother can hardly believe it, but admits you earned it.

Later visit. Once you're the Orange League Champion, Cissy's Gym can be battled again from its first trainer, as often as you like, with stronger levels and no badge.

Route 53

Route 53 is open sea between Mikan Island and Mandarin Island North, to the east. It's a busy stretch, with seven trainers out on the water.

Mandarin Island North

Mandarin Island North is the biggest city in the Orange Islands: a place for business, with a Pokémon Center, a Poké Mart and a police station, but not much for trainers. Route 53 comes in from the west and Route 54 leaves to the south. A big old power plant with a giant antenna stands in the north of the city. People say there have been a lot of shady types around these past weeks.

{{tables:MANDARIN_NORTH}}

The Full Heal is hidden in the city.

Officer Jenny

{{journal:orange_m3a}}

In the south-west of the city, Officer Jenny is looking into some strange incidents. Without a Gym badge, she won't let you in: rookie trainers have to stay out for their own safety. With the Coral-Eye Badge, you arrive just as some Pokémon go wild, and her Gastly calms them with Night Shade.

She explains that Pokémon all over Mandarin have been disobeying their trainers, and she thinks a Psychic Pokémon is behind it. Since you have a badge, she asks you to help find out what's going on. From now on, you won't leave the city until you've helped her.

The old power plant

{{journal:orange_m3b}}

A Team Rocket Grunt guards the door of the old power plant. He claims there's nothing to see and that he's never heard of disobedient Pokémon, then battles you when you want to look for yourself.

{{battle:MANDARIN_NORTH:Grunt}}

Beat him and the door, locked from the inside until now, opens into the Rocket Hideout.

Pokémon Center and Poké Mart

An old man in the Center tells you the big building in the north used to be a power plant. Mandarin's Mart sells the same as Mikan's. A trainer there is after an X Attack, which this Mart doesn't sell, and a shopper has heard there's a bigger store somewhere in the islands.

Police Station

The officers think the strange goings-on might be down to people in black. A burglar in the station insists they've got no proof.

Rocket Hideout

Team Rocket has taken over the old power plant. Its four floors are full of Grunts and scientists, with useful items lying in the corners. The lift on 1F and 3F needs the Lift Key, which is on 3F.

{{tables:MANDARIN_UNDERGROUND}}

1F

Take the stairs up to 2F. The lift here won't move yet. An HP Up is in the storage room.

{{trainers:MANDARIN_UNDERGROUND:1F}}

2F

Jessie and James are here, and they don't want you in their hideout.

After the battle, Meowth lets slip that Butch and Cassidy are down on B1F. Iron and a Protein are on this floor too. Take the stairs up to 3F.

{{trainers:MANDARIN_UNDERGROUND:2F}}

3F

Arrow tiles on this floor slide you along until something stops you, so pick your way across with care. The Lift Key is here, along with Carbos, Calcium, Zinc and a Rare Candy. With the key, the lift takes you down to B1F.

{{trainers:MANDARIN_UNDERGROUND:3F}}

B1F

In the basement, a Drowzee is wired to a strange machine. Butch and Cassidy are in charge: the Drowzee's waves go out through the antenna on the roof, and in a few more weeks every Pokémon on the island would obey them. First Butch battles you, then Cassidy takes her turn.

With both beaten, the antenna stops, and Mandarin's Pokémon are back to normal.

Mandarin Island North (again)

Officer Jenny thanks you for stopping Team Rocket's antenna. The city is open again, and a climber in the house just south of the Rocket Hideout wants to thank you too, before you Surf south along Route 54.
