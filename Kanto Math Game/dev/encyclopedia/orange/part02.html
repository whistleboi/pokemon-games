Route 49

Route 49 is the stretch of sea between Valencia Island and Tangelo Island. The blimp carries you right over it, so you won't swim it on the way. Once Lapras lets you Surf, you can come back this way from Tangelo Island's south shore, or head out from Valencia's west side.

Pokémon Park

Pokémon Park is the big green park on Tangelo Island, full of tall grass and Bug Pokémon. Your parachute sets you down here. The town is a short walk west through the park.

{{tables:TANGELO_JUNGLE}}

The Cheri Berry and the Pecha Berry are hidden. Bulbasaur is very rare here.

{{trainers:TANGELO_JUNGLE}}

A soft landing

You're not the only one who jumped. Misty runs over: the airport clerk offered her the same parachute. She has no idea where you've both landed, and starts to worry it's a desert island where you'll have to eat bugs forever.

{{journal:orange_m0f}}

Further along, a group of tourists laughs at the idea. They tell you this is Pokémon Park on Tangelo Island, and that the town is just west of here.

A collector in the park has found your parachute and asks if he can keep it for his collection. In return he gives you a Miracle Seed, which powers up Grass moves for the Pokémon holding it.

Tangelo Island

Tangelo Island is a lively holiday town with a Pokémon Center and a Poké Mart. Pokémon Park is on the island, Routes 50 and 51 lead north towards Mikan Island, and Route 49 lies to the south. Lots of older visitors come here on holiday, and showing them round the park is big business.

{{tables:TANGELO_ISLAND}}

The Revival Herb grows by the path. Lapras gives you HM Surf when it joins you.

Trouble by the water

{{journal:orange_m1a}}

At the north end of the island, by the water, a young Lapras is crying in the shallows. Misty is shouting at three punks who have been hurting it. They say they're training for the Orange League and tell you to mind your own business. If you want to argue, you'll have to battle them, one after another.

{{battle:TANGELO_ISLAND:Byron}} {{battle:TANGELO_ISLAND:Cameron}} {{battle:TANGELO_ISLAND:Mort}}

When all three are beaten, they run off. Tracey, a Pokémon Watcher, has been watching: your Pokémon are much healthier than theirs. The Lapras is badly hurt, though, so Misty and Tracey take it to the Pokémon Center, just east of here.

Pokémon Center

{{journal:orange_m1b}}

Nurse Joy thanks you for saving Lapras from the bullies. It's already feeling better and is resting in the pool behind the Center, but it's still wary of people. Misty and Tracey are here too. A reader is studying up on the Orange Crew, the four Gym Leaders across the islands. Upstairs, the Union Room, the Trade Center and the Colosseum are closed.

Lapras

{{journal:orange_m1c}}

Walk round to the pool behind the Pokémon Center. Before you can reach Lapras, Jessie, James and Meowth jump out. They followed you all the way to the Orange Islands, and they want the Lapras for their Boss.

{{battle:TANGELO_ISLAND:Jessie & James|Arbok knows Poison Sting, Glare, Wrap and Bite; Weezing knows Tackle, Smokescreen, Smog and Sludge; Lickitung knows Lick, Supersonic, Wrap and Stomp; Victreebel knows Vine Whip, Sleep Powder, Wrap and Razor Leaf. Each holds a Cheri Berry.}}

Once they're gone, talk to Lapras. It trusts you now and wants to come along: you get Lapras at Lv 10, and with it HM Surf. Lapras can carry you across the sea, so you can Surf from now on.

Telling your friends

{{journal:orange_m1d}}

Before you leave the island, go back into the Pokémon Center and tell Misty and Tracey that Lapras is travelling with you. Until you do, you won't leave town. Tracey is delighted that Lapras trusts you, and Misty asks you to take good care of it. They'll be travelling the islands too.

Poké Mart

Tangelo's Mart sells Poké Balls, Potions, Antidotes, Paralyze Heals, Awakenings, Burn Heals, Escape Ropes and Repels. A boy points out that no shop sells Rare Candy: you have to find it.

Houses

A kid's dad guides older visitors round Pokémon Park. An old man tells you that a herd of Lapras swims past Tangelo Island every year. In another house, a cook just wants to be left in peace.

Routes 50 and 51

Routes 50 and 51 are open sea running north from Tangelo Island to Mikan Island. This is your first swim with Lapras. Remoraid sometimes surface among the Tentacool.
