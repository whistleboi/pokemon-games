Route 58

Route 58 starts at Kinnow Island's north shore and runs east to Navel Island. Along the way there are islands with grass, a Pokémon Clinic, and deep water you can dive into once you have the right gear.

{{tables:ORANGE_ROUTE_58}}

The X Sp. Atk and a Big Pearl are hidden on the surface, and the Big Pearls, Pearl and Stardust on the seabed are hidden too: step on their spots to find them. The seabed needs the Scuba Gear.

{{trainers:ORANGE_ROUTE_58}}

Nurse Joy and the big Magikarp

Nurse Joy is out visiting the Pokémon on the islands nearby. A man is glad she drops in to check on his Mankey, and a hiker's Onix is suffering from "erocktion" from the wind. Near them floats a huge old Magikarp, an old friend of Nurse Joy's. It's low on Calcium, so she gives it some, and all at once Magikarp evolves into a strong Gyarados and swims away.

Nurse Joy can tell you care about Pokémon. She's off to help a Shellder under the sea, and gives you Scuba Gear so you can come too.

Pokémon Clinic

This is Nurse Joy's base, but she's hardly ever in. A trainer and an old man say she's usually out on the islands, caring for Pokémon, and only her Chansey is in.

Route 58 Seabed

With the Scuba Gear, dive down into the deep water. On the seabed, Nurse Joy is helping a Shellder in her own diving gear. Once it swims off, she points up to the surface to show she's going back, then waves at the reef around you, as if to say you should take a look. A Mystic Water lies among the seaweed, and several other treasures are hidden down here.

Navel Island

Navel Island is the steep, snowy island at the end of Route 58. The village by the sea has a lovely climate, but the mountain to the north is the coldest place in all the Orange Islands. Danny, the island's Gym Leader, is one of the Orange Crew. Route 59 leaves the island to the south.

{{tables:NAVEL_ISLAND}}

The Dire Hit and the Chesto Berry are hidden: step on their spots to find them.

An old man tells you there's no Gym building on Navel: Danny's challenge is up on the mountain, and you have to climb to the top first. A boy tries to leave footprints all over the beach, but the waves keep washing them away, and a swimmer admits to snacking on a few grapefruits while swimming over from Moro Island.

Pokémon Center

Heal up before the climb. A hiker says he was worn out by the time he reached the top, and Danny still beat him. Upstairs, the Union Room, the Trade Center and the Colosseum are all closed. There's no Poké Mart on Navel, so stock up on Kinnow Island.

Houses

In one house, a young man says trading can fill the gaps in your Pokédex, and a girl reminds you that some moves have to be taught. In another, a scientist warns that the Orange Crew mix their teams instead of sticking to one type, so they're harder to beat than Kanto's Leaders.

In a third house, a trader would love a Jynx. Bring one and he'll trade you his Magmar, which comes holding Charcoal. Wild Jynx live up on Navel Mountain.

Navel Mountain

Navel Mountain rises over the north of the island. It's snowing up here, and hot geysers shoot out of the rock: a sign warns you to keep your distance. Geodude and Machop live on the slopes, with Onix and Jynx too.

Meeting Danny

Soon after you start up the mountain, someone calls out to you. It's Danny himself, Navel Island's Gym Leader. If you want a Gym battle, he says, climb the mountain, and he'll meet you at the very top.

{{journal:orange_m7a}}

Tracey and Misty have made it to Navel too, but they're staying at the bottom: it's freezing up there! Misty thinks Danny won't be easy to beat.

{{tables:MT_NAVEL}}

The Guard Spec. and the Rare Candy are hidden: step on their spots to find them.

{{trainers:MT_NAVEL}}

The climb

Danny's test is the mountain itself. The path winds up past hikers and Pokémon Rangers, and in places it climbs straight up rock faces. Those walls need the Climb Kit from the climber on Mandarin Island North: with it in your Bag, you scale them. There's a cable car station at the foot and another at the top, but challengers must reach the top on foot. The attendants only let you ride once you've beaten Danny.

Danny

At the summit, Danny is waiting with a great view and clean mountain air. You climbed his mountain on your own, so you're a worthy challenger. He used to race challengers down the mountain on ice sleds, but the snow line moves too much with the seasons, so now it's a plain battle.

Like all the Orange Crew, Danny mixes his types. Geodude and Electrode both know Self-Destruct or Explosion, so be ready for a sudden blast, and his Nidoqueen knows Earthquake and Ice Beam.

{{battle:MT_NAVEL:Danny}}

Win and Danny gives you the Sea Ruby Badge, which makes your Pokémon a little faster. He also gives you TM135 Ice Beam. Now you can ride the cable car down the mountain whenever you like.

Route 59

With two badges, the Journal points you south. Route 59 runs from Navel Island down to the Seven Grapefruit Islands, and on to Moro Island and its famous Museum. Only one swimmer is waiting on this first stretch.

{{tables:ORANGE_ROUTE_59}} {{trainers:ORANGE_ROUTE_59}}

Seven Grapefruit Islands

The Seven Grapefruit Islands are a chain of small islands, numbered One to Seven, and every grapefruit tree on them belongs to Ruby. Grapefruit Island Three is the Grapefruit Headquarters. Mareep and Flaaffy live in the grass here, and Corsola in the sea.

{{tables:SEVEN_GRAPEFRUITS}}

The Nugget, Soda Pop and both Heart Scales are hidden: step on their spots to find them.

{{trainers:SEVEN_GRAPEFRUITS}}

The Grapefruit Thief

On Grapefruit Island One, you hear someone shouting at a Snorlax. It's Ruby, the islands' owner. Someone has been stealing the grapefruits, and it turns out the thief is a very hungry Snorlax. She asks you to stop it before it eats the lot.

{{journal:orange_s8a}}

Talk to the Snorlax. It doesn't even look up: it finishes off the tree, then lumbers away toward Grapefruit Island Three. Follow it there and battle it. Snorlax is Lv 30, and you can catch it if you like.

Go back to Ruby once it's dealt with. She's delighted, and gives you a Silk Scarf, which powers up Normal moves for the Pokémon holding it.

Grapefruit Headquarters

From the headquarters on Grapefruit Island Three, the supervisors keep an eye on the islands and on how the trees are doing. A worker says Ruby works harder than anyone.

Route 59 (south)

The last stretch of Route 59 runs south from the Grapefruit Islands to Moro Island. One more swimmer waits on the way.
