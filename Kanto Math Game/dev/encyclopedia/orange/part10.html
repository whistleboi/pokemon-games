Route 68

Route 68 runs east from Route 67 across open sea, with a strip of beach and grass along the way. North of it lies Ascorbia Island, and Cleopatra Island sits just off the route. Swimmers, tubers and fighters wait along the water.

{{tables:ORANGE_ROUTE_68}} {{trainers:ORANGE_ROUTE_68}}

Tad and his Poliwrath

On the beach by Cleopatra Island, a trainer called Tad is training his Poliwrath. He heard about your Spike Shell Badge from Rudy, and he's getting ready for Luana on Kumquat Island. He'd love a match.

{{journal:orange_s12b}} {{battle:ORANGE_ROUTE_68:Tad|His Poliwrath holds a Mystic Water. Electric and Grass moves are your best bet, and watch out for Hypnosis.}}

When you win, Tad thinks you have a real chance against Luana, and he gives you a Macho Brace. A Pokémon holding it grows faster, but it's a bit slower in battle. Afterwards he stays on the beach to keep training.

Politoed, Lv 50. A Politoed sits on the shore, croaking a happy tune, and hops over to you. Beat it and you can try to catch it. It only shows up once.

Cleopatra Island

Cleopatra Island is a quiet little island off Route 68. A sign says Psychic Pokémon love how calm it is, and the grass is full of them: look out for Abra, Drowzee, Kadabra and the rare Mr. Mime. Two Psychics train here, and there are two TMs to pick up, one at the top of the island and one by the pond.

Ascorbia Island

Ascorbia Island is one of the bigger islands, and most of it is forest. It's home to the fire station and Team Wartortle, the best firefighting squad in the islands. The town has a Pokémon Center and a Poké Mart, but no Gym: the nearest one is on Kumquat Island, to the west.

{{journal:orange_m12d}} {{tables:ASCORBIA_ISLAND}}

Team Wartortle taken

As you walk into town, a balloon shaped like a Meowth floats up from the fire station with something hanging under it, and drifts off towards the forest. A man runs up: he's Captain Aidan, and some people in black have just carried off Team Wartortle! He asks you to help him get them back, and heads for Ascorbia Forest.

Until Team Wartortle are safe, you won't go down to the north-west beach: you feel you should help Captain Aidan first. Follow him north into the forest.

Around town

In one house, a kid having a Body Slam contest teaches one of your Pokémon Body Slam, and you can come back for more lessons ours. A woman in another house is scared stiff about what happens if a fire breaks out with Team Wartortle gone. The Poké Mart sells Great Balls, Ultra Balls, Hyper Potions, Revives, Full Heals, Escape Ropes and Max Repels.

Ascorbia Forest

Ascorbia Forest fills the north of the island. A sign warns you to watch out for things people have thrown away. A walker in town says there are more shady types about than usual, and he's right: Team Rocket have set up here.

{{journal:orange_m13a}} {{tables:ASCORBIA_FOREST}} {{trainers:ASCORBIA_FOREST:Ascorbia Forest}}

Captain Aidan is waiting by the balloon. He's sure the crooks are hiding in the warehouse next to it, and he goes in with you.

The Rocket Warehouse

The warehouse is crawling with Team Rocket. Aidan doesn't have a Pokémon with him, so he waits by the door while you go on.

{{trainers:ASCORBIA_FOREST:Rocket Warehouse}}

Soon you get the feeling you're being watched. It's Jessie, James and Meowth! They took Team Wartortle all right, but they've handed them on to some colleagues.

{{battle:ASCORBIA_FOREST:Jessie & James}}

Further in, Butch and Cassidy are admiring the caged Wartortle and looking forward to pleasing their Boss after their mess on Mandarin North. Then they see you. They battle you one after the other.

{{battle:ASCORBIA_FOREST:Cassidy}} {{battle:ASCORBIA_FOREST:Butch}}

Beaten, the pair run off, promising they'll be back. Captain Aidan rushes in with a shiny key he found on the floor, and it fits the cage. Team Wartortle are free, and nobody's hurt! Aidan takes them straight back to town, because a firefighter never leaves their post for long.

Ascorbia Island (again)

Back in town, the people feel safe again. Visit Captain Aidan at the fire station: he never got to thank you properly, so he gives you TM Flamethrower, which he uses to train Team Wartortle.

Route 69

Route 69 runs west from Ascorbia Island to Kumquat Island. Butwal Island rises from the sea along the way, its high cliffs reached through a door in the rock. An old man sits here for the view, and a fisherman says Kumquat's east shore is the best place to fish.

{{tables:ORANGE_ROUTE_69}} {{trainers:ORANGE_ROUTE_69}}

Butwal Island

Butwal Island is a lush, green island on top of high cliffs, so high that supplies come in by plane on parachutes. A man here calls himself the best Grass-type trainer in the Orange Islands. A woman isn't so sure.

{{journal:orange_s14a}} {{tables:BUTWAL_ISLAND}}

Gulzar

As you walk through, someone shouts at you. It's Gulzar, and he challenges you to prove he's the best with Grass-types.

{{battle:BUTWAL_ISLAND:Gulzar|His Gloom holds a Miracle Seed and knows Solar Beam and Sunny Day. Fire and Flying moves work well.}}

When he loses, Gulzar heads off to the Pokémon Center to heal his team, and says he doubts you've got the guts to follow him there. Find him again and he admits you've got more guts than he thought. He gives you HM Cut, which lets a Pokémon chop down small trees and open narrow paths, and says you'll need it soon.

A hiker in one of the houses teaches Rock Slide, and you can come back for more lessons ours.

Kumquat Island

Kumquat Island is everyone's favourite holiday spot, with a long beach and a big hotel. The Kumquat Gym is inside the hotel, and its Leader is Luana, the strongest of the Orange Crew. Tourists keep the island going.

{{journal:orange_m14a}} {{tables:KUMQUAT_ISLAND}} {{trainers:KUMQUAT_ISLAND}}

The beach trainers battle anyone who comes by.

On the beach

Misty and Tracey are relaxing on the beach. Say hello to Misty, and a woman comes running across the sand, calling for her son. She thought you were Travis, her boy who went off on a journey years ago. When she hears you're taking on the Orange Crew, she tells you she's Luana, the Kumquat Gym Leader, and invites you to the hotel. Tracey suggests warming up against the trainers on the beach first.

Around the island

  • In one house, a boy gives you TM Return and a girl gives you TM Frustration.
  • Another house has a man who teaches Swords Dance, and you can come back for more lessons ours.
  • In the hotel lobby, a man whose Electric moves don't touch Ground-types gives you TM Iron Tail.
  • The Poké Mart sells Great Balls, Ultra Balls, Hyper Potions, Revives, Full Heals, Escape Ropes and Max Repels.
  • In the Pokémon Center, trainers warn you that the Gym battle is different here: both trainers send out two Pokémon at once.

The Kumquat Hotel

Before you meet Luana on the beach, the receptionist says the Gym Leader is out. Once you've met her, Luana is waiting for you in the lobby. She tells you to take the lift up to the battle arena when you're ready, and the lift attendant lets you on. Ride up to the top floor and go through to the Battle Arena.

Battle Arena

Luana holds battles here for the hotel's guests. To win the Jade Star Badge, you'll have to beat her two Pokémon working as one. This is a Double Battle, so bring two strong Pokémon that work well together. Water and Grass moves hit Marowak hard, and Dark, Bug and Ghost moves are good against Alakazam.

{{battle:KUMQUAT_ISLAND:Luana|A Double Battle: you each send out two Pokémon at once. Marowak holds a Thick Club and Alakazam a Twisted Spoon.}} {{badges:4}} {{cap}}

When you win, Luana gives you the Jade Star Badge, which makes your Pokémon a little stronger in Sp. Atk and Sp. Def, and TM Earthquake. She warns you to be careful with it in a Double Battle, because it hits your partner too. That's all four Orange Crew badges!
