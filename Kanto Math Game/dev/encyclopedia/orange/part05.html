Pinkan Island

Pinkan Island is a wildlife preserve east of Route 55. Many of its Pokémon are pink, because they eat the island's Pinkan Berries. Catching used to be banned here, but since last year trainers may catch Pokémon on Pinkan. Poaching is still against the law. Route 55 is to the west, and Route 56 leaves from the east side of the island.

{{tables:PINKAN_ISLAND}}

The two Moon Stones are hidden: step on their spots to find them. The Rare Candy is under a Pinkan Berry bush. The Pokémon listed by number (#9101 and up) are Pinkan's pink Pokémon.

{{trainers:PINKAN_ISLAND}}

The pink Rhyhorn

West of the Preserve's hut, Tracey is sketching an odd pink Rhyhorn. He tries to make friends with it, but the Rhyhorn notices you both and charges. There's nowhere to run, so you'll have to battle it: the Pink Rhyhorn is Lv 20.

{{journal:orange_m5a}}

As soon as it's beaten, Officer Jenny comes running. She took you for poachers! She explains the island's rules, and tells you her hut is just north-west of there if your Pokémon need a rest. Tracey goes off to explore the island some more.

Preserve Hut

Officer Jenny's hut is the place to heal your team on Pinkan: talk to her and she'll look after your Pokémon. The other officers explain why the Pokémon here are pink, and why poachers want them so badly.

The poachers

Head east across the island. A pair of poachers comes racing past you, running for their lives, and you soon see why: a furious pink Nidoking has cornered you, and you'll have to fight it. The Pink Nidoking is Lv 25.

{{journal:orange_m5b}}

Rival battle

Further east, your rival is waiting. After the Indigo League he came to the Orange Islands on holiday, and then found a Gym on Mikan Island. Now he wants to see how you've been doing.

{{battle:PINKAN_ISLAND:rival}}

Afterwards he tells you the next Gym is on Navel Island, the island with the big snowy mountain, east of here. He gives you an Itemfinder to help you on your way. It beeps near items hidden where you can't see them.

Route 56

Route 56 is a wide stretch of sea east of Pinkan Island. Fukuhara Island lies to the south, and a sign points the way to Fairchild Island, warning that very large Pokémon live there. Whirlpools swirl in the water around Fairchild's south beach, and you can't get through them yet. The routes leading north and east from here are for much later in your journey.

{{journal:orange_m5c}} {{tables:ORANGE_ROUTE_56}}

The Stardust and both PP Ups are hidden: step on their spots to find them.

{{trainers:ORANGE_ROUTE_56}}

Watch out for Kathy and Isaiah: their Pokémon are Lv 30, much stronger than the rest of the route's trainers.

Fukuhara Island

Fukuhara Island is a quiet, grassy island where diggers from the Moro Museum are looking for Pokémon fossils, helped by a Rhydon and a Machoke. Route 56 is to the west, Route 57 is to the east, and the Bay Cave is on the island itself.

{{journal:orange_m6a}} {{tables:FUKUHARA_ISLAND}}

The Great Ball and the PP Up are hidden: step on their spots to find them.

{{trainers:FUKUHARA_ISLAND}}

The Fukuhara Dig

Nurse Joy is helping with the dig. A fisherman hooked a scrap of what looks like a Dome Fossil, so everyone is searching. She asks you to help, and gives you a Pickaxe to break the small rocks in the cave behind her.

{{journal:orange_s7a}}

Find a fossil in the Bay Cave and bring it back to her. Nurse Joy says the Moro Museum can study it far better than they can, and asks you to take something else there too: a lump of Old Amber they dug up while you were away, which might have Pokémon DNA inside. The Moro Museum is on Moro Island, further along your journey.

Bay Cave

The Bay Cave is being studied by the Moro Museum's diggers. Zubat and Geodude live here, and now and then a Clefairy. Small rocks block parts of the cave, and Nurse Joy's Pickaxe breaks them.

{{tables:BAY_CAVE}}

The X Attack, Rare Candy and X Defend are hidden: step on their spots to find them.

{{trainers:BAY_CAVE}}

The fossil

Something hard is wedged in one of the broken rocks: a fossil! You choose which kind it looks like: the spiral Helix Fossil or the domed Dome Fossil. Take it back to Nurse Joy outside.

Route 57

Route 57 runs from Fukuhara Island north to Kinnow Island. It's dotted with small islands, and a few of them have grass where wild Pokémon live. Psyduck swim in the water here as well as Tentacool.

{{tables:ORANGE_ROUTE_57}}

The X Speed is hidden: step on its spot to find it. The Iron is on one of the little islands.

Misty

On a little island, Misty has just finished a battle with a swimmer called Marina. She thought her Psyduck had evolved, but the Golduck was a wild one all along! She wants a battle to take her mind off it.

{{battle:ORANGE_ROUTE_57:Misty}}

Afterwards Misty says she's off to rest on Navel Island, where one of the Orange Crew is said to be. Once you've battled Misty, Marina wants a battle too.

{{trainers:ORANGE_ROUTE_57}}

Kinnow Island

Kinnow Island is a busy port and a cultural centre, with a small town and plenty of nature too. Route 57 comes in from the south, and Route 58 leaves from the north shore toward Navel Island. The big ship docked here is the Pokémon Showboat.

{{journal:orange_m6b}} {{tables:KINNOW_ISLAND}}

The X Accuracy is hidden: step on its spot to find it.

Around town, people are talking about the Showboat's voice-acting show. A girl says a Clefable performs on board, and a man reminds you that you need a Showboat Pass to go aboard. A sailor explains that the crew mostly rest in their cabins while the ship is docked.

Pokémon Center and Poké Mart

Heal your team at the Center. A diver says Route 58 has good diving spots, if you've got the gear. Upstairs, the Union Room, the Trade Center and the Colosseum are all closed.

The Poké Mart sells Poké Balls, Great Balls, Super Potions, Revives, Full Heals, Escape Ropes and Super Repels. A shopper had hoped to buy a Showboat Pass here, but no luck.

Kinnow Hotel

The hotel is busy every day, and full when the Showboat docks. A man says people and Pokémon run away from his Growlithe because it knows Roar, and gives you TM172 Roar.

Houses

In the Fisherman's House, a fisherman sees a spark in your eyes and gives you a Good Rod. It hooks Pokémon wherever there's water.

In another house, a guest from the Showboat is staying with an old friend instead of in his cabin. He doesn't need his pass while he's here, so he gives you his Showboat Pass.

Pokémon Showboat

With the Showboat Pass, the deckhand at the gangway lets you on board as often as you like. The ship has a deck, a theatre downstairs, a backstage area, and a lower corridor of nine cabins where the sailors and old gentlemen aboard are glad of a battle.

{{journal:orange_s24a}} {{tables:SHOWBOAT}} {{trainers:SHOWBOAT}}

The show

In the theatre, the usher shows you to a seat in the front row for the new show, "The Heroic Hitmonchan". Hitmonchan and Machoke battle on stage, Abra counts Machoke out, and Hitmonchan celebrates with Clefable. But how can Pokémon talk? You notice the stage door beside the curtain is open a crack, and slip through it.

Backstage: Kay

Backstage, voice actors are busy speaking the Pokémon's lines. One of them, Kay, tells you her Raichu can't perform because it doesn't trust her: it shocked her when they first met, and she's been scared of it ever since. She decides a battle will show Raichu she isn't afraid.

{{battle:SHOWBOAT:Kay}}

It works! Raichu and Kay trust each other now, and Kay gives you TM126 Thunderbolt.
