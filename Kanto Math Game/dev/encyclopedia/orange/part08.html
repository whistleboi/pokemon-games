Route 63

Route 63 is a road across Mandarin Island South, running north from the town to the village of Yambera. It's a land road, so no Surf is needed. Look out for Hitmonlee and Hitmonchan: they're rare, but they live here.

{{journal:orange_m10a}} {{tables:ORANGE_ROUTE_63}}

The Rare Candy is hidden: step on its spot.

{{trainers:ORANGE_ROUTE_63}}

Team Rocket

Partway up the road, a balloon shaped like Meowth floats down and Jessie, James and Meowth jump out. They're still after Pikachu, and anything else worth taking. Psychic and Ground moves work well against Arbok, Weezing and Victreebel, and Fighting moves against Lickitung.

{{battle:ORANGE_ROUTE_63:Jessie}}

Beaten, Team Rocket blast off again. Carry on north to Yambera.

Yambera

Yambera is a pretty little village where people come to rest and unwind. It has a Pokémon Center, a small park, a church that's mostly used for weddings, and a few houses. Route 63 is back to the south, and Route 64 heads west into the Mandarin Desert.

{{journal:orange_m10b}} {{tables:YAMBERA}}

The Nugget is hidden: step on its spot. The water can be fished and surfed, and Slowpoke and Psyduck live in it.

Ralph and Emily

Ralph and Emily have competed with each other all their lives, and they even caught the same kind of Pokémon. Talk to Ralph: his Nidoran, Tony, keeps running off, and so does Emily's Nidoran, Maria. They ask you to look out for them.

{{journal:orange_s10a}}

Ralph thinks Tony might be near the park. Look around and you find the two Nidoran snuggled up together. Ralph and Emily come running and realise their Pokémon are in love, and the pair glow and evolve into Nidorino and Nidorina! Talk to Ralph again and he thanks you with a Poison Barb, which powers up Poison moves.

Other buildings

A man back from the Mandarin Desert says storms sweep across it, and lightning struck right next to him. He gives you a TM for Shock Wave, an Electric move that never misses. In another house, a trader will swap their Voltorb, holding a Bright Powder, for a Magnemite. You can catch Magnemite on Route 64.

Route 64

Route 64 crosses the Mandarin Desert, west of Yambera and then south to Trovitopolis. Along the way is Desert Town, a small oasis town with a Pokémon Center and a few houses. A Hiker there warns you that Pokémon you won't see anywhere else live out in the sand.

{{journal:orange_m10c}} {{tables:ORANGE_ROUTE_64}}

The Thick Club is hidden: step on its spot. Dratini can turn up in the oasis water, by Surf or with the Super Rod.

{{trainers:ORANGE_ROUTE_64}}

Ethan's Magnemite

Ethan is a Magnemite rancher. When storms hit the desert, his Magnemite soak up the lightning, and he carries the power to Desert Town. But one of them, Number 6, has gone missing.

Further on, you spot Team Rocket hauling a strange machine down the dunes, with a Magnemite following them. You can't go on past them until you've checked on them. Their new machine makes static to lure Magnemite in, and they mean to keep this one. Talk to them to battle.

{{battle:ORANGE_ROUTE_64:Jessie}}

Free of Team Rocket, Number 6 is so happy it evolves into Magneton, and Ethan arrives to collect it. Find him again in Desert Town and he gives you a Magnet, which powers up Electric moves. Desert Town's lights are back on.

Desert Town

Heal at the Pokémon Center here before the long walk. In one house, a trader will swap their Chansey, holding a Lucky Punch, for a Kangaskhan. Kangaskhan are rare, but you can catch one on this route.

Trovitopolis

Trovitopolis is one of the biggest cities in the islands, and the total opposite of quiet Desert Town. Its main sight is the Trovito Dept. Store. An election is coming, and the Mayor's campaign symbol is all over town. Route 64 is back to the north, and Route 65 leads west towards Trovita Island.

{{journal:orange_m10d}} {{tables:TROVITOPOLIS}}

The Elixir, the Revive and the Great Ball are hidden: step on their spots.

Pokémon Center

A Super Nerd here once won a Pokémon made of computer code, back when he gambled in Celadon City. He'd like it to have a new home, and gives you a Porygon (Lv 15).

{{journal:orange_s_porygon}}
Later, as Champion. Come back once you've become Orange League Champion and he gives you an Up-Grade. When Porygon holds it, it becomes Porygon2.

Trovito Dept. Store

The Dept. Store has five floors and a roof, with a lift between them. The board on 1F shows what's on each floor.

2F: Trainer's Market

Two counters. One sells everyday goods like Poké Balls, Super Potions and status healers. The other sells TMs: Hyper Beam, Light Screen, Safeguard, Dig, Brick Break, Reflect, Secret Power and Attract.

3F: TV Game Shop

Gamers chat about trading and ID numbers, and the shelves are full of games consoles.

4F: Wise Man Gifts

The clerk sells Poké Dolls, the Fire, Thunder, Water and Leaf Stones, and a Linking Cord.

5F: Drugstore

One clerk sells battle items like X Attack and Dire Hit. The other sells vitamins: HP Up, Protein, Iron, Calcium, Zinc and Carbos. They're only sold here.

Roof

An old lady sells Eggs the Day Care couldn't place, for $2,000 each. You choose which baby Pokémon's Egg you want: Togepi, Smoochum, Pichu, Magby, Igglybuff, Happiny, Elekid, Cleffa or Azurill. You need room in your team for it.

The Mayor's Office

The Mayor welcomes you to his city and asks you to vote for him. Meanwhile, a policeman stands guard by the sewers day and night on his orders, and won't let anyone near. People say a strange creature lives down there.

{{journal:orange_s11a}}

Once you've met the Mayor, the guard by the sewers dozes off on the job, and you can slip past him.

Other buildings

Two Psychics live in one house. The mother sensed you were coming and gives you a TM for Calm Mind. The father hides a coin in one hand and asks you to guess which. Guess right three times in a row and he gives you a Twisted Spoon, which powers up Psychic moves. Miss, and you can try again any time.

Trovitopolis Sewers

The sewers run under the city. They're dark and damp, with a main tunnel and five rooms off it, and plenty of Poison Pokémon live down here.

{{tables:TROVITOPOLIS_SEWERS}}

The Super Repel, the Full Heal and the PP Up are hidden: step on their spots.

{{trainers:TROVITOPOLIS_SEWERS}}

The Bulbasaur

In the far room, a Bulbasaur is living all alone. It wears a collar with the Mayor's campaign symbol on it. You pick it up to show the Mayor.

In the back room, a man won't say what he's doing down here. If you keep quiet about seeing him, he teaches one of your Pokémon Thunder Wave.

Back at the Mayor's Office

Take Bulbasaur to the Mayor's Office. Officer Jenny and Nurse Joy are there, and Bulbasaur runs straight to the Mayor. He has to admit he left it in the sewers when he was a boy. He begs them to keep it quiet, but Officer Jenny says the people have a right to know, and Nurse Joy takes Bulbasaur into her care.

Outside, Officer Jenny thanks you for showing the city who the Mayor really is, and gives you a TM for Solar Beam.

Route 65

Route 65 runs west from Trovitopolis along the beach, then out across the sea to Trovita Island.

{{tables:ORANGE_ROUTE_65}}

The Heart Scale and the Ether are hidden: step on their spots.
