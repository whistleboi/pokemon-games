Route 70

Route 70 leads west from Kumquat Island, then turns south towards Route 71. A sign says some rare Pokémon live around here. On the beach, two kids keep asking a fossil hunter what he's looking for. He says beaches are good for fossils, just not this one.

{{tables:ORANGE_ROUTE_70}} {{trainers:ORANGE_ROUTE_70}}

Mega Punch and Mega Kick

Two men on the route can't stand each other. One swears by Mega Punch and the other by Mega Kick, and each teaches his move to one of your Pokémon. You can come back to either for more lessons ours.

Route 71

Route 71 is open sea south of Route 70, with Rind Island just off it. Two Cooltrainers with full teams of five battle here, so heal up first.

{{tables:ORANGE_ROUTE_71}} {{trainers:ORANGE_ROUTE_71}}

Dr. Quackenpoker's Magikarp

A man is watching the sea. He's Quincy T. Quackenpoker, a Pokémon Watcher who has given his life to Magikarp, and you've arrived just in time: a whole school of Magikarp swims past, heading for Rind Island. A strong enough Magikarp can climb the island's waterfalls, and he's following them upstream. Go with him to Rind Island.

{{journal:orange_s15a}}

Once you've seen what happens at Rind Island Lake, come back to the spot on Route 71 where you met him. Magikarp and Gyarados swim out to sea together, an amazing sight he sees every year. Talk to Quackenpoker afterwards and he gives you a Dragon Fang, which powers up Dragon-type moves.

Rind Island

Rind Island is where the Magikarp of these waters are born. Its river climbs up to Rind Island Lake at the top.

{{tables:RIND_ISLAND}}

The Magikarp's journey

By the river, Quackenpoker explains that the Magikarp born here swim out to sea as they grow. Once a year they come home, climbing the waterfalls to prove they're strong enough. The ones that don't make it try again next year. He goes on up, and asks you to come and see what happens at the top.

At Rind Island Lake, the Magikarp circle faster and faster, and then, in a flash of light, one after another evolves into a mighty Gyarados. Quackenpoker hurries back to Route 71 to see them leave the island.

Route 72

Route 72 runs west to Pummelo Island, home of the Orange League. Salveyo Island sits off the route. A woman says nobody lives on Salveyo, but there are a few cabins for trainers.

{{tables:ORANGE_ROUTE_72}} {{trainers:ORANGE_ROUTE_72}}

Cooltrainers Paul and Alexa both have five Pokémon, and Paul's team is all Fire types.

Salveyo Island

Salveyo Island is a wild, empty island with a wooden cabin that any trainer may camp in. Misty and Tracey are staying in it, and something seems wrong.

{{journal:orange_s15b}} {{tables:SALVEYO_ISLAND}}

Salveyo Weed

Inside the cabin, Tracey feels terrible. Misty explains that he stuck his head in a Vileplume and got a face full of Stun Spore, and a Paralyze Heal isn't strong enough. Her book says the only cure is a tea made from Salveyo Weed, which grows somewhere on the island.

Look for a Poliwag in the water with some weed stuck on its tail. When it sees you looking, it dives down and comes back with a mouthful of weed for you. Take the Salveyo Weed back to Misty. Her tea works, and Tracey feels much better. He's glad you beat Luana, and Misty says they'll see you on Pummelo Island.

Pummelo Island

Pummelo Island is the home of the Orange League. Here the Winner's Trophy is won in a single battle against the Champion, Drake: there's no Elite Four to get through first. At the top of the island stands the Palace of Victory, the Orange League Hall of Fame, and Pummelo Stadium is where the match is held.

{{journal:orange_m15a}} {{tables:PUMMELO_ISLAND}}

Your rival

As you walk in, your rival calls out to you. He didn't think you'd make it to Pummelo with four badges, but he won all four on his holiday too. Now he wants to see which of you is stronger.

{{battle:PUMMELO_ISLAND:rival}}

When he loses, your rival wonders what the point of fighting Drake is if he can't beat you. Maybe it's time he started a new journey.

Misty

If you helped Tracey on Salveyo Island, Misty and Tracey are waiting here. Misty warns you not to underestimate Drake, and offers you some extra training first: a battle against her full team.

{{battle:PUMMELO_ISLAND:Misty}}

Around the island

  • The Tutors' House has five tutors: Substitute, Seismic Toss, Crunch, Double-Edge and Dragon Dance. You can come back to them for more lessons ours.
  • In another house, a Dragon Tamer sees the spark in your eyes and gives you TM Dragon Claw, for when you meet another Dragon.
  • The Poké Mart is the only one that sells Full Restores. It also sells Ultra Balls, Max Potions, Revives, Full Heals and Max Repels.
  • The Pokémon Center is your last chance to heal before Drake. A man outside says most trainers need more experience first, and train on the islands to the north-east.

The Palace of Victory

The Palace of Victory holds the footprints of every Orange League Champion and their Pokémon, Drake's among them. Drake himself is here, wondering whether a strong enough challenger will ever come. If you have all four Orange Crew badges, he introduces himself and says he'll be waiting for you in Pummelo Stadium. Without them, he just goes on wondering.

Pummelo Stadium: Drake

Until you've met Drake at the palace, the Stadium guard won't let you past. Once you have, he tells you Drake is waiting in the battle arena. The crowd is cheering, and Misty and Tracey are there too: Tracey is going to sketch the whole match.

This is a full six-on-six battle, and Drake has one rule: no switching. Each Pokémon battles until it faints, so think about the order you send them out. His team covers lots of types. Ice moves are your best weapon against his Dragonite, Water and Grass moves hit Onix hard, and Ground moves work on Electabuzz. His Ditto turns into a copy of your Pokémon.

{{battle:PUMMELO_ISLAND:Drake|Drake's rule: no switching. Your Pokémon battles on until it faints.}} {{badges:post}} {{cap}}

When you win, Drake congratulates you. He says your win isn't yours alone: it comes from the bond with your Pokémon. The Winner's Trophy is yours, and you're the Orange League Champion! Your team's footprints will go in the Palace of Victory.

Hall of Fame

Your team is entered in the Hall of Fame, each Pokémon shown one at a time and then all together with you. Then you head home to Valencia Island to share your victory.

After the title

You're the Champion of the Orange Islands now, and new things open up:

  • The Legend Festival. The festival is starting on Shamouti Island. Part 12 covers what happens there.
  • To Kanto. The Rocket Blimp on Valencia Island is fixed at last, and flies you to Vermilion City.
  • Legendary Pokémon. Ho-Oh lands on Route 50 (Lv 70), and Celebi appears on Route 73 (Lv 50).
  • An Up-Grade. If you took the Porygon from the Super Nerd in Trovitopolis's Pokémon Center, go back as Champion and he gives you an Up-Grade. A Porygon holding it becomes Porygon2.
  • Rematches. The Orange Crew and Drake battle you again, with much stronger teams. See part 12.
