Moro Island

Moro Island is a quiet island south of the Seven Grapefruit Islands, at the end of Route 59. There's no Gym here: the big sight is the Moro Island Museum, full of fossils and relics from the islands' past. Route 60 leads west from here.

Now that you've earned Danny's badge, the kids who were sitting still on the stairs have moved on to a new game: staring at the sea.

{{journal:orange_m8a}} {{tables:MORO_ISLAND}}

The Hyper Potion is hidden: step on its spot. The water around the island can be fished and surfed.

Around town, a man tells you the Museum's researchers are off digging on Fukuhara Island, and an old woman says one of them has made a great discovery.

Moro Island Museum

A ticket costs $50: the receptionist asks for it as you walk in, and won't let you past without paying. Downstairs, fossils of Aerodactyl and Kabutops are on show.

The upper floor

Upstairs, a researcher is working on a machine to bring Pokémon back from fossils. Show the researcher a Helix Fossil and it comes back as an Omanyte, and a Dome Fossil as a Kabuto. A piece of Old Amber becomes an Aerodactyl. Each one comes back at Lv 5.

{{journal:orange_s8b}} {{journal:orange_s_amber}}

The fossil you found in Bay Cave is the one to bring for Omanyte or Kabuto. The Old Amber comes from the dig on Fukuhara Island.

Other buildings

A sailor in one house is glad to meet someone who travels by sea, and gives you a TM for Hydro Pump. The Move Deleter lives next door, but these days your Pokémon remember every move they've learned, so he has very little to do. Heal at the Center, and stock up at the Mart before you set off.

Route 60

Route 60 is open sea west of Moro Island, with a grassy island near Moro's side. Surf south across it to reach Golden Island.

{{tables:ORANGE_ROUTE_60}}

The Moon Stone and the Heart Scale are hidden on the route, and the Star Piece is hidden on the seabed. The patch of dark, deep water can be dived with the Scuba Gear from Route 58. Down on the seabed, a passage leads into an Undersea Cave with a Spell Tag, and a Big Pearl and a Heart Scale hidden inside.

{{trainers:ORANGE_ROUTE_60}}

Golden Island

Golden Island is a strange little place. Everyone here wears whiskers and cat ears, and they keep watch from their towers for the legendary Meowth of Bounty. A big golden statue of Meowth stands in the middle of the island. Route 61 leads south from here.

{{tables:GOLDEN_ISLAND}}

The Meowth of Bounty

Walk up towards the statue and the whole crowd starts cheering. The elder's son announces that the Meowth of Bounty has come at last, and will shower everyone with coins using Pay Day. But the Meowth sitting on the statue can talk, and it can't even use Pay Day! It jumps down and runs off, leaving the islanders staring.

Elder's House

The elder is pleased to meet a visitor who's interested in his people. He gives you an Amulet Coin. When one of your Pokémon holds it, you earn more money from trainer battles.

Route 61

Route 61 is a stretch of sea between Golden Island and Murcott Island. Surf south from Golden Island, then east to Murcott.

Murcott Island

Murcott Island is the home of Bug Pokémon. The town is small, with a Pokémon Center and a few houses. Murcott Meadows lies just beyond the town, and Route 62 leads south.

{{journal:orange_m9a}} {{tables:MURCOTT_ISLAND}}

The Full Heal is hidden: step on its spot.

The bug catchers in town say Murcott is one of the best places to catch Bug Pokémon. A man in one of the houses can't stand all the bugs, so he gives you a TM for Aerial Ace, a Flying move that never misses. A tourist in the Center points out that there's no Orange Crew member anywhere near here: the next is Rudy, on Trovita Island.

Murcott Meadows

Murcott Meadows is a wide field of tall grass, full of Bug Pokémon. Scyther and Pinsir both live here, though they're rare. Bug Catchers come from all over to try their luck.

{{tables:MURCOTT_MEADOWS}}

The Rawst Berry and the Energy Root are hidden: step on their spots.

{{trainers:MURCOTT_MEADOWS}}

Tracey's Scyther

Tracey is in the meadow, looking at a wild Scyther that's badly hurt. He won't battle it like this: he catches it and hurries off to the Pokémon Center.

{{journal:orange_s9a}}

Follow him back to Murcott Island's Pokémon Center and talk to him. While he's telling you Scyther needs more rest, Nurse Joy rushes in: Scyther has flown out of the window, straight back to the meadow! Tracey runs after it.

Back in Murcott Meadows, Tracey waits near a swarm of Scyther. Walk up to them and Team Rocket appear, wanting the whole swarm. They stick the Scyther down with a glue gun, until the injured Scyther swoops in and drives them off. The swarm flies away, but the injured one stays behind. Tracey works out that it once led the swarm and lost the lead, so it can't go back. He decides to train it himself, starting with a battle against you.

{{battle:MURCOTT_MEADOWS:Tracey}}

Fire and Flying moves work well against Venonat and Scyther, and Electric or Grass moves against Marill.

Route 62

Route 62 runs south from Murcott Island, past a small, calm island where Farfetch'd nest, and then west to Mandarin Island South. Misty and Tracey are resting on the island. Misty thinks the Farfetch'd have plenty to eat, with so many Berry trees around.

{{tables:ORANGE_ROUTE_62}}

The Stardust and all the Berries are hidden: step on their spots.

{{trainers:ORANGE_ROUTE_62}}

A swimmer on the island is in such a good mood that they offer to teach one of your Pokémon Waterfall. A Pokémon that knows it can climb waterfalls on the map, so it's worth having.

Mandarin Island South

Mandarin Island South is the biggest of the Orange Islands, and its town shares the island's name. Don't mix it up with Mandarin Island North: the two aren't joined at all. Route 62 is back to the east, and a road leads north out of town to Route 63.

{{journal:orange_m9b}} {{tables:MANDARIN_SOUTH}}

The PP Up is hidden: step on its spot.

Fly

A man in a house at the top of town loves being up high. He gives you HM Fly. A Pokémon that knows it can carry you to any town you've visited before.

Other buildings

Heal at the Center and shop at the Mart. In the restaurant, a diner says Prima of the Elite Four eats there sometimes. A man in another house says Yambera, a quiet village a short trip north, is a nice place to unwind.

Prima's House

Prima of the Elite Four lives here, and she likes to battle trainers who visit. As soon as you step inside, she comes over to talk and the battle begins. Her Ice and Water Pokémon are all Lv 50, far above your team right now, so you may want to save this visit for later. Electric moves work well against all of them except Jynx.

{{battle:MANDARIN_SOUTH:Prima}}

If you lose, she says to come back whenever you like.

Rival battle

As you head for the north road, someone calls out for you to wait. It's your rival, still touring the Orange Islands, and he wants to see which of you has grown stronger. Water moves work well against four of his six Pokémon: Nidoking, Nidoqueen, Arcanine and Golem.

{{journal:orange_m9c}} {{battle:MANDARIN_SOUTH:rival}}

After the battle, he grumbles that it shouldn't have happened. He has two Orange Crew badges too, and he tells you Rudy's Gym on Trovita Island is next, with battles held on a high cliff.
