Your house on Valencia Island, if you start a new game here. Mom is downstairs, and your room is upstairs.

Professor Ivy's Pokémon Lab, on the west side of Valencia Island. She studies how the islands' Pokémon differ from the same kinds elsewhere. Here you pick your partner: Bulbasaur, Charmander or Squirtle, each at Lv 10. Brock is staying to help out, and a GS Ball nobody can open sits on the table. See Part 1.

After Lugia's song, Tracey comes to work here. Once you've caught 100 kinds of Kanto Pokémon, he gives you the MysticTicket for the ferry to New Island (Part 12).

Valencia's Pokémon Center. After the Stun Spore accident in Valencia Park, Professor Ivy rests here and thanks you with a Leaf Stone. Valencia has no Poké Mart. See Part 1.

The island school. Its blackboards explain that type matters and that a tired Pokémon is easier to catch.

A neighbour who has heard Professor Ivy's lab is terribly messy.

Valencia Airport, in the south of the island. The blimp to Kanto is grounded for repairs, but the one to Goldenrod City flies over Tangelo Island, and the clerk lets you jump with a parachute. That's how you leave Valencia (Part 1). Once you're the Orange League Champion, the blimp to Kanto is fixed and flies you to Vermilion City.

Tangelo's Pokémon Center. After you drive off the bullies, the hurt Lapras rests in the pool behind it, where Team Rocket try to take it. Win and Lapras joins you, bringing HM Surf. Tell Misty and Tracey inside before you leave the island. See Part 2.

Tangelo's Poké Mart, the first on your journey: Poké Balls, Potions, Antidotes, Paralyze Heals, Awakenings, Burn Heals, Escape Ropes and Repels. Each Orange Islands Mart has its own shelf. See Poké Marts.

A kid whose dad guides older visitors round Pokémon Park, a big business on Tangelo.

An old man says a herd of Lapras swims past Tangelo Island every year.

A cook who just wants to be left in peace.

Cissy's Gym, the first of the Orange Crew. Four trainers wait inside; you don't have to beat them to reach Cissy, but they're good practice.

Before the battle Cissy sets two tests as one game: the Water Gun test, hitting eight targets in the pool before six get away, and the Lapras race against her Seadra across the bay, dodging rocks. Win both to battle her. Beat her for the Coral-Eye Badge and TM011 Water Pulse. See Part 3 and Gyms.

Once you're Champion, her Gym can be battled again from its first trainer, at stronger levels.

Mikan's Mart adds Super Potions to Tangelo's shelf.

An old man worried that his Ground and Rock Pokémon can't beat Cissy gives you a Tyrogue (Lv 8) holding a Black Belt. See Part 3.

Mandarin Island North's Mart sells the same as Mikan's.

The police station. The officers suspect people in black are behind the disobedient Pokémon, and a burglar in the station says they've got no proof. Officer Jenny's investigation starts near here (Part 3).

The climber's house, just south of the old power plant. Once you've shut down Team Rocket's hideout, he gives you a Climb Kit he made himself. You need it to climb the rock faces of Navel Mountain. See Part 4.

Sunburst's Pokémon Center. A reader here is enjoying the legend of the Crystal Onix. Sunburst has no Poké Mart.

Mateo's Glass Workshop. Mateo is away searching the Crystal Cave when you arrive. After the Crystal Onix brings back his inspiration, he's working here again. See Part 4.

The Name Rater's house. He'll look at any of your Pokémon's names and help you change them.

An islander and a girl who both talk about Mateo and his glass sculptures.

A man thinks you look like a decent trainer and gives you TM058 Brick Break, which smashes through Reflect and Light Screen. See Part 4.

The Preserve Hut on Pinkan Island, Officer Jenny's base. Talk to her and she heals your team, the only place on Pinkan to do it. Her officers explain why the Pokémon here are pink, and why poachers want them. See Part 5.

Kinnow's Mart: Poké Balls, Great Balls, Super Potions, Revives, Full Heals, Escape Ropes and Super Repels. It doesn't sell Showboat Passes.

The Kinnow Hotel, full whenever the Showboat docks. A man whose Growlithe scares everyone away gives you TM172 Roar.

The Fisherman's House. The fisherman sees a spark in your eyes and gives you a Good Rod. See Part 5.

A guest from the Pokémon Showboat is staying here with an old friend instead of in his cabin. He doesn't need his pass while he's here, so he gives you his Showboat Pass, which gets you aboard. See Part 5.

Nurse Joy's Pokémon Clinic on Route 58. She's hardly ever in, because she's out caring for Pokémon on the islands; only her Chansey minds the place. Meet her out on the route and she gives you the Scuba Gear (Part 6).

Navel Island's Pokémon Center, the place to heal before climbing Navel Mountain. Navel has no Poké Mart, so stock up on Kinnow Island.

A young man says trading can fill the gaps in your Pokédex, and a girl reminds you that some moves have to be taught.

A scientist warns that the Orange Crew mix their teams' types, so they're harder to beat than Kanto's Leaders.

A trader swaps his Magmar, holding Charcoal, for a Jynx; wild Jynx live on Navel Mountain. See In-game trades.

The cable car station at the foot of Navel Mountain. Challengers must climb to Danny on foot, so the attendant only lets you ride once you've beaten him. After that you can ride whenever you like. See Part 6.

The cable car station at the top of Navel Mountain, by Danny's summit. Once you have his badge, ride it back down whenever you like.

The Grapefruit Headquarters on Grapefruit Island Three, where the supervisors keep an eye on the islands' trees. A worker says Ruby, the owner, works harder than anyone.

A sailor glad to meet someone who travels by sea gives you TM142 Hydro Pump. See Part 7.

The Move Deleter's house. Your Pokémon remember every move they've learned now, so he has very little to do.

The Moro Island Museum, full of fossils and relics from the islands' past. The receptionist asks $50 for a ticket. On this floor, fossils of Aerodactyl and Kabutops are on show. See Part 7.

The Museum's upper floor. A fossil researcher brings fossils back to life at Lv 5: a Helix Fossil as Omanyte, a Dome Fossil as Kabuto and Old Amber as Aerodactyl. The fossil comes from the Bay Cave and the Old Amber from the dig on Fukuhara Island.

An islander who dreams of travelling, but won't miss their share when the Meowth of Bounty comes.

The Elder's House. The elder is pleased to meet a visitor who's interested in his people and gives you an Amulet Coin. See Part 7.

Murcott's Pokémon Center. Tracey brings the hurt Scyther from Murcott Meadows here, and it flies out of the window back to the meadow. See Part 7.

A man who can't stand all the bugs on Murcott gives you TM027 Aerial Ace, a Flying move that never misses.

Mandarin Island South's restaurant. A diner says Prima of the Elite Four eats here sometimes.

A man at the top of town who loves being up high gives you HM Fly. See Part 7.

A man says Yambera, a short trip north, is a nice place to unwind.

Prima's house. Prima of the Elite Four lives here and battles anyone who steps inside. Her Ice and Water Pokémon are all Lv 50, far above your team when you first pass by, so you may want to save this for later. If you lose, she says to come back any time. Win for five Poké Balls and a PP Max. See Part 7.

A woman thinks Ralph and Emily are secretly sweet on each other.

A trader swaps a Voltorb holding a Bright Powder for a Magnemite, which you can catch on Route 64. See In-game trades.

A man back from the Mandarin Desert, where lightning struck right next to him, gives you TM298 Shock Wave. See Part 8.

Desert Town's Pokémon Center, the oasis stop on the long walk across the Mandarin Desert.

A house in Desert Town where a trader swaps a Chansey holding a Lucky Punch for a Kangaskhan. Kangaskhan are rare, but you can catch one on this route. See In-game trades.

Trovitopolis's Pokémon Center. A Super Nerd who once won a Porygon at the Celadon Game Corner gives you Porygon (Lv 15). Come back as Orange League Champion and he gives you an Up-Grade too. See Part 8.

The Trovito Dept. Store, the main sight of Trovitopolis: five floors and a roof, with a lift between them. The board on 1F shows each floor. See Department stores and Part 8.

2F: Trainer's Market

One counter sells everyday goods; the other sells TMs: Hyper Beam, Light Screen, Safeguard, Dig, Brick Break, Reflect, Secret Power and Attract.

3F: TV Game Shop

Gamers chat about trading.

4F: Wise Man Gifts

Poké Dolls, the Fire, Thunder, Water and Leaf Stones, and a Linking Cord.

5F: Drugstore

Battle items like X Attack and Dire Hit, and the vitamins HP Up, Protein, Iron, Calcium, Zinc and Carbos, sold nowhere else in the islands.

The Dept. Store's roof. An old lady sells Eggs the Day Care couldn't place, $2,000 each. You pick the baby Pokémon: Togepi, Smoochum, Pichu, Magby, Igglybuff, Happiny, Elekid, Cleffa or Azurill. You need room in your team.

The Mayor's Office. The Mayor asks for your vote; once you've met him, the guard by the sewers dozes off. Bring back the Bulbasaur with his campaign symbol on its collar from the Trovitopolis Sewers, and his secret comes out: he left it there as a boy. Officer Jenny then gives you TM Solar Beam. See Part 8.

A house of Psychics. The mother sensed you coming and gives you TM129 Calm Mind. The father hides a coin in one hand: guess right three times in a row for a Twisted Spoon, and try again any time you miss. See Part 8.

A sewer fan says rare Pokémon live down in the Trovitopolis Sewers.

Rudy's Gym, the third of the Orange Crew. His little sister Mahri greets you at the door, and two Cooltrainers watch the hall. Rudy waits at the far end, but he'd rather battle somewhere better: outside, his hot-air balloon carries you, Misty and Tracey up to a cliff-top.

His three Pokémon, Electabuzz, Exeggutor and Starmie, are all Lv 40. Win the Spike Shell Badge and TM120 Psychic; afterwards a man in town gives you the Whirlpuller. Once you're Champion, Rudy battles you again here. See Part 9.

A fisherman takes one look at you and gives you the Super Rod, the best rod there is. See Part 9.

The Move Tutor's house. Pokémon don't forget their moves any more, so he has nothing to remind them of.

Shamouti's Mart: Great Balls, Ultra Balls, Hyper Potions, Revives, Full Heals, Escape Ropes and Max Repels.

A trader swaps his Raichu, holding a Focus Band, for a Dodrio. See In-game trades.

An old man reminds you that the Chosen One is meant to set three treasures on the shrine.

A Kindler says nobody needs a move that hot with water everywhere, and gives you TM157 Overheat. See Part 12.

The Festival House, on the south side of Shamouti Island. Once you're Champion, the Legend Festival is on: at the banquet here, Melody, this year's Festival Maiden, dances, plays her ocarina and tells you the legend, and sends you, the Chosen One, to fetch the spheres from Fire, Ice and Lightning Island. See Part 12.

Ascorbia's Mart: Great Balls, Ultra Balls, Hyper Potions, Revives, Full Heals, Escape Ropes and Max Repels.

A man says Team Wartortle can do it all: the perfect firefighting squad.

A kid having a Body Slam contest teaches Body Slam, and you can come back for more lessons ours. See TMs and tutors.

A woman scared of what happens if a fire breaks out while Team Wartortle is gone.

Ascorbia's fire station, home of Team Wartortle, the best firefighting squad in the islands. As you arrive, Team Rocket carry them off in a balloon. After you rescue them from the warehouse in Ascorbia Forest, Captain Aidan thanks you here with TM125 Flamethrower. See Part 10.

Butwal's Pokémon Center. After you beat Gulzar he heals his team here; find him inside and he gives you HM Cut. See Part 10.

A hiker here teaches Rock Slide, and you can come back for more lessons ours. See TMs and tutors.

Kumquat's Pokémon Center. Trainers here warn you that Luana's Gym battle is a Double Battle: both sides send out two Pokémon at once.

Kumquat's Mart: Great Balls, Ultra Balls, Hyper Potions, Revives, Full Heals, Escape Ropes and Max Repels.

A man here teaches Swords Dance, and you can come back for more lessons ours. See TMs and tutors.

A boy gives you TM274 Return and a girl gives you TM273 Frustration. See Part 10.

The Kumquat Hotel, the big hotel on Kumquat's beach. Its Gym is upstairs. Until you've met Luana on the beach, the receptionist says the Leader is out; afterwards she's waiting in the lobby and sends you up in the lift to the Battle Arena. In the lobby, a man gives you TM265 Iron Tail. See Part 10.

The hotel lift. The attendant takes you up to the top floor once Luana has invited you.

The hotel's top floor, the way through to the Battle Arena.

Luana's Battle Arena, where she battles the hotel's guests. Luana is the strongest of the Orange Crew, and her challenge is a Double Battle against her Marowak and Alakazam, both Lv 46. Win the Jade Star Badge, your fourth, and TM149 Earthquake; she warns that Earthquake hits your partner too in a Double Battle. Once you're Champion, she battles you again here. See Part 10 and Gyms.

The wooden cabin on Salveyo Island, free for any trainer to camp in. Misty and Tracey are staying here, and Tracey is sick from Vileplume's Stun Spore. Bring Misty the Salveyo Weed from the island's Poliwag and her tea cures him. See Part 11.

Pummelo's Pokémon Center, your last chance to heal before Drake.

Pummelo's Mart is the only one in the islands that sells Full Restores. It also sells Ultra Balls, Max Potions, Revives, Full Heals and Max Repels.

The Tutors' House, with five tutors under one roof: Substitute, Seismic Toss, Crunch, Double-Edge and Dragon Dance. Each can also sell you the move's TM, and you can come back for more lessons ours. See TMs and tutors and Part 11.

An old woman tells you about the Champions' footprints in the Palace of Victory.

A Dragon Tamer sees the spark in your eyes and gives you TM078 Dragon Claw. See Part 11.

The Palace of Victory, the Orange League's Hall of Fame, at the top of Pummelo Island. It holds the footprints of every Champion and their Pokémon. Drake is here: with all four Orange Crew badges, he introduces himself and says he'll wait for you in Pummelo Stadium. See Part 11.

Pummelo Stadium, home of the Orange League. The guard won't let you through to the arena until you've met Drake at the Palace of Victory.

The Stadium's battle arena, where you face Drake for the Winner's Trophy. There's no Elite Four first: it's one full six-on-six battle, and Drake's rule is no switching, so each Pokémon battles until it faints. Win and you're the Orange League Champion; your team goes into the Hall of Fame. As Champion, you can battle Drake here again. See Part 11.

An old man who used to live on Hamlin Island, until an energy experiment went wrong and Electrode took over the town.

Two trades: a Magneton holding a Metal Coat for a Marowak, and a Clefairy holding a Lucky Egg for a Jigglypuff. See In-game trades and Part 12.

Hamlin's Pokémon Center is locked until Poncho and his Diglett have cleared the Electrode out of town. See Part 12.

Hamlin's Poké Mart, locked until the Electrode are gone.

Hamlin Harbor, the northern gateway to the islands. It's locked while the Electrode run the town, and even afterwards the ferry to Kanto doesn't run. Show the clerk the MysticTicket from Tracey and the ferry takes you to New Island, where Mewtwo waits. See Part 12.
