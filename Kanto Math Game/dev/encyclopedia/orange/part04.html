Route 54

With Team Rocket gone from Mandarin Island North, the island's Pokémon are calm again. Before you leave, call on the climber in the house just south of the Rocket Hideout: he's so pleased you drove the Rockets out of his home that he gives you a Climb Kit he made himself. Keep it safe, because you'll need it to climb a mountain later on.

Then Surf south. Route 54 is open sea between Mandarin Island North and Sunburst Island, with swimmers, a fisherman and a couple of other trainers out on the water.

Sunburst Island

Sunburst Island is the island of glassblowers. Route 54 comes in from the north, Route 55 leaves to the south, and the Crystal Cave, where the glassblowers get their glass, is on the island too. There's a Pokémon Center here, but no Poké Mart.

{{tables:SUNBURST_ISLAND}}

Tracey

Walk toward the east beach and Tracey runs to catch up with you. He's heard the same rumour as everyone else: a mysterious Pokémon lives in the Crystal Cave. Before you go and look, he wants a battle to get your Pokémon ready.

{{battle:SUNBURST_ISLAND:Tracey}}

Afterwards he heads off to the Crystal Cave, and asks you to meet him there.

{{journal:orange_m4a}}

Around Sunburst

Plenty of trainers are out enjoying the sun, and each one wants a battle. A camper dreams of taking on the Orange Crew, a picnicker has heard of an island nearby where the Pokémon are a different colour, and a girl with Fighting Pokémon warns you that the cave is full of Zubat.

{{trainers:SUNBURST_ISLAND}}

A girl called Marissa says her brother Mateo is one of the best glassblowers around, but nobody believes her, and he's lost his inspiration.

Pokémon Center

Heal your team here. A reader is enjoying a legend about a Crystal Onix, a Pokémon made of glass, and an old woman tells you her son on Mandarin Island North is a great rock climber. Upstairs, the Union Room, the Trade Center and the Colosseum are all closed.

Houses

The Name Rater lives here, and will look at any of your Pokémon's names. In another house, a man thinks you look like a decent trainer and gives you TM058 Brick Break, which smashes through Reflect and Light Screen. The people in a third house both talk about Mateo and his wonderful glass sculptures.

Mateo's Glass Workshop is here too, but he isn't home yet. Come back after the Crystal Cave to see him.

Crystal Cave

The Crystal Cave glitters with crystals, and a lake lies deep inside. Geodude, Zubat and Machop live here, with Marill and Onix now and then, and you can Surf on the water inside.

{{tables:CRYSTAL_CAVE}}

The Berry Juice and the Elixir are hidden: step on their spots to find them.

{{trainers:CRYSTAL_CAVE}}

The Crystal Onix

By the lake, Tracey is waiting with Mateo, the glassblower. Mateo's grandfather was the best glassblower on Sunburst, and people say seeing the Crystal Onix is what made him great. Mateo has been searching the cave for it, and he's starting to give up hope.

Then something stirs under the water, and a Pokémon of clear glass rises out of the lake: the Crystal Onix. Mateo doesn't want to fight it. Just seeing it is enough to bring back his inspiration. To thank you both, he gives you TM036 Rock Tomb. Tracey says he's heading east, and goes on ahead.

Challenging the Crystal Onix. Once you've seen it, you can come back to the lake and battle the Crystal Onix. It's Lv 40, far stronger than anything else around here, so there's no hurry. You can catch it if you win. Your Journal keeps it as a side mission until you beat or catch it.

Sunburst Island (again)

Back in town, Marissa can't thank you enough for helping her brother. Mateo is in his Glass Workshop now, and business is booming again.

Route 55

Route 55 runs south from Sunburst Island. It leads on south to Valencia Island, and it also turns east toward Pinkan Island, which is where the Journal sends you next.
