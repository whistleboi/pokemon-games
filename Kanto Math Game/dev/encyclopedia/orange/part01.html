Introduction

The Orange Islands are a chain of warm, sunny islands far to the south of Kanto. Instead of eight Gyms they have the Orange Crew: four Gym Leaders spread across the islands, and after them Drake, the Orange League's Champion, waits at Pummelo Stadium.

There are two ways to get here:

  • A new game. Pick the Orange Islands from the region list. Choose your look and type your name, then name your rival: he's Professor Oak's grandson, the same rival you'd have in Kanto. Professor Ivy welcomes you, and a blimp sets you down at Valencia Island's airport.
  • From Kanto. Once you're Kanto's Champion, a Rocket Blimp to the Orange Islands leaves from Vermilion City's harbour. It's a move to a new region: the Orange Islands keep their own party, PC and Bag, so you arrive with no Pokémon and Professor Ivy gives you a new partner.

Either way, your Journal (in the MENU) always shows your next main mission, and a gold marker on the map points the way.

How this guide plays

This guide is written for two settings in Options › Gameplay: Battle style: Overworld and Level cap: Story. The cap is on from the start; the battle style you switch on yourself.

Overworld battles

On the walked routes and in wild places like Pokémon Park, you can see the wild Pokémon moving about. Walk into one and the battle happens right there on the map: it fights from where it stands, yours steps up beside it, and small HP bars float over both. Trainers on these maps, and in places like the Rocket Hideout, spot you and walk over, send out their Pokémon in front of them, and you fight on the map too. In the trainer tables, "on the map" marks these.

Every battle still works the same way: pick a move and answer its math question to land it.

The important story battles always open the battle screen: the Orange Crew, Drake, your rival, Team Rocket's bosses, and anyone who stops you in a story scene, Jessie and James included. So do the trainers inside Gyms and towns and a bite on your rod. Each battle box says which.

The story level cap

Your Pokémon can't level past the next Gym Leader's best Pokémon plus two. At the cap they stop gaining Exp., and Rare Candies don't work. Beat the Leader and the cap moves up. Each part starts with a box giving the cap for that stretch.

Levels never rise to meet you: wild Pokémon come at their place's own levels, and trainers keep their own levels. So the levels in this guide's tables and battle boxes are the ones you'll face, however strong your team is.

Poké Marts and prizes

The Orange Islands' Poké Marts don't share one counter. Each island's Mart has its own short shelf, so this guide lists what each one sells when you get there. Valencia Island has no Mart at all, only a Pokémon Center.

Route trainers don't pay in Poké Balls: beating one gives you a healing prize that grows with your badges.

The Orange Islands have four badges, so the last row only comes once you're the Orange League Champion.

Valencia Island

Valencia Island is a bright, sandy island, and home to Professor Ivy's Pokémon Lab. The airport is in the south, and the lab is on the west side. A path through the trees leads into Valencia Park. Route 49 leaves to the west and Route 55 to the north, but both are open sea, so you can't take them until you can Surf.

{{tables:VALENCIA_ISLAND}}

A fisherman on the island gives you an Old Rod as soon as you talk to him, so you can fish Valencia's water right away. A rod never hooks anything below its own level: Old Rod Lv 5, Good Rod Lv 10, Super Rod Lv 15.

Arrival

You step off the blimp at Valencia Island's airport. Before you get far, one of Professor Ivy's aides comes to meet you. The professor is out on the beach, so the aides lead you there.

{{journal:orange_m0a}}

Professor Ivy is training her Gyarados by the water. She introduces herself: she studies how the Pokémon of these islands differ from the same kinds elsewhere. Professor Oak told her you were coming, and she asks you to come to her lab, just up the path.

Professor Ivy's Lab

{{journal:orange_m0b}}

Professor Ivy's three aides are busy with her research, and Brock is staying at the lab to help. An odd Poké Ball sits on the table with a G and an S carved on it. Nobody can open this GS Ball, so Professor Oak is looking into it.

Talk to Professor Ivy to pick your partner from her Pokémon: Bulbasaur, Charmander or Squirtle, each at Lv 10. She won't let you leave until you've chosen.

Once you have your partner, Professor Ivy tells you that she and Brock are off to Valencia Park to watch the wild Vileplume, and asks you to join them there.

Around the island

A girl tells you that the Pokémon on Valencia come in colours you won't see anywhere else. A neighbour has heard that Professor Ivy and her aides are terribly messy, and has never dared look inside the lab.

Your house

Mom is home, and reminds you to eat your vegetables and get some exercise. Upstairs in your room, the note on the wall says to open the MENU if you're stuck.

School

The blackboards have two useful tips: a Pokémon's type decides which moves hurt it most, and a tired Pokémon is easier to catch, so weaken it first.

Pokémon Center

Healing is free here, as it is on every island ahead. Upstairs, the Union Room, the Trade Center and the Colosseum are all closed.

Valencia Park

Valencia Park is a grassy wood on Valencia Island, reached through the trees from the town. It's full of Bug and Grass Pokémon, and a few of the island's own colourful kinds hide here too, though they're rare. Watch out for the grown-up ones, such as Valencia Raticate, Gyarados and Poliwhirl: they're Lv 20 or more, far stronger than anything else in the park.

{{journal:orange_m0c}} {{tables:VALENCIA_PARK}}

The Paralyze Heal and the Antidote are hidden. The signs remind you that shiny Pokémon are much rarer than the usual colours, and that you can't catch a Pokémon that belongs to another trainer.

The Vileplume

Professor Ivy and Brock are waiting quietly at the edge of a clearing, where the wild Vileplume mark their ground with pollen. Talk to the professor and keep still behind them.

The Vileplume circle the clearing and start giving off Stun Spore. Then Professor Ivy's Raticate dashes in, right into the spores, and the professor runs after it. Both of them are caught by the Stun Spore. Brock hurries everyone back to the Pokémon Center.

Pokémon Center

{{journal:orange_m0d}}

Nurse Joy has looked after Professor Ivy and her Raticate, and they just need some rest. The professor thanks you for helping in the park and gives you a Leaf Stone, which makes some of Valencia Park's Pokémon evolve. Then she heads back to the lab with Brock.

Back at the lab, Brock is happily cooking and cleaning, and wishes you luck.

The blimp over Tangelo

The way on is over the sea, and you can't Surf yet.

{{journal:orange_m0e}}

Go to the airport. A mechanic inside says the blimp to Kanto can't fly until it's fixed, and a traveller has heard that someone around the airport is crazy about parachuting. Talk to the blimp clerk: the blimp to Kanto is grounded, but the one to Goldenrod City in Johto flies right over Tangelo Island. The clerk lets you strap on a parachute, as long as you don't tell the bosses.

You climb aboard, and over Tangelo Island you jump. You land in Pokémon Park.

Later visits. The clerk's bosses find out about the jump, so there are no more parachutes. Once you're the Orange League Champion, the blimp to Kanto is fixed, and it takes you to Vermilion City. Later in the story, Tracey comes to help Professor Ivy at the lab, and he has news of a new island for a trainer who has caught plenty of Pokémon.
