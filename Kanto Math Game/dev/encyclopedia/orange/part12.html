Route 73

Route 73 leads north from Pummelo Island, then east to Tarroco Island. Nothing bars the way, so you can sail here before you face Drake too: trainers on Pummelo say the islands to the north-east are good for extra training. Its Cooltrainers have five Pokémon each.

{{tables:ORANGE_ROUTE_73}} {{trainers:ORANGE_ROUTE_73}}
Celebi, Lv 50. Once you're the Orange League Champion, a tiny light flits over the sea from Tarroco's woods: it's Celebi. Beat it and you can try to catch it. It only shows up once.

Tarroco Island

Tarroco Island is a quiet little island, with not much for a trainer except a Pokémon Center. Officer Jenny says poachers used to hunt the herds of Lapras round here, but most of them have been arrested and the sea is calm now. A Bug Catcher sometimes sees a herd of Lapras swim right past.

{{tables:TARROCO_ISLAND}}

The houses

An old man used to live on Hamlin Island, to the east. An energy experiment went wrong there, Electrode took over the town and everyone had to leave. In another house, two people want to trade:

  • A trader gives you his Magneton, holding a Metal Coat, for a Marowak.
  • A girl gives you her Clefairy, holding a Lucky Egg, for a Jigglypuff.

Route 74

Route 74 runs east from Tarroco Island to Hamlin Island. Two of the swimmers here have a Lapras on their team.

Hamlin Island

Hamlin Island, the northern gateway to the islands, stands empty. Its Pokémon Center, Poké Mart and harbour are all locked, with notices saying they're closed until the Electrode are gone. Something keeps exploding in the streets...

{{journal:orange_s21a}} {{tables:HAMLIN_ISLAND}}

The Electrode of Hamlin

Walk into town and there's a loud BOOM. An angry Electrode comes rolling up the street.

Electrode, Lv 40. It rolls up the street after the explosion. Beat it and you can try to catch it.

Walk back to where the explosion was, and more Electrode roll out from between the buildings and surround you. Then someone shouts at you not to move, and Diglett and Dugtrio pop up all round you and dig the Electrode away under the street. Their trainer is Poncho, the Diglett Rancher. He explains that the Electrode took over after the energy experiment went wrong, and it's his job to clear them out safely. His Diglett are taking them to Hamlin Fields, east of town, and he asks you to come and see.

After Hamlin Fields, Poncho is back in town. His Diglett often dig up useful things, and he gives you what they found on Hamlin: a Scope Lens. The Pokémon Center, the Poké Mart and the harbour open again, and people start coming back.

Hamlin Harbor

A traveller hoped to take the ferry back to Kanto from here, but it doesn't run any more. The clerk says the ferry isn't running for now. Later in your journey, a special ticket gets you on board (see New Island).

Hamlin Fields

Hamlin Fields lies east of the town: wide, open grassland with Pokémon Rangers and other trainers about. Across the fields, the Electrode roll about in the grass, calm and happy. Poncho tells you the wide open space is perfect for them. Here they relax, and they stop exploding! Then he hurries off to tell everyone the good news.

Shamouti Island

Shamouti Island is the heart of the islands, at the west end of Route 67. Three little islands lie off its shore: Fire Island, Lightning Island and Ice Island. Old signs by the shrine on the south side tell an ancient prophecy: if fire, ice and lightning ever clash, the guardian of the sea will rise to calm them, but its song alone isn't enough. A treasure from each island must rest on the altar, which has three hollows shaped like spheres.

You can visit the island before the title. The people are getting ready for the Legend Festival, the biggest party in the islands, and no one goes to the three islands without a very good reason.

{{journal:orange_s13b}} {{tables:SHAMOUTI_ISLAND}}

Around the island

  • A Kindler says nobody needs a move that hot with water everywhere, and gives you TM Overheat.
  • A trader gives you his Raichu, holding a Focus Band, for a Dodrio.
  • Slowking looks after the shrine on the south side.
  • The Poké Mart sells Great Balls, Ultra Balls, Hyper Potions, Revives, Full Heals, Escape Ropes and Max Repels.

The Legend Festival

Once you're the Champion, the festival is starting.

{{journal:orange_m16a}}

Walk into town and Misty and Tracey say hello, just as three islanders in strange masks walk up. They're festival costumes! One of the women says her little sister Melody is the Festival Maiden this year, and since you're a Pokémon trainer, the old legend says you're the Chosen One. It's only for the tourists, really. Melody decides you'll do, and invites you to the banquet at the Festival House on the south side of the island.

At the Festival House, Melody dances and plays her ocarina, telling the legend. Then she spells it out: bring the spheres from Fire, Ice and Lightning Island to the shrine. The Chosen Ones always start on Fire Island. Now the caves to Fire Island and Lightning Island are open.

Fire Island

Fire Island's cave is on Shamouti's east side. The cave climbs up to the summit, where the Fire Sphere rests on its altar.

{{journal:orange_m16b}} {{tables:FIRE_ISLAND}}

Fire Island Cave

Big rocks block the paths through the cave. Look for secret switches: pressing one shakes the cave and moves some of the rocks out of the way.

Fire Island Summit

Take the Fire Sphere from its altar. On your way back, Melody catches up with you. The weather keeps changing, she says, as if the elements have fallen out of balance. Then Zapdos, the bird of lightning, sweeps over the summit, though this is Moltres's home. A giant flying ship looms over the island, and a beam of light pulls Zapdos up inside. The beam sweeps over you and Melody, and the ground drops away...

The Flying Palace

You and Melody are inside the giant flying ship.

{{journal:orange_m16c}}

In the hall, Zapdos and Moltres are locked in cages. A carving on the wall warns never to disturb the harmony of fire, ice and lightning. Their owner walks in: Lawrence III, a collector who began with a single Mew card. With Articuno, his set would be complete, and when his computer says Articuno is coming, he goes off to catch it.

Melody finds the cage controls and opens them. Moltres and Zapdos fight their way free, the whole palace shakes, and it starts to crash. When you come round, you're lying on the ground, and the flying palace slides over the edge of a ravine and is gone.

Lightning Island

The palace crashed on Lightning Island. Melody is with you, and above you Moltres, Zapdos and Articuno fly past. If the three birds fight, the whole world will pay for it. Find the Lightning Sphere at the top of the island, fast.

{{journal:orange_m16d}} {{tables:LIGHTNING_ISLAND}}

Big rocks block some of the paths. Look for a secret switch to clear the way up to the summit, and take the Lightning Sphere from its altar.

Melody wants to get off the island quickly, but overhead the three birds clash in a storm of fire, ice and lightning. Then a deep, gentle song rolls in over the sea, and a mysterious force lifts you off your feet.

Shamouti Island (again): the shrine

You land in front of Shamouti's shrine. Snow is falling and the sea has frozen over.

{{journal:orange_m16e}}

Slowking asks you to set your spheres on the altar. The Fire and Lightning Spheres glow in their hollows, but the third stays dark. Over the frozen sea, Moltres, Zapdos and Articuno clash. Lugia, the great guardian of the sea, rises through the ice, cries out, and sinks back under the waves.

Melody realises that Lugia's cry sounds just like the song from the legend, and plays it on her ocarina. Lugia's strength comes back. It tells you it can only calm the three birds when all three spheres are together, and that the Ice Sphere will only glow for the Chosen One. With the sea frozen, you can now walk to Ice Island, the southernmost of the three.

Before you go, Tracey offers to heal your Pokémon.

Ice Island

Ice Island's cave is on Shamouti's south shore. The rocks that blocked the way are gone now.

{{journal:orange_m16f}} {{tables:ICE_ISLAND}}

Climb to the summit and take the Ice Sphere. In your hands, it starts to glow like the others. Carry it back to the shrine on Shamouti.

Lugia's song

Set the Ice Sphere on the altar, and all three spheres blaze with light. The storm breaks, and the ice and snow melt away. The six pillars round the shrine light up one by one, Lugia rises from the sea and sings, and the three birds fall silent and fly home. Lugia thanks you, Chosen One. Slowking, Melody, Misty and Tracey are all thrilled, and Misty and Tracey say it's time they went home.

The legendary birds

With the world in harmony again, the three birds have gone home to their islands, and Lugia waits too.

{{journal:orange_s22a}}
Moltres, Lv 50. At the summit of Fire Island. Beat it and you can try to catch it.
Zapdos, Lv 50. At the summit of Lightning Island. Beat it and you can try to catch it.
Articuno, Lv 50. At the summit of Ice Island. Beat it and you can try to catch it.
Lugia, Lv 50. The guardian of the sea rests at the summit of Ice Island, where the last sphere lay. Beat it and you can try to catch it.

New Island

After Lugia's song, Tracey goes to work for Professor Ivy in her lab on Valencia Island. Visit him there.

{{journal:orange_s23a}}

Tracey tells you to keep filling your Pokédex. Once you've caught 100 kinds of Kanto Pokémon, he tells you a new island has been found in the Orange Islands. Very strong Pokémon live there, so nobody has explored it properly. He gives you a MysticTicket: show it at Hamlin Harbor and the ferry takes you to New Island.

{{tables:NEW_ISLAND}}

New Island Lab

A lab stands on the island. Barriers block its halls, and statues of Pokémon hide secret switches that move them. Each switch moves some barriers and puts others back, so you may need to press them more than once. Upstairs on 2F there's a Master Ball. The way leads on to the Back Room.

Mewtwo, Lv 60. In the lab's Back Room. Beat it and you can try to catch it. Bring your strongest team and plenty of healing.

A sailor on the shore takes you back to Hamlin Harbor.

Orange Crew rematches

As Champion, you can battle the Orange Crew and Drake again, as often as you like. They use the same Pokémon and moves as before, but every one of them is many levels higher. You don't win the badges again.

  • Cissy on Mikan Island: her Gym starts again from its first trainer.
  • Danny at the top of Navel Mountain.
  • Rudy in the Trovita Island Gym.
  • Luana in the Kumquat Hotel's Battle Arena.
  • Drake in Pummelo Stadium.
